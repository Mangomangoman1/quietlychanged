Frequently Asked Questions
What is ESPN Unlimited? How is it different from ESPN Select (formerly ESPN+)?
ESPN Unlimited is a direct-to-consumer streaming service that allows you to stream all ESPN networks, including ESPN, ESPN2, ESPNU, SEC Network, ACC Network, ESPNEWS, ESPN Deportes - in addition to ESPN on ABC, ESPN+, SEC Network+, ACC Network Extra, and now NFL Network. With ESPN Unlimited you can watch sports like NFL, NBA, NHL, MLB, tennis and golf majors, WWE, WWE NXT, NASCAR, SEC, ACC and Big 12 football and basketball. Subscribers also get postseason coverage from the NFL, NBA, WNBA, NHL, College Football Playoff, and more, as well as studio shows like SportsCenter, First Take and College GameDay.
ESPN Select plan subscribers have access to ESPN+, with top-tier soccer, PGA TOUR, NHL, college sports, and a selection of other ESPN programming.
There are two available bundle plans – ESPN Select and ESPN Unlimited. Disney+ offers both within the following bundle plans:

How and where can I access Hulu titles on Disney+?
If you’re a Disney+ subscriber in the United States, you can stream a limited selection of Hulu titles on Disney+. Access the Hulu hub on the Disney+ homepage to preview the Hulu on Disney+ experience.
If you’re a bundle subscriber or you subscribe to both Disney+ and Hulu (as standalone services with the same email address), you can stream a more extensive collection of Hulu titles on Disney+. You can use the Hulu hub on the Disney+ homepage to jump right into streaming some of your favorite Hulu Originals, series, and more. Click to view a
list of supported devices
.
Eligible Disney+ and Hulu subscribers can watch a wide variety of Hulu’s streaming library on Disney+. Certain Hulu titles are available only via the Hulu app. In addition, the Hulu app will continue to be the streaming destination for Hulu’s entire streaming library, all Hulu premium add-on content as well as any content associated with a Live TV subscription.
How and where can I access ESPN on Disney+?
Subscribers with any linked ESPN plan can now stream live content from ESPN on Disney+.
Once accounts are linked, to access ESPN content on Disney+, follow these steps:
Open the Disney+ app or website
Choose the ESPN icon on the Disney+ homepage to enter the ESPN hub
You can also find select ESPN content by using the Search feature or by browsing the different rows on the Disney+ homepage.
Once you’re in the ESPN hub, you can filter through Sports, Teams, Leagues, and Tournament pages. You can also search for any of those categories. Note — the sports content available to you to depends on your plan.
Not sure if your accounts are linked? You can learn about how you can authenticate with your pay-tv provider
here
.
What are Playlists on Disney+?
Playlists are a new way to discover and watch content on Disney+. Each Playlist is a curated collection of movies and episodes grouped around a theme, franchise, character, genre, or mood, making it easy to jump right into something you'll enjoy. Simply select a Playlist and let the content play continuously, with the flexibility to pause, skip and rewind. Playlists can be found on the Disney+ Home.
What happened to Streams?
In the U.S., Playlists are replacing Streams, while ABC News will remain available as a livestream experience.
