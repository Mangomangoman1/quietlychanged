Sharing your Disney+ subscription
May 20, 2026
Disney+ cannot be shared outside of your Household. A Household
is a collection of devices associated with your primary personal residence that are used by the individuals who reside there. People outside of your Household can sign up for their own account
to watch Disney+ or can be added on as an Extra Member
.

How do I set up or update my Household?
Disney+ will automatically detect and establish your Household based on your subscription activity, linked devices, and internet connection among other factors – learn more about how we establish your Household
.
If your TV-connected devices
are used outside the Household, you may see a message about devices not being in Household
. If you see “This TV doesn’t seem to be part of the Disney+ Household for this account” , select UPDATE HOUSEHOLD to update your current location as your new Household. A one-time passcode will be sent to the email address on the account to confirm this update.
If you did not intend to update your Disney+ Household to your current location, you can select UPDATE HOUSEHOLD the next time you see the message when you’re at home. Please keep in mind that there may be a limit to the number of times you can update your Household.

How do I connect a device to my Household?
Set up your new device by simply logging in
to Disney+ and watching from home.
Once logged in, your device will be linked to your Disney+ subscription – and by connecting to your home internet and streaming, your device will be recognized as part of your Household.
If you would like to manage the devices connected to your account, you can log out of one or all devices
.

How do I add someone outside my Household to my subscription?
Account holders who want to add someone outside of their Household to their Disney+ subscription will be able to do so with the Disney+ Extra Member add-on
. Please refer to Disney+ pricing
to learn more about Extra Member pricing and plan features.
As an alternative to Extra Member, people outside of your Household can sign up for their own account
to watch Disney+.
Keep in mind that you can transfer a profile
to a new subscription to keep that profile’s Disney+ watch history and settings. Please note that primary profiles (account holders), profiles for viewers under 18 years of age, or profiles set to Junior Mode may not be transferred.

I’m getting a message about sharing my subscription or my device not being connected to my Household
TV-connected devices
If a TV-connected device
is not recognized as part of your Household, you may see one of the following messages when streaming Disney+:
“Sharing Disney+ outside your Household”
If a device is not part of the Household, you can choose to LOG OUT or create a new subscription
If a device is part of the Household, you can clear the message by selecting CONTINUE and verifying the device with a one-time passcode that’s sent to the email address on the account
“This TV doesn’t seem to be part of the Disney+ Household for this account”
If a device is not part of the Household, you can choose CREATE ACCOUNT to sign up for a new Disney+ subscription
If a device is part of the Household, you can clear the message by choosing from the options below and then following the directions on your TV:
Select the I’M AWAY FROM HOME option if you’re currently away
Select UPDATE HOUSEHOLD to reset the Household location for your Disney+ subscription
Keep in mind that by updating your Disney+ Household, your current location will become your new Household and that there may be a limit to the number of times you can update your Household.
Mobile devices
If a mobile device
is not recognized as part of your Household, you may see one of the following messages when streaming Disney+:
“Watching Disney+ outside your Household?”
If your device is not part of the Household, you can choose to LOG OUT or CREATE ACCOUNT
If your device is part of the Household, you can clear the message by selecting CONTINUE and choosing from the options below then following the directions on your device:
Select VERIFY DEVICE to verify by connecting to your Home Wi-Fi
Select SEND CODE and verify the device with a one-time passcode that’s sent to the email address on the account.
“This device isn’t connected to your Disney+ Household”
If a device is not part of the Household, you can choose CREATE ACCOUNT to sign up for a new Disney+ subscription
If a device is part of the Household, you can clear the message by choosing from the options below and then following the directions on your device:
Verify the device by selecting CONNECT TO HOUSEHOLD and following the instructions on your screen to verify the device
Select the I’M AWAY FROM HOME option if you’re currently away
NOTE: The above selections will require a one-time passcode that’s sent to the email address associated with the account.

Can I watch Disney+ outside of my Household?
If you’re away from home or on the go, you’ll still be able to watch Disney+ on your supported devices
. Just take your mobile device with you or log in
on a new TV-connected device wherever you’re staying.
If you see the message “This TV doesn’t seem to be part of the Household for this account” on a TV-connected device or “This device isn’t connected to your Disney+ Household” on a mobile device, you can select I’M AWAY FROM HOME . Alternatively, if you’re away for an extended period of time such as winter or summer holidays, you can also choose to update your Household.
NOTE: Please keep in mind that there may be limits on the number of times you can update your Household or choose that you are away from home. These selections will require a one-time passcode that’s sent to the email address associated with the account.

Notice of Right to Opt Out of Sale/Sharing
Some US states provide residents (or their authorized agents
) with the right to opt out of “targeted advertising,” “selling,” or “sharing” of personal information, as defined by applicable laws. You can move the toggle to the left and click “Submit” to opt out of such activities occurring through this digital property. If you are logged in, we may be able to associate your choice with your account and apply it to data sharing occurring through this digital property (across your browsers and devices) as well as other digital properties of The Walt Disney Family of Companies. You can confirm the status of your choice or opt out or in by clicking “Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information” or “Your Privacy Choices” on each property.

If you are not logged in, your opt-out will be specific to this property and device or browser. For our other digital properties, to opt out, select “Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information” or “Your Privacy Choices” on each. If you clear your tracking history or use another browser or device, you’ll need to opt out again.
You can also submit this opt-out form

You may enable online, where available, a universal tool that automatically communicates your opt-out, such as Global Privacy Control (“GPC”), which we’ll process as if you had opted out through the toggle.
Even if you opt out, you may continue to see advertising, including ads that may be based on personal information processed before you opted out. Please visit Your US State Privacy Rights
for more information about your rights and how to exercise them.
