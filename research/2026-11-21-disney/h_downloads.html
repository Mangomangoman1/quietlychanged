Downloads on Disney+

Jun 04, 2026

Download Disney+ content on your supported mobile device so you can watch on the go when you don’t have an internet connection. If you’re having trouble downloading or playing downloaded content, check out these download troubleshooting tips.

 

Keep in mind that you must be a subscriber of one of the following plans or bundles to download Disney+ content: 

 

  • Disney+ Premium 
  • Disney+, Hulu Bundle Premium 
  • Disney+, Hulu, ESPN Bundle Premium*
  • Disney+, Hulu, ESPN Select Bundle Legacy* 

Disney+ subscribers or subscribers with a linked standalone Hulu Premium subscription are able to download select Hulu content. Please keep in mind Hulu content may be limited to 25 downloads across five supported mobile devices at a time. Subscribers with eligible plans can also download Hulu content by using the Hulu app. 

 

NOTE: At the moment, Disney+ content cannot be downloaded on a TV-connected device, computer, or Windows 10 & 11 tablet.


*Please note the ESPN app does not offer downloads of ESPN content at this time. However, bundle subscribers with ESPN, or Disney+ subscribers with a linked standalone ESPN subscription, may be able to download certain ESPN content (if available) through Disney+.

Downloading Disney+ content

To download Disney+ content to your supported mobile device:

 

  1. Launch the Disney+ app
  2. Locate and select the content you want to download
  3. Tap the Download button (located on the episodes page for TV series or below the play button for movies) to begin downloading

    • Once started, you can view the progress of your download
    • Tap the download progress icon to pause or remove the partial download

  4. A device icon with a check mark will display when your download is complete

 

Downloaded content will remain on your device as long as you are an active Disney+ subscriber and connect to the internet with your device at least once every 30 days. You can download content on up to 10 supported mobile devices with available storage space.

 

Please note that some downloaded content may remain on your devices for a limited amount of time and some titles may be unavailable for download from Disney+. 

 

Keep in mind that logging out of your Disney+ account will remove all downloaded content from your device. If you wish to watch any previously downloaded content, you will need to download it again when you log back in.

Download settings and available device storage

To select the quality of downloaded content and view the available storage space on your supported mobile device:

 

  1. Launch the Disney+ app
  2. Tap the profile icon 
  3. Select App Settings
  4. Available device storage space is displayed at the bottom
  5. Select Video Quality or Download Quality and check Standard, Medium, or High

    • Standard requires the least storage and is fastest to download
    • Medium requires less storage than High and is faster to download
    • High requires the most storage and is slowest to download

Watching downloads offline

You can find all Disney+ content currently downloaded to your supported mobile device by selecting the Downloads icon on the Home screen. To play downloaded content on your device and watch offline:

 

  1. Launch the Disney+ app
  2. Tap the Downloads icon
    • Movies (or other single event): Press play on the title you want to watch
    • Episodic content: Tap the series, then press play on the episode you want to watch

Deleting downloaded content from your device

To delete downloaded movies, episodes and other Disney+ content from your device:

 

  1. Launch the Disney+ app
  2. Tap the Downloads icon

    • Movies (or other single event): Tap the device icon with a check mark next to the title you wish to delete
    • Episodic content: Tap the series, then tap the device icon with a check mark next to the title you wish to delete

  3. Select Remove Download to delete the content from your device


TIP: To remove multiple downloads at once, tap Edit, select the titles to delete, and tap the trash icon.

 

Keep in mind that logging out of your Disney+ account will remove all downloaded content from your device. If you wish to watch any previously downloaded content, you will need to download it again when you log back in.

Which devices can I use to download content?

You can download content onto the following mobile devices:

 

  • Apple iPhones and iPads
  • Apple Vision Pro
  • Android phones and tablets
  • Amazon Fire tablets
  • Meta Quest

 

Please note that at the moment, Disney+ content cannot be downloaded on TV-connected devices, computers, or Windows 10 & 11 tablets.
