What is an Extra Member?
The Extra Member add-on allows you to add a family member or friend to your subscription, even if they are not a part of your Household
. A Disney+ or bundle subscription can have one Extra Member. Please note that Extra Member is not currently available for purchase with a Disney+, Hulu, ESPN Unlimited Bundle plan , the Disney+, Hulu, ESPN Unlimited Bundle Premium plan , or one of the Disney+, Hulu, Max Bundle plans . If you choose to change to one of these bundle plans, the Extra Member Add-on will be removed.
The Extra Member must be at least 18 years old, live in the same country/region as the account holder, and cannot have an active Disney+, Hulu, or ESPN subscription.
NOTE: Extra Member is billed monthly to the account holder. The Extra Member will not be able to make changes to the service tiers or any other account options, other than removing themselves as an Extra Member.

What are the benefits of Extra Member?
Extra Members can enjoy the following benefits:
Access to thousands of shows and movies, including award-winning Originals
A unique profile
with their own login and password
Ability to stream and download content
on one device at a time
Enjoy the same video
and audio quality
as the account holder
Create their own personalized streaming experience with Watchlist
and recommendations based on watch history

How can I add an Extra Member to my Disney+ or Disney Bundle subscription?
If you are the account holder, and you are billed directly by Disney+, you can choose to add an Extra Member here
or by following the steps below:
Go to DisneyPlus.com
on a mobile or web browser and log in
Select your Profile
Select Account
Select Invite an Extra Member in the Your Plans and Billing section
Review the terms and select Continue
Review the changes to your plan and select Agree & Subscribe
Enter the email address of the Extra Member and select Send Invite
Choose the existing Profile for the Extra Member or choose New Profile if they’re new to the subscription
Select Next to send the invite
On the confirmation page, select Got it! to complete the process
If you have a bundle subscription billed by Hulu, you can add an Extra Member here
or visit the Hulu Help Center for instructions on how to add an Extra Member to a bundle subscription
.
*Extra Member is not currently available for purchase with a Disney+, Hulu, ESPN Unlimited Bundle plan, Disney+, Hulu, ESPN Unlimited Bundle Premium plan , or the Disney+, Hulu, Max Bundle plans .

How can I be added as an Extra Member to a Disney+ or Disney Bundle subscription?
If you have a friend or family member with a Disney+ subscription, you can ask them to add you as an Extra Member using the steps above. As an Extra Member, you will gain access to much of the same content and features available to the account holder, at no cost to you. You will use your own email address and password to log in, and you will have your own profile including your own personalized watch history.
If you have an existing Disney+ profile on the account holder’s subscription, the account holder can choose to transfer your existing profile on the account during the invite process so that you can retain your watch history and settings. You can choose to accept the profile or create a new profile. If you’re new to the subscription, you can create your own brand new profile. Keep in mind you will not be able to transfer a Hulu profile, and will need to create a new one.
Follow the steps below to accept an invite to become an Extra Member:
Check your inbox for an email with the subject “ You’re invited to join Disney+ as an Extra Member ”
Click the link in the email to open a page that says “ Let’s set up your Disney+ experience ”
Select Agree & Continue
Choose your existing Profile or select New Profile
Select Next
Create a password
Tick the box if you would like to receive updates, special offers and other information from The Walt Disney Family of Companies
Review the terms and select Agree & Create Account
If required, enter your date of birth and select Save & Continue
Select Continue to Disney+

Removing the add-on or removing an Extra Member
Removing the add-on
As the account holder, you may remove the Extra Member add-on
at any time. This change will take effect at the end of your current Extra Member monthly billing period. The Extra Member will lose access to the subscription(s) at the end of the Extra Member billing period. Once the removal is complete, the current Extra Member will no longer be able to access your Disney+ or bundle subscription; however, they can use their own login and billing information to create their own subscriptions
.
Removing an Extra Member
As the account holder, you may remove or replace the person you’ve added as an Extra Member at any time. However, this will not remove the Extra Member add-on, and you will continue to be charged for it until you remove the add-on from your Account page
. Additionally, the Extra Member may remove themselves
from their seat at any time or sign up for their own subscription
. Removing or replacing an Extra Member takes effect immediately, and the seat will remain vacant until a new Extra Member is selected.
