Who is eligible for the Disney+, Hulu Student Bundle Discount?
Students currently enrolled at a U.S. university or post-secondary degree or certificate-granting, accredited institution of education, who meet verification qualifications. For current Disney+ subscribers, only those billed directly by Disney+ are eligible.
NOTE : Students will need to verify their status for the Disney+, Hulu Student Bundle Discount through SheerID. For questions related to student verification, please contact SheerID
.

How do I sign up for the Disney+, Hulu Student Bundle Discount?
Eligible new subscribers can sign up for the Disney+, Hulu Student Bundle Discount by following the steps below:
Visit DisneyPlus.com/student
on a mobile or web browser
Click CLAIM YOUR DISCOUNT
Under Create Your Account , enter your information
You’ll be redirected to SheerID to provide additional details and verify your student status
You will need to verify your student status for the Disney+, Hulu Student Bundle Discount through SheerID.
Once your student status is verified, select Continue
Add your billing information, and you’re ready to start streaming!
Current Subscribers If you’re a current subscriber and eligible for this discount, you can sign up by following the steps below:
Visit DisneyPlus.com/student
on a mobile or web browser
Click CLAIM YOUR DISCOUNT
Login to your account
Use SheerID to enter and verify your student information
You will need to verify your student status for the Disney+, Hulu Student Bundle Discount through SheerID
Once verified, select Continue to review your billing information
Click Submit and you’re done!

Can I change my plan or sign up for add-ons?
You can change your plan at any time, however, the student discount only applies to the Disney+, Hulu bundle plan. If you’d like to change to a premium plan or a different bundle
, the discounted price will not apply.
You can change back to the Disney+, Hulu Student Bundle Discount plan as long as you are still eligible and verified as a valid student.
Additionally, subscribers with a student discount plan are able to sign up for add-ons at any time. You can add eligible add-ons from your Account page
.

I’m having trouble redeeming the Disney+, Hulu Student Bundle Discount
If you’re having trouble redeeming the discount due to errors from SheerID, please contact SheerID
for support.
Still need help? If none of the information above applies to you, please contact us for additional support.
