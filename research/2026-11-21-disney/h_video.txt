Mar 20, 2026

Which video formats are available?
Disney+ offers a growing library of content that supports high-quality streaming. The following video quality options may be available, depending on the title and streaming device:
Full HD: Full HD video features a 1080p resolution and a higher definition image compared to standard HD (720p).
4K UHD & HDR: This format displays a 3840 x 2160 pixel resolution and a clearer picture than full HD video.
HDR10: HDR10 is the default HDR format. It stands for High Dynamic Range, which displays a broader range of colors and brightness.
Dolby Vision: Dolby Vision is a more dynamic version of HDR. It adjusts the colors and brightness so each frame of video is optimized for your screen.
IMAX Enhanced
: * This content features an expanded aspect ratio of 1.90:1 which allows the picture to cover the full height of the screen.
3D: Some content is available in 3D only on Apple Vision Pro
**
*Please note that IMAX Enhanced on Disney+ only includes an expanded aspect ratio and enhanced sound with DTS:X. Other IMAX Enhanced features and functionality are not currently available on Disney+.
**If you’d like to watch Hulu content through the Disney+ app on Vision Pro
, you must be subscribed to a plan that includes Hulu Premium. Hulu (With Ads) is not currently available to stream on Vision Pro, and the Hulu app is not currently available for this device.

Which shows and movies support advanced video formats?
To see which video formats are available for each title:
Select the show or movie you want to watch
Navigate to the Details tab
Some movies will also have a Versions tab available, where you can access both the Widescreen and IMAX Enhanced versions
To quickly find content with high video quality options, you can do any of the following:
Go to the 4K Ultra HD HDR collection on the Home page
Use the Ultra HD and HDR filter on the movies and series pages
Use the IMAX Enhanced filter on the Movies page to see all movies available in IMAX’s expanded aspect ratio.
To locate 3D content on
Apple Vision Pro
, you can select the
3D Collection icon
from the navigation bar. 3D content will automatically play in 3D, however if you would like to view 3D content in 2D, you can go to
Settings >
App Settings and turn off the
Watch in 3D toggle.

For your convenience, Disney+ automatically displays the highest quality video your device can support.
Support for 4K UHD & HDR**
, and HDR10 can vary based on your device’s make and model. Please check with the manufacturer to determine if your device is compatible with these formats.
All Disney+

*Disney+ (With Ads) is currently limited to 1080p on Xbox. Apple TV devices support Disney+ (With Ads) in HDR on iOS 16.2 and later.

How do I change my video quality settings?
Disney+ detects your system’s capabilities and will automatically stream in the best video format your device supports. Movies available in the IMAX Enhanced format will automatically play in IMAX’s expanded aspect ratio during the full movie (if available) or during certain sequences that were filmed or formatted for IMAX.
Alternatively, you can also manually adjust your data usage
to change video streaming quality on Disney+ using any of your compatible devices. To find out how video quality affects your device’s data usage, learn about managing data usage settings
.

How do I troubleshoot low video quality?
There are a few different factors that determine video quality. Try the following steps to improve your streaming experience:
Confirm that the title you are watching is available in your preferred video format
on Disney+
Verify that your TV, device, or display is capable of streaming high-quality video content
Avoid streaming through an in-line video or game recorder — use of these devices may prevent playback or limit the video quality in order to protect from piracy
Check your device’s
data usage settings
— a lower data usage setting will deliver lower visual quality
Make sure your internet connection meets our
speed recommendations
