Disney+ offers different plan options
to fit a variety of budgets and entertainment needs. If you’re interested in changing your plan
, you can manage your Disney+ plan, billing, and settings from your Account page
. If you are a new subscriber, you can choose your plan when you sign up for Disney+
.
Access to certain features will vary depending on your Disney+ plan
.. Please note that the plans available to you may vary if you’re billed by Hulu or a third party
. Additionally, some billing partners may determine which Disney+ subscription is included in the packages they offer. If you would like to change your plan, you will need to contact your billing partner directly.
IMPORTANT : By changing your plan, you will lose the benefit of any current promotion or price hold.

How do I change my Disney+ subscription?
To make changes to your Disney+ subscription:
Log in to DisneyPlus.com
through a mobile or web browser
Select your profile
Select Account
Under the Your Plans & Billing section, select the subscription that you want to change
If you are billed by a third party,* you will see a link to complete the change through your billing partner, if available
Select Change next to the name of your subscription
Choose the plan you want to change to, and select CONTINUE
Confirm that you have read and agree to the Disney+ Subscriber Agreement
Review your billing details and then select AGREE & SUBSCRIBE
If you are billed by a third party,* you may need to click the button with the new price listed to complete the transaction
Plan change options can vary by third-party partners — please refer to the billing partner's policies and site for more details. Any questions related to plan changing and related billing should be inquired with the relevant third party billing partner.
After that, you’ll receive a confirmation email, and your Account page will reflect the change to your new subscription.
*If you currently pay for Disney+ through Apple, Google, or Amazon, please go to Apple Support
, Google Play Help
, or Amazon Help
for instructions on how to manage your subscription. Please note that certain plan options may not be available to you. If you no longer wish to be billed by Apple, Google, or Amazon, you can cancel your existing subscription and sign up directly at DisneyPlus.com
.

When will I be charged for my new subscription?
Changing to a higher-tiered plan: If you're changing to a higher-tiered plan, the change takes effect immediately. You will be charged a prorated amount for the first month or year of your new plan. Moving forward, you will remain on your new plan for the life of your subscription and will be charged in accordance with the then applicable price of this plan.
Changing to a lower-tiered plan: If you're changing to a lower-tiered plan, the change takes effect on your next billing date.
Changing between monthly and annual payment: Changing between monthly and annual billing for the same plan will take effect on your next billing date. If you have an annual plan billed directly by Disney+, you also have the option to change to any bundle plan, and you will receive a prorated billing credit for your monthly plan, which takes effect on your next billing date.
If you need to change your billing cycle, contact us
to see what options may be available.

Notice of Right to Opt Out of Sale/Sharing
Some US states provide residents (or their authorized agents
) with the right to opt out of “targeted advertising,” “selling,” or “sharing” of personal information, as defined by applicable laws. You can move the toggle to the left and click “Submit” to opt out of such activities occurring through this digital property. If you are logged in, we may be able to associate your choice with your account and apply it to data sharing occurring through this digital property (across your browsers and devices) as well as other digital properties of The Walt Disney Family of Companies. You can confirm the status of your choice or opt out or in by clicking “Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information” or “Your Privacy Choices” on each property.
