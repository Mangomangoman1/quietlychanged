Signing up for Disney+
Sep 03, 2025
So you’re ready to stream your favorite shows and movies? You’ve come to the right place! Sign up for Disney+ to start watching all the latest original and classic titles from Disney, Marvel, Star Wars, Pixar, and National Geographic.
You can sign up, change
your subscription, or cancel
at any time, with cancelation taking effect at the end of your billing period.
TIP: The email address and password you use when signing up will be your MyDisney login
. With MyDisney, you can use one email address and password to log in to services and experiences across The Walt Disney Family of Companies
, such as Disney+, ESPN, Walt Disney World, and more.
To sign up for Disney+, select the device you’re using and we’ll guide you through the steps:
How to sign up for Disney+ on a web browser
How to sign up for Disney+ on a mobile device
How to sign up for Disney+ on a TV-Connected device
How to sign up for Disney+ on a web browser
Sign up for Disney+
Go to DisneyPlus.com
and sign up
Enter your email address and a password
If you have an account with another service within The Walt Disney Family of Companies
, enter your existing login information to keep your logins connected
Choose your subscription type and enter your payment information
Select START WATCHING
After signing up, you can also choose to set up MyDisney Wallet
on your Account page
and store your card information for future payments within The Walt Disney Family of Companies
.

How to sign up for Disney+ on a mobile device
Download the Disney+ app from your device’s app store
Open the Disney+ app and sign up
Enter your email address and a password
If you have an account with another service within The Walt Disney Family of Companies
, enter your existing login information to keep your logins connected
Choose your subscription type
Make an in-app purchase*
* New and returning subscribers are no longer able to sign up and pay for Disney+ through Apple, Google, or Amazon. However, you can see all plans and sign up directly on DisneyPlus.com
and can still watch Disney+ content on supported Apple, Android, and Amazon devices
. These changes do not impact existing Apple-billed, Google-billed, or Amazon-billed subscribers.

How to sign up for Disney+ on a TV-Connected device
Depending on your TV-connected device, you will sign up in one of the following ways:
Go to the app store on your TV-connected device
Download the Disney+ app
Open the Disney+ app and sign up
Enter your email address and a password
If you have an account with another service within The Walt Disney Family of Companies
, enter your existing login information to keep your logins connected
Make an in-app purchase*
Select START WATCHING
If your device directs you to navigate to a URL, use the following steps:
Go to the app store on your TV-connected device
Download the Disney+ app
Open the Disney+ app and sign up
Enter the URL into your browser on a computer or mobile device
Enter the 8-digit code you see on your TV screen
Enter your email address and create a password
Enter your payment information; review your order and pay
Your device is now synced and you can start streaming!
Learn more about the devices and platforms
supported by Disney+.

and can still watch Disney+ content on supported Apple, Android, and Amazon devices
. These changes do not impact existing Apple-billed, Google-billed, or Amazon-billed subscribers.

Notice of Right to Opt Out of Sale/Sharing
Some US states provide residents (or their authorized agents
) with the right to opt out of “targeted advertising,” “selling,” or “sharing” of personal information, as defined by applicable laws. You can move the toggle to the left and click “Submit” to opt out of such activities occurring through this digital property. If you are logged in, we may be able to associate your choice with your account and apply it to data sharing occurring through this digital property (across your browsers and devices) as well as other digital properties of The Walt Disney Family of Companies. You can confirm the status of your choice or opt out or in by clicking “Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information” or “Your Privacy Choices” on each property.
Manage Consent Preferences
Selling, Sharing, Targeted Advertising
Selling, Sharing, Targeted Advertising
If you are not logged in, your opt-out will be specific to this property and device or browser. For our other digital properties, to opt out, select “Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information” or “Your Privacy Choices” on each. If you clear your tracking history or use another browser or device, you’ll need to opt out again.
You can also submit this opt-out form
, which may enable us to apply your opt-out more broadly, for example, to stop data sharing in connection with advertising intended to reach you on platforms not operated by Disney (e.g., social media platforms).
You may enable online, where available, a universal tool that automatically communicates your opt-out, such as Global Privacy Control (“GPC”), which we’ll process as if you had opted out through the toggle.
Even if you opt out, you may continue to see advertising, including ads that may be based on personal information processed before you opted out. Please visit Your US State Privacy Rights
for more information about your rights and how to exercise them.
