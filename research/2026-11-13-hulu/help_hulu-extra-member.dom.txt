What is an Extra Member?
The Extra Member add-on allows you to add a family member or friend to your subscription, even if they are not a part of your 
Household
. A Hulu or bundle subscription can have one Extra Member. The Extra Member must be at least 18 years old, live in the same country/region as the account holder, and cannot have an active Hulu, Disney+ or ESPN subscription.
NOTE:
Extra Member
 is billed monthly to the Hulu account holder. The Extra Member will not be able to make changes to the bundle service tiers or any other account options including the Extra Member add-on and will not be able to purchase any add-ons.

How can I be added as an Extra Member to a bundle subscription?
As an Extra Member, you will gain access to much of the same content and features available to the account holder, at no cost to you. You will use your own email address and password to log in, and you will have your own profile including your own personalized watch history. 
You may have a single profile on each service, and stream on one device at a time. You will not be able to access any Hulu or ESPN Select 
Add-ons
. Extra Member is not currently available with Hulu + Live TV plans. Please also note that Extra Member is not currently available for purchase with a Disney+, Hulu, ESPN Unlimited Bundle plan or Disney+, Hulu, ESPN Unlimited Bundle Premium plan.
Follow the steps below to accept an invite to become an Extra Member:
Check your inbox for an email
From the email, select 
BECOME EXTRA MEMBER
Select 
Get Started
Fill in your information and password
This email address and password are your 
MyDisney
 login details, which you can use to log in to services across 
The Walt Disney Family of Companies
Review the terms and select 
Agree & Create Account

How do I remove the add-on or remove an Extra Member?
Removing the add-on
As the account holder, you may 
remove the Extra Member add-on
 at any time. This change will take effect at the end of your current Extra Member monthly billing period. The Extra Member will lose access to the subscription at the end of the billing period for the Extra Member add-on. Once the removal is complete, the current Extra Member will no longer be able to access your subscription; however, they can use their own login and billing information to 
create their own subscription(s)
. Follow the steps below to remove the Extra Member add-on:
Go to 
Hulu.com
 on a mobile or web browser and log in
Select your 
Profile
Select 
Account
Go to the 
Extra Member
 section under 
Your Subscription
Select 
Remove Extra Member
Review the changes to your plan and select 
Submit
Removing an Extra Member
You may also remove or replace the person you added as an Extra Member at any time. However, this will not remove the Extra Member add-on and you will continue to be charged for it until you remove the add-on from your Account page. Additionally, the Extra Member may remove themselves at any time or 
sign up for their own subscription
. Removing or replacing an Extra Member takes effect immediately, and the seat will remain vacant until a new Extra Member is selected. Follow these steps to remove an Extra Member:
Go to 
Hulu.com
 on a mobile or web browser and log in
Select your 
Profile
Select 
Account
Go to the 
Extra Member
 section under 
Your Subscription
Select 
Remove
Confirm by selecting 
Remove 
again
