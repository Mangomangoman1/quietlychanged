Changing your plans and add-ons
Apr 22, 2026

Hulu subscribers can 
change plans
 or 
manage add-ons
 directly from their 
Account page
 on 
Hulu.com
. 
The steps to change your plan vary depending on your billing party. To confirm your billing party, go to your Hulu 
Account page
 and go to the 
Payment Information
 section. Then, select one of the options below:
I am billed directly by Hulu and want to make changes to my plan
I am billed by a third party and want to make changes to my plan
I am billed directly by Hulu and want to make changes to my plan
If you’re 
billed directly by Hulu
, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan by following these steps: 
Log in to your 
Account page
 on a mobile or web browser
Navigate to the 
Your Subscription
 section
Select 
MANAGE PLAN 
— your current plan will be highlighted in green
To switch to a different plan, toggle the plan you want from 
OFF
 to 
ON
Select 
MANAGE ADD-ONS 
— there will be a 
(✓) 
next to any add-ons you currently subscribe to
Select 
(+)
 next to add-ons you want to add, or toggle the 
(✓)
 to an 
(x)
 for any add-ons you’d like to remove
Select 
Review Changes
 to confirm
NOTE
: Eligible subscribers can 
update payment information
 or 
pause a subscription for up to 12 weeks
.
