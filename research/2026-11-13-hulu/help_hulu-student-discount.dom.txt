Who is eligible for the Disney+, Hulu Student Bundle Discount?
Students currently enrolled at a U.S. university or post-secondary degree or certificate-granting, accredited institution of education, who meet verification qualifications. For current Disney+ or Hulu subscribers, only those billed directly are eligible.
NOTE
: During sign up, students will need to verify their status for the Disney+, Hulu Student Bundle Discount through SheerID. For questions related to student verification, please 
contact SheerID
.

How do I sign up for the Disney+, Hulu Student Bundle Discount?
Eligible new subscribers can sign up for the Disney+, Hulu Student Bundle Discount by following the steps below:
Visit 
Hulu.com/student
 on a mobile or web browser 
Click 
CLAIM YOUR DISCOUNT
Under 
Create Your Account
, enter your information 
You’ll be redirected to SheerID to provide additional details and verify your student status 
You will need to verify your student status for the Disney+, Hulu Student Bundle Discount through SheerID.
Once your student status is verified, select 
Continue
Add your billing information, and you’re ready to start streaming!
Current Subscribers
If you’re a current Hulu subscriber and eligible for this discount, you can sign up by following the steps below:
Visit 
Hulu.com/student
 on a mobile or web browser
Click 
CLAIM YOUR DISCOUNT
Login to your account
Use SheerID to enter and verify your student information 
You will need to verify your student status for the Disney+, Hulu Student Bundle Discount through SheerID
Once verified, select 
Continue
 to review your billing information 
Click 
Submit
 and you’re done!

Can I change my plan or sign up for add-ons? 
You can change your plan at any time, however, the student discount only applies to the Disney+, Hulu bundle plan. If you’d like to change to a 
premium plan or a different bundle
, the discounted price will not apply.
You can change back to the Disney+, Hulu Student Bundle Discount plan as long as you are still eligible and verified as a valid student.
Additionally, subscribers with a student discount plan are able to sign up for add-ons at any time. You can add eligible add-ons from your 
Account page
.
I already have the Hulu Student Discount. How do I upgrade to the Disney+, Hulu Student Bundle Discount?
If you already have the Hulu Student Discount, you are able to switch to the Disney+, Hulu Student Bundle Discount. Visit 
Hulu.com/Student
 to change your current plan. Some students may need to re-verify their student status. 
IMPORTANT
: You will not be able to switch back into the Hulu Student Discount once you’ve switched out of the offer.
