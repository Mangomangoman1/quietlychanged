Why am I still seeing ads when I subscribe to Hulu Premium (No Ads)?
Nov 28, 2025
Depending on what you’re watching and the plan/add-ons you subscribe to, your content may include ads. Select from the following options for more information:
Excluded shows
Network on-demand
Cloud DVR recordings
Live TV
Live or linear channels and content
Premium or Partner Add-ons
I still need help
Excluded shows
Due to streaming rights, there may be 
select shows
 in the Hulu streaming library that will play with a short ad break before and after each episode for Hulu Premium (No Ads) subscribers. Please note that this list of shows is subject to change.
BACK TO TOP
Network on-demand
Hulu Premium subscribers can stream everything in the Hulu streaming library without ad breaks (excluding the shows that were 
previously outlined
). If you’re a 
Hulu Premium + Live TV
 subscriber, your plan unlocks additional on-demand content provided by our network partners that may have ads.
There isn’t a way to identify which videos will have ads while browsing, but if you have ideas on how we can improve this experience, submit them through our 
Feedback page
.
BACK TO TOP
Cloud DVR recordings
Cloud DVR
 recordings include the same ad breaks seen during a live broadcast, but you do have the ability to fast-forward through them. Navigate to the 
Recordings
 section in 
My Stuff
 to play your recorded content.
BACK TO TOP
Live TV
Hulu Premium + Live TV
 subscribers have access to live streams from a wide variety of networks. When you’re watching live TV on Hulu, you’ll still experience the same ad breaks as traditional broadcast television.
BACK TO TOP
Live or linear channels and content
Hulu may make available to all subscribers certain live or linear channels and content (including replays or additional viewings thereof) which contain commercial breaks. Ads may be served to Hulu Premium subscribers in these channels and content, as they are not part of the Hulu on-demand streaming library.
BACK TO TOP
Premium and Partner Add-ons
Shows and movies from 
HBO Max
, 
Paramount+ with SHOWTIME
, 
Cinemax
, and 
STARZ
 don’t have ad breaks, but a short pre-roll ad may play before some videos.
Certain content available through the 
ESPN Select (With Ads) Add-on
 will include ads.
BACK TO TOP
I still need help
If none of the previous scenarios apply to you, go to your 
Account
 page to confirm you’re subscribed to one of our ad-free plans. If everything checks out but you’re still seeing ads, 
contact us
 so we can investigate and explain.
