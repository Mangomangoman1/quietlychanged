Free trials on Hulu
Jun 15, 2026
Hulu offers free trials on select services, including Hulu + Live TV, Disney+, and ESPN for new and eligible returning subscribers. During your free trial period, you’ll have access just like any other paid subscriber would. Watch at home or on-the-go using a variety of 
supported devices
 just like any other paid subscriber would.
To get started, you’ll need to 
sign up
 first. This helps create a seamless transition into a paid subscription if you choose to stick around.

Am I eligible for a free trial?
Free trials are available on select services for new and eligible returning subscribers who have not subscribed to Hulu in the past 12 months.

How long does a free trial last?
The length of your free trial period depends on the subscription that you’re signing up for. Trial lengths for our plans and add-ons are subject to change and don’t reflect any special promotional offers. You can easily view the end date for your free trial on your 
Account page
.

How do I cancel during my free trial?
If Hulu isn’t a good fit, you’re free to cancel at any time during your trial to avoid being charged. To cancel during your free trial:
Go to your 
Account page
 on a computer or mobile browser
Select 
Cancel
 under the Your Account section and follow the on-screen instructions
Once you're done, you’ll receive a confirmation email from us
BACK TO TOP
Will I see prorated charges?
The free trial length for our plans and select add-ons vary. In some circumstances, if one trial ends before another one, or if a free trial happens to end before your next regular billing date, you will see a prorated charge from us. From there on out, your monthly recurring total will reflect the total charges for the plan and add-ons that you’ve signed up for.
BACK TO TOP
What happens after my free trial ends?
Once your free trial ends, you will automatically be charged the 
current monthly rate
 of the plan you selected when you signed up.
If you decide Hulu isn’t right for you, be sure to cancel before your trial ends. If you don’t cancel, you’ll be charged once your free trial ends, and your subscription will renew until you cancel.
