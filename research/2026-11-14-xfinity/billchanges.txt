Frequently asked questions
Why are prices changing?
Starting in December 2026, some of our prices will increase to help cover rising costs and support continued investment in our network and services. 
We know you count on us for value and service you can trust. That’s why we’re strengthening our network security and reliability to deliver the connectivity and entertainment you rely on every day. Your TV costs reflect what we pay to to carry the shows and content you enjoy, so when programming providers raise their prices, it can affect your bill. 
Sign in to your account
 and view the important information section of your bill for full details of these changes.

Are there ways to save on my Xfinity plan?
Members with Xfinity internet can save $10/mo when you sign up for Paperless Billing and Automatic Payments, by adding your bank account as the preferred payment method. Visit your account to manage your 
bill settings
.
You can also save over 25% on streaming service with your favorite StreamSaver app bundle. Visit 
Xfinity.com/streamsaver
 to learn more. 
What if I currently have a promotional price or contract?
If you have a promotional rate or a minimum-term agreement, your service prices won't change until that period ends. However, taxes, fees, and equipment charges may change during and after any promotional period or contract term.
