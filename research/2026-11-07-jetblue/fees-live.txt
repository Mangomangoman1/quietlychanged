Overweight Bags Overweight, oversized, and excess baggage may be checked on a load-availability basis, per the guidelines detailed below. Please note, fees will be assessed for each category that applies. Example #1 - if a bag is overweight and oversized, the overweight fee plus the oversized fee will apply. Example #2 - if a bag is the third bag and is also overweight and oversized, the three additional fees (overweight, oversized, excess bag) will be assessed. Fees for Main Base Customers Unlike our other fare options, Main Base does not include free seat selection. You may select seats for Main Base fares at any time for a fee. If you don’t, your seats will be assigned before departure. Reservations Service Fee JetBlue charges a $25 nonrefundable fee per-person on the reservation when you change, cancel, or complete a new booking over the phone or through chat. Customers can save this $25 per-person fee by booking online at jetblue.com or managing an existing reservation from the Manage trips page. Some exceptions to this fee are: Mosaic members Lap infants Extra seats Customers who are physically, emotionally, or cognitively unable to book online. For example: Tremor in hands Physical effects of a stroke Anxiety Learning disability Bookings of more than seven (7) people Child traveling with adult(s) booked on a separate reservation Customers paying with payment methods not available online Multiple credit cards Multiple Travel Bank credits (only one credit per person) Western Union JetBlue Travel Certificates JetBlue Vacations packages Bookings/changes made at the airport or city ticket offices Military or Veteran’s Advantage bookings EvenMore® seat selection Price varies by market and fare option purchased. The EvenMore seat selection fee is only refundable if the booking is cancelled prior to scheduled departure. Learn more about EvenMore . Priority Security Priority security price: Starting at $20 one way and varies by market. The priority security fee is only refundable if the booking is cancelled prior to scheduled departure. You can also pay for priority security with TrueBlue points any time before check-in in Manage Trips on jetblue.com, or in My Trips on the JetBlue app. To see if Priority Security is available at your departure airport, visit our Airport Information pages and select your departure airport for details.
