Black Friday and Cyber Monday 2026: a first look at Amazon’s best deals

Millions of deals across electronics, beauty, fashion, toys, and more drop now through November 30, with new daily themed deals and double drops on Black Friday and Cyber Monday.

    Written by Jacquelyn Smith

    Published: November 09, 2026

    Updated: November 19, 2026

    9 min read

    Key takeaways

    • Amazon’s Black Friday Week and Cyber Monday deal events run now through November 30, with millions of deals across popular categories and new daily themed deal drops—spanning toys, tech, fashion, beauty, premium gifts, and stocking stuffers.
    • For the first time, Today’s Big Deals feature savings of up to 60% off, dropping twice on Black Friday and Cyber Monday—at midnight and 8 a.m. PST.
    • Customers can save on holiday feasts with Same-Day Delivery on fresh groceries in more than 2,300 cities.
    The biggest holiday shopping moment of the year is here. Amazon’s Black Friday Week and Cyber Monday deal events officially kicked off on November 19, featuring millions of deals across electronics, beauty, fashion, toys, home, and more from brands like medicube, Le Creuset, HP, Google, and Stanley.

    Amazon's Holiday Kids Gift Book is back with 600+ toys, games, and gifts for every age

This year, customers can save even more with Today’s Big Deals daily themed deal drops throughout the events, featuring brands like Theragun, Balsam Hill, Frye & Co., and HydroJug. And for the first time ever, limited-time deals of up to 60% off will drop at midnight and 8 a.m. PST on both Black Friday and Cyber Monday.
    Here’s a look at some of the best deals to shop now—and all the ways to save.
    All deals and availability are subject to change.

    When does Black Friday start on Amazon?

    Amazon’s Black Friday Week and Cyber Monday deal events kicked off at 12:01 a.m. PST on November 19 and will run through November 30. New daily themed deal drops launch throughout the events, with double drops on Black Friday and Cyber Monday at midnight and 8 a.m. PST.

    What daily deal drops can you expect?

    Today’s Big Deals features 12 days of daily themed deal drops—spanning toys, tech, fashion, beauty, premium gifts, and stocking stuffers, with savings up to 60% off. For the first time ever, deals will drop twice on both Black Friday and Cyber Monday at midnight and 8 a.m. PST. This year’s lineup includes fan favorites from Theragun, Balsam Hill, Frye & Co., and HydroJug. Deals are available only while supplies last; customers will want to check back daily for the latest offers across the season’s most-wanted brands and products.

    How can you find the best deals during Amazon’s Black Friday event?

    Alexa for Shopping can do the deal hunting for you, surfacing relevant deals, tracking prices, and keeping you informed so you never miss a savings opportunity.
    • Watch for deals and price drops: Ask Alexa for Shopping to monitor items you’re eyeing. Set up with auto-buy, it can even complete the purchase for you when the price is right.
    • See up to a year of price history: Tap “Price history” on any product page, or ask Alexa for Shopping, to view up to 365 days of pricing data.
    • Build and manage holiday lists with your voice: Create Wish Lists, organize a registry, or move items between lists—all by asking. Tap the heart icon on any product to save it, then set deal alerts on your lists page.
    • Get more done with just a conversation: Alexa for Shopping can now handle more than 100 everyday shopping actions through simple conversation. Beyond lists, it can also help with everyday account tasks like adjusting Subscribe & Save deliveries, checking your gift card balance, tracking refunds, updating delivery instructions, and staying on top of your Prime and Audible memberships.

    Anyone can join Prime for $14.99 a month or $139 a year, or start a free 30-day trial, if eligible.
