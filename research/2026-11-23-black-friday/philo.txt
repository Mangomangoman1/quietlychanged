What does Philo offer?
Philo is a live TV streaming service that offers two plans to fit your entertainment needs:
Essential ( $25 /month): 70+ live channels and over 75,000 movies and shows on-demand. New users get a 7 -day free trial!
Bundle+ ( $33 /month): Includes all the channels and entire streaming library available in the Essential plan, the entire AMC+ library, and access to HBO Max Basic with Ads plan and discovery+ included.
Both plans: offer 1-year unlimited cloud DVR, let you stream on up to 3 devices at once, create up to 10 profiles, and cancel anytime with no contract.
Free Channels : Not ready to subscribe? You can also enjoy Free Channels with no credit card required—simply create your account and start watching.
Does Philo offer a free trial?
Yes! New users that sign up for the Essential plan can try it free for 7 days. You can explore our channels, features, and content before your subscription begins. No contract required—cancel anytime before your trial ends with no charges.
What do I need to get started?
An email address

Web, iPhone & iPad, Android phones & tablets, Roku, Fire TV, Samsung TV, VIZIO TV, LG TV, Apple TV, Android TV, and Chromecast via Android
Can I record my favorite shows?
Yes! You can record an unlimited amount of live or upcoming content to your cloud DVR
and save them for up to a year. No equipment needed!
Do I need to buy or rent a DVR box to record shows on Philo?
Nope! No hardware is required, ever. Philo's DVR is already built into the app, so there's nothing to plug in, set up, or pay extra for.
With an Essential or Bundle+ subscription, you get unlimited DVR storage included, and your recordings are saved for a full year. If you're watching with the Free Channels , recordings are saved for 30 days.
