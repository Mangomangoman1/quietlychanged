Questions?
Am I up for this offer?
You are eligible if you meet all the following criteria: Provide Peacock with a valid payment method;
Are 18 years of age or older;
Reside in the United States (including its territories, commonwealths, and possessions); and
Are not a current paying Peacock subscriber.
What can I watch with Peacock Select?
The Peacock Select Tier is Peacock's most affordable subscription tier. It unlocks Peacock’s core TV offerings, including Bravo, NBC, Telemundo, and more. It does not include Sports, Movies, Live Programming (including the Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade) or Peacock Original series. There is always something new to discover on Peacock Select. For more details about the Peacock Select Tier, see our Help Article
.
What can I watch with Peacock Premium?
Premium unlocks even more, including Sports, Films, and Peacock Originals, on top of core TV offerings, including Bravo, NBC, Telemundo, and more. There is always something new to discover on Peacock Premium.
What if I already have a Peacock account?
If you already have a Peacock account and meet the eligibility criteria, you can click the link above to Sign In and Redeem your offer. If the link does not work, sign into your Peacock account
. Go to your Account and apply the code on the “Plans & Payments” page.
Do I need to provide payment information?
Yes, you will need to provide a valid credit card or PayPal account to redeem this promotional offer. If you cancel your subscription before your offer period expires, you will not be charged when your offer expires. Cancellation instructions can be found at PeacockTV.com/cancellation.
If you redeem the 3 month Peacock Select offer: After offer ends, each month you will be charged $8.99 (or the then-current retail price) plus tax until you cancel.
If you redeem the 1 year Peacock Select offer: After offer ends, each year you will be charged $89.99 (or the then-current retail price) plus tax until you cancel.
If you redeem the 3 month Peacock Premium offer: After offer ends, each month you will be charged $12.99 (or the then-current retail price) plus tax until you cancel.
