Tax on Amazon Prime
Amazon Prime includes multiple benefits such as free delivery, unlimited streaming,
exclusive deals, and more. access to movies, music and Kindle books. For a full list
of current Prime benefits, visit https://www.amazon.com/amazonprime
.
Amazon Prime is subject to tax in certain states and local jurisdictions as determined
by tax authorities.
By signing up to Prime you agree to the Amazon Prime Terms & Conditions
. Your subscription continues until cancelled and you'll automatically be charged
for Prime plus any applicable taxes. You may cancel any time by visiting Cancel Your Amazon Prime Membership
.
