You may be wondering
What is Prime Access, and how does it differ from the full-price Prime membership?
Prime Access is a discounted version of the Prime membership, available to eligible government assistance recipients or income-verified customers. It offers all the same benefits as the full-price Prime membership, but at half the cost—just $6.99/month.
Who is eligible for Prime Access, and how can I qualify?
You can qualify for Prime Access in two ways: based on your household income or by being enrolled in a government assistance program. If you qualify through income, our secure third-party partner will verify that your household income is at or below 200% of the Federal Poverty Guideline based on the information you enter. This verification is quick and easy. Alternatively, if you're enrolled in a government assistance program, you can upload documentation showing your participation. Eligible programs are listed below. This process is secure and your documentation is deleted from Amazon after the verification.
- Supplemental Security Income (SSI)
- SNAP (Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program)
- Medicaid
- Direct Express Debit Card (DE)
- Temporary Assistance for Needy Families (TANF)
- National School Lunch Program (NSLP)
- Low Income Home Energy Assistance Program (LIHEAP)
- Women, Infants, and Children Program (WIC)
- Tribal Assistance Eligibility Letter (TTANF)
- Puerto Rico’s Nutrition Assistance Program (NAP)
- Federal Public Housing Assistance (FPHA)
- Veterans & Survivors Pension Benefits (VSPB)
- Social Security Disability Insurance (SSDI)
Once your eligibility is confirmed, you can start enjoying the Prime benefits.
If you don’t qualify for Prime Access but are a 18-24 years old or a student, check out Prime for Young Adults, or see if you are eligible for a 30-day free trial of the full-priced Prime membership.
Can I share my Prime benefits with other household members?
Yes! As a Prime Access member, you can share certain benefits with other adults in your Amazon Family. Learn more about Amazon Family and sharing benefits here: www.amazon.com/myh/amazonFamily
When will I be billed for my membership?
Once your eligibility for Prime Access is verified and you've provided your payment details, you’ll be billed monthly on the same day you signed up. You must have a valid payment method to complete the sign-up process. If you’re eligible for a free trial, you’ll be automatically charged for the next membership period once the trial ends.
Can I cancel my membership at any time if I’m not satisfied? Will I receive a refund?
Yes, you can cancel your Prime Access membership at any time by updating your account preferences. If you haven’t used your benefits yet, you may be eligible for a full refund for the current membership period.
What other programs make shopping on Amazon easier and more affordable?
Amazon Access hub provides a range of programs, discounts, and features to make shopping more affordable and accessible. Learn more about initiatives like paying with EBT and more here: www.amazon.com/access
