Amazon Prime Shipping Plan
As an Amazon Prime Shipping plan member, you receive the same shipping and shopping
benefits as the Amazon Prime plan. This plan does not include digital benefits like,
Prime Video, Prime Music, Prime Gaming, Prime Reading, Photos, and Amazon Kids+.
