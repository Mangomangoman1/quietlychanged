Share Your Amazon Prime Benefits
Prime members can share Prime benefits and digital content with other members in their
household through Amazon Family. An Amazon Family can include two adults each with
their own account, up to four teen accounts (added before April 7, 2025) or teen profiles,
and up to four child profiles.
Amazon Family lets an
Organizer
share select benefits with the people in their household. An Amazon Family can include:
Up to 2 adults , each with their own Amazon account.
Up to 4 teens , added as a profile or as a teen account created before April 7, 2025.
Up to 4 children , added as profiles managed by a parent or guardian.
Note: To share Prime benefits, the Family
Organizer
must share a payment method for household verification. It can be used to make purchases
within the Family.

Amazon Family

Fast and free
delivery on Prime eligible items.
Access to exclusive Prime events and deals
Prime Video (with ads)

Sharing Prime benefits
To share your select Amazon Prime benefits through Amazon Family:
Visit
Amazon Prime Membership
.
Locate the
Share your Prime Benefits
section.
Select
Manage Your Amazon Family
.
To create an Amazon Family with another adult, select
Add Adult
. You can either send an email invitation or sign up together to verify your accounts.
You must agree to share a payment method in order to share Prime benefits. As a security
measure, we'll notify you if your invitee moves your credit or debit card to their
wallet.
To create an Amazon Family with a child, select
Add Child
.
Amazon Family members must live together at the same primary residential address.
Note: To share Prime benefits, the Family
Organizer
must share a payment method for household verification. It can be used to make purchases
within the Family.
When you create your Amazon Family, you'll be prompted to review your content sharing
settings through Family Library. If you don't wish to share any content, uncheck all
of the boxes next to the content types. You can always go back and choose to share
content later.
You can stop sharing benefits with your Amazon Family member at any time. To do this:
Go to
Manage Your Amazon Family
Select the member
Select
Remove [member name] from your Amazon Family
.
Amazon Family members can leave and re-join your Amazon Family anytime, but they must
wait 12 months before joining a different Amazon Family.
