Cancel Your Amazon Prime Membership
To cancel Amazon Prime, visit Cancel Your Prime Membership
and follow the prompts.
Cancel Your Prime Membership
For more options on managing your Prime Membership, visit your Prime Membership
page.
If you need more support in canceling your Prime Membership, you can:
Go to the Customer Service Homepage
Select Something else
under Can't find what you're looking for?
Then select I need help with something else
Refund Eligibility:
Paid members who haven't used their benefits are eligible for a full refund of the
current membership period. Amazon will process the refund in three to five business
days.
For more information, visit the Cancel Membership
section of the Amazon Prime Terms and Conditions
.
Subscriptions:
Subscriptions on Prime Video, like Paramount+, will continue once your Prime Membership
ends.
For more information, review your Memberships and Subscriptions
.
Canceling Third-Party Memberships:
If you signed up for Amazon Prime through Google Play using Android, cancel your
subscription through Google Subscription services
.
If your Prime Membership is associated with another company's service, contact them
to cancel your Prime.
