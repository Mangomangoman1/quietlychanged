Prime Membership Plans Comparison
Amazon Prime and Amazon Prime Shipping membership plans offer a variety of benefits.
Amazon Prime
: As an Amazon Prime member, you receive the best of shopping and entertainment. You
will get all the benefits of Prime Shipping and Prime Video, and more.
Amazon Prime Shipping
: As an Amazon Prime Shipping member, you receive the same great shipping and shopping
benefits as Amazon Prime. However, unlike Amazon Prime, this program does not include
any digital benefits, like Prime Video, and is available for a limited time only to
eligible customers.
