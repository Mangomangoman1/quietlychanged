What is Prime Limited Time Offers (LTO)?
Prime Limited Time Offers is a program that gives Prime members exclusive, limited-time
offers [such as discounts, early access, etc.] on popular restaurants, stores, services,
and experiences, enhancing the value of your Prime membership along with existing
benefits. Offers are refreshed throughout the year to bring you new and seasonally
relevant rewards.
How It Works
Discover limited-time offers [such as discounts, early access, etc.] across dining,
travel, entertainment, and more, exclusively for Prime members. New offers are added
throughout the year, so check back often to explore what's available. Unlike ongoing
Prime benefits that are usually available, these offers rotate regularly to match
what's relevant to your life throughout the year.
What Makes It Different
Seasonal Relevance:
New offers are added periodically throughout the year, timed to align with key shopping
moments, holidays, and seasonal activities that matter most to you.
Quality Over Quantity:
We focus on high-value partnerships and meaningful benefits [discounts, early access,
etc.], so every offer is worth your attention.
Exclusive Access:
Prime members get exclusive access to these offers during the promotional period,
with deeper benefits [discounts, early access, etc.]
Easy Discovery:
Find all available offers in one place at
amazon.com/prime/partner-offers
How to Use Prime Limited Time Offers
Visit the Prime Partner Offers page
Browse 'Limited time offers' section for periodic offers
Click "Redeem Offer"* on any deal you want
Redeem directly with the partner through their website, app, or physical location
*By redeeming a limited time offer, you will agree to Amazon's Conditions of Use and
Privacy Notice, as well as the terms and conditions of the third-party partner associated
with each offer redemption.
