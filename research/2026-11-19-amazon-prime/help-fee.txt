The Amazon Prime Membership Fee
When your free trial or membership period ends, we'll automatically charge for the
next membership period.
How much is Amazon Prime?
Current Amazon Prime membership pricing:
$14.99 per month
$139 per year
Prime Video membership is $8.99 per month
Current Amazon Prime for Young Adults membership pricing (visit www.amazon.com/joinyoungadult
):
$7.49 per month
$69 per year
Prime Shipping Only Plan (Invitation only)
$14.99 per month
EBT, Medicaid, SNAP, and other select government assistance recipients can qualify
for a Prime Membership discount. Visit www.amazon.com/primeaccess
and verify your eligibility to pay $6.99 per month.
Your Prime Membership may be subject to sales tax in some states. To know more about
taxes, go to Tax on Amazon Prime
.
Amazon Prime in Colombia
Note: This section applies only to Amazon Prime in Colombia members.
Customers based in Colombia can enjoy localized Amazon Prime in Colombia benefits
for the following membership pricing:
COP 24,900 per month
COP 165,600 per year
Cancellation
To cancel any of your Amazon Prime memberships, go to Manage Prime Membership
.
Note:
If your Prime Membership is through a service from another company, contact that company
about membership rate and management.
