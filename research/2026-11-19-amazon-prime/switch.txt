Switch Amazon Prime Membership
You can switch from an Amazon Prime membership for a specific country to a Prime
membership in another country.
To switch your Amazon Prime membership:
End your current Prime membership or cancel your free trial membership.
Go to Your Amazon Prime Membership
.
Depending on whether you have a regular Amazon Prime membership or a free trial membership,
do the following:
To cancel your membership, select Update, cancel, and more
, and then select Cancel Membership - forgo benefits
. If you and your invited household members haven't yet taken advantage of Prime during
the paid period, we'll refund the full membership fee for that period. Otherwise,
we'll reimburse the membership fee pro rata, calculated based on the remaining (full)
months of the current membership period.
To cancel a free trial, select Update, cancel, and more
and then select End trial and benefits
.
Sign up for the Prime membership you want in your preferred country on the Amazon Prime
page. Select the Amazon Prime membership country name to switch countries.
You can also find this information on our End Your Amazon Prime Membership
help page.
Note: If you cancel a monthly Prime membership, you can only register again after the month
you already paid for. You can see the date under Manage My Amazon Prime Membership
under Next Payment
.
If you would like your monthly Prime membership terminated immediately, contact customer service
.
Note: If you are based in Colombia, check out the Amazon Prime in Colombia - Membership Plans Comparison Help Page
for localized membership information and perks.
