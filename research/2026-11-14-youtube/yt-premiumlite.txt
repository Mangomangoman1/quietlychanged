Your questions, answered
What's included with YouTube Premium Lite?
Premium Lite benefits (background play, downloads, ad-free) available on most non-music content, excluding Shorts. Ads may appear when you search or browse. Learn more.
Where will I see ads?
Ads may appear on music content, Shorts, and when you search or browse. Music content includes any music focused video, for example, official music videos, covers, and dance videos. Learn more.
What’s the difference between Premium and Premium Lite?
Premium Lite's features apply to most non-music content on YouTube. This means you'll be able to watch your favorite podcasts and creator videos ad-free, offline, and in the background.

Ad-free streaming, background play, and downloads aren't available for music-focused content like official music videos, covers, and dance videos. Learn more.

Premium unlocks all of YouTube and YouTube Music ad-free, offline, and in the background. Plus, enjoy exclusive Premium features across all content without interruptions.
How do background play and downloads work with Premium Lite?
Background play is on by default for YouTube and YouTube Kids. If you lock your screen while a video is playing, the audio will keep playing in the background. You can turn off background play anytime in your settings.
With Premium Lite, you can download and watch most videos on your mobile device using the YouTube app, or the YouTube Kids app. You can also download videos and watch on your computer using Chrome, Edge & Opera browsers.
With Premium Lite, background play is on by default for YouTube and YouTube Kids and will keep most videos playing. This means if you're watching a video on YouTube and you leave the YouTube app, the video will keep playing in the background until you pause it. You can turn off background play anytime in your settings.
Music focused content like official music videos, covers, dance videos, and official content from music labels and publishers won't be playable offline or in the background.

Can I upgrade to Premium after I sign up for Premium Lite?
Yes, you can upgrade anytime by visiting the Paid membership page and selecting the Premium membership option.
How can I cancel my membership?
Cancel your membership by visiting the Purchases and memberships page. You can rejoin Premium or Premium Lite at any time.
