Sign up for a YouTube Premium or YouTube Music Premium annual plan
Premium annual plans are pre-paid, non-recurring memberships. These plans are only available for individual users. You may have up to 24 months of access to Premium membership benefits through pre-paid plans at any time. See below for eligibility details.
How the annual plan works
When you purchase an annual plan, you pay upfront for a non-recurring individual subscription. This means your paid membership benefits will end after the 12-month period you paid for. You can purchase up to 24 months of pre-paid access to YouTube Music Premium or YouTube Premium at a given time. To keep access to your benefits, you’ll need to purchase another plan once your membership expires.
Annual plans are not refundable, cannot be paused, and are subject to our travel policy. If you have questions about your annual plan or wish to terminate access before your annual plan expires, get in touch with YouTube Support. Once your access is terminated, you will lose access to your benefits. If you want to report a fraudulent transaction, file a claim here.
Learn more below about how to sign up below. You can also explore our paid membership options to decide which one is right for you.
Sign up for an annual plan
To sign up for a YouTube Premium or Music Premium annual plan, follow the steps below.
Sign up for a YouTube Premium annual plan
From a mobile or desktop web browser, go to youtube.com/premium/annual/inapp.
Sign in to the Google Account where you’d like to start your membership.
Select Get annual plan.
Enter your payment details.
Complete your purchase.
Sign up for a YouTube Music Premium annual plan
Learn more about the YouTube Music Premium membership
From a mobile or desktop web browser, go to youtube.com/musicpremium/annual/inapp.
Sign in to the Google Account where you’d like to start your membership.
Select Get annual plan.
Enter your payment details.
Complete your purchase.
After completing your purchase, you can use your YouTube Premium or YouTube Music Premium benefits across all devices and platforms with access to YouTube by signing into the Google Account you used to purchase your membership. You can check the date your plan ends by going to the Purchases & Memberships section of your account.
Annual Plan Requirements
To sign up for an annual plan, you must:
Not be a current YouTube Premium or Music Premium subscriber. If you have an existing YouTube Premium or YouTube Music Premium membership and want to switch to an annual plan, follow the instructions here.
Live in a location where annual plans are available:
Brazil, Canada, Germany, India, Japan, Mexico, Thailand, Turkey, and the United States.
Note: Annual plans are only available for individual users. We do not offer annual plans for multi-person plan users.
Change your membership type
To switch to an annual plan if you have an existing YouTube Premium or Music Premium membership:
Confirm you live in a location where annual plans are available:
United States, Canada, Mexico, Brazil, Germany, Thailand, India, Japan, and Turkey.
Cancel your existing membership. Click here to learn more about canceling your existing membership.
Sign up for an annual plan for YouTube Premium or Music Premium.
If you have an annual plan, and you want to switch to a multi-person plan you'll have to wait until your plan expires before upgrading to a multi-person plan. Learn how to set up and manage a YouTube multi-person. If you need help with your multi-person, you can also get in touch with support at any time.
