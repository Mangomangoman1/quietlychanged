Questions? We’re here to help.
How do I play videos and music in the background?
Background play is on by default for YouTube, YouTube Music, and YouTube Kids. If you lock your screen while a video is playing, the audio will keep playing in the background. You can turn off background play anytime in your settings.
Learn more about Background Play
What products, services, and devices support YouTube Music Premium?
The YouTube Music app is supported on devices like a smartphones, laptops, tablets, and smart TVs. Check out our list of compatible products and services to stay up to date and learn more about other devices.
How many members can I add to a family plan?
You can share your YouTube Music Premium membership with up to 5 additional household members with a family plan. Once you get a family plan, you can add members if you’re the family manager of your plan. Family members sharing a YouTube Music Premium family plan must live in the same household as the family manager and have a Google account. Each family member gets their own private, personalized account.
Learn more about our family plan
How can I cancel my subscription?
Cancel your membership by visiting the Paid Membership page. You can rejoin YouTube Music Premium at any time.
