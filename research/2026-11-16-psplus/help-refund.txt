How to request a refund for a digital purchase on PlayStation Store
Find out if you're eligible for a PlayStation®Store refund and how to request a refund on PlayStation Store purchases.

Request refund
Depending on the type of product, you may be able to request a refund if you’ve changed your mind about a PlayStation Store purchase.
In addition to the below, you may have additional rights under applicable local law, such as if your content is faulty. Nothing in the below limits or replaces any such rights under local law.
How to request a refund for a digital purchase on PlayStation Store 
Open your Order details and select an eligible transaction.
 How to check order details 
Select Request Refund and follow the on-screen instructions.
If you’re still having issues, please contact PlayStation Support.
 Contact PlayStation Support 
You can also use our online assistant (at the bottom right of your screen) to find out if your purchase is eligible for a refund.
How to check the refund eligibility of your digital purchase on PlayStation Store 
Select your purchase type to check your refund eligibility.

Subscriptions 
A subscription includes any purchase of a service with recurring fees from PlayStation Store, such as PlayStation®Plus.
All criteria in the checklist below must be satisfied to be eligible for a refund.
Subscriptions refund checklist
Refund request has been made within 14 days from signing up.*
✓
Refund request is for your first payment, not a recurring fee.
✓
*This is based on the agreement and acknowledgement you made at the time of purchasing your subscription.
If you take advantage of a free trial when you sign up, your 14-day cancellation period includes any time included in your free trial. For example, if you sign up for a free trial which lasts 7 days and you are charged for the subscription at the end of the trial, you only have a further 7 days after your payment to cancel.
 Check purchase date 
If you're eligible for a refund, but you've started using your subscription (for PlayStation Plus this might include: playing online, downloading monthly games or using cloud storage) we may reduce your refund based on your use of the subscription.
You can check the guide below to learn how to stop future payments at any time. Your subscription will continue until the next payment is due, at which point it will end.
 Cancel subscription 
Pre-orders 
A pre-order refers to any purchase of content from PlayStation Store before its release date.
Pre-ordered more than 14 days before the release date
All criteria in the checklist below must be satisfied to be eligible for a refund unless the content is faulty.
Pre-order refund checklist
Refund request has been made before the release date.
✓
Main part of the content has not been downloaded.
✓
If your pre-order includes immediately accessible extra content (for example, pre-order bonus content) and you have not started to download the main part of your pre-order, the extra content stops working if you cancel your pre-order. 
Pre-ordered less than 14 days before the release date
If your purchase meets all criteria in the checklist below, you can request a refund at any time before the release date (if the release date has passed, within 14 days from purchase).
If your purchase doesn’t meet all criteria, it isn’t eligible for a refund unless the content is faulty.

When can I receive my refund?
You may receive your refund in 30 to 60 days.
Where can I receive my refund?
Where possible, refunds are issued to the payment method used to make the purchase. However, there may be some payment methods or instances where this may not be possible, such as purchases made using wallet fund vouchers. If for any reason we are unable to issue the refund to your original payment method, the amount is credited to your wallet instead.
My refund hasn’t arrived, what can I do?
If you’ve received a confirmation from us that you will be refunded and you haven’t received your refund, please check below.
If your refund is payable to a payment method other than the wallet, check your wallet balance (as we may have been unable to refund to your original payment method) or contact your payment method provider for further assistance. 
If your refund is payable to your wallet, please contact us.
Can I request a refund for wallet funds?
You cannot cancel a wallet top-up. We seek your consent to top up your wallet immediately and acknowledgment that you understand you will not be able to cancel your top-up.
Can I request a refund for an online ID change?
You can revert to a previous online ID for free, but you can't obtain a refund for a purchase of a new online ID unless your purchase was faulty.
 Troubleshoot online ID issues 
Can I request a refund for voucher codes? 
As purchases of voucher codes are made outside of PlayStation Store, you need to contact the retailer that sold the voucher code to you for their refunds and cancellation policy.
If you purchased the code from PlayStation Direct you can check your refund eligibility in the guide below.
 PlayStation Direct return and refund policy
