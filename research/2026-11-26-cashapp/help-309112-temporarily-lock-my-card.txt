 If you’ve misplaced your Cash App Card, you can temporarily lock it in-app or online to prevent new charges. To lock your card in-app: Tap the Money tab Select the card at the top of the screen Select the lock icon at the top right of the screen Select Lock card To lock your card online: Log into your Cash App account at cash.app/account On the left, select Money Toggle Lock Card in the Card section on the right You can unlock your card at any time by following the same steps above. You'll need to enter your Cash PIN to unlock your card. Please note: Once you use your card for a purchase, the transaction automatically enters into a pending state. Locking your card does not prevent pending charges from completing.
