 Blocking Cash App Card & Cash App Pay Payments If you would like to block a business from using your Cash App Card or Cash App Pay information, you may have the option to do so directly from your app: Go to the Activity tab (clock icon) Choose the transaction from the business you’d like to block Select the icon of the business to navigate to their profile Select Block/unblock You can view and manage a list of your recently unblocked and currently blocked businesses from the Card tab. Blocking a business will not prevent you from receiving a refund. If you don't see the block option, try these steps: Select the business icon or name at the top of the transaction details screen, not the transaction row itself. This opens the business's profile. Close and reopen Cash App, then repeat the steps above. Go to the Card tab and look for the business in your list of blocked businesses. Remove the business's access instead: select your profile icon , select Linked apps and businesses then find the business and select Remove . Removing access may not stop future charges if your card number is stored directly with the business. Some app store businesses, like Google Play, Apple, or Amazon, may not display a profile page in the Activity tab. You can manage those subscriptions directly through the app store: Google Play: Open the Google Play Store, select your profile, then select Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions Apple: Open Settings , select your name, then select Subscriptions If none of these work, contact Cash App Support. Unblock a business To allow a blocked business to charge your Cash App Card again: Go to the Card tab Select the business in your list of blocked businesses Select Unblock You can also choose a previous transaction from the business in the Activity tab, select the business icon to open their profile, then select Block/unblock . Blocking ACH Bank Payments Blocking a business from charging your Cash App Card or using Cash App Pay is different from blocking ACH (bank) payments. An ACH payment is a bank-to-bank transfer made using your routing and account numbers. These payments are often used for things like subscriptions, bill payments, or other recurring charges that pull funds directly from your bank account. At this time, ACH payment blocks can’t be managed directly in the app. If you’d like to stop future ACH debits from a specific business, you’ll need to contact Cash App Support for help. When an ACH block is placed: Future ACH debit attempts from that business may be declined Pending ACH payments may be prevented from settling Refunds can still be received if the business issues one To request an ACH payment block, contact Cash App Support through the app. Please note that blocking a business does not cancel existing subscriptions, recurring charges, or outstanding account balances. To resolve any of these issues, contact the business directly. You must have a previous transaction with a business to follow the steps above. You can view and manage a list of your recently unblocked and currently blocked businesses from the Card tab. Blocking a business will not prevent you from receiving a refund. If you do not see the option to block a business, contact Cash App Support for further assistance.
