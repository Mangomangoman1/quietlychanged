Important Policy Updates
Discord Policy
November 23, 2026
Document Version
No items found.
We're updating our Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and Paid Services Terms, effective December 15, 2026 . Here’s a look at what’s changing and what it means for you.
Previously announced changes
Discoverable content on the web. As we shared in October
, admins of Discoverable servers can choose which channels appear on their Server’s Web Page, making answers, guides, and updates easier to find on the web. Channel Summaries, automated summaries of active conversations, are rolling out now under our current terms. Channel Messages, which lets messages from public channels in Discoverable servers appear on the web, will begin rolling out gradually after these updated terms take effect. Only messages sent after that point are included. Server admins decide which channels take part, and server members can choose whether their own messages appear.
Age assurance. We launched our new approach to age assurance teen experience
in September, which included new ways to confirm your age group
. We're updating our Privacy Policy to reflect how we handle data as part of that process, and our Terms of Service give parents and guardians a clearer picture of Discord and the protections in place for teens. This includes more information on Family Center and how it can give parents and guardians visibility into their teen's Discord activity and tools to help guide their experience.
Clearer explanations of how things already work
Nitro and partner perks. We're updating our Paid Services Terms to be clearer that third-party partner perks are limited-time offers, not permanent benefits. While we're not removing any current perks, they're offered by partners and can change or end at any time.
How we approach privacy. Our Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, where it comes from, and how we use it, along with the choices you have. Updates include more detail about how we use data to build and improve the machine learning models behind safety, personalization and other Discord features, and the settings you can use to control whether your data helps improve those models.
A few legal updates
Legal and dispute updates. We added a standard security disclaimer and a written-notice path for intellectual property claims, gave users outside the US and Canada the option to choose arbitration, and clarified how users in the European Economic Area can use an official Out-of-Court Dispute Settlement body under the Digital Services Act. We also updated local information for users in certain regions.
For more information, check out the FAQs below.
FAQs
Is any of this new? ‍
Some is, but most of it isn't. Discoverable content on the web and our update to age assurance were previously announced. This update makes sure our terms describe them accurately. The main additions are the legal updates listed above, but make sure to check out the policies to see everything that's changed.
‍
How will I know if I am in a server or channel that has enabled Discoverable content on the web?
Discoverable Server admins choose which channels to make available on the web. Those channels will be labeled, so you'll know before you send a message. As always, you can customize your own privacy choices in your user settings under Data & Privacy. Learn more
.
‍
What if I decide later that I don't want my messages shown on the web?
You can turn off Channel Messages anytime in User Settings > Data and Privacy
. Once you do, new messages won't appear on the web. Messages sent while the setting was on remain available unless you delete them from Discord. Only messages from mid-December 2026 onward are eligible to appear on the web, regardless of your setting. Learn more
.
‍
If I delete a message, does it disappear from search results?
Deleting a message removes it from Discord and from Discord's Server Web Pages. Learn more about how long Discord keeps your information in our data retention policy
. Search engines may keep their own copies of pages, so it can take some time, or an extra removal request, before it stops appearing in their results. Discord doesn't control how long they keep cached copies. Learn more
.
‍
