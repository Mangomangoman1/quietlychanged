### If I have given a third-party AI agent permission, will you restrict it?

We want AI interacting with our products and services safely, which requires us to restrict what AI personal assistants can do. Just because you’ve given access to AI assistants doesn’t mean United will permit an AI assistant to transact with United.

### How do I know if a tool is an unauthorized AI agent or assistant?

If a third-party AI agent, bot, browser extension or browser feature searches, books, buys, manages or collects information on united.com or the United app for you, and United hasn't given permission, it's unauthorized. You can still use AI to research and plan trips, as long as the tool doesn't access united.com or the United app. Assistive technology for customers with disabilities isn't affected.

### Does United work with any third-party AI agents?

Not yet, but we are always on the lookout for new ways to serve our customers! Stay tuned, and if we do this page will be updated to keep you informed of the latest and greatest.

### What happens to a booking a third-party AI agent already made?

If you’ve previously used a third-party AI agent to make travel plans, agent bookings made prior to November 11, 2026 will not be canceled. However, we recommend you review all recent travel plans to ensure they have been booked and ticketed correctly.

### What happens if an unauthorized third-party AI agent checks me into my flight?

Using a third-party AI agent to check you into your flight automatically could result in you missing important safety content. These automated AI actions have the potential to violate FAA and DOT regulations - as an example, flight check-in requires human acknowledgment that bags do not contain banned substances like explosives.

### What should I do if I shared my MileagePlus information?

If you’ve previously shared any MileagePlus login information with a third-party AI agent, we recommend you change your password immediately.
