HBO Max price history
All services 
↑
Launched as HBO Now in April 2015. Rebranded to HBO Max in May 2020. Rebranded to 'Max' in May 2023 to reflect expanded Warner Bros. Discovery content. Rebranded back to HBO Max in Summer 2025. Ad-supported tier launched June 2021. Paramount Skydance completed its $110 billion acquisition of Warner Bros. Discovery on October 6, 2026, creating a combined company called Skydance, which plans to merge HBO Max and Paramount+ into a single streaming service. Skydance CEO David Ellison said that in the short term the two services will be operated separately and bundled together before they are combined. Pricing for the combined service has not been announced.

fuboTV price history
All services 
↑
Live TV with a heavy sports focus (always ad-supported; there is no ad-free option). The base plan launched in 2017 as Fubo Premier and has since been called Fubo and Standard before its current name, Pro; it is charted here. The Elite tier is a premium top bundle launched in September 2020 (an earlier, smaller Fubo Extra tier from 2018 later became a channel add-on and is not tracked here). Fubo Sports + News is a lower-cost skinny sports bundle launched in September 2025. In December 2025 Fubo lowered prices during a carriage dispute with NBCUniversal, then raised prices again in June 2026 after the dispute was resolved and NBCUniversal channels were restored. In October 2025, Fubo and Hulu + Live TV completed a corporate combination (Disney owns about 70% of Fubo), creating the sixth-largest US pay TV company; the two continue operating as separate consumer brands with separate pricing.

DIRECTV STREAM price history
All services 
↑
Always ad-supported live TV; no ad-free option (the Entertainment base plan is charted). Renamed repeatedly: launched as DIRECTV NOW (2016), became AT&T TV Now (2019), then ran alongside AT&T TV (2020), which was rebranded DIRECTV STREAM when AT&T spun off DIRECTV with TPG. In April 2025 DIRECTV retired the standalone 'DIRECTV STREAM' brand and folded the streaming service into its main DIRECTV offering. The charted line follows the entry-level base package through these renames (Live a Little to Plus to Entertainment). Signature tiers (Choice and above) carry a Regional Sports Network fee up to $23.99/mo, and leasing the optional Gemini device adds $10/mo (streaming on your own device avoids it); these fees are not included in the tracked headline price. A free ad-supported tier, MyFree DIRECTV, also exists. Genre Packs (MySports, MyEntertainment, MyNews, MiEspañol, MyKids) launched in 2024-2025 as lower-cost curated bundles and are listed as a current snapshot rather than charted. On June 25, 2026, several Genre Packs rose to the prices listed below (MyEntertainment by $8, MyNews by $5, MiEspañol by $3 and MyKids by $1) while the new MySports Genre Pack held its price; the legacy 'MySports Original' plan rose by $10, and the MySports Extra and MyCinema add-ons each rose by $1. Signature packages (Choice and above) rose in the same round, but DIRECTV communicated those per-customer without publishing uniform figures.
