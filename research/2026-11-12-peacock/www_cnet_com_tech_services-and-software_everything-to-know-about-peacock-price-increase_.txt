Everything to Know About Peacock’s Price Increase
New subscribers will see the changes today.
Kourtnee Jackson
Senior Editor
					Kourtnee covers streaming services and entertainment.… 
Read full bio
Kourtnee Jackson
Senior Editor
Read full bio
July 18, 2024, 4:15 pm ET
						1 min read					
Peacock last raised its subscription prices back in July 2023.
CNET
Peacock
‘s new streaming prices begin rolling out today. The NBCUniversal brand first announced the increase in May for its monthly and annual plans, saying the changes would go into effect this summer. The news arrived after parent company 
Comcast said
 that the streaming platform added 3 million new paid subscribers in the first quarter of 2024. 
As of July 18, new customers will pay more for subscriptions: $8 per month for Peacock Premium and $14 a month for Peacock Premium Plus — both are $2 increases. Annual plans will see a significant bump from $60 per year to $80 for Premium, while Premium Plus will go up from $120 to $140 a year. The price hike will be implemented for existing subscribers starting on Aug. 17.

: How to Watch the Olympics: When the 2024 Games Start and the Best Olympic Streaming Service
More from CNET
The move aligns with the 
2024 Summer Olympics
 broadcasting live on the platform from Paris. The games kick off on July 26 and run through Aug. 11. To enhance the viewing experience, the streamer is offering features such as watch parties with host Alex Cooper, an interactive tool called Peacock Live Actions and multiview, which allows for four simultaneous views. Additionally, the streamer will air the NFL’s Eagles vs. Packers overseas game live from São Paulo. 
While most major streaming services raised prices in 2023, Peacock was the first to do it this year among mainstream platforms. Other platforms such as 
Max
 and 
Paramount Plus
 have since followed suit with their own price hikes. Last July, Peacock 
upped the prices on all of its plans
. Monthly rates for Peacock Premium went from $5 to $6, and Peacock Premium Plus saw a $2 increase, jumping from $10 to $12. For annual subscriptions, the basic premium plan went up by $10 a year to $60, with the more expensive subscription increasing from $100 to $120. 
There’s still a 
free, ad-supported tier
, but new accounts are not eligible; existing subscribers can downgrade their plans, however. If you have Xfinity internet or Instacart Plus, you may qualify for 
free Peacock Premium
.

 and news on
