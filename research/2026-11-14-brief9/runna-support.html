Changes to our UK subscription

Written by Tech Team
Updated this week
Table of contents

At Runna, we are continually investing in creating a world-class coaching experience for runners around the world.

Whether that be through developing new plans designed by our experienced and qualified coaches, embedding the latest sports science into all of our plans, or continuing to introduce new product features and improvements - we are committed to best serving the needs of our global community.

Just this year alone, we’ve introduced more than 20 new product features and improvements. Some of our favourites include Adapt for Heat, which helps people train smarter during the hot weather, and Not Feeling 100%, which helps runners adapt their plans around life without losing momentum.

We always listen to the needs of our community, and our investment into product development isn’t stalling. Soon, we will be releasing new updates that will make our training plans even more adaptable, make it easier for runners to maintain training, and also understand their progress towards their goals.

To continue that level of investment, we have increased our subscription prices for users in the UK for the first time in our history.

What’s changing

  • Monthly subscription price: £15.99 > £17.99

  • Annual subscription price: £99.99 > £109.99

  • Strava + Runna annual subscription price: £119.99 > £124.99*

When are these changes happening

  • For existing Runna subscribers: the new prices apply from your first renewal on or after 10 December, 2026. We’ll email you on 10 November to let you know.

  • For new Runna subscribers: the new prices apply from 10 November, 2026.

Whether you’re just starting out on your running journey, training for an event or using running as means to stay active, thank you for training with us. We’ll be there to keep running alongside you, and hope to see you on your next run.

The Runna Team.

*This increase reflects Runna’s new price. The Strava price within the combined subscription remains the same.
