discovery+ plan price changes

discovery+ plan prices increased for new subscriptions in the U.S. on October 13, 2026.

The discovery+ plan increased by $1 to $6.99/month (plus applicable taxes). The discovery+ (Ad-Free) plan increased by $2 to $11.99/month (plus applicable taxes).

When will this happen?

New subscribers

This price increase began on October 13, 2026.

Current subscribers

You’ll be charged the new monthly price on your next billing date on or after November 12, 2026.

Billed through us or an app store

If you're billed through us or an app store, your discovery+ subscription will automatically renew at the new price (plus applicable taxes) on your first billing date on or after November 12, 2026. If you have a promotional discount, your price won't increase until your promotional period ends.

Billed through another provider

If your discovery+ subscription is billed through another provider (e.g. Amazon Prime Video Channels, DIRECTV, the Roku Channel, or Sling TV), your provider will let you know of any price changes (as applicable).

Frequently asked questions

Who bills me for discovery+?

To find who bills you for your subscription:

Sign in to the discovery+ app or discoveryplus.com. Forgot your password?

Choose your profile (upper right) and then Account .

In the Subscription section:

If an app store bills you, you'll see 'Changes to this subscription must be made on the app store' message.

If discovery+ bills you, you'll see the option to cancel.

If a TV, broadband, or mobile provider bills you, you'll see discovery+ via (company name) .

When is my next billing date?

To find your next billing date, choose who bills you below:

Amazon appstore : See Manage your subscriptions .

Apple App Store : See View your purchase history .

discoveryplus.com : Sign in at discoveryplus.com/my-account and in the Subscription section you'll find your next billing date.

Google Play : See Cancel, pause, or change a subscription .

Roku : Sign in at my.roku.com and choose View your purchase history .

Samsung : Go to samsungcheckout.com and sign in to your Samsung account. From the Purchase History menu, choose Subscriptions .

Other provider : Check your monthly billing statement.
