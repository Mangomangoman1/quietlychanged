The Amazon Prime Membership Fee

At the end of the Amazon Prime free trial your card will be charged $99 for an annual
 membership or $12.99 per month with a monthly membership.

The $99 annual charge or $12.99 monthly charge for Amazon Prime includes same benefits
 as outlined here .

If you don't intend to convert your membership to an annual or monthly membership,
 you can cancel your Amazon Prime membership on the Manage Prime Membership page. If you haven't placed an Amazon Prime-eligible order or used any Prime benefit,
 you are eligible for a full refund.

Once your membership ends, we will auto-renew your membership by charging your payment
 method provided at the time of sign-up. If we can't successfully charge your preferred
 payment method, other cards on file may be charged. Your initial sign-up authorizes
 us to use the preferred or other available payment methods. To update your preferred
 payment method, go to Manage Prime Membership .
