Frequently Asked Questions

How to migrate to cPanel?

Simplify your migration with cPanel & WHM’s built-in Transfer Tool . If you need assistance, our expert Support Team is ready to handle your migration request.

What is an Account?

An account is another term for a cPanel user account that manages websites, files, domains, and email accounts. Hosting providers create and manage cPanel accounts through WHM. WHM reseller accounts are included as an account.

What IP is required to purchase a license?

As cPanel does not provide web-hosting services, to purchase a monthly cPanel & WHM license through the cPanel Store, it is required to have the public-facing static IP address of the server. The server must meet the minimum requirements listed in the System Requirements documentation which can be found in the Installation Guide .

What payment methods does the cPanel Store accept?

The cPanel store accepts Credit Card and PayPal payment methods. The payment methods can be added and managed by going logging into your store account and going to Account Settings then Payment Methods. Please note: The store does not currently support OTP or 2 Factor authentication for credit card payments.

When does my cPanel license renew?

All new cPanel licenses purchased through the cPanel Store renew automatically on the same day of the month (or year, for annual licenses) on which the service was originally purchased. At the time of renewal, the primary payment method associated with your cPanel Store account will be charged for your licenses. To prevent a license from renewing, you must cancel it before the renewal date by following the instructions in the article How do I cancel my cPanel license?

Do additional IP addresses need to be licensed?

Additional IP addresses do not need to be licensed, as the only the server’s main, public IPv4 is required to be licensed. Additional IP addresses can be utilized for numerous purposes such as being dedicated to an account, utilized for certain services, or reserved for later use.

How can I obtain a Certificate of Tax Residency?

To obtain a Certificate of Residency, also known as IRS Form 6166, please contact the Accounting Department at [email protected] . Please include the following information in the email: Company Name, Requestor Name, Country, Email.
