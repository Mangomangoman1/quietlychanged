Earning on Food & Beverages Purchases of food and beverage, except alcohol where prohibited by local and state law, earn Game Chips. A maximum of 50 Game Chips can be earned each day for Food & Beverage purchases and must be in the same transaction. All transactions within four hours from the first transaction of the day are counted towards the maximum of 50 game chips. Any alcohol purchased will not qualify unless permissible by local and state law and purchased along with a minimum of $5.29 food purchase within the four hour period. $1 = 1 Game Chip, rounded down $0-0.49, and rounded up at $0.50-$0.99. Online and delivery purchases do not earn Game Chips. Any game chips earned on food and beverage purchases will be posted as an offer to the member’s account within 48 hours of the date of the transaction. These offers will have a 60 day expiration date, and once redeemed, the game chips will expire if unused after 45 days. MEMBER TIERS Player Tier All members are automatically in the Level 1 Player Tier upon enrollment. Subject to removal from the Program (as explained below), a member will remain a Player indefinitely unless the minimum number of Game Chips played required to achieve Icon Tier or Legend Tier status is met. Player status is conferred on Dave & Buster’s Rewards members who play zero chips up to 1,399 chips. Icon Tier Icon status is conferred on those registered Dave & Buster’s Rewards members who play a minimum of 1,400 Game Chips during one defined 12 month period (February 1st - January 31st). Icon status is good for the 12 month time period year in which the Dave & Buster’s Rewards member qualifies and the following full calendar year; after that, annual requalification is required. If a member does not requalify for Icon status and does not qualify for Legend status at the end of the stated 12 month time period, the member will revert to Player status. Icon Tier members receive a 10% Game Chips bonus on any Power Card recharge or new Power Card purchase. Member must enter his phone number or present a linked to link the new card to his account to receive the bonus. Members earn 10% bonus chips on any Power Card purchase or recharge if provided their rewards phone on their transaction, or when using a Power Card linked to a Icon Level Rewards account. Members will not receive 10% bonus chips on any Power Card purchases or recharges paid for with a Gift Card. Legend Tier Legend status is conferred on those registered Rewards members who play a minimum of 5,000 Game Chips during one defined 12 month period (February 1st - January 31st). Legend status is good for the 12 month time period year in which the Dave & Buster’s Rewards member qualifies and the following full calendar year; after that, annual requalification is required. If a member does not requalify for Legend status at the end of the following full calendar year and does not qualify for Icon status, the member will revert to Player status. Legend Tier members receive a 15% Game Chips bonus on any Power Card recharge or new Power Card purchase. Member must enter his phone number or present a linked to link the new card to his account to receive the bonus. Members earn 15% bonus chips on any Power Card purchase or recharge if provided their rewards phone on their transaction, or when using a Power Card linked to a Icon Level Rewards account. Members will not receive 15% bonus chips on any Power Card purchases or recharges paid for with a Gift Card. Status Expiration The date for the “chips played’ reset has been extended indefinitely until further notice for all Dave & Buster’s Rewards members. Members who have achieved Icon status will retain their 10% Game Chips Bonus on any Power Card recharge or new Power Card purchase until further notice. Members who have achieved Legend status will retain their 15% Game Chips Bonus on any Power Card recharge or new Power Card purchase until further notice. Current Player tiered members who achieve Icon status will be eligible to receive a 10% Game Chips Bonus on any Power Card recharge or new Power Card purchase. Current Icon tiered members who achieve Legend status will be eligible to receive a 15% Game Chips Bonus on any Power Card recharge or new Power Card purchase.
