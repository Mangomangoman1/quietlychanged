# X scan: adult children handling aging parents' tech (Sep 28 – Oct 19, 2026)

Read-only. Source: public X search (x MCP `search_posts_all`, plus `get_posts_by_ids` for full text), 17 calls in total.
Queries combined "my mom/dad/parents/grandma/grandpa/mother/father" or "elderly/aging parents" with: scam, gift cards, pop-up, clicked a link, subscriptions/cancel/charges, password/locked out/Apple ID/2FA/verification code, hacked Facebook/Gmail/account recovery, Medicare/patient portal/MyChart/Social Security, new phone/iPad setup, TV/Roku/Firestick/Netflix/cable, remote/FaceTime/over the phone, Geek Squad/paid someone/tech support pay, and accounts after a death. I also read the replies to the viral @MrPitbull07 post.

**About the counts:** X search is noisy. Most hits were jokes, fiction threads or off-topic, and I dropped them. Each count below is the number of on-topic posts I actually read in this window. It is not a measure of total volume. Many account-recovery posts were addressed to support handles and came from India; I've marked that where it matters.

## Top recurring pains

## Takeaways for the opportunity hunt
- The most concrete recurring chores are account recovery or lockout and 2FA proxying (the child becomes the parent's second factor), then device migration.
- Scam aftermath has a clear repeatable piece: after a bank-account change, someone has to update Medicare, Social Security, the pension and insurers one by one.
- Willingness to pay is barely visible on X. People describe the work as unpaid family duty and joke about it, and more of them are trying AI agents than hiring local techs.
