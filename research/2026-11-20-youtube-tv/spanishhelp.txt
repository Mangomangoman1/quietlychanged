Get Spanish-language programming with Spanish Plus or Spanish Plan
You can get Spanish-language programming on YouTube TV in 2 ways:
Add the Spanish Plus add-on to your YouTube TV plan to get 25+ additional Spanish-language networks.
Sign up for the YouTube TV Spanish Plan to get 28+ Spanish-language networks on YouTube TV without a YouTube TV membership.
Did you know you can set up your YouTube TV app in Spanish? Learn more.
Add Spanish Plus to your YouTube TV Plan
You can get more Spanish-language programming with your membership when you add the Spanish Plus add-on.
To purchase the Spanish Plus add-on, follow the steps to add a network
to your YouTube TV membership.
Sign up for a YouTube TV Spanish Plan membership
You can purchase a YouTube TV Spanish Plan membership if you're not already signed up for the YouTube TV plan. The Spanish Plan costs $24.99 + tax/month.
To purchase a Spanish Plan membership from your computer:
Go to tv.youtube.com
.
Sign in with your Google Account.
Click your profile photo Settings .
On the Membership tab, scroll to find "Spanish Plan."
Click ADD to complete your purchase.
Switch between Spanish Plus & Spanish Plan
Switch to Spanish Plus from Spanish Plan
Sign up for a YouTube TV plan and add Spanish Plus to your membership.
To purchase a Spanish Plan membership from your computer:
Go to tv.youtube.com
.
Sign in with your Google Account.
Click your profile photo Settings .
On the Membership tab, scroll to find "YouTube TV."
Click ADD choose your plan.
Click NEXT add to your plan.
Click International to find and add "Spanish Plus."
Click NEXT to check out.
After you purchase the plan, we recommend you remove the Spanish Plan from your subscriptions. Learn more
.
