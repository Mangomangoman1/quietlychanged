YouTube TV & YouTube Announced 2026 NFL Sunday Ticket Pricing Starting at Just $192 a Year
By
Luke Bouma
on
February 6, 2026
in
All News
,
News
The NFL Sunday Ticket package for the 2026 NFL season is now available for purchase through YouTube TV and YouTube
, allowing fans to secure access to out-of-market Sunday afternoon games well in advance of the upcoming campaign.
The service continues to be offered via two primary options: integration with a YouTube TV subscription or as a standalone add-on through YouTube Primetime Channels. This structure has remained consistent since the package moved to YouTube’s ecosystem.
For individuals new to the service, promotional pricing provides an entry point at a reduced rate. New subscribers signing up via YouTube TV can access the season for $192, while those opting for Primetime Channels pay $240. An additional NFL RedZone package, which delivers live coverage of key moments and touchdowns across multiple games each Sunday afternoon, is available for an extra $42 when added to these introductory offers.
Returning subscribers face higher standard rates. Those with YouTube TV pay $378 for the full season, and Primetime Channels users pay $480. These figures represent no increase from the previous season’s standard pricing for existing members, reflecting an effort to maintain consistency for loyal users.
Payment flexibility is available for many subscribers. Options include a one-time lump-sum payment or spreading the cost across 12 monthly installments. The installment plan is particularly accessible when signing up early, before the season commences, though it is unavailable in certain states including Colorado, Maine, Utah, and Wisconsin. Once the regular season begins, the package becomes non-cancellable and non-refundable, with full responsibility for any remaining installments on payment plans.
Military personnel qualify for a discounted rate equivalent to last season’s offering: $198 for the base package or $240 when bundled with NFL RedZone. Student discounts are not currently active but are expected to become available closer to the season start, with details to follow in future updates.
Purchases must currently be completed through a web browser, as the checkout process is not supported directly on Android or iOS apps. Users encountering issues on mobile devices are directed to switch to a desktop or mobile browser to finalize transactions. Instructions for both YouTube TV and Primetime Channels sign-ups are accessible online.
Auto-renewal is set for many prior subscribers, with billing scheduled for August. Those wishing to adjust their plan, such as updating payment methods or confirming their selection, have the option to renew early through their account settings on the respective platforms.
The NFL Sunday Ticket
delivers access to nearly all out-of-market NFL regular-season Sunday games broadcast on CBS and Fox, a feature that has made it popular among fans whose local markets do not cover their preferred teams. While the introductory offers target first-time users, the unchanged base rates for returning subscribers aim to provide predictability amid ongoing changes in sports broadcasting landscapes.
