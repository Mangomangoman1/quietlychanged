What is NFL Sunday Ticket and what games are
included?
NFL Sunday Ticket is a premium out-of-market sports package
that broadcasts all out-of-market regular season Sunday
National Football League games not available on your local
broadcast in your area (NBC, CBS or FOX affiliate). NFLST
excludes digital only games. NFLST is not available for
commercial usage. This means it's intended for private homes
and apartments. For example, if you're a Dallas Cowboys fan
but live in Miami, you'd be able to watch all of the Sunday
Cowboys games that aren't shown on NBC, CBS or FOX in your
local market.
See what remaining NFL games are included with each
plan

Do I need NFL Sunday Ticket to see my favorite teams?
The first initial weeks of the NFL season can be the most
exciting for a fan - the NFL has finally returned and every
team starts with the same record! But did you know that
during the first four weeks of the season, 94% of teams tend
to have games on CBS and FOX that are shown in less than
half of the country? With NFL Sunday Ticket and YouTube TV,
you can be sure you’ll be able to see those games, no
matter where you live in the US. Even fans of the Cowboys,
Chiefs, Eagles, Packers, 49ers, Lions, (and fans of every
other team!) have needed NFL Sunday Ticket to ensure they
could watch every regular season game of their favorite team
along with a live TV service like YouTube TV.

TV subscription?
Absolutely! NFL Sunday Ticket allows you to watch
out-of-market Sunday NFL games online without needing a
cable or satellite TV subscription. All you need is an
internet connection and a compatible device. In the US, you
can subscribe through YouTube TV or YouTube Primetime
Channels.

What is NFL RedZone?
NFL Sunday Ticket with NFL RedZone included is an option at
checkout to both YouTube TV and YouTube users. NFL RedZone
brings you every touchdown, from every game, every Sunday
afternoon during the regular season, with 7 hours of live
football coverage, featuring up to 8 games at once. NFL
RedZone can also be purchased with the NFL RedZone with
Sports Plus add on in the YouTube TV onboarding flow or as a
standalone add on in YouTube TV.

How do I purchase NFL RedZone with YouTube TV?
NFL RedZone is available for YouTube TV members to purchase.
There are three ways for users to do this:
If you are signing up for YouTube TV and NFL Sunday Ticket
together, you can add the NFL Redzone with Sports Plus
add-on for $10.99/month.
If you already have YouTube TV and want to add NFL RedZone
to your plan, you have the option to purchase the NFL Sunday
Ticket with NFL RedZone or the NFL RedZone with Sports Plus
add-on from your
Settings page .
If you already have both YouTube TV and NFL Sunday Ticket
(without RedZone), you can add the NFL RedZone with Sports
Plus add on for $10.99/month.
Lastly, if you purchased YouTube TV with NFL Sunday Ticket +
NFL RedZone last season and are on an auto-renewing plan,
you do not need to purchase it again. You can check your
YouTube TV Settings
to see your membership status.

If I'm a commercial establishment, can I use my NFL
Sunday Ticket subscription?
No. NFL Sunday Ticket on YouTube and YouTube TV is
exclusively licensed for residential use. This means it's
intended for private homes and apartments.
Commercial establishments, such as bars, restaurants, and
stores, are not permitted to use NFL Sunday Ticket unless
they obtain specific commercial rights. These commercial
rights are not offered through YouTube or YouTube TV. They
are available through Everpass Media. For more information,
please visit:
https://everpass.com/live-sports/nfl-sunday-ticket/
