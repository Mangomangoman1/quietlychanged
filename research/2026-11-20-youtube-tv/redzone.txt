Add NFL RedZone with Sports Plus to your YouTube TV membership
You can add extra sports channels to your YouTube TV membership by signing up for the NFL RedZone with Sports Plus option. NFL RedZone with Sports Plus is a package of channels that you can add to your YouTube TV plan
for $10.99 per month .
