Add or remove networks
Tailor your membership with add-on networks like Paramount+ with SHOWTIME, STARZ, or NFL RedZone with Sports Plus. You can also buy or rent movies to watch
on certain TV devices.
To buy add-on networks using your TV device:
Open the YouTube TV app using your smart TV device.
Go to the store icon next to your profile photo in the top right.
Select the network you’d like to add. You'll see additional info and the price of the add-on.
Select Add network , and confirm the purchase on the next screen.
You can also browse for networks using your TV device by going to the New & Noteworthy tab. If you’d like to make a purchase, select the network you’d like to add, and follow the steps to subscribe.
Note:
Paramount+ with SHOWTIME no longer allows you to use your YouTube/YouTube TV credentials to view their content directly from their website or app.
Pricing and content availability for Paramount+ with SHOWTIME differs between YouTube TV
and YouTube Primetime Channels
.
YouTube TV subscribers:
There is limited program availability for this add-on option on YouTube TV, including for Entertainment Plus
subscribers. To get the full content offering, consider subscribing on Primetime Channels.
You may experience a delay between when programs air, and when you can view programs in your YouTube TV Library.
Read below to learn more about billing, refunds, and how to add or remove networks using other devices.
Add networks
Go to tv.youtube.com
on a web browser.
Click your profile photo Settings Membership .
Click ADD next to a network you’d like to add. You'll also see the price of each add-on network.
Click Purchase to finish.
Billing for add-on networks
If you have an active membership and sign up for any add-on networks, you'll be charged a prorated amount for the time between when you added the network and your next billing date. You'll then be billed for the add-on network as part of your monthly membership.
Here's an example of how this works:
Date
What happens?
January 1
Your regular monthly billing date
January 10
You add an add-on network to your membership
January 10 - February 1
You're charged a prorated amount for the add-on network. You only have to pay for the time between when you added the network (January 10) and your next billing date (February 1)
February 1 - ongoing
You're billed normally for your membership, including the add-on network
Remove networks
Go to https://tv.youtube.com
on a web browser.
Click your profile photo Settings Membership .
Click CANCEL next to a network you’d like to remove.
Click Yes, Cancel to finish.

Go to this article
to request a refund, or learn more about our refund policy.
