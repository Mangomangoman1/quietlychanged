Sling is No Longer Offering Short-Term Sling Passes
By
Jess Barnes
on
October 5, 2026
in
All News
,
News
,
Sling
Sling TV is no longer offering its short-term, called Passes, that allowed users to sign up for just one to seven days at a time.
A message to customers on the Sling website
says: “Sling was built to give our customers the ultimate control over their entertainment, and Sling Passes delivered on that promise for millions. Though Sling Passes are no longer available, we look forward to continuing to innovate and showing our customers new ways to experience the content they love.”
Sling launched the passes in August 2025
, giving streamers more flexible options to watch content without signing up for a lengthy subscription. At launch, there was a trio of short-term streaming options: the $4.99 Day Pass, $9.99 Weekend Pass, and $14.99 Week Pass. The timing of the launch was intentional. The short-term options popped up just before football season. Cord cutters could use the passes, which included ESPN, to access Sling just long enough to catch a college football or NFL game over the weekend.
There was immediate pushback on the packages. Disney sued DISH
, Sling’s parent company, accusing the platform of breaching its licensing agreement by not discussing the short-term offerings with Disney before launching. Sling called the lawsuit “meritless.” Warner Bros. followed with their own lawsuit
, also citing breach of contract.
It’s likely that the lawsuits were at least partially behind the decision to shut down the passes, but there was no official explanation given.
Sling is now back to its regular lineup of packages
: Sling Orange and Sling Blue; the combined Sling Orange and Blue; and the newer “Basic” plans, Sling Essentials and Sling Select, which are each $19.99 per month.
