1, 3, & 7 Day Passes by Sling - Information & Details
1, 3, & 7 Day Passes Give You Even More Flexibility To Stream Sling
With 1, 3, & 7 Day Passes from Sling, new customers and existing Sling Blue and Select subscribers can now pick the window to watch their favorite live sports, news, and entertainment on Sling Orange.
IMPORTANT UPDATE ON DAY PASSES : Sling was built to give our customers the ultimate control over their entertainment, and Sling Passes delivered on that promise for millions. Though Sling Passes are no longer available, we look forward to continuing to innovate and showing our customers new ways to experience the content they love.
When it comes to streaming your favorite live sports, news, and entertainment, Sling unlocked a new level of flexibility with the launch of our Passes subscription
earlier this year. We’re still not done.
Sling is now offering 1, 3, & 7 Day Passes , allowing subscribers to watch the 34 channels available with Sling Orange
. While our 1 Day Pass ($4.99)
and 7 Day Pass ($14.99)
remain the same, the new 3 Day Pass ($9.99)
can be used for any consecutive three day block. So if you want to watch college football
on Saturday as well as Monday Night Football , get the 3 Day Pass on Saturday.
What Are the 1, 3, & 7 Day Passes?
UPDATE: The channels available with Day Passes are still available with a monthly subscription to Sling Orange. If you’re specifically interested in sports programming on ESPN/ESPN2, a $19.99/mo subscription to Sling Essentials is the most affordable way to watch with Sling.
If you've previously purchased a subscription to one of our Passes, nothing is changing. You still get access to popular sports channels like ESPN
and ESPN2 , top entertainment channels like TNT, TBS, and AMC , breaking news on CNN and Bloomberg TV , home and food programming on HGTV and Food Network , children’s programming on Disney Channel and Nick Jr. , true crime on Investigation Discovery and A&E , hit movies on MGM+ Drive-In , adult animation on Comedy Central , and much more!
When your 1, 3, or 7 Day Pass expires, you won’t need to remember to cancel; you’ll never be charged beyond what you paid for. You can record up to 50 hours of programming with our free Cloud DVR
, and rewatch those recordings whenever you’re subscribed to Sling. And even after your Pass expires, you can watch over 600 channels at no charge with Sling Freestream
.
Can I Add Extras to Day, Week, and Weekend Passes?
Yes! In addition to the 34 channels available with 1, 3, or 7 Day Passes, you can add a variety of Extras to watch even more programming! The following Extra Packs are available for $1 each with the 1 Day Pass, $2 with a 3 Day Pass, and $3 with the 7 Day Pass :
Sports Extra - Includes SEC Network, ACC Network, ESPNU, NBA TV, and more.
News Extra - NewsMax, NewsNation, OAN Network, Law & Crime, Science Channel, and more.
Entertainment Extra - Paramount Network, MTV, Game Show Network, TV Land and more.
Hollywood Extra - Turner Classic Movies, SundanceTV, REELZ, and more.
Lifestyle Extra - Hallmark Channel, VH1, Lifetime Movie Network, and more.
Heartland Extra - Great American Family, Outdoor Channel, INSP, and more.
Kids Extra - Disney Junior, Disney XD, NickToons, TeenNick, and more.
Pay-Per-View is Available with Passes
If there’s a new release you’re dying to watch or a boxing Pay-Per-View
you want to order, you can access PPV and VOD content with 1, 3, or 7 Day Passes. After you complete your order, simply navigate to the “Rentals” section of the Sling app to find whatever you’d like to order on Pay-Per-View.
Sling Blue and Select Subscribers Can Now Add Passes to Their Account
If you have a subscription to Sling Blue
or Select
and there’s a game you want to watch on ESPN or other channels available only in Sling Orange, we have great news: You can now add a 1, 3, or 7 Day Pass to your existing subscription ! To add a Pass to your Sling Blue or Select subscription, sign in to your Sling account and visit the “My Account” page
on our website or in the Sling app. Conversely, if you subscribe to a 1, 3, or 7 Day Pass, you can add a monthly subscription to Sling Orange, Blue, Orange + Blue, or Select by visiting “My Account.”
