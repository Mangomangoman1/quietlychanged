Any extras you add to your Sling Select service will be included in your three streams. If you subscribe to both services in Sling Essentials + Sling Select, you can enjoy up to four streams at the same time. Because you are purchasing two separate services in Sling Essentials + Sling Select, you can get the total number of streams included on each separate service—one stream for any channel on the single-stream Sling Essentials service and three streams for channels on the multi-stream Sling Select service.
