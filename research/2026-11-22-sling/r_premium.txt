What premium options are available to add to my Sling subscription?
There are 40+ premium streaming subscriptions that can be added directly to Sling so you can watch all your movies and shows from one app.
How much do premium subscription add-ons cost?
Prices vary by service. From
your account
select
Add channels and services
then scroll to
PREMIUM PLANS
to view all of our options and their prices.
How do I add premium content to my account?
From a web browser
1.
Sign into your account
.
2. Select the
Add channels and services
tab near the top of the page.
3. Select
Add
from any card to include those channels in your subscription.
4. When prompted, select
Confirm
to apply the changes to your account.
Directly from the Sling app
On most devices you can add premium services directly from the Sling app.
Programming that isn’t a part of your current subscription will be shown with a lock icon. Once you select it, you can review the price and confirm your choice to add it to your subscription or press
back
to cancel.
Do I need a Sling base plan to add premium content?
No! You can add premium subscriptions to your Sling account without a base plan. You can subscribe to any of our premium subscriptions with just your Sling Freestream account, or get more channels by
including a Sling base plan
.
Are there any free trials of premium content?
We don’t have free trials for premium subscriptions. Sometimes we do have promotional pricing.

your account
select
Add channels and services

How do I cancel a premium subscription?
You can make changes to your Sling account anytime:
1. From a web browser,
sign in to your account
.

tab.
2. Find any subscription you no longer want, and select
