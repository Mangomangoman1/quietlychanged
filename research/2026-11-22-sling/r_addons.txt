About
What’s on Sling?

What is Sling TV?
Sling is a flexible TV streaming service that connects you to the best live TV without rigid contracts.
Get monthly access to your favorite channels, add just the extras you’ll watch, and stop paying for all the fluff.
On a tight budget? Watch 600+ free channels with
Freestream
.
Can I record programs on Sling?
Subscribers can record live TV and save it to their DVR with 50 hours of free DVR storage, and can extend to unlimited storage by adding Unlimited DVR for just $5/mo.
Sling’s DVR is in the cloud, which means you can watch your recorded content from any logged-in device, wherever you have Wi-Fi.
Local Now, Pluto, and any local channels added with an over-the-air antenna can’t be recorded.
Can I watch Sling on multiple devices at the same time?
The amount of devices you can watch Sling on at the same time varies depending on your base plan and which channels you’re watching.
Learn more about streaming on multiple devices
.
Are shows available on demand?
We have an ever-changing list of thousands of TV shows and movies available on demand!
Use the search bar in your guide to see if your favorites are available.
What if I want to cancel?
Monthly subscribers can cancel anytime by
visiting their account
. You’ll continue to have access to Sling until the period you’ve paid for ends and won’t be charged again until you resubscribe.
Sling is proud to have flexible options. Come and go as you please!
How do I get started?
Start watching live sports, news, and entertainment in just a few steps.
1.
Create an account
2. Choose your channel lineup
3. Start watching
You can also watch
Freestream
until you’re ready to decide on the best plan for you! No account needed.
What do I need to get started with Sling?
You’ll need a reliable internet connection of at least 3 Mbps and a
supported device
.
Sling works on streaming devices, smart TVs, mobile phones, computers, tablets, and more!
For a great experience watching on multiple devices, an internet speed of 25 Mbps is recommended.
Check your internet speed
.
Does Sling offer a free trial?
Although there’s no free trial for Sling, anyone can watch limited channels on
Freestream
at no charge, and access doesn’t end after a few days like a free trial!
How long is a Sling subscription?
Sling is a monthly subscription that you can customize on your schedule. Add content or change plans depending on what you want to watch in a given month.

Watch CNN and BBC World News with any Sling subscription.
For more news, choose
Sling Blue

What live sports can I watch on Sling?
Watch College NCAA football, NFL, WNBA, and more, live.
Pick the plan that includes the games, matches, and races you live for. Sling has ESPN, FS1, TNT, TBS and more.
Explore sports channels

Do I get local channels with Sling?
Sling Blue and Sling Orange & Blue include live streaming of some local channels, but access depends on where you live.
Check which local channels are included in your area
Local channels are not included with Sling Orange.
If your area or preferred Sling plan doesn’t include local channels, you may be able to get local channels for free with an over-the-air (OTA) antenna.
Can I add my favorite show?

Sling is the most flexible TV streaming service, and also the best TV streaming value on the market.
For under $51 you can choose a monthly plan with the channels that matter most to you.
