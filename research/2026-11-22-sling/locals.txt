Missing local channels?
If you’re not able to see the local channels that are listed for your zip code, try:
In your Sling app, visit Settings
then select Location
Select Current location
then follow the instructions on screen to confirm or update your location.
If you don’t see the option to edit your location, you can try restarting your router.
Get local channels for free with an Over the Air antenna
Using an over the air (OTA) antenna, most people can watch live local channels on their TV for free.
Adding an OTA antenna can help you add local channels to Sling for free no matter what your base plan is.
Before you buy an OTA antenna, confirm that the signal in your area is strong enough.
Check if an OTA will work in your area.
You can find OTA antennas at any big box store or major online retailer.
You'll complete the OTA setup through your TV's settings.
LG and Vizio TV's made in 2020 or later can use an OTA antenna to add local channels directly to your Sling guide.
Any local channels available by OTA antenna in your area, including channels not available with any Sling subscription, will show up in your Sling guide.

Frequently asked questions
Why don't I see local channels in my Sling guide?
The local channels available on Sling vary by location. Depending on where you live, you may have limited access to local channels through Sling.
Most people can get access to live local programming for free using an over-the-air antenna.
What if I need help installing an indoor OTA antenna?
If you need help installing an indoor OTA antenna, you can schedule an appointment with OnTech services
. They will send a technician to install and teach you how to use your antenna.
My area doesn't receive strong OTA signal, what now?
For some customers, an outdoor antenna can improve signal enough to receive local channels.
You can get help installing and learning to use an outdoor antenna with our OnTech service.
Where can I find local channels in my guide?
If local channels are included with your Sling plan
you'll see a Locals tab in your guide, and local programming will be integrated across your Sling experience.
If you’re using an OTA antenna with a 2020 or newer LG or Vizio TV
, local channels will be integrated into your Sling guide on that device.
If you've used an OTA antenna to add local channels
and aren't seeing them in your Sling guide, try switching your TV to a different input.
If you’re watching from a mobile device or location away from home
, channels may vary.
Why can't I record local channels?
Sling subscribers can record local channels that come with their Sling subscription, but not those channels that are added to your guide using an OTA antenna.
Use the chart above to see which channels are included with Sling in your area - those channels can be recorded!
Why don't I see all my local channels on my mobile device?
Channels you're getting by over the air antenna will only appear on the device that's directly connected to the antenna.
