'Apple One' Just Went Up in Price
Monday July 20, 2026 12:18 pm PDT
by
Joe Rossignol
As a result of
Apple Music going up in price
last week, two
Apple One
plans have also received price increases around the world.
In the U.S., the Apple One Family and Apple One Premier both went up by $2 per month.

The individual Apple One plan is the clear winner in the table above, as it did not receive a price increase despite an individual subscription to Apple Music increasing from $10.99 per month to $11.99 per month in the U.S. last week.
Even the Apple One Family and Apple One Premier plans technically became a bit more valuable, as they each only went up by $2 per month, despite an Apple Music Family subscription seeing a $3 increase from $16.99 per month to $19.99 per month.
The price increases extend to Australia, Canada, the U.K., and other countries around the world.
