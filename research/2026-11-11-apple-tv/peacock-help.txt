Apple TV and Peacock Bundle
Full guide
What is the Apple TV and Peacock Bundle?
The Apple TV and Peacock bundle is a streaming plan that gives you access to both platforms in one convenient plan.
With this bundle, you can enjoy Apple TV, including exclusive Apple Originals, and Peacock Premium content – including hit series, fan-favorite shows from NBC and Bravo, blockbuster movies, and live sports – all starting at $17.99 a month.
You can subscribe to both services in a single transaction, through either Peacock or Apple. To review the plans and pricing, please visit the
Plan Picker
page.
How much does the bundle cost?
There are two different Peacock and Apple TV subscription bundles available:
Peacock Premium + Apple TV/$17.99 a month
Peacock Premium Plus + Apple TV/$22.99 a month
Where can I watch content from both services?
You can stream Apple TV content in the Apple TV app, and Peacock content via the Peacock app. However, there will be a limited amount of Apple TV and Peacock episodes available to sample on both platforms.
Am
I able to purchase the bundle if I am already subscribed to Peacock?
If you are already an existing Peacock subscriber, you are still able to upgrade to the Apple TV and Peacock bundle by signing up in the Apple TV app, the Peacock app on iOS, iPadOS and tvOS, or on tv.apple.com. To avoid having multiple subscriptions, you may need to manage any existing subscriptions before signing up for the bundle.
If your existing Peacock subscription is billed by Peacock directly, you may also purchase the bundle by signing into your
Account
on the Peacock website and navigating to the “Plans & Payment” page. Under “Your Plan”, you can select “Change Plan” to review the Apple TV and Peacock Bundle options. Please note, if you are currently on a Peacock Annual plan, your access to the bundle will not begin until your existing Annual plan expires.
