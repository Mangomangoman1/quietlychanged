What is the Apple TV and Peacock bundle?
It’s a streaming plan that allows you to subscribe to both Apple TV and Peacock for one discounted price.
**Apple
TV subscription (formerly Apple
TV+) does not include third party subscription services, MLS
Season
Pass, or content available for rental or purchase in the Apple
TV
app.
How much does it cost and what plans are available?
There are two plans—Apple TV and Peacock Premium for $17.99/month (includes ads for Peacock), and Apple TV and Peacock Premium Plus for $22.99/month (ad-free with limited exclusions for Peacock). There are no ads for Apple TV regardless of price.
Can I get Peacock with Apple One?
Yes, if you subscribe to Apple One Family or Apple One Premier, you can get
Peacock Premium Plus
for a discounted price when you sign up from your iOS device (desktop sign up is not currently supported). If you don’t have Apple One Family or Apple One Premier, you can
sign up
and then add Peacock Premium Plus. Learn more about
Apple One
.
How do I sign up for the Apple TV and Peacock bundle?
You can sign up in the Apple TV app, the Peacock app, or on
tv.apple.com
.
Is the Apple TV and Peacock bundle available in my country?
For now, it’s available in the US only.
Where can I watch Apple TV and Peacock?
You can watch Apple TV in the Apple TV app. It’s already on your favorite Apple devices as well as smart TVs, gaming consoles,
tv.apple.com
, and more. You can watch Peacock on the Peacock app,
peacocktv.com
, and Peacock-supported streaming platforms.
What if I am already subscribed to Apple TV and want to sign up for the bundle?
If you’re an Apple TV subscriber, you can sign in to your account to bundle Peacock with your existing subscription and save over 30%. If you’re subscribed to the annual plan, you can still bundle and save. To avoid having multiple subscriptions, you may need to manage any existing subscriptions before signing up for the bundle.
What if I am already subscribed to Peacock and want to sign up for the bundle?
If you’re a Peacock subscriber, you can sign in to your account to bundle Apple TV with your existing subscription and save over 30%. If you’ve subscribed to the annual plan directly with Peacock, your access to the bundle won’t begin until your annual plan ends. If you’ve subscribed to the annual plan directly with Apple, your access to the bundle will begin immediately. To avoid having multiple subscriptions, you may need to manage any existing subscriptions before signing up for the bundle.
