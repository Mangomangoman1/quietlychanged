## Post (274 weighted chars)

California adds sales tax to software on Jan 1, 2027.

Downloaded and cloud software (SaaS) gets 7.25%+. Streaming, music, audiobooks, e-books and games don't.

Prepaying in December may not help: draft rules tax each subscription period from Jan 1.

## Optional self-reply (265 weighted chars, no link)

What's taxed: prewritten (off-the-shelf) software, however it's delivered. Custom software stays exempt.

If a seller doesn't charge California tax, you may owe use tax yourself.

The rules for subscriptions are still in draft at CDTFA, so the details could change.
