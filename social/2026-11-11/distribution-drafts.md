Title: PSA: From Nov 18 you're auto-enrolled in Robinhood's "Class Action Service", which keeps 20% of any settlement payout (how to opt out)

Body:
Robinhood's Customer Agreement (current version "Revised November 2, 2026") has a new Section 36A:

> "The terms of this Section 36A are effective on or after November 18, 2026. Your Account is automatically enrolled in Robinhood's Class Action Service ... unless you unenroll"

What it does: a third-party vendor files securities class-action claims for you on stocks you bought, as your "agent and attorney-in-fact".

What it costs: the RHF Fee Schedule lists the Class Action Service Fee as "20% of the class action settlement proceeds received on your behalf." Filing the claim form yourself with the settlement administrator is normally free.

Other things in 36A:
- You get 7 business days after Robinhood's notice to skip a specific case, or it's filed automatically.
- Once a claim is filed for you, "you cannot separately bring a lawsuit, at any time, against any named defendant" in that case, and you can't submit your own claim form for it.
- If a payout reaches you without the fee taken out, the unpaid fee "creates an Obligation owed by you" and they can debit your account.
- You can unenroll any time "through the Platform" or via Customer Service. Unenrolling after a claim is filed doesn't pull you out of that case.

Whether 20% is worth it depends on how much you value not tracking settlements yourself. But it's opt-out, so it's worth knowing about before the 18th.

Sources:
- Customer Agreement (Section 36A): https://cdn.robinhood.com/assets/robinhood/legal/Robinhood-Customer-Agreement.pdf
- RHF Fee Schedule: https://cdn.robinhood.com/assets/robinhood/legal/RHF%20Fee%20Schedule.pdf
