Hi Scott,

A follow-up to your August 2025 story on Verizon's fee increases: the Administrative and Telco Recovery Charge is going up again, by more this time.

Verizon's Important Plan Information page (figures "As of October 1, 2026") now lists: "Administrative and Telco Recovery Charge ($3.78 per voice line (Increases to $4.69 effective Nov 15, 2026), $3.97 per data-only line)." https://www.verizon.com/support/important-plan-information/

That's +$0.91 per voice line a month (+24%), the biggest single jump since the fee was renamed in 2022. Two weeks earlier, on Nov 1, the Regulatory Charge goes from $0.21 to $0.26 (also on the surcharges page, https://www.verizon.com/support/surcharges/). Together Verizon's two flat per-line charges go from $3.99 to $4.95, so a four-line account pays $3.84 a month more, about $46 a year, before USF and taxes.

A few details that might be useful:
- Verizon's surcharges FAQ says the charge "is retained by Verizon" and is "not a governmental tax or a government imposed fee."
- The myPlan 3-year price guarantee doesn't stop it. The same Verizon page says "Plan perks, taxes, fees, discounts, promotions and third-party services are subject to change."
- Data-only lines stay at $3.97, and Verizon Home Internet and Verizon One plans are excluded.
- After Nov 15, Verizon's $4.95 sits between T-Mobile's $5.49 per voice line (since Oct 12) and AT&T's $4.99.
- Interestingly, the surcharges page still only mentions the Nov 1 change. The $4.69 figure is on the plan-information page.

Title: Administrative and Telco Recovery Charge goes from $3.78 to $4.69 per voice line on Nov 15 (Verizon's Important Plan Information page)
Body:
Heads up for anyone who budgets their bill to the cent. Verizon's Important Plan Information page (https://www.verizon.com/support/important-plan-information/), in the "Taxes, Surcharges and Fees" section dated "As of October 1, 2026," now says:

"Regulatory Charge ($0.21 per voice line (Increases to $0.26 effective Nov 1, 2026), $0.02 per data-only line) and Administrative and Telco Recovery Charge ($3.78 per voice line (Increases to $4.69 effective Nov 15, 2026), $3.97 per data-only line)."

So per voice line, the two flat Verizon charges go from $3.99 now, to $4.04 on Nov 1, to $4.95 on Nov 15. On 4 lines that's $15.96 to $19.80 a month (+$3.84), before USF and taxes.

Things worth knowing:
- It's a Verizon charge, not a tax. The surcharges FAQ (https://www.verizon.com/support/surcharges/) says it "is retained by Verizon" and is "not a governmental tax or a government imposed fee."
- The myPlan 3-year price guarantee doesn't cover it. Same IPI page: "Plan perks, taxes, fees, discounts, promotions and third-party services are subject to change."
- Data-only lines (tablets, watches) stay at $3.97 + $0.02. Verizon Home Internet isn't subject to these charges, and Verizon One plans include them.
- The surcharges page currently only mentions the Nov 1 Regulatory Charge change, so you won't see the $4.69 there yet.

For comparison: T-Mobile is $5.49 per voice line since Oct 12, and AT&T's fee schedule lists $4.99 per line.
