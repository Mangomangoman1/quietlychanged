Wells Fargo's Nov 24 online agreement adds a brand-new AI Agent section.

An "AI Agent" includes tools that take "non-autonomous" action — not just chatbots. Whatever it does in your account is treated as authorized by you.

To revoke one, keep proof and tell the bank. Until then, you're still on the hook.
