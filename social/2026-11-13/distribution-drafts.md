Body:
Temu posted new US Terms of Use and a new Privacy Policy that take effect December 7, 2026. The banner at the top of the terms page says the new text "will become effective from December 7, 2026."

What's new in the arbitration section (Section 19):

> If an offer made by one party is not accepted by the other party, and the other party fails to obtain a more favorable award on the claim at issue, the other party shall pay the offering party's costs incurred after the offer was made, including any attorney's fees and fees paid to the arbitration provider

So if you ever have a dispute, Temu offers to settle, you say no, and the arbitrator awards you less (or nothing), you could owe Temu's lawyer costs from that point on. The clause says it's "limited or eliminated" where a law bars this.

Also new: a ban on "buy-for-me agents, large language model-driven bots" placing orders without human review, and the privacy policy no longer says Temu won't use your name or email address to show you interest-based ads.

How to reject: Section 19.12 says you can reject a material change to the arbitration agreement "within thirty (30) days of such change becoming effective" by writing to Whaleco Inc., 1666 Connecticut Ave NW #500, Washington, DC 20009, USA. That's about Jan 6, 2027. Include your name and account email/phone, say you reject the Dec 7, 2026 changes to the Arbitration Agreement, and keep proof of mailing.
