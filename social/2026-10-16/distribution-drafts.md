## r/RobinHood (text post; check sub rules for links first, otherwise link only the PDFs)
Title: Heads up: new Customer Agreement §36A auto-enrolls you in a class-action claims service that keeps 20% (starts Nov 18)
Body: The Oct 1, 2026 Customer Agreement adds Section 36A "Class Action Service." Accounts are automatically enrolled; a third-party vendor files claims in securities class-action settlements for stocks you held, acting as your "attorney-in-fact." The RHF Fee Schedule lists the fee as "20% of the class action settlement proceeds received on your behalf." Once a claim is filed for you, you can't separately sue that defendant or file your own claim form for that settlement. They say they'll notify you before each filing and you get 7 business days to exclude a case; you can unenroll any time in the app or through support. For reference, E*TRADE's similar service takes 6% and Edward Jones' takes 15%. Filing a claim form yourself normally costs nothing. Sources: [Customer Agreement PDF] [Fee Schedule PDF]
