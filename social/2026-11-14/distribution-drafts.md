Hi Reece,

A service-y privacy story that hasn't been reported: Hinge has a new setting, Chat Insights, and its help center now says: "We use OpenAI to look for patterns and make suggestions that can help improve your experience."

A second new page, "How Does Hinge Use My Messages" (created Oct 28, edited Nov 13), says your messages may be used to recommend other daters, give "in-chat suggestions or feedback," and train "the specific models behind these features." To turn it off: photo icon > Settings > Chat Insights.

Title: PSA: Hinge's new "Chat Insights" setting uses OpenAI on your chats, and Hinge says messages may be used to train its models. Here's where to turn it off.

Body:
Hinge added two help-center pages in the last couple of weeks about a feature called Chat Insights.

From "What are Chat Insights?":
> Chat Insights helps us understand how your chats are going, so we can support you in the right moments. We use OpenAI to look for patterns and make suggestions that can help improve your experience.

From "How Does Hinge Use My Messages":
> This may include recommending other daters who we think better reflect your preferences, offering in-chat suggestions or feedback, tailoring features to your interests, or training the specific models behind these features.

> However, some use cases may require Hinge to process raw messages, which may include identifiable information or data considered sensitive under applicable law.

> Where and as legally required, Hinge will not process messages in a conversation unless all parties are opted in.

Neither page says whether it's on or off by default, so worth checking. To turn it off: tap your photo icon > Settings (gear) > scroll to Chat Insights > toggle off. You can still chat with it off. Safety/moderation scanning isn't affected by the toggle either way.

For comparison, Hinge's page for its older AI feature (Convo Starters) says it doesn't process private messages and that OpenAI can't train on that data and deletes it within 30 days. The Chat Insights pages don't say that.
