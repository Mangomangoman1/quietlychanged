Hinge’s OpenAI features: then and now
Last year’s AI feature skipped private messages. This one reads them.
Convo Starters · launched Dec 2025
“Convo Starters do not analyze or otherwise process facial geometry, biometric data, or private messages.”
OpenAI “cannot use the data to train its models, and deletes the data within 30 days of receipt.”
Chat Insights · help page Nov 2026
“Chat Insights helps us understand how your chats are going … We use OpenAI to look for patterns and make suggestions”
No mention of OpenAI training or deletion limits.
Sources: help.hinge.co “What are Convo Starters?” and “What are Chat Insights?”, both checked Nov 14, 2026.
