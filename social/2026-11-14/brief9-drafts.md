Subject: Five things you own that lose features on New Year's Day

Hi Ben,

You were first to flag that Hunter is ending the HunterSMART cloud link to Google Home and Alexa on January 1, 2027. Hunter has since posted the same facts on its own blog, and it is not the only product that loses a cloud feature at the turn of the year.

We put five of them in one table, each checked against the manufacturer's page, with what still works, what to save, and a calendar file:

1. HunterSMART fans, January 1, 2027. Voice commands, scenes and automations that use the legacy account link stop. The app and remote still work. Matter is only for eligible models, and you have to re-pair. Official announcement (Nov 5): https://www.hunterfan.com/blogs/hunter-blog/important-update-to-huntersmart%E2%84%A2-google-home-and-amazon-alexa-connectivity-via-app-to-app-linking
2. KYMCO Noodoe scooter dash, January 1, 2027, worldwide. The app will not launch and the cloud is shut down. Navigation, messages, weather and the gallery stop. Only the speed reading keeps working as normal. Official notice: https://kymcousa.com/kymco-noodoe-system-discontinuation/
3. ZWILLING Fresh & Save app, December 31, 2026. From January 2027 you cannot log in, even if the app is still installed. The vacuum boxes still work; box contents and expiry dates need to be copied first. Official FAQ: https://www.zwilling.com/us/magazine/faqs-app-exit.html
4. Nedap MyPowerRouter.com, December 31, 2026. Online monitoring, energy data, remote updates and remaining support end. The unit's local screen still works. Official notice: https://www.powerrouter.com/en/discontinuation-mypowerrouter-com/
5. IKEA Gift Registry, after December 27, 2026. Saved registries are deleted and are not moved anywhere. Official U.S. registry page: https://www.ikea.com/us/en/gift-registry/

A sixth December deadline, not in the "five" headline but in the same table, is remove.bg on December 1 (unused credits expire and do not transfer).
