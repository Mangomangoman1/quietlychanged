Title: PSA: Cricket's new terms (Dec 1) add a 60-day wait before small claims court. The window to reject them by mail closes ~30 days after the Oct 1 text

Body:
If you got Cricket's "terms updated" text around Oct 1, here's what changes on Dec 1, straight from the new terms (Revised October 1, 2026):

- Small claims now has a waiting period. "Any arbitration or small claims court proceeding cannot begin until after the Informal Resolution Period has ended." That period is at least 60 days after Cricket receives your signed Notice of Dispute. Under the previous version you could go straight to small claims.
- The Notice of Dispute is mail or courier only. The old terms also allowed submitting it electronically.
- Arbitration moves from AAA to JAMS, and the class action and jury waivers now also apply in court.

You can keep service and reject the arbitration changes, but only by U.S. mail within 30 days of the first notice. Send your name, address, phone number, account number, and a statement personally signed by you that you reject the change to the arbitration provision, to:

Legal Department – Revised Arbitration Opt-Out, Cricket, 208 S. Akard, Office #2900.13, Dallas, Texas 75202

If your text came around Oct 1, that means mailing it by about Oct 31. Get a certificate of mailing or tracking and keep a copy.

Sources:
- New terms: https://www.cricketwireless.com/terms (section 1.3.2.2 and 1.3.2.8)
- Cricket's summary of the update: https://www.cricketwireless.com/legal-info/termsupdate
- Previous version: https://www.cricketwireless.com/legal-info/terms-previous-version

Not legal advice. AT&T made the same small-claims change in its consumer agreement effective Nov 1.
