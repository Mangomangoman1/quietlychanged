## Post (258 weighted chars)

New FCC rule, Nov 25: companies that robocall or text you can pick the ONLY way you're allowed to opt out.

Reply "please stop texting me"? If they chose STOP, they can ignore it.

And STOP on one alert type no longer stops the rest.
