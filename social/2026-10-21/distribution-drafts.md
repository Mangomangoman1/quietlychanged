Key lines:
- "Effective November 9, 2026, IPL will be officially retired" and "the site will be taken offline permanently."
- "We are not migrating any content or product features from IPL to a new platform."
- "Public pages on IPL will stop being available after November 9, 2026," and they say cached copies on archive sites are "outside our control."
- Accounts and data get deleted; logins end Nov 9.

Context: the domain was the nonprofit Internet Public Library from 1995 to 2015 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_Public_Library). Since about 2020 it has been a commercial student-essay site, which is what's closing. So the current pages are mostly user essays, but a lot of old links across the web point at ipl.org.

If you've uploaded anything there, the notice says you can download it until Nov 9. Posting in case anyone wants to grab public pages before they go.

Hi Ernie,

A Tedium-shaped one, we think: ipl.org is shutting down for the second time.

The Internet Public Library opened in March 1995 out of a graduate seminar Joseph Janes led, became ipl2 under a Drexel-led consortium, and closed in 2015 (Janes wrote "RIP, IPL" for American Libraries at the time). Around 2020 the domain came back as a commercial student-essay site, owned by Barnes & Noble Education as of 2020 per Wikipedia and now one of the "Learneo businesses" alongside Course Hero, QuillBot and Scribbr.

That site now has a quiet retirement notice, dated Sep 28: "Effective November 9, 2026, IPL will be officially retired," "We are not migrating any content or product features," public pages "will stop being available," and accounts and data will be deleted. We haven't found any coverage.
