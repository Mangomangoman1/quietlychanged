Hi Suzanne,

A follow-on to your Oct 29 piece on the FTC's personalized-pricing orders. Rakuten, the cash back site with more than 17 million members, wasn't one of the nine companies. The new terms (Section 4): "Rewards rates and offers available through the Services are personalized and may vary by Member based on factors including your account history, shopping behavior, and membership status as described in our Privacy Policy.
