Grammarly / Superhuman privacy policy · changed this week
It can now license data on people who
don’t use Grammarly, social profiles included
BEFORE
“we may license data such as your contact info or information about your company or job function from third parties.”
NOW
“We may collect or license information about users and non-users from third parties, such as contact information, information about company or job function, or links to social media and online profiles.”
New: some of it “may be visible to other users.”
Source: grammarly.com/privacy-policy, snapshots Nov 16 vs Nov 19, 2026. Also adds GPTZero.quietlychanged.com
