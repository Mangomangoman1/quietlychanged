## Reddit r/Evernote (text post; read the sub rules first. If self-promo / off-site links aren’t allowed, use only the two Evernote URLs below)
Title: Dec 1 terms update: Italian affiliate, Brazil arbitration, no password-manager use
Body:
Evernote’s help article (updated Oct 1) says if you signed up before October 1, 2026, the new Terms take effect December 1, 2026. I read the help page against the live Terms (Last update: October 1, 2026). The parts that stood out:
- Your contract transfers to Bending Spoons Operations S.p.A. in Milan.
- EEA / UK / Switzerland: Italian law, no arbitration agreement.
- Brazil: no longer Evernote do Brasil / Brazilian law; you get the non-EEA terms including their arbitration agreement. Those Terms still include a 30-day arbitration opt-out by emailing optout@evernote.com.
- Evernote Points are no longer issued.
- AI features: your inputs/outputs are yours; Evernote says it doesn’t use them to train models.
- New conduct rule vs the Jan 1, 2026 Terms: you may not use Evernote “as a password manager or to store sensitive financial information,” or to back up a hard drive / archive files.
Not legal advice — check the primary pages if this affects you.
Sources: https://help.evernote.com/hc/en-us/articles/55887630067987-An-update-on-your-Evernote-agreements-our-Privacy-Policy-and-the-Terms-of-Service and https://evernote.com/legal/terms-of-service
