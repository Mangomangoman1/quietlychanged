AMAZON'S $25 THANKSGIVING MEAL · THROUGH NOV 26

What delivery adds to the $25

PRIME MEMBERS
Free Same-Day Delivery on grocery orders of $25 or more. Under $25: $2.99.
NOT A PRIME MEMBER
$12.99 Same-Day Delivery fee, regardless of order size. $25 + $12.99 = $37.99 before tax.
WHAT PRIME COSTS
$14.99 a month or $139 a year. Last raised Feb 18, 2022 (from $12.99 and $119).
