"Whether a message can appear on the web depends on your Channel Messages setting when you send it. [...] Turning the setting off doesn't change this for messages you sent while it was on. [...] To prevent one of those messages from appearing [...] delete it from Discord."

Setting: User Settings > Data & Privacy > "Show my message content from Discoverable channels on web pages" (on by default in the US and most regions)
