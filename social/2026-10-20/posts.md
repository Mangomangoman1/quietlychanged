## 1. Proton
Image: /workspace/quietlychanged/social/2026-10-20/proton-inactive-deletion-notice.png

Proton just softened a promise in its terms.

Free accounts inactive 12+ months can be deleted, mail, files and passwords included.

The old terms: "You will receive notices" 30, 15 and 7 days before.
Now: "As a mere courtesy," Proton "will aim to."

https://quietlychanged.com/changes/2026-10-20-proton-inactive-free-accounts-deletion-warnings-are-now-only-a-courtesy/

## 2. Spotify
Image: /workspace/quietlychanged/social/2026-10-20/spotify-2-month-trial.png

Spotify just doubled its free trial.

New US users now get 2 months of Premium Individual for $0 instead of 1, then $12.99/mo.

Only if you've never had Premium, and the offer ends November 1.

https://quietlychanged.com/changes/2026-10-20-spotify-premium-free-trial-doubled-to-2-months-for-new-users-until-november-1/
