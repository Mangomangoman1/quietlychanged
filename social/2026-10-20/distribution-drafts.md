Effective Nov 12. Section 11 is rewritten: JAMS instead of AAA, each case "a single, confidential arbitration," and when 25+ similar claims are filed within 90 days or through coordinated lawyers they're "consolidated into one arbitration before one arbitrator" (the current terms batch them with one arbitrator per batch). It applies to claims that arose before, adds CCP 998 offers, and lets the arbitrator split fees with claimants' lawyers or funders. The upside is a new opt-out: email litigation@pinterest.com "within 30 days of first becoming subject to it," which for existing users means Nov 12 to Dec 12. Title: PSA: Pinterest's new Terms (Nov 12) add a 30-day window to opt out of forced arbitration
Body:
Pinterest's updated Terms of Service take effect November 12, 2026, and continuing to use Pinterest after that date counts as accepting them (per their help page: https://help.pinterest.com/en/article/changes-to-our-policies).

The arbitration section (Section 11, applies outside the EEA, Switzerland and the UK) is rewritten. A few things in the preview (https://policy.pinterest.com/en/terms-of-service-preview):
- Arbitration moves to JAMS, and each case is "a single, confidential arbitration."
- If 25+ people file similar claims, they get consolidated into one arbitration before one arbitrator.
- It covers claims that arose before the new terms.

The new part that's actually in users' favor: "You may opt out of this Section 11 within 30 days of first becoming subject to it by emailing litigation@pinterest.com with your name, account email, and a statement that you're opting out." The current terms (https://policy.pinterest.com/en/terms-of-service) have no opt-out.

For existing accounts that means emailing between Nov 12 and Dec 12, 2026. Include your name, the email on your account, and a line saying you're opting out of Section 11 (Arbitration) of the Terms of Service. Keep a copy of what you sent.

Not legal advice, just reading the text.

Pinterest's new Terms of Service take effect November 12, and the arbitration section has been rewritten in a way we haven't seen reported:

- When 25 or more similar claims are filed, they're "consolidated into one arbitration before one arbitrator." Today they're split into AAA batches with one arbitrator per batch.
- Each case is "a single, confidential arbitration," it moves from AAA to JAMS, and it covers claims that arose before the new terms.
- New 30-day opt-out: users can email litigation@pinterest.com "within 30 days of first becoming subject to it." For existing users that's Nov 12 to Dec 12. The current terms have no opt-out.

The new privacy policy, effective the same day, adds ads "when you're streaming a show on your connected TV" and "associating devices that may relate to the same person or household." Pinterest's help page tells users "Your Pinterest experience won't change."
