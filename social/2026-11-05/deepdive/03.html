Smule · the opt-out, and the catch
WHEN YOU CAN OPT OUT (SUMMARY OF CHANGES)
Beginning on December 4, 2026, you may opt out of Smule’s AI training, our licensing of de-identified data to third parties, or both ... under Settings > Privacy > AI & Data
WHAT AN OPT-OUT COVERS (NEW PRIVACY POLICY)
Your opt-out applies to all of your User Content, including User Content you posted before opting out, but applies only to De-Identified Data we make available after we process your opt-out. It does not affect De-Identified Data provided to Recording Licensees before then, consistent with applicable law.
Sources: smule.com/en/summary_of_changes/updated and smule.com/en/privacy/updated (effective Dec 4, 2026)
