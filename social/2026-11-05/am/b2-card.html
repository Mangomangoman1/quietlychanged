NINTENDO SWITCH 2, U.S. PRICE

$449.99 → $499.99

“Beginning on September 1, 2026, Nintendo of America will revise the MSRP of the Nintendo Switch 2 system in the U.S. from $449.99 to $499.99… in response to various changes in market conditions, which are expected to extend over the medium to long term.”
Nov 5: Furukawa says Nintendo is not considering another increase. That describes today, not a price lock.
Sources: Nintendo of America notice (May 7, 2026); Nintendo Q2 briefing, Nov 5, 2026 (via Nintendo Everything, OtakuKart)
