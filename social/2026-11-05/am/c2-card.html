ICLOUD “ANYONE WITH THE LINK”, iOS/macOS 27.3+

Which iCloud shares this covers

  • Public-link items are no longer end-to-end encrypted, even with Advanced Data Protection
  • Apple may “move, refuse, modify, remove, or delete” flagged content
  • Not affected: “People you choose” sharing and shared albums
  • 27.3 is in developer beta; public release expected in December
Source: Apple Support 149168 (Nov 3, 2026); MacRumors on the beta and December timing
