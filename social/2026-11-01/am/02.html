CLAUDE CODE AND GAMES · NOV 1, 2026

Not a ban on making games

WHAT CHANGED
Anthropic added two sentences to Claude Code's prompt on Opus 5.5, steering it away from recreating someone else's specific game.
STILL MEANT TO WORK
Original games, genre games, and games built from your own designs and assets.
THE RULE BEHIND IT ISN'T NEW
Anthropic's Usage Policy already bars using Claude to "infringe, misappropriate, or violate the intellectual property rights of a third party."
IF IT REFUSES YOUR OWN WORK
Anthropic says it will "continue to iterate" and asked devs to report wrongful refusals.
Sources: Addy Osmani (Anthropic) on X, Oct 30, 2026 · anthropic.com/legal/aup
