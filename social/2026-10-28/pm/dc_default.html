"Can my messages appear on the web by default?"
"Yes, in most regions. Your Channel Messages setting is on by default [...] It's off by default if your account is in the EU/EEA, UK, Canada, Brazil, Australia, or South Korea."

Switch: User Settings > Data & Privacy > "Show my message content from Discoverable channels on web pages" (desktop or browser; covers your whole account)
