Subject: Booking.com's price match stops promising refunds Dec 10 (it already pays credits)

Hi Gary,

Booking.com's new US Customer Terms of Service, dated December 10, 2026, change the We Price Match promise from a refund to wallet credits.

Today's live terms (updated August 25, 2025) say "we promise to refund the difference." The December 10 PDF says "we promise you Wallet Credits for the difference." After validation they can still "amend the reservation price (if possible)"; otherwise the after-stay step changes from "claim an after-stay refund of the price difference" to "claim after stay Wallet credits of the price difference." The clause doesn't say whether those are Travel Credits (the same document says they "can't 'cash out'") or Cash Credits.

The live We Price Match help page still says "You can claim a refund for the difference" and tells you how to "claim an after-stay refund of the price difference" (I re-checked it on November 12). In January 2026, the u/bookingcom account told a customer on r/Bookingcom: "For We Price Match claims, compensation is provided as travel credit to your Booking.com Wallet... Once added as wallet credit, it cannot be converted back to a refund to the original payment method." The December 10 terms put that practice in writing.

I haven't found US coverage of the December terms. Side-by-side quotes and the official PDF: https://quietlychanged.com/changes/2026-11-08-booking-com-price-match-will-pay-wallet-credits-not-a-refund/

Happy to send the raw text comparison.
