Disney+ price (November 2026): $12.49 with ads, $21.49 Premium, every bundle, Extra Member and every price increase since 2019

Short answer: when we checked Disney+'s US site and help center on November 21, 2026, Disney+ cost $12.49 a month with ads and $21.49 a month for Premium (no ads), or $214.99 a year. The Disney+, Hulu Bundle is $12.99 with ads ($11.99 a month on a 6-month price lock) and $21.99 without; with HBO Max it's $21.99 or $34.99, and with ESPN Unlimited $35.99 or $44.99. Adding someone outside your home as an Extra Member costs $6.99 to $14.99 a month depending on your plan. Ad-free Disney+ launched at $6.99 in November 2019 and has risen six times since, up about 207%.

Published November 7, 2026 · Updated November 21, 2026

These are the US prices on Disney+'s help center page "Disney+ plans and prices" when we checked on November 21, 2026, and they match the plan list on disneyplus.com. The help center says: "The following prices are for new subscriptions as of September 23, 2026."

Both plans list "Stream on multiple devices at a time"; the help center's plan table doesn't give a number, so check your account if you need a specific count. Disney's fine print adds that "Video and audio quality vary depending on your supported device, internet download speed, and the content you are watching at the time," and the video quality page notes that "Disney+ (With Ads) is currently limited to 1080p on Xbox" and "4K streaming is not available on computer browsers." Premium is not entirely ad-free either: "Ads will be served in select live and linear programming."

Disney+ has no free trial ("Please note that Disney+ does not currently offer a free trial"), and you can no longer start a subscription through an app store: "New and returning subscribers are no longer able to sign up and pay for Disney+ through Apple, Google, or Amazon." Existing app-store-billed subscribers aren't affected.

Monthly vs yearly billing

Premium is the only plan with a yearly price. Twelve monthly payments of $21.49 come to $257.88, while a year paid up front is $214.99. That saves $42.89, about 16.6% (our math), which matches Disney's "Save over 16%" label. Disney+ (With Ads) is monthly only, $149.88 over 12 months (our math).

How much more is ad-free Disney+?

Going from Disney+ (With Ads) at $12.49 to Premium at $21.49 costs $9.00 more a month, or $108 over 12 months if you pay monthly (our math). For that you get no ad breaks in movies and series, downloads (only Premium plans can download: Disney+ Premium, the Disney+, Hulu Bundle Premium, the Premium ESPN bundle and the Legacy bundle), and Dolby Atmos instead of 5.1 audio. Both plans list video up to 4K UHD & HDR.

The 6-month price lock on the Disney+, Hulu Bundle

Disney+ now offers the Disney+, Hulu Bundle on a 6-month term as well as monthly: "Reg. monthly or 6-month price lock plans avail." The terms on disneyplus.com: "Cost for 6 months: $71.94, then renews to monthly plan at $12.99/month." The Premium version costs $119.94 for 6 months and then renews at $21.99 a month. Compared with six monthly payments ($77.94 and $131.94), that saves $6.00 on the ad-supported bundle and $12.00 on Premium over the six months (our math). The page showed no end date for this offer.

The catch is cancelling: "If cancelled during the initial 6 month term, effective at the end of your 6 month term." In other words, you pay for the full six months. And changing plans gives up the lock; the help center says: "By changing your plan, you will lose the benefit of any current promotion or price hold."

Is the Disney+ and Hulu bundle worth it?

Hulu on its own costs the same as Disney+ ($12.49 with ads, $21.49 without), so buying both separately costs $24.98 or $42.98 a month. The bundle is $12.99 or $21.99, which saves $11.99 or $20.99 a month, roughly half (our math). Put another way, the bundle costs 50 cents a month ($6 a year) more than Disney+ alone and adds Hulu.

The one case where Disney+ alone can still win on price is yearly Premium: $214.99 a year, against $263.88 for 12 months of the Disney+, Hulu Bundle Premium (our math), because the bundle has no yearly option. For the HBO Max bundle, Disney's own FAQ claims "savings of more than $13 per month" with ads and "savings of $26 per month" without, compared with buying the three services separately; Disney adds that "there is no annual subscription for the Disney+, Hulu, HBO Max Bundle."

Disney+ student discount

College and graduate students in the US can get the ad-supported Disney+, Hulu Bundle for $6.49 a month instead of $12.99, verified through SheerID. Disney's terms: "$6.49/month so long as student enrollment status remains verified, then $12.99/month or then-current, regular monthly price." That's $6.50 a month, or $78 a year, off (our math). It applies to the ad-supported Disney+, Hulu Bundle only, and "ESPN subscribers and HBO Max-billed bundle subscribers are not eligible to redeem offer."

Disney+ Extra Member: what password sharing costs

Disney+ "cannot be shared outside of your Household," which it defines as "a collection of devices associated with your primary personal residence that are used by the individuals who reside there." To give someone who lives elsewhere access, the account holder can buy the Extra Member add-on. Prices from the help center on November 21, 2026:

The Extra Member prices for the standalone plans are the same as when paid sharing launched in the US in September 2024: Disney announced "an additional $6.99 per month for Disney+ Basic subscriptions and $9.99 per month for Disney+ Premium subscriptions." Since then the Premium plan itself has gone from $13.99 to $21.49. On Premium, adding an Extra Member ($31.48 in total) costs $11.50 a month less than two separate Premium subscriptions ($42.98), and $9.99 is less than the $12.49 the other person would pay for their own Disney+ With Ads (our math).

The rules, from Disney's help center: "A Disney+ or bundle subscription can have one Extra Member." "The Extra Member must be at least 18 years old, live in the same country/region as the account holder, and cannot have an active Disney+, Hulu, or ESPN subscription." The Extra Member gets one profile and can stream and download "on one device at a time," with "the same video" and audio quality as the account holder. "Extra Member is billed monthly to the account holder," who adds one by going to Account and selecting Invite an Extra Member in the Your Plans and Billing section. If you switch to a bundle that doesn't support it, "the Extra Member Add-on will be removed."

Away from home yourself? You can still watch on your own devices; if a TV asks, choose I'M AWAY FROM HOME, or UPDATE HOUSEHOLD if you've moved. Disney notes "there may be a limit to the number of times you can update your Household."

Changing plans, pausing and cancelling

  • Upgrading (for example With Ads to Premium): "If you're changing to a higher-tiered plan, the change takes effect immediately. You will be charged a prorated amount for the first month or year of your new plan."
  • Downgrading: "If you're changing to a lower-tiered plan, the change takes effect on your next billing date." Switching between monthly and annual billing also takes effect on your next billing date.
  • Promotions: changing plans ends any promotion or price hold, including the 6-month price lock.
  • Pausing: monthly subscriptions billed by Disney+ can be paused from Account > your subscription > Pause Subscription ("Certain plans may not be eligible for a subscription pause"). "The pause will take effect on the first day of your next billing period," you aren't charged during it, and pausing a bundle pauses the other services and any Extra Member too.
  • Cancelling: Account > your subscription > Cancel Subscription. You keep access "until the end of your current subscription term," and cancelling doesn't delete your Disney+ or MyDisney account. If you pay through Apple, Google, Amazon or another partner, cancel or change your plan with them.

That is six increases and about +207% in total, and the ad-free price has never gone down.

In the latest change, on September 23, 2026, ad-free Disney+ Premium went from $18.99 to $21.49 and Disney+ with ads from $11.99 to $12.49 for new subscribers. Disney's help center told existing subscribers "you will see a price change in your billing cycle on or after October 21, 2026." Bundles went up at the same time: the Disney+, Hulu Bundle Premium from $19.99 to $21.99 (the ad-supported bundle stayed at $12.99), the Disney+, Hulu, ESPN Select bundles from $19.99 to $21.99 and from $29.99 to $32.99, and the Disney+, Hulu, HBO Max bundles from $19.99 to $21.99 and from $32.99 to $34.99, according to CBS News and MacRumors. The ESPN Unlimited bundles stayed at $35.99 and $44.99.

How often does Disney+ raise prices?

There has been at least one increase every year since 2022. The ad-free price went up in 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026; since 2022 the gap between increases has been roughly 10 to 12 months. The ad plan held at $7.99 for almost two years after launch, then went up in 2024, 2025 and 2026. These dates describe the past and don't predict the next change.

What is the Disney+ price change history?

Ad-free Disney+ (now Premium) launched at $6.99 a month on November 12, 2019, then went to $7.99 on March 26, 2021, $10.99 on December 8, 2022, $13.99 on October 12, 2023, $15.99 on October 17, 2024, $18.99 on October 21, 2025 and $21.49 on September 23, 2026. Disney+ with ads launched at $7.99 on December 8, 2022, then went to $9.99 on October 17, 2024, $11.99 on October 21, 2025 and $12.49 on September 23, 2026. Dates are when new subscribers started paying the new price.

What you can do

  1. Check what you pay. Open Account on disneyplus.com and compare your plan and price with the tables above. If you pay through a bundle, an app store or another company, your price can differ.
  2. If you also pay for Hulu, bundle them. The Disney+, Hulu Bundle costs 50 cents a month more than Disney+ alone; if you keep it at least six months, the 6-month price lock saves another $6 (with ads) or $12 (Premium).
  3. Consider yearly Premium if you only want Disney+ without ads: $214.99 a year costs $42.89 less than 12 monthly payments.
  4. Sharing with someone outside your home? An Extra Member ($6.99 to $14.99 a month) is usually cheaper than a second subscription, but it isn't available on ESPN Unlimited, HBO Max or NFL+ Premium bundles.
  5. Students: verify through SheerID to get the Disney+, Hulu Bundle for $6.49 a month.
  6. Compare with other services. Our streaming price history table lists launch price, today's price and the last increase for each service, and our Disney+ price history page has the same history with a chart.
