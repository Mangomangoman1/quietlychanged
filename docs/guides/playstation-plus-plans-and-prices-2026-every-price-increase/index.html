PlayStation Plus price (November 2026): Essential, Extra and Premium, 1-, 3- and 12-month plans and every price increase

Short answer: when we checked PlayStation's US PS Plus page on November 16, 2026, PlayStation Plus Essential was $10.99 a month, $27.99 for 3 months or $79.99 for 12 months; Extra was $16.99, $43.99 or $134.99; and Premium was $19.99, $54.99 or $159.99. The 1- and 3-month prices went up on May 20, 2026; the 12-month prices last went up on September 6, 2023.

Published November 16, 2026

How much is PlayStation Plus in 2026?

These are the regular US prices on PlayStation's PS Plus page when we checked on November 16, 2026, before tax:

Plan1 month3 months12 months
Essential$10.99$27.99$79.99
Extra$16.99$43.99$134.99

They match our sourced PlayStation Plus price history: nothing has changed in the US since May 2026. The 1- and 3-month prices took effect for new subscribers on May 20, 2026. Sony's announcement on X said: "Starting May 20, PlayStation Plus prices for new customers will increase in select regions. Due to ongoing market conditions, prices will start at $10.99 USD / €9.99 EUR / £7.99 GBP for 1-month subscriptions and $27.99 USD / €27.99 EUR / £21.99 GBP for 3-month subscriptions" (quoted by Engadget). Only Essential was named, but Polygon reported that "On Wednesday, prices across all tiers of PlayStation Plus went up."

Existing subscribers kept their old price. Polygon: "Those currently subscribed will only see their price affected if “the existing subscription changes or lapses.”" So upgrading, downgrading or letting a membership run out can move you to the new price.

Black Friday 2026: 12-month plans at 20% to 33% off until November 30

On November 16 the same page also showed a Black Friday offer on 12-month plans, marked "Offer ends 11/30/2026": Essential $63.99 ("Save 20%"), Extra $101.24 ("Save 25%") and Premium $107.19 ("Save 33%"). The fine print says "Qualifying users only" and the page asks you to "Sign in to see offer", so not every account gets it.

After the discounted year it renews at the full price: the page says "20% off 12 months. Then $79.99 every 12 months." (and $134.99 for Extra, $159.99 for Premium). Our math: at $107.19, a year of Premium costs $27.80 less than a year of Extra at its regular $134.99. If you already have a membership, PlayStation's page says you can buy a new one that "will start when your existing plan ends", so you can stack a discounted year on top of the time you have left.

What Essential, Extra and Premium include

From PlayStation's plan comparison on the US PS Plus page:

  • Essential: monthly games, online multiplayer, exclusive discounts, exclusive content, cloud storage ("Back up your game saves and download them to other consoles") and Share Play.
  • Extra: everything in Essential plus the Game Catalog ("hundreds of PS4 and PS5 titles") and Ubisoft+ Classics, which the page says is "Worth $7.99 per month."
  • Premium: everything in Extra plus the Classics Catalog, Game Trials, cloud streaming and the Sony Pictures Catalog ("Watch up to 100 movies on demand at no extra cost").

Premium's extras in more detail:

  • Cloud streaming: "Stream select PS5 titles on-demand to your PS5 console from the PlayStation Plus Game Catalogue, as well as select games from your own personal collection bought from PlayStation™Store." Cloud streaming also covers games from the Game Catalog and "hundreds of PS3 and PS2 games from the Classics Catalog." It works on PS5 and the PlayStation Portal. The fine print: "Cloud Streaming requires minimum internet speed of 5mbps (15 mbps for 1080p)."
  • Game Trials: "Try games before you buy them." The page says "You can even try out some of the biggest recent releases with streamed PS5 Game Trials."
  • Classics Catalog: "Enjoy classic games from PlayStation history."

One change to note for Extra and Premium: the US page's footnote now says "From January 2026, PS4 games will be added only intermittently."

Online play is the main reason many people pay for Essential. PlayStation's FAQ: "For the majority of games that you have purchased on disc or downloaded from PlayStation Store, you need a PlayStation Plus membership plan to play online." It adds: "Most free to play games such as Fortnite, Apex Legends and Call of Duty Warzone do not require a membership."

1, 3 or 12 months: what a year really costs

Our math, using the regular US prices above:

Essential$131.88$111.96$79.99$51.89 (39%)
Extra$203.88$175.96$134.99$68.89 (34%)

Break-even: the 12-month Essential plan costs less than paying monthly if you would otherwise keep Essential for 8 months or more (7 months is $76.93, 8 months is $87.92). Extra also breaks even at 8 months ($135.92 vs $134.99). Premium needs 9 months: 8 months at $19.99 is $159.92, 7 cents under the yearly price.

The 3-month plan only makes sense if you know you'll quit within a few months: it saves $4.98 on Essential, $6.98 on Extra and $4.98 on Premium compared with three single months. Sony's 2023 announcement said the 12-month price "will remain at a discounted rate when compared to purchasing the 1-Month or 3-Month subscriptions over a 12-month period."

Upgrading, downgrading and the price you keep

From PlayStation's US PS Plus page:

  • Upgrading costs the difference for the time you have left: "To upgrade your membership plan to PlayStation Plus Extra or Premium, you just need to purchase a membership at that level. You will pay a pro-rated fee to bring your payments to the level of your new plan for the remainder of the paid membership period."
  • Downgrading waits until your paid time runs out: "When you downgrade your membership level, the downgrade will take place at the end of your existing pre-paid (stacked) subscription period, and you will lose access to the benefits exclusive to your previous subscription level at that time."

Our rough example (Sony doesn't publish its formula): with 6 months left on a 12-month Essential plan, the gap to 12-month Premium is $80.00 a year ($159.99 minus $79.99), so expect an upgrade charge of around half that, about $40. The PlayStation Store shows the exact amount before you pay. Whether you are charged the gap to the old or new price depends on what you have: in 2023 Sony said "any membership changes you make on or after September 6, such as upgrades, downgrades or buying additional time, will update your plan reflecting the new prices."

That is the catch for anyone on a pre-2026 (or pre-2023) price: changing tiers counts as a change, and Sony said existing subscribers keep their price unless "the existing subscription changes or lapses."

What happens to your games if you cancel or lapse

Monthly games aren't yours to keep. PlayStation's FAQ answers "Are PlayStation Plus games mine to keep?" with: "Yes, for as long as you remain an active member." Its cancellation help page is plainer:

Once your PlayStation Plus subscription ends, content you previously downloaded at no cost as part of the subscription (such as monthly games) will no longer be available. However, redeemed PlayStation Plus packs and avatars, and PlayStation Plus discounted purchases are yours to keep.

— PlayStation Support, How to cancel PlayStation Plus

Add-ons you bought for a catalog game are also stuck. The PS Plus Usage Terms: "If your subscription lapses (or a game is removed from the service), you won't be able to use any add-ons, virtual currency, or other similar consumables you've separately purchased for a PlayStation Plus game, unless and until you renew your PlayStation Plus subscription or purchase the same game from the PlayStation Store." Catalog games can also leave; PlayStation's FAQ says: "Some games in the Game Catalogue and Classics Catalogue will have end dates when they leave the service, which will be publicised ahead of time."

PlayStation Plus price history: every US change since 2022

US list prices for new subscribers, from Sony's announcements and the coverage listed below:

  • June 13, 2022: the new three-tier PlayStation Plus launches in the Americas, merging PlayStation Now into Premium. Sony's press release: Essential "$9.99 monthly / $24.99 quarterly / $59.99 yearly", Extra "$14.99 monthly / $39.99 quarterly / $99.99 yearly" and Premium "$17.99 monthly / $49.99 quarterly / $119.99 yearly." Essential kept the old PlayStation Plus price.
  • September 6, 2023: 12-month plans go up "globally across all benefit plans" (PlayStation Blog): Essential $59.99 to $79.99, Extra $99.99 to $134.99, Premium $119.99 to $159.99. Existing 12-month members paid the new price from "your next renewal date that occurs on or after November 6."
  • May 20, 2026: 1- and 3-month plans go up for new subscribers: Essential $9.99 to $10.99 ($24.99 to $27.99 for 3 months), Extra $14.99 to $16.99 ($39.99 to $43.99), Premium $17.99 to $19.99 ($49.99 to $54.99). 12-month plans stay the same (Polygon, Engadget).

Our math: since 2022 a year of Essential is up 33% ($59.99 to $79.99), Extra 35% ($99.99 to $134.99) and Premium 33% ($119.99 to $159.99). Month to month, Essential is up 10%, Extra 13% and Premium 11%. Our subscription price history has the dated table and the same sources.

Outside the US: in April 2025 Sony raised PS Plus prices in 16 Latin American countries from April 16, by between "8.34% to 21.43%" depending on tier (Game Rant), along with Southeast Asia and Australia. Canada and Türkiye followed: VGChartz reported that in Canada "it is only the price of the one-year plan that is seeing an increase" (Essential CAD $94.99 to $109.99, Extra $154.99 to $189.99, Premium $189.99 to $224.99), the same 12-month prices Canada's page shows today. In May 2026 India's prices rose too: Hindustan Times reported Essential going from ₹499 to ₹649 a month, with existing subscribers paying the new price on "the next payment date on or after 19 July." Engadget noted the May 2026 increase applied to new subscribers only "except in Turkey and India."

How often does PlayStation Plus raise prices?

Twice in the US since the 2022 relaunch, 2 years and 8 months apart, and each time Sony raised different plan lengths: 12-month plans in September 2023, 1- and 3-month plans in May 2026. The 12-month prices have been unchanged since September 2023, so they are the likelier target next time, but Sony hasn't announced anything. Sony has never announced a US increase far ahead: the 2023 change came with a week's notice in a monthly-games blog post, and the 2026 change was announced on X two days before it started. Other regions have had more frequent increases (Latin America in 2023 and 2025).

PlayStation Plus vs Xbox Game Pass

Comparing monthly prices with our Xbox Game Pass guide (Microsoft's US prices checked November 9, 2026): PS Plus Essential ($10.99) costs $1 more than Game Pass Essential ($9.99); PS Plus Extra ($16.99) costs $2 more than Game Pass Premium ($14.99); and PS Plus Premium ($19.99) costs $3 less than Game Pass Ultimate ($22.99), though Ultimate adds new games on day one. Prepaid, the cheapest tiers tie: the Xbox guide found the 12-month Game Pass Essential listing at $79.99 (October 22, 2026), the same as 12 months of PS Plus Essential. Prepaid Game Pass Ultimate cost slightly more than paying monthly, while PS Plus's 12-month plans save a third or more.

What you can do

  1. Check your plan, price and renewal date. On the web, sign in to Account Management and select Subscription; on PS5, go to Settings > Users and Accounts > Account > Payment and Subscriptions > Subscriptions (PlayStation Support).
  2. If you have an old price, think before changing tiers. Sony says existing subscribers keep their price unless "the existing subscription changes or lapses." An upgrade or a lapse can move you to the current price.
  3. Prepay if you play 8+ months a year (9+ for Premium). If you qualify for the Black Friday offer before November 30, the 12-month plans are $63.99, $101.24 and $107.19; a new membership starts when your current one ends.
  4. Turn off auto-renew if you only want one period. PlayStation Support: on the web, select Cancel Subscription next to PlayStation Plus; on PS5, Settings > Users and Accounts > Account > Payment and Subscriptions > Subscriptions > PlayStation Plus > Cancel Subscription; on PS4, PlayStation Plus > Manage Membership > Subscription. You keep the benefits "until the end of any previously purchased subscription payment period." You can "select Reactivate Subscription before the next payment date" to undo it.
  5. Know the 14-day refund window. PlayStation's refund page says a subscription refund must be requested "within 14 days from signing up" and must be for "your first payment, not a recurring fee." If you've already played online or downloaded monthly games, "we may reduce your refund based on your use of the subscription." A free trial counts toward the 14 days.
  6. Before you lapse, finish or buy what matters. Monthly games and catalog games stop working when your membership ends; discounted purchases and redeemed packs are yours to keep.
