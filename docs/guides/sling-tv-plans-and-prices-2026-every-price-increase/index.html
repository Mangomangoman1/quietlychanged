Sling TV price (November 2026): $19.99 Essentials and Select, $45.99 Orange and Blue, local-channel fees, extras and every price increase

Short answer: when we checked sling.com on November 22, 2026, Sling Essentials (ESPN and ESPN2) was $19.99 a month, Sling Select (FOX News, FS1, NFL Network) started at $19.99, Essentials & Select at $34.99, Sling Orange $45.99, Sling Blue from $45.99 and Orange & Blue from $60.99, plus tax. "Starting at" matters: in the cities where Sling carries local ABC, FOX or NBC, Blue costs $4 or $9 more and Select $5 or $10 more. There is no free trial, the $4.99 Day Passes are gone, and Orange has gone from $20 in 2015 to $45.99 (+130%).

Published November 22, 2026

Blue, Select, Essentials & Select and Orange & Blue also say "App access to FOX One is included."

Why your Sling price may be higher: local channels

Sling only carries local broadcast stations in some cities, and you pay for them where it does. Its help center says Blue, Select, Essentials & Select and Orange & Blue "include live streaming of some local channels, but access depends on where you live." and "Local channels are not included with Sling Orange." On November 22, 2026 its FOX list had 18 markets, from Atlanta, Chicago and Dallas/Fort Worth to Los Angeles, New York, Philadelphia, San Francisco/Oakland/San Jose and Washington, DC; most of the country gets no locals from Sling at all. Antenna Land wrote in February 2026: "Only five markets get all three local stations through Sling: Chicago, Los Angeles, New York, Philadelphia, and San Francisco."

Where those numbers come from: Sling's Select announcement says "If you live in a market with one or two local channels, a $5 monthly surcharge will apply. And if you live in a market with all three local channels, a $10 monthly surcharge will apply." For Blue, Sling told Cord Cutters News in January 2026: "Sling Blue with all three of ABC/NBC/Fox: Was $5 for locals, now $9/mo." and "Sling Blue with one or two of ABC/NBC/Fox: Was $0 for locals, now $4/mo." Cord Cutters News reported Orange & Blue going to $64.99; the $69.99 all-three figure is our math ($60.99 + $9), and it matches the regular Orange & Blue price Tom's Guide saw on sling.com for a Los Angeles ZIP code. Tom's Guide: "I did try different zip codes on the Sling website, and I got different prices depending on where the zipcode was located."

Two ways to avoid the surcharge: Sling Orange never includes locals, so it is never surcharged; and Sling's help center suggests an antenna: "Using an over the air (OTA) antenna, most people can watch live local channels on their TV for free." Sling says LG and Vizio TVs made in 2020 or later can put antenna channels straight into the Sling guide.

Extras: Sports Extra, News Extra and the other add-on packs

Extras are themed channel packs added to a base plan. Sling's add-ons page: "The price and channels included with each extra vary depending on your base plan." On November 22, 2026 it listed:

  • Sports Extra: $11 a month on Essentials, Select, Orange or Blue, and $15 a month on Essentials & Select or Orange & Blue. Sling's sign-up box lists "24/7 access, $45.99 plus $11/mo" for Orange + Sports Extra ($56.99). Sports Extra is where NFL RedZone lives; Sling's Select post describes it as "Includes NFL RedZone, Big Ten Network and more."
  • News Extra, Entertainment Extra, Kids Extra, Lifestyle Extra, Heartland Extra and Hollywood Extra: $6 a month each on every base plan ("Themed channel packs start at $6/mo").
  • Unlimited DVR: $5 a month. Every plan includes cloud DVR: "Subscribers can record live TV and save it to their DVR with 50 hours of free DVR storage, and can extend to unlimited storage by adding Unlimited DVR for just $5/mo." Sling's deals page listed Unlimited DVR with a "Free first month" offer.
  • Total TV deal: Sling's name for a base plan plus all seven Extras and Unlimited DVR ("Kids Extra", "Entertainment Extra", "News Extra", "Lifestyle Extra", "Sports Extra", "Heartland Extra", "Hollywood Extra" and "Unlimited DVR"). Sling's deals page promotes it with a banner whose text reads "Total TV Deal, 60% off, $21 per month"; the page doesn't spell out what the $21 covers, so check the cart before you count on it.

Our math: Orange plus Sports Extra and Unlimited DVR is $61.99 a month, about the same as Orange & Blue ($60.99) without either.

Sling also sells streaming services as add-ons. Its add-ons page says you can "Watch everything in one place, no base plan needed". Prices listed on November 22, 2026:

  • Paramount+ with SHOWTIME: $10/mo, "$5 for your first month"
  • STARZ: $11.99/mo, "$5 for your first month"
  • AMC+: $10.99/mo, or $7.99/mo with ads
  • discovery+: $11.99/mo ad-free, $6.99/mo with ads
  • MGM+: $8/mo; Hallmark+: $8.99/mo; FOX Nation: $9.99/mo; Shudder: $9.99/mo
  • MLB.TV: $29.99/mo ("Blackouts and other restrictions apply.")

Sling's 3-month prepay

Some plan pages also offer a prepaid bundle to new customers. Sling Blue showed "3 month prepay, then starting at $45.99/mo" for "Starting at $114.99", which is $22.98 less than three months at $45.99 (our math). Orange & Blue was $159.99 (saves $22.98), Select $49.99 (saves $9.98), Essentials & Select $94.99 (saves $9.98) and Essentials $54.99 (saves $4.98); we did not see a prepay option on the Orange page. The terms: "Available to new customers upon account activation. One per customer." After the prepaid period you pay the everyday price, and prepaid months are not refunded if you cancel (see below). One oddity: the prepay terms box on the Blue page is headed "Five Month Prepay Offer Details" while the button says 3 months, so confirm the length at checkout.

How many people can watch at once?

It depends on the plan, and add-ons don't add streams. From Sling's offer details (updated April 13, 2026):

  • "If you subscribe to our Sling Orange service, you can enjoy one stream at a time." Essentials is also one stream.
  • "If you subscribe to our Sling Blue service, you can enjoy up to three streams of these channels at the same time." Select is also three.
  • "If you subscribe to both services in Sling Orange + Sling Blue, you can enjoy up to four streams at the same time." Essentials & Select works the same way: one stream for the single-stream half plus three for the other.
  • "Any extras you add to your Sling Orange service will be included in your single stream."

So Orange or Essentials is a one-TV plan. If two people in the house want to watch ESPN at the same time, Orange won't do it; neither will Essentials.

Free trial, Day Passes and Freestream

Sling has no free trial: "Although there’s no free trial for Sling, anyone can watch limited channels on Freestream". Freestream is Sling's free, ad-supported tier: "Get 600+ free live TV channels plus free DVR." No paid plan is needed.

The Day Pass is gone. Sling launched a $4.99 Day Pass, $9.99 Weekend Pass and $14.99 Week Pass in August 2025 (Cord Cutters News), switched in December 2025 to 1, 3 and 7 Day Passes ($4.99, $9.99 and $14.99), and offered a $1 Day Pass from November 19 to 30, 2025. Its pages now carry this notice: "Though Sling Passes are no longer available, we look forward to continuing to innovate and showing our customers new ways to experience the content they love." Cord Cutters News reported the end on October 5, 2026; Disney and Warner Bros. had both sued over the passes, and Sling gave no official reason. The old sling.com/service/day-pass page now redirects to the plans page. If you only want one game, the cheapest Sling route with ESPN is now one month of Essentials.

How to cancel Sling, and what you get back

Sling is month to month with no contract. Its FAQ: "Monthly subscribers can cancel anytime by visiting their account". "You’ll continue to have access to Sling until the period you’ve paid for ends and won’t be charged again until you resubscribe." But no money comes back: "Programming fees are charged monthly in advance and no credits or refunds will be issued for partial or prepaid months after cancellation." Cancel a day or two before your billing date, and remember a prepay is gone once paid. New-customer offers say that after the offer you're "billed for Sling monthly at the everyday price unless you go online to cancel."

Sling TV vs YouTube TV on price

Sling's biggest plan is still far cheaper than YouTube TV's main plan ($82.99 a month in November 2026; see our YouTube TV price guide): Orange & Blue is $60.99, or $69.99 with all three locals. The trade-off is local channels. Every YouTube TV plan, even its Sports Plan, comes with your local channels; Sling has locals only in a limited number of big markets and never on Orange. Sling's lighter plans also limit streams: one on Orange and Essentials versus three on YouTube TV. Our math for a sports fan: Orange plus Sports Extra is $56.99, against $64.99 for YouTube TV's Sports Plan.

Checklist: picking (or trimming) a Sling plan

  1. Enter your ZIP on sling.com first. The "from" prices only apply where Sling has no locals; in the bigger markets Blue is $49.99 or $54.99.
  2. Only want ESPN? Essentials ($19.99) has ESPN and ESPN2 for less than half of Orange. Orange adds TNT, TBS, CNN, AMC and more for $26 a month more.
  3. Only want FOX News, FS1 or NFL Network? Select (from $19.99) instead of Blue.
  4. Count your screens. Orange and Essentials are one stream; Blue and Select three; the bundles four.
  5. Price the Extras. Sports Extra is $11 or $15; most other packs $6; Unlimited DVR $5.
  6. New customer? Compare the 3-month prepay against monthly, but remember it's not refundable.
  7. Leaving? Cancel online before your billing date; there are no partial-month refunds. Freestream stays free.
