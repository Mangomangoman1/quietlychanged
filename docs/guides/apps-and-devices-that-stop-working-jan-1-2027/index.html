Apps and devices that stop working Jan 1, 2027

HunterSMART's Google Home and Alexa link, KYMCO's Noodoe dash, the ZWILLING Fresh & Save app and Nedap's MyPowerRouter portal all lose cloud features at the turn of the year. This table also includes the December shutdowns already on our site: remove.bg on December 1 and IKEA's gift registry after December 27. Each row has a calendar file.

Published November 14, 2026

What this list covers

These are cloud and app cut-offs with a hard date, not products being taken off sale. In each case the company has said, in its own words, that an app, a portal or a cloud link ends on a set day. Some hardware still works without that link; some features do not. The table is ordered by the date you have to act.

Every row links to our sourced write-up and to a calendar file for that deadline. Things can still change: check the official page before you act.

remove.bg — December 1, 2026

remove.bg's standalone site, accounts and self-service API close on December 1, 2026 at 9:00 a.m. CET. Unused credits expire that day and are non-refundable; nothing transfers to Canva or Leonardo.Ai. Export invoices and usage reports from your account first. Our full write-up is remove.bg: Dec 1 shutdown.

IKEA Gift Registry — December 27, 2026

After December 27, 2026, IKEA's U.S. gift registry is no longer available. "Items saved in your registry will be fully deleted. Items will not be automatically transferred or saved elsewhere." Move items to a Favorites list, re-share the link, and take a screenshot. Our full write-up is IKEA Gift Registry shuts down.

ZWILLING Fresh & Save app — December 31, 2026

ZWILLING is discontinuing its app by December 31, 2026. "From January 2027, you will no longer be able to log in or use the app," even if it is still installed. Fresh & Save products continue to work; storage tracking and expiry notifications do not. Save box contents and lists first. Our full write-up is ZWILLING Fresh & Save app shuts Dec 31.

Nedap MyPowerRouter.com — December 31, 2026

Nedap, which stopped selling the PowerRouter in 2016, will "terminate the MyPowerRouter.com environment as of 31 December 2026." Online logins, energy data, remote updates and remaining support end that day. The integrated screen still works. Save your data, and file any warranty claim in writing before the deadline. Our full write-up is MyPowerRouter.com and support end Dec 31.

HunterSMART Google Home and Alexa — January 1, 2027

Hunter's November 5, 2026 announcement says that beginning January 1, 2027 it will "discontinue the legacy cloud-based connections" between HunterSMART app accounts and Google Home and Amazon Alexa, including "voice commands, scenes, and automations." The remote and the HunterSMART app still work. Matter is only for eligible models, and you have to set the fan up again in Google Home or Alexa. Check the Matter compatibility page. Our full write-up is HunterSMART cloud link ends Jan 1.

KYMCO Noodoe — January 1, 2027

KYMCO's official notice says that effective January 1, 2027, "all services, functions, and technical support will be terminated worldwide." The Noodoe app "will no longer launch," and the cloud is shut down. Navigation, messages, weather and the gallery stop. "Speed Display: This function will continue to operate normally." Our full write-up is KYMCO Noodoe shuts down Jan 1.

A note on prices

This page is only for apps and devices that lose features. A related deadline in the same window, but not a shutdown, is Runna's UK price rise, which applies from the first renewal on or after December 10, 2026.
