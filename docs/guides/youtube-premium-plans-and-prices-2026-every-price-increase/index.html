YouTube Premium price (November 2026): Individual, Family, Student, Lite, the annual plan and every price increase

Short answer: when we checked youtube.com/premium on November 14, 2026, YouTube Premium was $15.99 a month for one person, $159.99 for a prepaid 12-month plan, $26.99 a month for Family (you plus up to 5 people in your household) and $8.99 a month for students. Premium Lite, which keeps ads on music and Shorts, was also $8.99. Those prices started on April 10, 2026.

Published November 14, 2026

How much is YouTube Premium in 2026?

These are the US prices on YouTube's own Premium, Premium Lite and Music Premium pages when we checked on November 14, 2026, for paying on the web, before tax:

  • YouTube Premium Individual: $15.99 a month, with a 1-month free trial for eligible new members.
  • YouTube Premium annual plan (Individual only): "12 months for $159.99". It "Doesn't automatically renew."
  • YouTube Premium Family: $26.99 a month. "Add up to 5 family members (ages 13+) in your household."
  • YouTube Premium Student: $8.99 a month. "Eligible students only. Annual verification required."
  • Premium Lite: $8.99 a month, "ad-free video, except music and Shorts".
  • YouTube Music Premium (music app only, no ad-free videos): $11.99 a month, $119.99 for a prepaid year, $18.99 Family, $5.99 Student.

All of these took effect for new sign-ups on April 10, 2026, when The Verge reported that "Premium’s Individual plan now costs $15.99 per month, up from $13.99, while the five-person Family plan is $26.99, previously $22.99." Existing subscribers were moved later. The email YouTube sent them, quoted by The Verge and Ars Technica, said: "You will see the change reflected on your June 7, 2026 billing date."

What YouTube Premium includes

YouTube's Premium page sums it up as "YouTube and YouTube Music ad-free, offline, and in the background." Every Premium plan (Individual, annual, Family, Student) gets the same features:

  • Ad-free videos on YouTube, including music videos and Shorts.
  • Downloads. YouTube's FAQ says "You can watch or listen to content offline for up to 30 days without being connected to the internet."
  • Background play, so audio keeps going with your screen off.
  • YouTube Music Premium: "YouTube Music Premium is included in your YouTube Premium membership."
  • Extras the Premium page lists: picture-in-picture, Jump ahead, continue watching across devices, "enhanced bitrate 1080p", 256kbps audio on eligible music videos, smart downloads and queuing.

Premium vs Premium Lite: $7 a month for music and Shorts

Premium Lite costs $8.99, $7 less than Premium. Since February 24, 2026 it includes downloads and background play for most videos (YouTube's blog: "subscribers will be able to watch most videos ad-free, offline and in the background"). What it leaves out, according to YouTube's Premium Lite help page:

  • Ads on music, Shorts and search: "Ads, however, may still appear on music content, Shorts, and when you search or browse."
  • No background play or downloads for music. Those features "are not available on Shorts or on music content, such as official/premium music videos, Art Tracks, children’s songs, as well as user generated content that includes content from our music partners (for example: covers, dance videos, or a vlog with a popular song in the background)."
  • No YouTube Music Premium and fewer extras: "Premium Lite does not include YouTube Music Premium, or additional features such as continue watching, Jump ahead, queuing, or high quality audio and video, and more."

Our math: if you would otherwise pay for YouTube Music Premium ($11.99) on top of Lite ($8.99), that is $20.98 a month, $4.99 more than full Premium at $15.99, which already includes YouTube Music. Lite only makes sense if you don't use YouTube for music.

Students: get full Premium, not Lite

YouTube Premium Student and Premium Lite cost exactly the same, $8.99 a month. Student is the full Premium plan (ad-free music, Shorts and YouTube Music included), so any eligible student paying for Lite is getting less for the same money. The catch: "Eligible students only. Annual verification required." The Student plan was $6.99 a month until July 2023 (Android Authority), then $7.99, and went to $8.99 in April 2026.

Family plan: cheaper from the second person, but same household only

Family costs $26.99 a month for the family manager plus up to 5 others, so six accounts in total. Our math: two Individual plans would cost $31.98, so Family is cheaper as soon as two people in the house want Premium; with all six seats used it works out to $4.50 per person.

The rules on YouTube's family plan help page: family members must "Live within the same residential address as the family manager", must not be part of another family group, and can't have "switched family groups in the last 12 months." The manager must be 18 or older. Each person keeps their own library, subscriptions and recommendations. YouTube also says that if the app warns your Premium access will be paused, "it is because we cannot confirm that you are in the same household as your family manager." There is no annual Family plan: "Annual plans are only available for individual users."

The annual plan: 12 months for $159.99, and it doesn't renew

Our math: 12 months at $15.99 is $191.88, so the $159.99 annual plan saves $31.89 a year (YouTube's page says "Save 15% vs. monthly plan"; we get 16.6%). That is about $13.33 a month. Ten monthly payments come to $159.90, nine cents under the yearly price, so prepaying only saves money if you would keep Premium for 11 months or more.

How it works, from YouTube's annual-plan help page:

  • It is prepaid and "non-recurring": "your paid membership benefits will end after the 12-month period you paid for." Nothing renews automatically, so there is no surprise renewal charge, but Premium simply stops at the end unless you buy again.
  • "You can purchase up to 24 months of pre-paid access to YouTube Music Premium or YouTube Premium at a given time." Prepaying locks in today's price for that time.
  • "Annual plans are not refundable, cannot be paused, and are subject to our travel policy."
  • To switch from monthly you have to cancel your existing membership first, then sign up at youtube.com/premium/annual/inapp.
  • If you're on an annual plan and want Family, "you'll have to wait until your plan expires before upgrading to a multi-person plan."

The annual plan has gone up with the monthly price each time: $119.99 before July 2023 (YouTube introduced it in January 2022, per 9to5Google), $139.99 from July 2023 and $159.99 from April 2026.

Don't subscribe through the iPhone app

YouTube charges more if you start Premium inside the YouTube app on an iPhone or iPad, where Apple bills you. Android Authority reported in April 2026 that "If you subscribe on Apple devices, prices go up further, with the YouTube Premium Individual plan jumping to $20.99 monthly." That is $5 a month, or $60 a year, more than the $15.99 web price for the same plan. (In 2023 it was $18.99 on iOS vs $13.99 on the web, per 9to5Google.) Apple-billed memberships also can't be paused: YouTube's help page says "Pausing and resuming your paid membership is not available to users who are billed through Apple."

If Apple bills you now, cancel in your Apple subscriptions, let the paid period run out, then sign up again at youtube.com/premium in a browser.

YouTube Premium vs Spotify and Netflix

At our November 2026 price checks, YouTube Premium Individual ($15.99) costs $3 more than Spotify Premium Individual ($12.99) but also removes ads from all of YouTube; YouTube Music Premium alone ($11.99) is $1 less than Spotify. For families, YouTube Premium Family ($26.99) costs $5 more than Spotify Premium Family ($21.99). Netflix Standard without ads is $19.99. See our Spotify price guide, Netflix price guide and the streaming price history page. YouTube TV, the live TV service, is a separate subscription ($82.99 a month for the main plan); see our YouTube TV price guide.

YouTube Premium price history: every US change since 2015

US list prices for new subscribers paying on the web, from the coverage linked below and our sourced price history.

Individual (monthly):

  • 2015: launches as YouTube Red at $9.99 a month (Ars Technica).
  • 2018: renamed YouTube Premium at $11.99. YouTube Red subscribers kept $9.99 until 2023.
  • July 2023: $11.99 to $13.99, the first US increase since the 2018 launch (Reuters). Annual plan $119.99 to $139.99; Student $6.99 to $7.99.
  • April 10, 2026: $13.99 to $15.99. Annual plan $139.99 to $159.99; Student $7.99 to $8.99.

Family:

  • 2018 to October 2022: $17.99 a month.
  • October 20, 2022: $17.99 to $22.99 for new subscribers; existing members on their "next billing cycle starting on or after November 21, 2022" (The Verge).
  • July 2023: unchanged at $22.99.
  • April 10, 2026: $22.99 to $26.99.

Premium Lite:

  • March 5, 2025: launches in the US at $7.99 a month (YouTube blog), ad-free on most videos but without downloads or background play.
  • February 24, 2026: adds downloads and background play for most non-music videos.
  • April 10, 2026: $7.99 to $8.99.

YouTube Music Premium: $9.99 to $10.99 in July 2023, then $11.99 in April 2026 (Family $16.99 to $18.99, per MakeUseOf).

Our math: the individual plan is up 60% since YouTube Red ($9.99 to $15.99) and 33% since the 2018 rename. Family is up 50% since 2022 ($17.99 to $26.99).

How often does YouTube Premium raise prices?

Less often than most streaming services, but in bigger steps. In the US: Family in October 2022, Individual in July 2023, then everything in April 2026. The Verge noted in 2026 that "YouTube Premium last raised its US prices in 2023." YouTube says it will "notify you via email at least 30 days ahead of a price increase in your country/territory." YouTube hasn't announced another US increase. A prepaid annual plan (up to 24 months) is the only way to lock in the current price.

What you can do

  1. Check who bills you. Go to youtube.com/paid_memberships. If Apple bills you, you are probably paying $20.99 instead of $15.99; switch to web billing when your paid period ends.
  2. Students: switch Lite to Student. Same $8.99, full Premium.
  3. Two or more Premium users at one address: use Family at $26.99 instead of two $15.99 plans. Everyone must live at the family manager's address.
  4. Prepay only if you'll keep it 11+ months. The $159.99 annual plan saves $31.89 a year and doesn't auto-renew, but there are no refunds and no pausing.
  5. Don't stack Lite and Music Premium. $20.98 together vs $15.99 for Premium, which includes both.
  6. Pause instead of cancelling: monthly members billed by YouTube can pause for 1 to 6 months at youtube.com/paid_memberships > Manage membership > Deactivate > Pause instead. You aren't charged while paused, but "You’ll be billed at the new price when you resume your membership."
  7. To cancel: youtube.com/paid_memberships > Manage membership > Deactivate > Continue to cancel.
