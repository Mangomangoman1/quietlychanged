Hulu plans and prices (November 2026): With Ads vs Premium, the annual plan, bundles and every price increase

Short answer: when we checked Hulu's plans-and-prices help page and hulu.com on November 13, 2026, Hulu (With Ads) was $12.49 a month or $124.99 a year, and Hulu Premium (No Ads) was $21.49 a month, with no yearly option. Those prices started on September 23, 2026. The Disney+ and Hulu bundle with ads costs $12.99, only 50 cents more than Hulu on its own.

Published November 13, 2026

How much does Hulu cost in 2026?

Hulu sells two standalone plans in the US. These are the prices on Hulu's "Hulu plans and prices" help page (updated September 30, 2026) and hulu.com when we checked on November 13, 2026, before tax:

  • Hulu (With Ads): $12.49 a month, or $124.99 a year with annual billing.
  • Hulu Premium (No Ads): $21.49 a month. Hulu lists no yearly price for it.
  • Disney+, Hulu Bundle (both with ads): $12.99 a month.
  • Disney+, Hulu Bundle Premium (both without ads): $21.99 a month.
  • Hulu + Live TV (with Disney+ and ESPN Select): $99.99 a month, or $109.99 with Hulu Premium and Disney+ Premium.
  • Students: the Disney+, Hulu Bundle with ads for $6.49 a month.

Hulu's help page says: "The following prices are for new subscriptions as of September 23, 2026. If you signed up for a Hulu subscription prior to this date, you will see a price change in your billing cycle on or after October 21, 2026."

What each Hulu plan includes

Both plans get the full Hulu streaming library: "full seasons of exclusively streaming series, classic favorites, Hulu Originals, hit movies, current episodes, kids shows, and tons more." Hulu's plan table on hulu.com lists the same basics for both: most new episodes the day after they air (for current-season shows), up to 6 profiles, and 2 screens streaming at the same time. The help page lists video "in up to 4K UHD" and "up to 5.1 audio" for both.

Hulu (With Ads), $12.49

  • The whole streaming library, with ad breaks.
  • 2 screens at once. Hulu's help page: "You can download the Hulu app and activate your account on as many supported devices as you’d like, but only two of them can be streaming simultaneously."
  • No downloads. Hulu's plan table marks downloads as Premium only.

Hulu Premium (No Ads), $21.49

  • The streaming library without ad breaks, plus downloads of select shows and movies and Hulu Watch Party.
  • Not 100% ad-free. Hulu says: "Due to streaming rights, a few shows are not included in the Hulu Premium plan and will instead play interruption-free with a short ad break before and after each episode."
  • Live or linear channels still carry ads. Hulu's help page says ads "may be served to Hulu Premium subscribers in these channels and content, as they are not part of the Hulu on-demand streaming library."

Is Hulu Premium worth $9 more?

Hulu Premium costs $9 more a month than Hulu with ads, or $108 more over a year. For that you get no ad breaks in the on-demand library (except the few excluded shows) and downloads. The screens, profiles, library, 4K and 5.1 sound are the same on both. That is a 72% markup for removing ads, the same as Disney+. Among the services we track, only Netflix charges a bigger one (Standard at $19.99 vs Standard with ads at $8.99).

The Disney+ bundle costs 50 cents more than Hulu alone

This is the most useful thing to know about Hulu pricing in 2026. Disney+ on its own is also $12.49 with ads and $21.49 without, so at current prices:

  • With ads: Hulu alone is $12.49. The Disney+, Hulu Bundle is $12.99. For 50 cents more a month you also get Disney+ (with ads), which would be another $12.49 on its own. Bought separately the two would cost $24.98, so the bundle saves $11.99 a month.
  • Without ads: Hulu Premium alone is $21.49. The Disney+, Hulu Bundle Premium is $21.99. Again 50 cents more for ad-free Disney+ too. Separately the two would cost $42.98, so the bundle saves $20.99 a month.

Variety called this "clearly aimed at pushing consumers toward bundles" when the September 2026 prices came out. Unless you are on the annual plan below, paying for Hulu by itself rarely makes sense.

hulu.com also listed a 6-month price-lock version of the bundles on November 13, 2026, for eligible subscribers: $11.99 a month for 6 months with ads, or $19.99 a month without. Its fine print: "Cost for Disney+, Hulu Bundle for 6 months: $71.94, then renews to monthly plan at $12.99/month. Cost for Disney+, Hulu Bundle Premium for 6 months: $119.94, then renews to monthly plan at $21.99/month." Cancelling during the 6 months only takes effect at the end of the 6-month term. For those 6 months, both Disney+ and Hulu cost less than Hulu alone.

Other Hulu bundles

  • Disney+, Hulu, HBO Max Bundle: $21.99 a month with ads, or $34.99 without. hulu.com compares that with $35.97 and $61.47 for the three bought separately. See our HBO Max price guide.
  • Disney+, Hulu, ESPN Select Bundle: $21.99 a month with ads, or $32.99 with Hulu and Disney+ ad-free (ESPN Select keeps ads).
  • Disney+, Hulu, ESPN Unlimited Bundle: $35.99 a month, or $44.99 with Hulu and Disney+ ad-free.
  • Spotify Premium Student: Spotify's pricing page (our daily snapshot, November 13, 2026) says its $6.99 Student plan "currently includes access to Hulu plan on us, subject to eligibility." See our Spotify price guide.

Monthly vs yearly: the annual plan saves 16%, with a catch

Only Hulu (With Ads) has a yearly option. Hulu's annual offer page says "GET 1 YEAR OF HULU (WITH ADS) FOR $124.99" against $149.88 for 12 months at $12.49, and "Save 16%". Our math: you save $24.89 a year, which works out to about $10.42 a month.

The catch is that ten monthly payments come to $124.90, nine cents less than the yearly price. So the annual plan only saves money if you would otherwise keep Hulu for at least 11 months. And once you are on it, you are locked in. The fine print says: "Cancel anytime; switches to monthly Hulu base plans not permitted. Switches to annual billing will result in immediate payment of subscription fee and forfeiture of any free trial or promotional pricing. No refunds or credits for partial months or years."

Compared with the bundle: a year of the Disney+, Hulu Bundle on the 6-month price lock costs $71.94 plus six months at $12.99, or $149.88. That is $24.89 more than annual Hulu, but it includes Disney+ and doesn't tie you to a yearly payment.

Hulu + Live TV prices

Hulu + Live TV is a separate, much pricier product. Its plans on Hulu's help page, November 13, 2026:

  • Hulu + Live TV, Disney+, and ESPN Select: $99.99 a month.
  • Hulu Premium + Live TV, Disney+ Premium, and ESPN Select: $109.99 a month.
  • Live TV Only (no Hulu library, no Disney+): $98.99 a month, $1 less than the full package.
  • Hulu + Live TV Español, Disney+: $29.99 a month. Live TV Only Español: $28.99.

Variety reported both main Live TV plans went up $10 on September 23, 2026. Hulu's own November 2025 Live TV promo said the regular price was then "currently $89.99/month". For scale, it was $44.99 in February 2019 (CNBC), $64.99 after a December 2020 increase (Deadline), $69.99 in October 2022 (TechCrunch), $76.99 from October 12, 2023 and $82.99 from October 17, 2024 (Variety).

Hulu student discount and Extra Member

Hulu's student page offers the Disney+, Hulu Bundle with ads for $6.49 a month, half the regular $12.99, for students at accredited US colleges, verified through SheerID. It lasts "so long as student enrollment status remains verified, then $12.99/month or then-current, regular monthly price", status is re-verified every year, and there is no free trial. The page also says: "Currently, new subscribers are only able to sign up for the Disney+, Hulu Student Bundle discount."

To share an account with someone outside your household, Hulu sells an Extra Member add-on: $6.99 a month on Hulu (With Ads) and $9.99 on Hulu Premium ($7.99 and $10.99 on the Disney+, Hulu bundles). One Extra Member per account, who must be 18 or older and can stream on one device at a time. It isn't available with Hulu + Live TV.

How Hulu compares with other streaming services

At November 2026 US list prices on our service pages, Hulu with ads at $12.49 costs more than the ad-supported plans at Netflix (Standard with ads, $8.99), Paramount+ (Essential, $8.99) and HBO Max (Basic with Ads, $10.99), the same as Disney+ ($12.49) and 50 cents less than Peacock Premium ($12.99). Without ads, Hulu Premium at $21.49 matches Disney+ Premium and costs more than Netflix Standard ($19.99), Peacock Premium Plus ($19.99) and HBO Max Standard ($18.49). The streaming price history page compares them all.

Hulu price history: every change since 2010

These are US list prices for new monthly subscribers, from the coverage linked below and our sourced price history. Dates are when new subscribers started paying the new price.

Hulu (With Ads):

  • June 2010: Hulu Plus launches at $9.99 a month. Hulu's free, ad-supported site ran separately until 2016.
  • November 2010: cut to $7.99.
  • February 26, 2019: cut to $5.99 (CNBC: "Hulu is dropping the price of its most popular plan to $5.99 per month").
  • October 8, 2021: $5.99 to $6.99.
  • October 10, 2022: $6.99 to $7.99.
  • October 2023: unchanged at $7.99.
  • October 17, 2024: $7.99 to $9.99.
  • October 21, 2025: $9.99 to $11.99.
  • September 23, 2026: $11.99 to $12.49.

Hulu (No Ads), now Hulu Premium:

  • September 2015: launches at $11.99 a month.
  • October 8, 2021: $11.99 to $12.99.
  • October 10, 2022: $12.99 to $14.99.
  • October 12, 2023: $14.99 to $17.99.
  • October 17, 2024: $17.99 to $18.99.
  • October 21, 2025: unchanged at $18.99.
  • September 23, 2026: $18.99 to $21.49.

Since its 2015 launch, the ad-free plan is up 79%, from $11.99 to $21.49, after five increases. The plan with ads has more than doubled since the 2019 cut, from $5.99 to $12.49 (+109%), and now costs 25% more than Hulu Plus did at launch in 2010.

How often does Hulu raise prices?

Every year lately. At least one Hulu plan went up in October of every year from 2021 through 2025, and both went up on September 23, 2026, a month earlier than usual. Increases hit new subscribers first; existing monthly subscribers usually see them at their next bill, in 2026 "on or after October 21, 2026". Hulu hasn't announced another increase. If the pattern holds, the next would come in fall 2027.

The plan with ads has taken most of the recent increases (+$2 in 2024, +$2 in 2025, +$0.50 in 2026), while Disney left the Disney+, Hulu Bundle with ads at $12.99 in the September 2026 increase. As a result, the gap between Hulu alone and the bundle is down to 50 cents (in October 2024 the bundle with ads was $10.99 and Hulu with ads $9.99, a $1 gap).

What you can do

  1. Check which plan you have and who bills you. Hulu's help page says to go to your Account page and look at the Payment Information section. If Apple, Google, Amazon, Roku, Spotify, a phone carrier or another company bills you, you have to change the plan through that company, and "certain plan options may not be available to you."
  2. If you pay for Hulu alone, look at the bundle. For 50 cents more a month you get Disney+ too. If you are billed directly by Hulu, switch under Your Subscription, then MANAGE PLAN on your Account page.
  3. Drop to Hulu (With Ads) if ads don't bother you. It saves $9 a month, or $108 a year, over Hulu Premium.
  4. Take the $124.99 annual plan only if you'll keep Hulu 11 months or more, and only if you don't want Disney+. You can't switch back to monthly, and there are no refunds for a partial year.
  5. Students: the $6.49 bundle is the cheapest way to get Hulu, and Spotify Premium Student at $6.99 says it includes a Hulu plan.
  6. Pause instead of paying: Hulu's help page says eligible subscribers can "pause a subscription for up to 12 weeks".
  7. To cancel, Hulu's help center says to go to your Account page and select Cancel under the Your Account section. Hulu's annual-offer page says you keep access "until the end of your billing cycle" and get no refund for the rest of it.
