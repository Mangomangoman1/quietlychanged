Peacock plans and prices (November 2026): Select vs Premium vs Premium Plus, monthly vs yearly

Short answer: when we checked peacocktv.com on November 12, 2026, Peacock Select was $8.99 a month, Premium (with ads) $12.99 and Premium Plus $19.99, or $89.99, $129.99 and $199.99 a year. Those prices took effect on August 18, 2026. Peacock has raised prices every summer since 2023, and Premium now costs more than two and a half times its 2020 launch price.

Published November 12, 2026

How much does Peacock cost in 2026?

Peacock sells three plans in the US, each billed monthly or yearly. These are the prices on peacocktv.com when we checked on November 12, 2026, before tax:

  • Peacock Select: $8.99 a month or $89.99 a year.
  • Peacock Premium (with ads): $12.99 a month or $129.99 a year. Peacock labels it "MOST POPULAR".
  • Peacock Premium Plus (mostly no ads): $19.99 a month or $199.99 a year.
  • Apple TV and Peacock bundle: $17.99 a month with Premium, or $22.99 a month with Premium Plus.
  • Students: Premium for $6.99 a month for 12 months, instead of $12.99.

All six plan prices took effect on August 18, 2026 for new and returning subscribers, and they match Peacock's own price-increase help page. People who already subscribed moved to them at their next billing date on or after September 17, 2026.

What each Peacock plan includes

Here is how peacocktv.com describes each plan as of November 12, 2026.

Select ($8.99)

  • "Watch TV, with ads."
  • "TV Favorites from NBC, Bravo, & More".
  • What you don't get: the plan card says it "Excludes Sports, Movies, Live Programming & Peacock Originals". Select is current and past seasons of NBC and Bravo shows plus library titles.

Premium ($12.99)

  • "Watch TV, movies, sports & more, with ads."
  • Everything in Select, plus "LIVE Sports & Events" and "Hit Movies & Peacock Originals".
  • Peacock's FAQ adds kids' movies and shows, Spanish-language shows, news from Telemundo, daily live news and Peacock Channels.

Premium Plus ($19.99)

  • Everything in Premium, "with no ads (limited exceptions)".
  • Downloads of select shows and movies to watch offline on your phone or tablet.
  • Your local NBC station live. The FAQ says: "You'll also get access to your local NBC channel LIVE, 24/7."

Premium Plus is not completely ad-free. The plan card's footnote says: "Some features and content are sponsored; ads remain in channels, live sports/events, and a few shows and movies; not all content available for download."

Monthly vs yearly: the yearly plans save about 17%

Peacock advertises its yearly plans as "12 months for the Price of 10". Our math, using the November 2026 prices:

  • Select: $107.88 over 12 months, or $89.99 a year. You save $17.89 (about 17%), which works out to about $7.50 a month.
  • Premium: $155.88 over 12 months, or $129.99 a year. You save $25.89 (about 17%), which works out to about $10.83 a month.
  • Premium Plus: $239.88 over 12 months, or $199.99 a year. You save $39.89 (about 17%), which works out to about $16.67 a month.

The catch is in the "price of 10". Ten monthly payments come to $89.90, $129.90 and $199.90, nine cents less than each yearly price. So a yearly plan only saves money if you would otherwise keep Peacock for at least 11 months. If you only watch for a season of football or a big event, paying monthly and canceling afterwards is cheaper.

A yearly plan does lock in your price for the year. Peacock's help page says: "Current annual subscribers, or any users on active promotional offers, will continue at their current prices until those plans or offers expire. Subscriptions will then renew at the new price."

Is Peacock Premium Plus worth $7 more?

Premium Plus costs $7 more a month than Premium, or $84 more over 12 months of monthly billing. Paid yearly, the gap is $70. For that you get fewer ads on on-demand shows and movies, downloads and your live local NBC station. The live sports are the same on both, and they still carry ads on Premium Plus. If you mainly use Peacock for live sports, Premium gets you the same games for less.

Select is $4 a month cheaper than Premium but leaves out sports, movies, live programming and Peacock originals. It only makes sense if all you watch is NBC and Bravo shows.

Is the Apple TV and Peacock bundle worth it?

Peacock's home page lists two bundles, both monthly only: Apple TV with Peacock Premium for $17.99 and Apple TV with Peacock Premium Plus for $22.99. Apple TV on its own is $14.99 a month (see our Apple TV price guide), so at current prices:

  • With Premium: $14.99 + $12.99 = $27.98 separately, or $17.99 bundled. You save $9.99 a month.
  • With Premium Plus: $14.99 + $19.99 = $34.98 separately, or $22.99 bundled. You save $11.99 a month.

The bundle launched on October 20, 2025 at $14.99 and $19.99. Peacock raised both by $3 on September 1, 2026. Its help page says people who subscribed before then pay the new price "on your next billing date on or after October 1, 2026." If you already pay Peacock directly, the home page says to sign in "and upgrade to the bundle in your account."

Peacock student discount

Peacock's student page offers Premium for $6.99 a month for 12 months to eligible college and university students, verified through SheerID. That is $6 a month or $72 a year less than the regular $12.99. Read the fine print before signing up: "Annual verification required. After 12 months, you will be charged $12.99 or the then-current retail price (+tax) each month until you cancel." The same page has a separate check for people aged 18 to 24 who aren't students; look at Peacock's page for that offer's current terms.

How Peacock compares with other streaming services

At November 2026 US list prices on our service pages, Peacock Premium at $12.99 costs more than the ad-supported plans at Netflix (Standard with ads, $8.99), Paramount+ (Essential, $8.99), HBO Max (Basic with Ads, $10.99), Disney+ (Basic, $12.49) and Hulu (with ads, $12.49). Only Peacock Select, at $8.99, matches the cheapest of them, and it drops sports and movies. The streaming price history table has every service side by side.

Peacock price history: every increase since 2020

These are US list prices, from NBCUniversal's announcements and the coverage linked below. Peacock launched nationwide on July 15, 2020, with a free tier, Premium with ads at $4.99 and an ad-free version at $9.99. The free tier closed to new users in 2023.

  • July 2020: launch. Premium (with ads) $4.99 a month, ad-free $9.99 a month.
  • July 2023: first increase. Premium $4.99 to $5.99 and Premium Plus $9.99 to $11.99, right away for new customers and from August 17, 2023 for existing ones. CNET put the yearly plans at about $60 and $120 after this increase.
  • July 18, 2024: Premium $5.99 to $7.99 and Premium Plus $11.99 to $13.99. Yearly Premium went from $59.99 to $79.99 and yearly Premium Plus from $119.99 to $139.99. Existing subscribers paid more from August 17, 2024.
  • July 23, 2025: Premium $7.99 to $10.99 and Premium Plus $13.99 to $16.99, both +$3. Yearly plans went to $109.99 and $169.99 (+$30 each). Peacock started testing Select at $7.99 a month or $79.99 a year. Existing subscribers paid more on or after August 22, 2025.
  • August 18, 2026: Select $7.99 to $8.99, Premium $10.99 to $12.99 and Premium Plus $16.99 to $19.99. Yearly Select $79.99 to $89.99, Premium $109.99 to $129.99 and Premium Plus $169.99 to $199.99. Existing subscribers paid more on or after September 17, 2026.

That is four increases in four years. Premium (with ads) is up 160% since launch, from $4.99 to $12.99. Premium Plus has doubled, from $9.99 to $19.99. Yearly Premium has gone from $59.99 to $129.99 (+117%) since 2023, and the 2025 increase alone added $30 to both yearly plans.

These price changes allow Peacock to continue to create the best experience for its viewers, remain competitive in the marketplace, and deliver unique content across all genres.

— Peacock Help Center, on the August 18, 2026 increase

How often does Peacock raise prices?

Every summer since 2023: July 2023, July 2024, July 2025 and August 2026, about 12 months apart each time. Existing monthly subscribers have usually paid the new price about a month after new customers. The last increase was on August 18, 2026, less than three months before this guide was written. Peacock hasn't announced another one. If the pattern held, the next would come in summer 2027.

Peacock says existing users are told by email: "If you are an existing Peacock user, you will be notified of the price change via email to the address currently associated with your account."

What you can do

  1. Check which plan you have and when it renews in the Subscriptions tab of your Peacock account. If another company bills you for Peacock, such as Apple for the bundle, check your account with that company instead.
  2. Pick the cheapest plan that fits. If you mostly watch live sports, which carry ads on both plans, Premium is $7 a month less than Premium Plus.
  3. Switch to yearly only if you'll keep Peacock 11 months or more. Peacock's steps: in the Subscriptions tab of your Account page, choose Change Base Plan, then the annual or monthly plan you want. Peacock warns that "you will lose that promotional pricing when you switch plans" if you're on a promotion, and the change may only take effect at the end of your current billing cycle.
  4. If you also pay for Apple TV, the bundle saves $9.99 or $11.99 a month over two monthly plans.
  5. Students: the $6.99 Premium offer saves $72 over a year, but it renews at the full price after 12 months.
  6. To cancel, Peacock's help page says to visit the Subscriptions tab in your Account and select "Cancel All Subscriptions".
