Pinterest's new terms are live, and the 30-day arbitration opt-out window is open

Two real changes today. Pinterest's rewritten Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, which we covered as a preview on October 20, are now the live versions (effective November 12, 2026). Section 11 moves disputes to JAMS, sends 25 or more similar claims to "one arbitration before one arbitrator", and lets you opt out "within 30 days of first becoming subject to it" by emailing litigation@pinterest.com, which for existing users means by December 12. The live privacy policy names Pins "regardless of when they were posted" as training material for generative AI models and says automated tools may "scan and analyze your messages". Two connected-TV sentences we quoted from the preview are not in the live text we captured. JetBlue also added March 18–31, 2027 to its peak checked-bag dates, so late-March flyers pay $5 more per prepaid bag and $10 more at the airport.

Noise skipped: Spotify's audiobook catalog line changed from "over 700,000 titles" to "hundreds of thousands of titles"; Notion's refund FAQ swapped its support email for a "Message support" link (same refund windows); DoorDash dropped Amplitude from the list of vendors covered by its arbitration clause; Figma moved feature rows; Audible's plan page rotated back to "24 audiobooks" on the $229.50 plan (yesterday it showed "12 creditos/año"); Reddit added a Marathi language list; Venmo's legal nav collapsed; Mailchimp's price rendered again; Tubi's device ID rotated; Delta's tracked baggage URL still returns Delta's 404 page. Fetch failures: PayPal terms and privacy (HTTP 403), Roblox (403), Shein (bot wall), Emotiv privacy (empty page).
