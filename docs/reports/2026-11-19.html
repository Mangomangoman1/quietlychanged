Canva starts charging for Team seats the moment you invite someone

9 changes · checked November 19, 2026

What changed

  • TermsCanvaNotableNov 19

    Canva: Canva now bills Teams for a new seat the moment it's invited, even before anyone accepts

    Canva's Nov 16 terms charge Teams for an added seat right away, prorated, and count a seat as soon as an invite goes out, accepted or not.

    inserted: A seat is added when a Team Owner invites a person to your Team or adds a seat, whether or not the invitation has been accepted.

    Who it affects: Canva customers on plans with a Team (multi-seat plans), especially Team Owners and Administrators who invite people

    Read the change →Source ↗ for Canva
  • PrivacyGrammarlyNotableNov 19

    Grammarly: Grammarly's owner Superhuman adds GPTZero to its privacy policy and may license your social media profiles

    Superhuman's privacy policy now covers GPTZero and your prompts to its AI agents, and says it may license social media profile info about users and non-users from third parties.

    inserted: As described above, we may collect or license information about users and non-users. For certain products, some of this information may be visible to other users of that product.

    Who it affects: Grammarly, Superhuman Mail, Superhuman Go, Docs (Coda) and GPTZero users, and people who aren't users but whose details could be licensed from data providers

    Read the change →Source ↗ for Grammarly
  • PricingNotionNotableNov 19

    Notion: Notion's pricing FAQ drops the promise of unlimited blocks for solo Free users

    Notion's FAQ used to say a solo Free workspace gets unlimited pages and blocks. Now it says any Free workspace gets a limited number, though the plan table still says 'Limited for 2+ members'.

    If you’re using a Free Plan deleted: workspace by yourself, inserted: workspace, you’ll be able to add deleted: as many pages and blocks as you like. inserted: a limited number of blocks before you have to upgrade.

    Who it affects: People using Notion's Free plan on their own

    Read the change →Source ↗ for Notion
  • PricingShopifyNotableNov 19

    Shopify: Shopify's pricing page adds a 0.35% tax calculation fee with 'Tax managed by Shopify'

    Shopify's plan table now lists 'Tax managed by Shopify' and a tax calculation fee of 0.35% on Basic, Grow and Advanced (0.25% on Plus) after an introductory discount.

    inserted: Tax managed by Shopify

    Who it affects: Shopify merchants on Basic, Grow, Advanced and Plus who use Shopify's built-in tax calculation

    Read the change →Source ↗ for Shopify
  • TermsGoogle PlayMinorNov 19

    Google Play: Google Play's new terms are now in force: broken purchases go to the developer

    The Google Play Terms of Service previewed in October took effect Nov 17. You're now told to report defective purchases to the developer, not Google.

    and notify deleted: us or the Provider as soon as reasonably possible if you find any errors or defect.

    Who it affects: Everyone who uses the Google Play Store or an Android device with Google Play services

    Read the change →Source ↗ for Google Play
  • PricingSlackMinorNov 19

    Slack: Slack swaps 'Unlimited Slackbot' for 'no weekly message limits, subject to our usage guidelines' on Enterprise+

    Slack's Enterprise+ plan no longer advertises 'Unlimited Slackbot'. It now promises no weekly message limits, subject to usage guidelines and 'advanced feature limits'.

    Who it affects: Slack Enterprise+ customers and teams comparing Slack plans for its AI assistant

    Read the change →Source ↗ for Slack
  • PricingTodoistMinorNov 19

    Todoist: Todoist caps Ramble on the free plan at 10 sessions a month and adds Automations (beta) to paid plans

    Todoist's free Beginner plan now lists 10 Ramble voice-capture sessions a month. Pro and Business get Todoist Automations in beta; Task Assist is gone from the table.

    deleted: Ramble Limited sessions inserted: AI-powered task capture, including Ramble 10 sessions/month

    Who it affects: Todoist users, especially free Beginner users of Ramble voice task capture

    Read the change →Source ↗ for Todoist
  • PrivacySocial SecurityMinorNov 19

    Social Security: Social Security will get federal workers' comp payment data from Labor every 28 days to offset disability benefits

    A new SSA–Labor matching program sends FECA workers' comp payment data to SSA every 28 days to reduce disability benefits. Comments close Dec 21.

    inserted: DOL will provide the data to SSA every 28 days, consistent with its FECA benefit payment cycle. Each data transmission is expected to include payment information for approximately 40,000 FECA beneficiaries.

    Who it affects: About 40,000 people getting Social Security Disability Insurance (and their auxiliaries) who also receive Federal Employees' Compensation Act benefits

    Comments close Dec 21 · in 30 daysRead the change →Source ↗ for Social Security
  • PricingSpotifyMinorNov 19

    Spotify: Spotify Premium free trial is now 3 months for new users, until December 31

    New Spotify Premium Individual subscribers get 3 months for $0, then $12.99 a month. The offer ends December 31, 2026.

    Who it affects: People in the US who have never tried Spotify Premium

    Offer ends Dec 31 · in 40 daysRead the change →Source ↗ for Spotify

Canva's Terms of Use, effective November 16, drop "Pay as You Grow": a new Team seat is now charged right away (prorated), an invite counts as a seat whether or not it's accepted, and the 3-day warning email before seat charges is gone. This report covers November 16 to 19 (the November 17 and 18 runs didn't happen).

Also: Superhuman (Grammarly's owner) added GPTZero to its privacy policy and says it may license social media profile info about users and non-users, some of which other users can see. Notion's pricing FAQ no longer says solo Free workspaces get unlimited blocks, though its plan table still says "Limited for 2+ members". Shopify's plan table now lists a 0.35% tax calculation fee (0.25% on Plus) after an introductory discount. Google Play's new terms, previewed in October, took effect November 17. Smaller edits: Slack's Enterprise+ "Unlimited Slackbot" became "no weekly message limits, subject to our usage guidelines"; Todoist's free plan now lists 10 Ramble sessions a month and paid plans get Automations (beta); and Social Security announced a new data match with the Labor Department on federal workers' comp payments (comments close December 21).

Spotify's Premium trial for new users is now 3 months for $0 (until December 31). Noise: Netflix's "Sign in" capitalization, Paramount+'s $2.99 promo swap, Audible's recurring plan-card rewrite, Mailchimp's two-version flip, Venmo's legal menu, Tubi's device ID, Wix's terms page (full text captured again; no wording changed), BeReal footer, Booking.com calendar IDs, ZWILLING's Black Friday banner, Delta's 404 title and footnote links, Figma/Wix/Vimeo/1Password/Proton layout edits, a Google One YouTube Premium Lite footnote, and Anthropic's button label. PayPal terms and privacy and Roblox terms are still HTTP 403; Shein terms is still bot-walled.
