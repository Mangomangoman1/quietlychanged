Claude's $200 plan becomes "Ultra" with no 5-hour limit

2 changes · checked October 31, 2026

What changed

  • PricingAnthropic ClaudeNotableOct 31

    Anthropic Claude: Claude's $200 plan becomes "Ultra": no 5-hour limit, but 10x Pro's weekly usage instead of 20x per session

    Claude Max 20x is gone. The $200 plan is now Ultra: no 5-hour limit for verified accounts and 10x Pro's weekly usage. Max is a single $100 plan with 5x.

    deleted: Choose 5x or 20x more usage than Pro* inserted: No 5-hour limit 10x Pro usage every week*

    Who it affects: Claude Max subscribers, especially those paying $200 a month for Max 20x, and Pro users who want Anthropic's newest model.

  • PrivacyToyotaNotableOct 31

    Toyota: Toyota now lists training large language models as a use of your personal information, and adds Acxiom tracking across your devices

    Toyota's Oct 28 privacy notices add "training large language models" as a use of personal data, including connected-car data, plus Acxiom cross-device tracking and AI in customer support.

    inserted: For the purpose of training large language models;

    Who it affects: Toyota owners and lessees in the US, people who use the Toyota app or Connected Services, anyone who has called, chatted with or emailed Toyota, and visitors to toyota.com. Lexus owners are covered by a separate Lexus notice that has the same language-model line.

    Read the change →Source ↗ for Toyota

Anthropic reworked its Claude plans. Max 20x is gone: the $200 tier is now Ultra, which promises 10x Pro's weekly usage and no 5-hour limit for verified accounts, where the old plan promised 20x Pro's usage per 5-hour session. Max is a single $100 plan with 5x Pro usage, and a new top model, Fable, is included up to half your weekly limit on Max and Ultra but costs usage credits on Pro. The other diffs were noise or already covered. Slack's and Shopify's pricing pages showed the other versions they rotate between, Delta's contract of carriage got a formatting and encoding cleanup, PayPal's privacy statement shows its contact emails again, Venmo's privacy page added a navigation menu, and Figma listed a new October 30 version of its privacy policy with no wording change in the text we track. Pinterest and PlayStation pages failed with SSL errors and Roblox's terms returned a 403.
