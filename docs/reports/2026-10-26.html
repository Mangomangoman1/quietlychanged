    California: From Jan 1, California charges sales tax on software subscriptions, but not on streaming, music, e-books or games

    Starting January 1, 2027, downloaded and cloud software (SaaS) sold to Californians gets sales tax of 7.25% or more. Streaming, music, audiobooks, e-books and games are excluded.

    deleted: The sale or lease of a prewritten program is not a taxable transaction if the program is transferred by remote telecommunications inserted: A digital product and any copyright or patent interests associated therewith.

    Who it affects: Anyone who buys prewritten software for use in California, whether downloaded, used in a browser or app, or sold as a subscription (SaaS), including people and businesses. Sales are sourced to the buyer's California address. Streaming video, music, audiobooks, e-books, entertainment games, crypto and cloud infrastructure services are excluded, and so is custom software.
