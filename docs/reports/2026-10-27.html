Slack drops "24/7 support" from its plan table

4 changes · checked October 27, 2026

What changed

  • PricingSlackNotableOct 27

    Slack: Slack drops "24/7 support" from its plan table; the free support tier now targets a 2-business-day reply

    Slack's pricing page no longer lists 24/7 support with a 4-hour first response. The free Standard Success Plan targets a 2-business-day case reply; faster help is a paid add-on.

    deleted: 24/7 support with 4-hr. first response time inserted: Free, always-on resources and essential support — live help via Help Agent and chat, with 2-business-day target case response.

    Who it affects: Teams on paid Slack plans (Pro, Business+, Enterprise) who depend on Slack's customer support, especially admins who counted on 24/7 help with a 4-hour first response.

    Read the change →Source ↗ for Slack
  • TermsXeroNotableOct 27

    Xero: Xero can now require ID and a face scan or cut off your account; US disputes get a 1-year deadline from Dec 28

    Xero's new terms let it demand a government ID and a biometric face scan, and suspend or close accounts that don't pass. US customers also get new arbitration rules, including a one-year limit on claims.

    inserted: we may ask you to provide things like government-issued identification documents and a biometric facial scan so we can confirm the document belongs to you.

    Who it affects: Everyone with a Xero subscription, plus invited users such as bookkeepers and accountants (the US arbitration terms "apply to you and your invited users"). New customers are on the new terms now; existing customers from December 28, 2026. The arbitration changes apply to the US edition; identity verification and the bank-feed wording are in the global terms.

    Takes effect Dec 28 · in 58 daysRead the change →Source ↗ for Xero
  • Terms1PasswordMinorOct 27

    1Password: 1Password's business terms add paid Professional Services and drop the right to cancel if setup fails

    1Password's business subscription terms now cover paid setup and training services. A clause letting customers terminate if 1Password couldn't finish setting up the service was removed.

    deleted: 1Password will provide standard support services as provided on the website at inserted: 1Password makes web-based support resources and self-guided onboarding materials available through its support portal at

    Who it affects: Businesses on 1Password Teams or Business plans, especially ones buying onboarding or setup help. Personal and Family users aren't covered by these terms.

    Read the change →Source ↗ for 1Password
  • PricingNotionMinorOct 27

    Notion: Notion's pricing page stops saying "unlimited" file uploads, listing "Up to 5 GB" on paid plans

    Notion's pricing table now lists paid-plan file uploads as "Up to 5 GB" instead of "Unlimited", and drops "unlimited" wording from several other rows.

    deleted: Upgrade to a paid plan for unlimited file uploads with a ~5GB max per file. inserted: Upgrade to a paid plan to upload files up to 5 GB each.

    Who it affects: People comparing or paying for Notion's paid plans, especially teams that rely on large file uploads, many published pages or many guests.

    Read the change →Source ↗ for Notion

Slack replaced the 24/7 support row on its pricing page with "Success Plans": the free Standard tier targets a 2-business-day case reply, and faster help (1-hour or 15-minute targets) is a paid add-on sold through sales reps. 1Password's business terms add paid Professional Services and remove the clause that let customers cancel if setup failed (and still contain "[LINK]" placeholders). Notion's pricing page now lists paid-plan uploads as "Up to 5 GB" instead of "Unlimited" and drops "unlimited" from several rows.

Noise we skipped: Slack's Enterprise+ price flip, Squarespace's alternate layout, Todoist's billing-toggle text, Audible's plan-card reshuffle (same $8.99 and $14.95 prices), Mailchimp's price render, Venmo's navigation menu, Anthropic relabeling "Extra usage" as "Usage credits", and email addresses that PayPal, Shein and Cloudflare pages un-hid or reformatted. Roblox's terms page still blocks our fetch (HTTP 403).
