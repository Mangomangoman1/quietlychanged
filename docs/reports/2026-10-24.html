Todoist drops the "generalized" loophole from its no-AI-training promise

2 changes · checked October 24, 2026

What changed

  • TermsFCCMajorOct 24

    FCC: From Nov 25, companies can choose the only way you're allowed to stop their robocalls and texts

    From Nov 25, a company can name the only way you're allowed to stop its robocalls and texts, and replying STOP to one kind of alert may not stop the others.

    deleted: may not designate an exclusive means to request revocation of consent inserted: Callers may designate any of the following methods as the exclusive means to revoke consent

    Who it affects: Anyone in the U.S. who gets automated calls or texts that need consent: payment and appointment reminders, delivery and account alerts, bank fraud alerts and marketing texts. That's nearly everyone with a cell phone. It applies to autodialed and prerecorded calls and automated texts, not to ordinary calls from a person.

    Rule takes effect Nov 25 · in 27 daysRead the change →Source ↗ for FCC
  • PrivacyTodoistNotableOct 24

    Todoist: Privacy policy now says user data isn't used to train any AI models

    Todoist's privacy policy now says your data isn't used to train any AI models. It used to rule out only "generalized" ones.

    Who it affects: Todoist users concerned about their tasks and data being used for AI training

    Read the change →Source ↗ for Todoist

Doist's privacy policy now says Todoist user data isn't used to develop, improve, or train AI or machine learning models, with no "generalized/non-personalized" qualifier (privacy policy).

Everything else today was noise: Shopify's and Slack's pricing pages rotated between versions we've seen before, Squarespace swapped back to its alternate plan-card layout with the same prices, Audible and Grammarly reworded marketing copy, PayPal's privacy page un-obfuscated contact emails, and Wix's terms page loaded only partly. Seven pages (Delta, Pinterest, Verizon) failed to fetch during the run but loaded fine on a retry minutes later; Roblox's terms page is still blocked (HTTP 403).
