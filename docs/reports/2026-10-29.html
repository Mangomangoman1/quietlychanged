GitHub Enterprise now includes Copilot, with AI usage billed against your budget

2 changes · checked October 29, 2026

What changed

  • PricingGitHubNotableOct 29

    GitHub: GitHub Enterprise now includes Copilot for every licensed user, with AI usage billed against your budget from the first request

    GitHub's pricing page now lists "GitHub Copilot access" in the Enterprise plan. There are no per-seat AI credits: Chat, code review and cloud agents draw on the company's AI budget.

    deleted: Security, compliance, and flexible deployment inserted: AI-powered development, governed at enterprise scale

    Who it affects: Companies on GitHub Enterprise Cloud, and the developers and finance teams who manage their Copilot spend. Individuals and teams on Free or Team aren't affected, and self-serve Enterprise customers can't switch yet.

    Read the change →Source ↗ for GitHub
  • PrivacySocial SecurityNotableOct 29

    Social Security: Social Security is building one 'Client Data System' profile on everyone with an SSN, combining 12 record systems

    A new SSA records system keeps one profile per SSN: citizenship, earnings, benefits, marriage history, call notes, alert flags. Email may be used for surveys. Comments close Nov 29.

    inserted: We may also use contact information (e.g., email addresses) provided by individuals who conduct business with SSA for program outreach (e.g., notification about our programs, online services, SSA events, and surveys)

    Who it affects: Anyone who has been issued a Social Security number, including people who have never claimed benefits. Retirees, SSI recipients, people with representative payees or appointed representatives, and anyone who has contacted SSA have the most data in it.

    Comments close Nov 29 · in 30 daysRead the change →Source ↗ for Social Security

GitHub's pricing page now lists "GitHub Copilot access" and "Agent governance" in the Enterprise plan, which it now pitches as "AI-powered development, governed at enterprise scale." Per GitHub's Oct 28 changelog, the bundled Copilot has no monthly AI credits per seat: usage is metered against the enterprise budget from the first request, and for now it's only for Enterprise Cloud customers with an account team. The other diffs were noise: Squarespace and Audible served the other version of the pricing pages they rotate between, Figma's Specs beta line flipped back after a one-day blip, Shein's terms now hide their legal email behind an "[email protected]" placeholder, and AT&T's terms page changed a navigation link. Roblox's terms page is still blocking our fetch (HTTP 403).
