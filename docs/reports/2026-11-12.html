Robinhood's KPI binary options get a fee formula from Jan 1, 2027

5 changes · checked November 12, 2026

What changed

  • PricingRobinhoodNotableNov 12

    Robinhood: Robinhood sets fees for its KPI binary options from Jan 1, 2027: up to 3 cents a contract, 6% of a 50-cent bet

    Robinhood's earnings-metric contracts are free only through Dec 31, 2026. From Jan 1 a fee formula kicks in: 3 cents on a 50-cent contract without Gold, 1.75 cents with it.

    inserted: Contract Fee: $0 through December 31, 2026 Effective January 1, 2027 Total Contract Fee = (k × p × (1 − p) × c)

    Who it affects: Robinhood customers who trade KPI binary options, the Cboe-listed yes/no contracts that pay $1 if a company's reported metric (like EPS or revenue) hits a strike; cheap long-shot contracts are hit hardest as a share of what you pay

    Fees start Jan 1 · in 49 daysRead the change →Source ↗ for Robinhood
  • TermsUnited AirlinesNotableNov 12

    United Airlines: United's new contract of carriage doubles how early kids flying alone must be dropped off, to 60 minutes, next to its AI-agent ban

    United's Nov 11 contract of carriage says an unaccompanied minor must be brought to the airport 60 minutes early, up from 30. United's own help page still says 30. The same revision bans booking with AI agents.

    inserted: The use of AI Agents to make, modify or service a Ticket, and/or to purchase any service or product.

    Who it affects: Parents and guardians sending a child aged 5 to 14 alone on United or United Express (unaccompanied minor service is mandatory at those ages, optional at 15 to 17), and anyone who uses an AI assistant, AI browser or travel-agency AI tool to book or manage United trips.

    Read the change →Source ↗ for United Airlines
  • TermsGoogle PlayNotableNov 12

    Google Play: Dec 17 terms in many countries: same Play-services Content change as the US Nov 17 preview

    In Australia, New Zealand and several other countries, Google Play's terms preview is dated Dec 17, not Nov 17. It makes the same Play-services Content change as the US preview.

    deleted: in connection with your use and viewing of Content and Google Play inserted: in connection with Content and Google Play, including any use, installation, viewing, and maintenance on your device of Content and Google Play

    Who it affects: People who use Google Play under a country page that shows a December 17, 2026 update notice, including Australia, New Zealand, Singapore, South Africa, Thailand, and Indonesia. US and Canadian pages still say November 17.

    New terms take effect Dec 17 · in 34 daysRead the change →Source ↗ for Google Play
  • PricingXbox Game PassMinorNov 12

    Xbox Game Pass: Xbox Game Pass fine print: promo subscribers may not get Fortnite Crew benefits

    Xbox's Game Pass page now warns that some perks, including Fortnite Crew benefits, aren't available during a promotional period, and says a payment method rather than a credit card is required.

    inserted: Some experiences not available during the promotional period, including Fortnite Crew benefits.

    Who it affects: People signing up for Xbox Game Pass on a trial or promotional price, especially for the Fortnite Crew perk

    Read the change →Source ↗ for Xbox Game Pass
  • PricingAudibleMinorNov 12

    Audible: Audible's plan page drops the 'don't roll over' warning and lists its $229.50 plan as 12 credits

    Audible's Standard plan card no longer says unused monthly picks don't roll over, and the $229.50-a-year Premium card now reads "12 creditos/año" where it said 24 audiobooks.

    Auto-renews at $8.99/mo after 30 days. Cancel anytime. deleted: Unused monthly audiobook selections don't roll over.

    Who it affects: People comparing Audible's Standard and annual Premium plans before signing up

    Read the change →Source ↗ for Audible

Three real changes today. Robinhood's fee schedule now says its KPI binary options (Cboe's yes/no contracts on company metrics like EPS and revenue) are "$0 through December 31, 2026", then charge a contract fee of 10% × p × (1 − p) per contract (5% with Gold) plus a 2% exchange fee on the same formula: $3.00 on 100 contracts at 50 cents without Gold. Xbox's Game Pass fine print now warns that "Fortnite Crew benefits" may not be included during a promotional period. Audible's plan page dropped its "don't roll over" warning on Standard and now labels the $229.50 annual plan "12 creditos/año" where it said 24 audiobooks, which looks like a page error.

Noise skipped: Chase's Digital Services Agreement PDF was re-extracted with different line breaks (the live text, including the Oct 27, 2026 and Jan 1, 2027 dates, is unchanged); the rest of Robinhood's fee-schedule diff was layout reflow; Reddit added a Kannada language list; Mailchimp's starting price didn't render; Tubi's device ID rotated. Fetch failures: PayPal terms and privacy (HTTP 403), Roblox (403), Shein (bot wall), Emotiv privacy (empty page).

Later today we also logged a Google Play follow-up: official Play terms pages for Australia, New Zealand, Singapore, South Africa, Thailand, and Indonesia say the same preview takes effect December 17, 2026, not November 17. The US and Canadian pages still say November 17. This is not a second US update.
