Spotify's two-month free trial ends; Premium is back to one free month

2 changes · checked November 1, 2026

What changed

  • PrivacyTubiNotableNov 1

    Tubi: Tubi moves onto Fox's privacy policy: it can now collect your precise location, and its no-targeted-ads age for teens drops from 18 to 16

    Tubi's Oct 22 privacy policy is Fox's joint policy: it allows precise location, adds AI tools for ads and support, and cuts a teen targeted-ads limit from under-18 to under-16.

    deleted: In certain circumstances, we also may collect general location information, such as the city, state, and country where the device is located. inserted: In certain circumstances, we also may collect geolocation data (including your precise location).

    Who it affects: Anyone who watches Tubi in the US on the web, the mobile apps or a TV app, especially people who let the Tubi app use location and teens aged 16 and 17. Parents of children who use Tubi Kids are covered by the new Children's Privacy Policy.

  • PricingSpotifyMinorNov 1

    Spotify: Premium free trial back to 1 month as the 2-month offer ends

    Spotify's two-month free Premium trial ended on schedule. New subscribers now get one free month, then $12.99 a month.

    Try deleted: 2 months inserted: 1 month for $0

    Who it affects: People in the US who have never had Spotify Premium; past or current subscribers didn't qualify for either trial

    Read the change →Source ↗ for Spotify

A quiet day: one small real update out of six raw page diffs. Spotify's US Premium page went back to "Try 1 month for $0" on November 1, the day its two-month trial offer (which we reported on October 20) was set to end; it's still $12.99 a month afterward and only for people who've never had Premium. The rest was noise: Slack's Enterprise+ price flipped to $45 again (it alternates with "Contact sales for pricing"), Shopify's, Audible's and Mailchimp's pricing pages served the other versions they rotate between, and Venmo's privacy page dropped its navigation menu. Four pages failed to fetch: PlayStation's terms (an SSL error for a third day; all of playstation.com is unreachable from our fetcher), Roblox's terms (HTTP 403), Microsoft 365's pricing page (a bot wall) and Fitbit's privacy page (HTTP 429, rate limited).
