A quiet day: three small real updates out of five raw page diffs. Squarespace redesigned the plan cards on its pricing page. Prices are the same ($25, $39, $65 and $139 a month, or $19, $29, $49 and $99 billed yearly), but the Basic card now spells out a "2% online store transaction fee" and lists AI credits up front (10 one-time on Basic, then 20, 40 and 120 a month), and the cards no longer show the per-transaction percentage for automated tax calculation. Shopify's terms now point to a single Shopify Tax Terms of Service for all "tax-related tools" and drop the separate reference to Automated Filing terms. Figma's pricing page added "Audio imports" to its list of motion design features. The rest was noise: Audible's plan page went back to its English layout with the same prices and credits, and Mailchimp's page showed a "$20" starting price that our earlier copies didn't capture and swapped one item in a feature list. Roblox's terms page blocked our fetch.
