cPanel takes 50% off new licenses until December 7

1 change · checked November 23, 2026

What changed

  • PricingcPanelNotableNov 23

    cPanel: cPanel takes 50% off new licenses until Dec 7, nine days before its Dec 16 price increase

    cPanel's Black Friday sale halves new license prices until December 7: Solo is $13.73 a month billed annually, down from $27.46. Prices rise for new orders and renewals on December 16.

    deleted: Save 8.4% with annual billing $ 29.99/mo inserted: Annual billing 50% OFF $ 27.46/mo

    Who it affects: Anyone buying a new cPanel Solo, Admin, Pro, Premier or WP Squared license from the cPanel Store, especially those weighing a purchase before the December 16, 2026 price increase

    Offer ends Dec 7 · in 11 daysRead the change →Source ↗ for cPanel

cPanel's pricing page now runs a Black Friday sale: "50% off new licenses. Till December 7." Solo drops from $27.46 to $13.73 a month billed annually, Admin from $32.96 to $16.48, Pro from $49.46 to $24.73; Premier ($34.99) and WP Squared ($42.49) are halved on monthly billing. The sale ends nine days before cPanel's December 16 price increase for new orders and renewals.

Noise: Slack's Enterprise+ price flipped back to "$45" (it has flipped between $45 and "Contact sales" for days), Venmo's legal menu reappeared on its terms and privacy pages, Booking.com reordered two calendar IDs, and Tubi's device ID changed. YouTube TV pricing (first diff since tracking began Nov 22) and Disney+ pricing: no change. PayPal terms and privacy and Roblox terms are still HTTP 403; Shein terms is still bot-walled.
