Fable 5.2 lands 20% cheaper; Microsoft adds a new data source

2 changes · checked November 10, 2026

What changed

  • PricingAnthropic ClaudeNotableNov 10

    Anthropic Claude: Fable 5.2 launches at 20% lower output and caching prices than Fable 5.1

    Anthropic's pricing page now lists Fable 5.2 at $40 per million output tokens, down from $50 for Fable 5.1, with prompt caching also 20% cheaper. Fable 5.1 moves to the legacy list.

    Fable deleted: 5.1 inserted: 5.2 Next generation intelligence for long-running agents

    Who it affects: Developers and companies paying for Anthropic's top Fable model through the Claude API, especially long-running agent workloads that produce a lot of output or reuse cached prompts

    Read the change →Source ↗ for Anthropic Claude
  • PrivacyMicrosoft 365NotableNov 10

    Microsoft 365: Microsoft adds outside "workplace and productivity data" companies as a data source, and age checks for logged-out users

    Microsoft's privacy statement now says it gets data from companies that supply workplace and productivity data for product research, and may use device info to apply age protections when you aren't signed in.

    inserted: Companies that provide workplace and productivity data for research, development, and improvement of our products and services.

    Who it affects: Anyone with a Microsoft account or Windows PC, including people who use Microsoft services without signing in and Phone Link users

    Read the change →Source ↗ for Microsoft 365

Two real changes today. Anthropic's pricing page swapped Fable 5.1 for Fable 5.2, with output at $40 per million tokens (was $50) and prompt caching 20% cheaper; Fable 5.1 stays listed at its old rates. Microsoft's privacy statement adds "companies that provide workplace and productivity data" as a source of personal data, uses device-based information for age protections when you aren't signed in, and rewrites its Phone Link section with new location sharing.

Noise skipped: Proton's pricing page switched to a different plan comparison variant; Squarespace added a 30%-off promo; Audible's plan-card layout rotated; Mailchimp rotated versions; Netlify, Muse, Vrew and Cloudflare diffs were text-extraction differences (date normalization, nbsp, page chrome); Grindr renamed a help link; Tubi's device ID and a Booking.com calendar value rotated. Fetch failures: PayPal terms and privacy (HTTP 403), Xbox Game Pass (SSLError), Roblox (403), Shein (bot wall), Emotiv privacy (empty page).
