What changed

  • TermsNetlifyMajorNov 9

    Netlify: Dec 4 terms: a verified email domain can see your account and public sites, and may absorb the account after notice

    From Dec 4, an org that verifies your email's domain can see that your Netlify account exists, its plan, and your public sites' names, URLs and last deploy dates. If it also has a Netlify subscription, your account can move under its control unless you change your email in the notice window.

    Who it affects: Anyone with a Netlify account whose email is on a company or school domain, especially personal projects signed up with a work email

    Takes effect Dec 4 · in 25 daysRead the change →Source ↗ for Netlify
  • TermsVrewNotableNov 9

    Vrew: Dec 1: one account per person, and AI will analyze project content and editing history until you delete the account

    From Dec 1, Vrew allows only one account per person (no team sharing on one login) and will use AI to analyze project content, assets and editing history for personalization, kept until the account is deleted. Object via chat support by Dec 1.

    Who it affects: Vrew users who share a paid login across a team, or who keep project footage, captions and edit history they do not want AI-analyzed
