Discord's Dec 15 terms let servers show content in search results; Disney+ bundle $4.99 for a year

3 changes · checked November 24, 2026

What changed

  • TermsDiscordMajorNov 24

    Discord: Discord's Dec 15 terms let server admins show server content in search engine results

    Discord's updated terms, in force December 15, say admins can have server content "appear off of Discord (such as in search engine results)", and hand all arbitrability questions to the arbitrator.

    Who it affects: Every Discord user, especially people who post in large or public servers, and U.S. and Canadian users bound by Discord's arbitration agreement

    Takes effect Dec 15 · in 18 daysRead the change →Source ↗ for Discord
  • PricingDisney+NotableNov 24

    Disney+: Disney+ and Hulu bundle drops to $4.99 a month for a year in a Black Friday deal that ends Nov 30

    Disney+'s Black Friday offer cuts the ad-supported Disney+, Hulu Bundle from $12.99 to $4.99 a month for 12 months (Premium: $14.99, from $21.99). New and eligible returning subscribers only; ends Nov 30.

    deleted: Disney+, Hulu Bundle for $12.99/mo or inserted: Disney+, Hulu Bundle, which includes Disney+ (With Ads) and Hulu (With Ads), is

    Who it affects: People in the U.S. who don't currently subscribe to Disney+, Hulu, ESPN or a bundle and are thinking about the Disney+, Hulu Bundle; current subscribers are excluded

    Offer ends Nov 30 · in 3 daysRead the change →Source ↗ for Disney+
  • PrivacyDiscordNotableNov 24

    Discord: Discord's Dec 15 privacy policy adds advertising IDs for sponsored content, personalized by default in some regions

    From December 15, Discord's privacy policy says sponsored content can use your device or advertising ID, and that in some regions it "may be personalized by default (and you can disable it)".

    inserted: If you interact with sponsored content, this may also include, consistent with your settings, information like device ID, advertising ID (such as IDFA or Android Ad ID), or similar unique IDs (such as a click or interaction ID).

    Who it affects: Discord users who see Quests or other sponsored content, and anyone asked to verify their age on Discord

    Takes effect Dec 15 · in 18 daysRead the change →Source ↗ for Discord

Discord posted new Terms of Service and a new Privacy Policy, both effective December 15, 2026. The terms say server admins can "have certain content from the server appear off of Discord (such as in search engine results)", that Discord may delete content "for any reason", and that disputes now start with a form at dis.gd/disputes; the arbitrator, not a court, decides whether a claim is arbitrable. The privacy policy adds device and advertising IDs for sponsored content and says that in some regions "sponsored content may be personalized by default (and you can disable it)". https://discord.com/terms · https://discord.com/privacy

Disney+'s Black Friday offer cuts the ad-supported Disney+, Hulu Bundle to $4.99 a month for 12 months (regular $12.99) and the Premium bundle to $14.99 (regular $21.99), for new and eligible returning U.S. subscribers until 11:59 PM PST on November 30. The 6-month price lock and the $6.49 student bundle are no longer shown on the home page. https://www.disneyplus.com/
