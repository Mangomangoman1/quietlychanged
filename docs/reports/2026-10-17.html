Google's privacy policy now lets Gmail, Photos and Drive data shape ads if you share it with Search or Gemini

4 changes · checked October 17, 2026

What changed

  • PrivacyGoogleMajorOct 17

    Google: Gmail, Photos and Drive data you share with Search or Gemini can now shape ads

    Google's promise not to use Gmail, Drive and Photos content for ads now has an exception: if you let Search or Gemini read that data, it can shape your ads.

    Who it affects: Anyone with a Google account who turns on, or is offered, features that let Search or Gemini read Gmail, Google Photos, or Drive, such as Personal Intelligence

    Read the change →Source ↗ for Google
  • TermsOuraMajorOct 17

    Oura: App locks Nov 4 unless you accept new arbitration terms

    Oura's app stops working Nov 4 unless you accept new terms: arbitration with a jury waiver, mass claims handled 20 at a time, and no opt-out.

    deleted: at least thirty (30) days in advance inserted: in advance of the effective date and within the timeframe required by applicable law

    Who it affects: Oura Ring owners and Oura Membership subscribers, especially in the U.S., where the new arbitration rules apply. Everyone has to accept in the app by November 4, 2026 to keep using it

    Accept in the app by Nov 4 · in 6 daysRead the change →Source ↗ for Oura
  • TermsRocket MoneyNotableOct 17

    Rocket Money: Bill negotiation moves to $15/month Premium+ on Nov 2

    From Nov 2, Rocket Money only negotiates bills for $15-a-month Premium+ members. The pay-only-if-it-works option and automatic renegotiations end.

    inserted: As of November 2, 2026, Bill Negotiation is exclusively a Premium+ feature. Negotiations already in progress or on a payment plan will continue. Auto-renegotiations will sunset on November 2, 2026.

    Who it affects: Rocket Money members on the free or Premium plans who use bill negotiation, and anyone with an automatic renegotiation scheduled for November or later

    Moves to Premium+ Nov 2 · in 4 daysRead the change →Source ↗ for Rocket Money
  • PricingGitHubMinorOct 17

    GitHub: AI secret detection now covers push protection, billed by usage

    GitHub's AI password detection can now block pushes, but that feature is billed by usage instead of being included in Secret Protection.

    inserted: Use AI to detect unstructured secrets, like passwords, and block pushes that contain them.

    Who it affects: Teams paying for GitHub Secret Protection, especially admins who want AI-based password detection to block pushes and need to budget for usage charges

    Read the change →Source ↗ for GitHub

Two real updates today out of nine raw page diffs. Google's privacy policy, now effective October 16, 2026, drops its flat promise that it doesn't show personalized ads based on your content from Drive, Gmail, or Photos: the line now ends "unless you choose to make data from these services available to other Google services that support personalized ads," and a new passage says Search may use data you let it access from those apps to "personalize content, including ads." GitHub's pricing page renamed Copilot secret scanning to "AI detection for alerts" and added a usage-based "AI detection for push protection" to Secret Protection. DoorDash's new terms showed up in our snapshot today but were already covered on Oct 16. Audible's plan page flipped back to its "Cancel anytime" and "Save 16%" wording, and Squarespace, Wix, Grammarly, PayPal, and Mailchimp changes were layout, localized pricing, email de-obfuscation, and marketing copy, not real policy or price changes.
