Airbnb renamed its off-platform fee rules; Audible's Standard plan card now warns picks don't roll over

October 23, 2026 · 2 changes

What changed

  • Terms

    Airbnb: Terms rename the Offline Fee and Off-Platform policies

    What changed
    Airbnb's Terms of Service now refer to the "Offline Fee Policy" as its "policy on pricing and payment transparency" and to the "Off-Platform Policy" as its "policy on limiting host-imposed contracts and other requirements." The rules around them read the same: hosts must describe all additional fees in the listing, and guests and hosts must not request, make, or accept bookings or payments outside Airbnb to avoid fees or taxes. The new names suggest a broader focus on price transparency and on extra contracts or requirements hosts impose on guests.
    Who it affects
    Airbnb hosts who charge extra fees or ask guests to sign agreements, and guests booking stays
    October 23, 2026View source page for Airbnb →
  • Pricing

    Audible: Standard plan card now says unused monthly picks don't roll over

    What changed
    Audible's membership page now states on the Standard plan card ($8.99/mo after a 30-day trial, 1 audiobook a month): "Unused monthly audiobook selections don't roll over." The same rule was already in the page's FAQ. The $229.50-a-year Premium plan is now labeled "24 audiobooks" (it previously showed a Spanish "12 creditos/año" label). Prices are unchanged: Premium $14.95/mo, Premium Annual $149.50/yr for 12 audiobooks.
    Who it affects
    People choosing between Audible Standard and Premium

A quiet day: two small real updates out of nine raw page diffs. Airbnb's Terms of Service renamed the two policies that govern fees and payments outside its platform: the "Offline Fee Policy" is now called the "policy on pricing and payment transparency," and the "Off-Platform Policy" is now the "policy on limiting host-imposed contracts and other requirements." The surrounding rules in the terms, such as not taking bookings or payments off Airbnb to avoid fees, read the same. Audible's membership page now says right on the Standard plan card that "Unused monthly audiobook selections don't roll over" (this was already in the page's FAQ), and its $229.50-a-year Premium plan is now labeled "24 audiobooks." Prices didn't change. The rest was layout or noise: Squarespace and Shopify redesigned their plan cards with the same prices (Squarespace's cards no longer list AI credit counts, and Shopify moved card rates off the cards into its comparison table, still 2.9% + 30¢ on Basic), Mailchimp's starting price didn't load in our copy, Grammarly swapped its "Try Pro for $0 today" headline for "Great Writing Starts With a Plan," PayPal's contact emails changed format only, and Canva removed a "[Placeholder]" footer link.
