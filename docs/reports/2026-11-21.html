Stripe's rewritten privacy policy is now in force; Dropbox puts a number on AI use

2 changes · checked November 21, 2026

What changed

  • PrivacyStripeMinorNov 21

    Stripe: Stripe's rewritten privacy policy is now in force: AI voice agents, data to your card's bank, AI-agent payments

    The Stripe Privacy Policy we previewed on Nov 3 is now the live version, dated Nov 20. It covers AI voice-agent calls, data sent to your card's bank and payments made by AI agents.

    deleted: This Privacy Policy will be updated on <DATE>. Please review the upcoming changes

    Who it affects: Anyone who has paid through a Stripe-powered checkout or has a Link account, plus businesses and sales leads Stripe contacts

    Read the change →Source ↗ for Stripe
  • PricingDropboxMinorNov 21

    Dropbox: Dropbox's team plans now list 'AI in Dropbox' with 250 AI credits per person a month

    Dropbox's plans page now lists "AI in Dropbox" on Standard ($15/user) and Advanced ($24/user), with a stated allowance of 250 AI credits per person, per month.

    inserted: AI in Dropbox Organize and rename files in bulk Transform existing content into new work Approve AI actions and undo changes Get answers grounded in your files 250 AI credits per person, per month

    Who it affects: Dropbox Standard and Advanced team customers and anyone comparing Dropbox plans

    Read the change →Source ↗ for Dropbox

Stripe's new Privacy Policy, which we previewed on November 3, is now the live version at stripe.com/privacy, "Last updated: November 20, 2026": AI voice agents that may call you, fraud signals (your IP address, email, phone and shipping address) shared with your card-issuing bank, and a new "Agentic Payments" section for purchases an AI agent makes on your behalf.

Also: Dropbox's plans page now lists "AI in Dropbox" on its Standard ($15/user/month) and Advanced ($24/user/month) plans, with "250 AI credits per person, per month".

Noise: Shopify rewrote its pricing-page marketing blocks (the "Earn 1% back" credit lines and AI-chat selling were already on the page), Audible moved its plan cards back up the page, Wix's terms page was fully captured again (same text as November 19), Venmo's legal menu, Hunter's blog navigation and Tubi's device ID. PayPal terms and privacy and Roblox terms are still HTTP 403; Shein terms is still bot-walled. Calm terms returned 403 to our crawler but fetched normally on retry and hasn't changed.
