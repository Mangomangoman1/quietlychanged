Shopify shrinks its annual discount, Slack and Claude Enterprise get price tags

8 changes · checked October 15, 2026

What changed

  • TermsBank of AmericaMajorOct 15

    Bank of America: Narrows error and fraud protection for linked external-account transactions (Oct 19)

    If you pay a Bank of America card or loan from an account at another bank, BofA no longer investigates most errors or fraud on those payments. The other bank has to.

    deleted: if the problem or error relates to a transfer from an account maintained at another financial institution

    Who it affects: Bank of America online and mobile banking customers who link accounts at other banks, especially those who pay BofA credit cards, mortgages, or HELOCs from an outside account

    Read the change →Source ↗ for Bank of America
  • PricingShopifyNotableOct 15

    Shopify: Yearly prices up 13% to 20% on Grow and Advanced

    Paying yearly for Shopify Grow or Advanced now shows $89 or $359 a month instead of $79 or $299. Monthly prices didn't move.

    deleted: $79 inserted: $89 USD/mo

    Who it affects: Shopify merchants on Grow or Advanced who pay yearly or are about to renew or sign up on annual billing

    Read the change →Source ↗ for Shopify
  • PricingAudibleNotableOct 15

    Audible: Annual plans: cancellation now effective next billing cycle

    Audible's annual Premium plans now say cancellation takes effect at the next billing cycle, not right away.

    deleted: Auto-renews at $229.50/yr. Cancel anytime. inserted: Auto-renews at $229.50/yr. Cancel anytime, effective next billing cycle.

    Who it affects: Audible members on, or considering, annual Premium plans

    Read the change →Source ↗ for Audible
  • PricingVerizonNotableOct 15

    Verizon: Regulatory Charge rises 24% to $0.26 per voice line on Nov 1

    Verizon's own Regulatory Charge goes from $0.21 to $0.26 per voice line on Nov 1. It's a Verizon fee, not a government tax.

    inserted: Effective November 1, 2026, the monthly Verizon Wireless Regulatory Charge for voice-capable devices will increase from $0.21 to $0.26 per line.

    Who it affects: Verizon postpaid wireless customers with voice-capable lines; federal USF percentage-based charges affect wireless bills industry-wide

    Charge rises Nov 1 · in 4 daysRead the change →Source ↗ for Verizon
  • TermsU.S. DOTNotableOct 15

    U.S. DOT: Airline delay-category rule pushed to Feb 1, 2027 (flights from Jan 1)

    DOT's rule letting airlines stop counting ten kinds of disruptions as their fault now starts Feb 1, 2027, so holiday flights stay under the old categories.

    Who it affects: U.S. air travelers; meal and hotel help still depends on each airline's voluntary customer-service commitments

    Rule takes effect Feb 1 · in 96 daysRead the change →Source ↗ for U.S. DOT
  • PricingSlackMinorOct 15

    Slack: Enterprise+ now has a public price: $45 per user

    Slack Enterprise+ used to say "Contact sales." It now shows a price: $45 per user a month, billed annually.

    deleted: Contact sales for pricing inserted: $45 USD per user / month, when paying annually

    Who it affects: Organizations evaluating or negotiating Slack Enterprise+ plans

    Read the change →Source ↗ for Slack
  • PricingAnthropic ClaudeMinorOct 15

    Anthropic Claude: Claude Enterprise now lists a 20-seat minimum

    Claude Enterprise now requires at least 20 seats, which means at least $4,800 a year before any usage.

    US$20/seat/month, billed deleted: annually. inserted: annually, 20 seat minimum. Usage cost scales with model and task.

    Who it affects: Small companies and teams considering Claude Enterprise instead of the Team plan

    Read the change →Source ↗ for Anthropic Claude
  • PricingFigmaMinorOct 15

    Figma: New Figma Specs beta added to paid seats

    Paid Figma seats now include Figma Specs (beta), and Figma Make gets secrets management and a built-in backend. Prices didn't change.

    inserted: Add a spec directly on the canvas, right next to the designs and code it describes.

    Who it affects: Figma customers on paid Full, Dev, or Collab seats

    Read the change →Source ↗ for Figma

Five real updates today out of nine raw page diffs. Shopify raised its pay-yearly prices for Grow ($79 to $89 a month) and Advanced ($299 to $359 a month) while leaving monthly billing untouched, so the annual discount is much smaller. Slack now publishes a $45 per user price for Enterprise+, Anthropic's Claude Enterprise plan now lists a 20-seat minimum, Figma added a new Specs beta to its seats, and Audible's annual plans now say cancellation takes effect at the next billing cycle. Tinder, PayPal, Mailchimp, and Todoist also shifted, but those were a Dutch-language version of the page, email de-obfuscation, a price that failed to render, and a label tweak, not real policy or price changes.
