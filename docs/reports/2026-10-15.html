Shopify shrinks its annual discount, Slack and Claude Enterprise get price tags

October 15, 2026 · 5 changes

What changed

  • Pricing

    Shopify: Yearly prices up 13% to 20% on Grow and Advanced

    What changed
    Shopify's pricing page now shows pay-yearly prices of $89/mo for Grow (was $79) and $359/mo for Advanced (was $299). Pay-monthly prices are unchanged at $105 and $399, and Basic is unchanged at $29 yearly / $39 monthly. That cuts the annual-billing discount from about 25% to about 15% on Grow and about 10% on Advanced, and raises a year of Grow from $948 to $1,068 and a year of Advanced from $3,588 to $4,308.
    Who it affects
    Shopify merchants on Grow or Advanced who pay yearly or are about to renew or sign up on annual billing
    October 15, 2026View source page for Shopify →
  • Pricing

    Slack: Enterprise+ now has a public price: $45 per user

    What changed
    Slack's pricing page used to say "Contact sales for pricing" for Enterprise+. It now lists $45 USD per user per month when paying annually, with the Contact Sales button still shown.
    Who it affects
    Organizations evaluating or negotiating Slack Enterprise+ plans
    October 15, 2026View source page for Slack →
  • Pricing

    Anthropic Claude: Claude Enterprise now lists a 20-seat minimum

    What changed
    The Enterprise plan on Anthropic's pricing page still reads US$20/seat/month billed annually plus usage at API rates, but now adds "20 seat minimum." That implies at least $4,800 a year in seat fees before any usage.
    Who it affects
    Small companies and teams considering Claude Enterprise instead of the Team plan
    October 15, 2026View source page for Anthropic Claude →
  • Pricing

    Figma: New Figma Specs beta added to paid seats

    What changed
    Figma's pricing page adds Figma Specs, a beta for writing structured specs on the canvas, available on paid seats. Full, Dev, and Collab seat descriptions now all include Figma Specs, and Full seats now explicitly list Figma Motion. Figma Make also gains secrets management and a scaffolded Payload backend (database, APIs, auth, admin panel).
    Who it affects
    Figma customers on paid Full, Dev, or Collab seats
    October 15, 2026View source page for Figma →
  • Pricing

    Audible: Annual plans: cancellation now effective next billing cycle

    What changed
    Audible's plan page now says its annual Premium plans ($149.50/yr for 12 credits, $229.50/yr for 24 credits) can be canceled anytime "effective next billing cycle," where it previously just said "Cancel anytime." The "Save 16%" labels on the annual plans were also removed. Prices did not change.
    Who it affects
    Audible members on, or considering, annual Premium plans
    October 15, 2026View source page for Audible →

Five real updates today out of nine raw page diffs. Shopify raised its pay-yearly prices for Grow ($79 to $89 a month) and Advanced ($299 to $359 a month) while leaving monthly billing untouched, so the annual discount is much smaller. Slack now publishes a $45 per user price for Enterprise+, Anthropic's Claude Enterprise plan now lists a 20-seat minimum, Figma added a new Specs beta to its seats, and Audible's annual plans now say cancellation takes effect at the next billing cycle. Tinder, PayPal, Mailchimp, and Todoist also shifted, but those were a Dutch-language version of the page, email de-obfuscation, a price that failed to render, and a label tweak, not real policy or price changes.
