Day 0: We're watching the fine print

October 13, 2026 · 0 changes

Companies change their prices, plan limits, terms of service, and privacy policies all the time, often without telling anyone. Starting today, QuietlyChanged keeps a dated public record of those changes.

Today we took baseline snapshots of 54 services (121 pages), including Netflix, Spotify, Disney+, Apple, Google, Microsoft 365, Amazon, Dropbox, Zoom, Slack, PayPal, and Delta. Starting tomorrow, each report will list what changed since the day before, with a card for every meaningful change.
