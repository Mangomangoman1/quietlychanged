    Wells Fargo's Online Access Agreement with the AI-agent section took effect Nov 24. One change from the preview: you now "should" keep proof you revoked an agent, not "must".

    You deleted: must inserted: should retain evidence of such revocation and deleted: must provide such evidence to us, at our request.

    Who it affects: Wells Fargo online and mobile banking customers, especially anyone who lets an AI assistant, budgeting app, script or Delegate act in their accounts

Notion's pricing page now lists total file storage per workspace: 512 MB on Free, 256 GB plus 5 GB per added seat on Plus, 512 GB plus 10 GB per added seat on Business. Business now lists 1,500 Standard and 500 Select AI credits per seat a month. https://www.notion.com/pricing

Wells Fargo's Online Access Agreement marked "Version Effective November 24, 2026" is now the live version, including the AI agent section we covered on November 6.
