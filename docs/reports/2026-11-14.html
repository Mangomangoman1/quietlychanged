Leave Verizon Dollars and your balance is forfeited

1 change · checked November 14, 2026

What changed

  • TermsVerizonNotableNov 14

    Verizon: Leaving Verizon Dollars forfeits your balance and fee-free upgrades

    Verizon's loyalty terms now spell out the cost of opting out: your Verizon Dollars are forfeited on the spot, and fee-free activations and upgrades end.

    inserted: If you choose to opt out of Verizon Dollars, you agree: All accumulated Verizon Dollars will be immediately forfeited, with the exception of Verizon Dollars earned by Verizon Visa Card holders for eligible Verizon Visa Card spend.

    Who it affects: Verizon postpaid mobile, Home Internet and Fios customers enrolled in Verizon Dollars, Verizon's loyalty program, especially anyone who joined for the waived activation and upgrade fees or the Glance lock-screen rewards and is thinking of leaving
