7 changes · checked November 14, 2026

  • PrivacyHingeMajorNov 14

    Hinge: Hinge now uses OpenAI to analyze your chats, and may train its own models on your messages

    Hinge's new Chat Insights runs your messages through OpenAI to make suggestions and personalize matches, and Hinge may train its models on them. There's a toggle in Settings.

    inserted: Chat Insights helps us understand how your chats are going, so we can support you in the right moments. We use OpenAI to look for patterns and make suggestions that can help improve your experience.

    Who it affects: Anyone who messages matches on Hinge, and the people they message, especially anyone who shares sensitive details (health, sexuality, location, plans to meet) in Hinge chat.

  • TermsHunterMajorNov 14

    Hunter: HunterSMART Google Home and Alexa cloud link ends Jan 1, 2027

    From Jan 1, 2027, HunterSMART's legacy cloud link to Google Home and Alexa stops. The app and remote still work; Matter is only for eligible models.

    inserted: Beginning January 1, 2027, Hunter will discontinue the legacy cloud-based connections that currently enable cloud-to-cloud linking between HunterSMART™ app accounts and Google Home and Amazon Alexa.

    Who it affects: Anyone who controls a HunterSMART ceiling fan through Google Home or Amazon Alexa by linking their HunterSMART account, especially owners of models that are not eligible for Matter

    Cloud link ends Jan 1 · in 34 daysRead the change →Source ↗ for Hunter
  • TermsKYMCOMajorNov 14

    KYMCO: Noodoe app and cloud shut down worldwide on Jan 1, 2027

    From Jan 1, 2027 the KYMCO Noodoe app will not launch and the cloud shuts down worldwide. On the dash, only the speed reading keeps working as normal.

    inserted: Effective January 1, 2027, all services, functions, and technical support will be terminated worldwide.

    Who it affects: Anyone who uses the KYMCO Noodoe app or the Noodoe-connected dash features on a KYMCO scooter, anywhere in the world

    Shuts down Jan 1 · in 34 daysRead the change →Source ↗ for KYMCO
  • TermsNedap PowerRouterMajorNov 14

    Nedap PowerRouter: MyPowerRouter.com and support end Dec 31, 2026

    Nedap shuts MyPowerRouter.com on December 31, 2026, along with online monitoring, energy data, remote updates and all remaining support. The device's local screen still works; save your data first.

    inserted: Nedap will terminate the MyPowerRouter.com environment as of 31 December 2026.

    Who it affects: Anyone who still monitors a Nedap PowerRouter through MyPowerRouter.com, or who may need firmware updates, remote assistance or a new warranty claim after 2016 sales ended

    Service ends Dec 31 · in 33 daysRead the change →Source ↗ for Nedap PowerRouter
  • TermsVerizonNotableNov 14

    Verizon: Leaving Verizon Dollars forfeits your balance and fee-free upgrades

    Verizon's loyalty terms now spell out the cost of opting out: your Verizon Dollars are forfeited on the spot, and fee-free activations and upgrades end.

    inserted: If you choose to opt out of Verizon Dollars, you agree: All accumulated Verizon Dollars will be immediately forfeited, with the exception of Verizon Dollars earned by Verizon Visa Card holders for eligible Verizon Visa Card spend.

    Who it affects: Verizon postpaid mobile, Home Internet and Fios customers enrolled in Verizon Dollars, Verizon's loyalty program, especially anyone who joined for the waived activation and upgrade fees or the Glance lock-screen rewards and is thinking of leaving

    Read the change →Source ↗ for Verizon
  • TermsZWILLINGNotableNov 14

    ZWILLING: Fresh & Save app shuts Dec 31, 2026; save your data first

    The ZWILLING app, used with Fresh & Save, shuts December 31, 2026. After that you cannot log in. Vacuum boxes still work; save box contents, expiry dates and lists first.

    inserted: we have made the strategic decision to discontinue the ZWILLING app by December 31, 2026.

    Who it affects: Anyone who uses the ZWILLING app to track Fresh & Save boxes, expiry dates, shopping lists or other app-only features

    App shuts down Dec 31 · in 33 daysRead the change →Source ↗ for ZWILLING
  • PricingRunnaNotableNov 14

    Runna: UK prices rise from first renewal on or after Dec 10

    Runna's UK annual plan rises from £99.99 to £109.99, and monthly from £15.99 to £17.99, at the first renewal on or after Dec 10, 2026. The Strava + Runna bundle goes to £124.99. US prices are unchanged.

    New prices start Dec 10 · in 12 daysRead the change →Source ↗ for Runna

Verizon's loyalty-program terms gain a new opt-out section: quitting Verizon Dollars forfeits your unspent Dollars, ends refunds on anything you bought with them, and takes away the waived activation and upgrade fees. Elsewhere, Slack hid its $45 Enterprise+ price behind "Contact sales" again, and the Audible and Mailchimp pricing pages flipped between the versions we've seen before.

Also today: a January 1 cloud cut-off cluster, and a UK Runna price rise

Four services lose cloud or app features at the turn of the year, all verified against official pages on November 14. Hunter ends the HunterSMART cloud-to-cloud link to Google Home and Alexa on January 1, 2027 (the app and remote still work; Matter is only for eligible models). KYMCO shuts the Noodoe app and cloud worldwide the same day; only the speed reading on the dash keeps working as normal. The ZWILLING app used with Fresh & Save shuts December 31, 2026, with no logins after that, so owners should save box contents and lists first; the vacuum boxes still work. Nedap ends MyPowerRouter.com on December 31, 2026, along with online monitoring, energy data, remote updates and remaining support; the unit's local screen still works.

Separately, Runna is raising UK prices from the first renewal on or after December 10, 2026: annual £99.99 to £109.99, monthly £15.99 to £17.99, and the Strava + Runna bundle £119.99 to £124.99, per Runna's own support page. US prices are unchanged.
