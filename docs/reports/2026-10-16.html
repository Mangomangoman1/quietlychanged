Cloudflare's "free during beta" data pipeline now has a price, and Audible runs a $99 Premium year

5 changes · checked October 16, 2026

What changed

  • TermsRobinhoodMajorOct 16

    Robinhood: Auto-enrolls accounts in class-action claims, keeps 20% of payouts

    Robinhood will file class-action claims for you by default and keep 20% of the payout. In those cases you also lose the right to sue. You can unenroll.

    inserted: Your Account is automatically enrolled in Robinhood's Class Action Service (the "Class Action Service"), unless you unenroll as described in this Section 36A.

    Who it affects: Robinhood brokerage customers, especially anyone holding individual stocks that later become part of securities class-action settlements

    Auto-enrollment starts Nov 18 · in 20 daysRead the change →Source ↗ for Robinhood
  • TermsDoorDashNotableOct 16

    DoorDash: Arbitration now covers vendors like Stripe; robot deliveries added

    DoorDash's arbitration clause now covers its vendors too, like Stripe and Persona. All U.S. cases move to JAMS, and robot deliveries are written in.

    inserted: affiliates, subsidiaries, successors, related entities, agents, employees, contractors, subcontractors, insurers, licensees, assignees, vendors, and suppliers

    Who it affects: U.S. DoorDash and Caviar customers. The vendor clause matters if you ever have a claim against a company DoorDash works with, such as Stripe (payments) or Persona (ID checks)

    Read the change →Source ↗ for DoorDash
  • TermsProtonNotableOct 16

    Proton: Terms now let Proton discontinue any service or feature without notice

    Proton can now change, suspend or shut down any service or feature, with or without notice. It says it will try to warn you but isn't obliged to.

    Who it affects: Proton Mail, Drive, VPN, Pass, and other Proton users, especially paid subscribers and anyone who relies on a specific Proton app or feature

    Read the change →Source ↗ for Proton
  • PricingCloudflareMinorOct 16

    Cloudflare: Data pipeline pricing replaces "free during beta"

    Cloudflare Pipelines is no longer free during beta. It now has real prices, with monthly free allowances before per-GB charges start.

    deleted: TBD (free during beta)

    Who it affects: Developers and companies streaming events or logs through Cloudflare Pipelines into R2, especially anyone who set it up while it was free during beta

    Read the change →Source ↗ for Cloudflare
  • PricingAudibleMinorOct 16

    Audible: Limited-time offer: $99 for a year of Premium

    A banner offers a year of Audible Premium for $99, about $8.25 a month. The page doesn't say when the offer ends or what its terms are.

    inserted: LIMITED TIME OFFER | $99 for a year of Premium

    Who it affects: Audible members and anyone considering an annual Audible Premium plan, who should check the offer's terms before renewing or signing up

    Read the change →Source ↗ for Audible

Two real updates today out of nine raw page diffs. Cloudflare's plans page replaced "TBD (free during beta)" for its data pipeline with published prices, now under the new Basin name: SQL transforms cost $0.04 per GB after 50 GB a month, and delivering data costs $0.03 per GB for JSON or $0.06 per GB for Parquet and Iceberg, with new catalog and query charges alongside. Audible's plans page added a limited-time offer of $99 for a year of Premium, against regular annual prices of $149.50 and $229.50. Squarespace and Tinder flipped back to the same page versions we saw on Oct 13, and Grammarly, PayPal, Cash App, Wix, and Mailchimp changes were wording, email obfuscation, formatting, localized pricing, and marketing copy, not real policy or price changes.
