Spotify Premium free trial is back to 1 month (Nov 1, 2026), then $12.99/mo

Spotify's two-month free Premium trial ended on schedule. New subscribers now get one free month, then $12.99 a month.

Took effect Nov 1yesterday

The wording

struck removed underlined added

What changed

Spotify's US Premium page went back from "Try 2 months for $0" to "Try 1 month for $0" on November 1, 2026, the day its two-month offer was set to end. We reported the longer trial on October 20; the page had said "Offer ends November 1, 2026." The new wording reads "$0 for 1 month, then $12.99 per month after. Offer only available if you haven't tried Premium before." The trial still covers Premium Individual only, and the price after it is unchanged at $12.99 a month.

Who it affects

People in the US who have never had Spotify Premium; past or current subscribers didn't qualify for either trial
