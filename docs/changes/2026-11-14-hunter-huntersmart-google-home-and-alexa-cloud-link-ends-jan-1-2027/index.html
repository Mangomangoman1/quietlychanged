HunterSMART Google Home and Alexa cloud link ends January 1, 2027

From Jan 1, 2027, HunterSMART's legacy cloud link to Google Home and Alexa stops. The app and remote still work; Matter is only for eligible models.

Cloud link ends Jan 1, 2027in 35 days

What changed

Hunter, the ceiling-fan maker, is ending the legacy cloud link that connects HunterSMART fans to Google Home and Amazon Alexa. The official announcement, posted November 5, 2026 and fetched November 14, 2026, is clear about the date: "Beginning January 1, 2027, Hunter will discontinue the legacy cloud-based connections that currently enable cloud-to-cloud linking between HunterSMART™ app accounts and Google Home and Amazon Alexa." "After this date, Google Home and Alexa functionality that relies on this legacy cloud connection will no longer work for HunterSMART™ fans, including voice commands, scenes, and automations."

What stays the same

The fan itself does not shut off. Hunter says: "Your fan's core operation will continue as designed, including fan and lighting control through the included remote control, other installed controls, and smart controls via the HunterSMART™ app, as applicable." If you already control the fan from the HunterSMART app or the remote in the box, that path is not what is being discontinued.

Matter is only for eligible models

Hunter is moving eligible HunterSMART products to Matter. "If your fan is eligible for Matter compatibility, you will be able to continue using supported smart functionality within the Google Home and/or Alexa ecosystems after completing the applicable Matter migration and setup." After that, you no longer link your HunterSMART account to Google Home or Alexa. You add the fan directly in those apps using their Matter setup. "Matter-enabled devices do not use the legacy cloud-linking service for smart controls that is being discontinued."

Eligibility is not universal. Hunter's [Matter compatibility page](https://www.hunterfan.com/pages/matter) says the path "will vary depending on your fan model and hardware configuration" and that Matter "may require a firmware update or updated hardware components." Customers who do not complete an available migration "will no longer be able to use certain HunterSMART™ cloud-based smart-home features after the transition," though core operation through the remote or other installed controls continues. After a Matter migration you may need to add the fan as a new device and "recreate scenes, routines, automations, schedules, or other customized settings."

What you can do

Look up your model on the Matter page and, if an upgrade is offered, finish it and re-pair the fan before January 1, 2027. If your fan is not eligible, treat the HunterSMART app and the included remote as the controls that will still work.

Coverage (checked November 14, 2026): [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/11/09/hunter-ceiling-fans-google-home-amazon-alexa-support/) reported the change on November 9, quoting a Hunter customer email that matches the official blog. Android Authority also noted the November 5 announcement.

Who it affects

Anyone who controls a HunterSMART ceiling fan through Google Home or Amazon Alexa by linking their HunterSMART account, especially owners of models that are not eligible for Matter

Quick answers

When do HunterSMART Google Home and Alexa controls stop?

The legacy cloud-to-cloud account link ends January 1, 2027. Voice commands, scenes and automations that depend on that link stop on that date. Hunter's official blog, fetched November 14, 2026, is the source.

Will my Hunter ceiling fan still work?

Yes. Hunter says core operation continues, including the included remote, other installed controls, and the HunterSMART app.

Can I keep using Google Home or Alexa after January 1?

Only if your fan is eligible for Matter and you complete the migration and set the fan up directly in the Google Home or Alexa app. Matter is not offered for every model; check Hunter's Matter compatibility page.
