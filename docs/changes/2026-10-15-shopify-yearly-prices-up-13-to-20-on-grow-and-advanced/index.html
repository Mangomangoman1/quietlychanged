Shopify raised annual prices: Grow now $89/mo, Advanced $359/mo (pricing update)

Paying yearly for Shopify Grow or Advanced now shows $89 or $359 a month instead of $79 or $299. Monthly prices didn't move.

The wording

struck removed underlined added
UpdateUpdate (Oct 19, 2026): on October 19, Shopify's pricing page showed the older $79/mo Grow and $299/mo Advanced yearly prices again, and repeated checks returned both versions (six of ten checks showed $79/$299, four showed $89/$359). The higher yearly prices look like a price test shown to some visitors rather than a completed increase. See our Oct 19 update.

What changed

Shopify's pricing page now shows pay-yearly prices of $89/mo for Grow (was $79) and $359/mo for Advanced (was $299). Pay-monthly prices are unchanged at $105 and $399, and Basic is unchanged at $29 yearly / $39 monthly. That cuts the annual-billing discount from about 25% to about 15% on Grow and about 10% on Advanced, and raises a year of Grow from $948 to $1,068 and a year of Advanced from $3,588 to $4,308.

Who it affects

Shopify merchants on Grow or Advanced who pay yearly or are about to renew or sign up on annual billing
