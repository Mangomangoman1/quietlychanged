Slack adds a Frontline teams add-on for Enterprise+ (Oct 2026)

Slack lists a new "Frontline teams" add-on for Enterprise+, with no price shown.

The wording

struck removed underlined added

What changed

Slack's pricing page now lists a new "Frontline teams" add-on, marked "New." Slack describes it as an "Enterprise+ add-on giving field workers Slack for task management, agentic support & secure Salesforce access." It appears in the feature list of every plan and in the comparison table as an add-on. The page shows no price for it.

Who it affects

Companies on Slack Enterprise+ with field, retail or other frontline staff
