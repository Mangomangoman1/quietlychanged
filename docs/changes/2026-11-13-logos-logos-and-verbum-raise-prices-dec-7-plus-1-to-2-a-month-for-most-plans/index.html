Logos and Verbum price increase Dec 7, 2026: +$1 to $2 a month for most plans

English Logos and Verbum Premium, Pro and Max plans go up Dec 7. Most monthly customers see +$1 to $2; the new everyday list prices are $10.99, $16.49 and $21.99 a month. Check your account for your rate.

New prices start Dec 7in 23 days

What changed

Faithlife is raising prices on English Logos and Verbum subscriptions beginning at 12:00 a.m. Pacific Time on December 7, 2026. The announcement was posted on November 2, 2026, in a [Logos Community thread](https://community.logos.com/forums/topic/239417-subscription-prices-are-changing-december-7-faq/) by Jason Stone of Logos. Matching FAQs are live on [logos.com/subscription-faq](https://www.logos.com/subscription-faq) and [verbum.com/subscription-faq](https://verbum.com/subscription-faq) (all fetched November 13, 2026).

The change covers Logos and Verbum Premium, Pro and Max. It "doesn't affect Logos Plus, Logos for Church, Logos for Education, or Logos for Ministry."

How much it goes up

Logos says most customers will see their current rates increase by these amounts, depending on plan and billing cycle:

| | Monthly | Annually | 2-year | |---|---|---|---| | Premium | +$1.00 | +$10.00 | +$18.99 | | Pro | +$1.50 | +$15.00 | +$28.49 | | Max | +$2.00 | +$20.00 | +$37.99 |

The FAQs also state the new everyday list prices for people who still have an ownership discount: they "keep paying less than the new everyday price of $10.99/month for Premium, $16.49/month for Pro, and $21.99/month for Max." Academic discounts stay at the same percentage (the Verbum FAQ says students still get 30% off and faculty 40% off) on the new base rate. Your exact figure is on your account subscription page.

When you are charged

The FAQs: "If you're on a monthly subscription, the new price starts with your first payment on or after 12 a.m. (PST) December 7, 2026. If you're on an annual or two-year subscription, nothing changes until your next renewal after December 7, 2026." Logos will email you 30 days before the first payment at the new price (monthly users also see it on the prior month's receipt).

A current two-year term is not cut short. When it renews, the FAQ says it "renews for a year at the new annual price," unless you buy another two-year term.

What you can do before December 7

You can switch to annual billing before December 7 and "pay the current annual price for your next 12 months." The FAQ notes that an annual plan "costs about the same as 10 monthly payments," and a two-year plan "about the same as 19 monthly payments."

December 7 is also the last day to start earning a Legacy Fallback License. If you own a Logos or Verbum package and are not yet subscribed, you need to subscribe by then and stay subscribed for 24 months in a row. After December 7, "no one can start earning a Legacy Fallback License."

The FAQs say nothing else about the plan is changing: "You'll get the same features, books, and billing options. Only the price is changing."

Coverage (checked Nov 13, 2026): We are citing Logos's own community announcement and the Logos and Verbum subscription FAQs.

Who it affects

People on English Logos or Verbum Premium, Pro or Max subscriptions, including monthly, annual and two-year plans. Logos Plus, Logos for Church, Logos for Education and Logos for Ministry are not included.

Quick answers

When do Logos and Verbum prices go up?

At 12:00 a.m. Pacific Time on December 7, 2026, for the first renewal on or after that time. Monthly plans are charged the new price on that first payment; annual and two-year plans wait until their next renewal.

How much is the increase?

Logos's November 2 community post says most customers see +$1.00, +$1.50 or +$2.00 a month on Premium, Pro and Max, or +$10, +$15 or +$20 a year. The new everyday list prices in the FAQs are $10.99, $16.49 and $21.99 a month. Check your account page for your rate.

Does this affect Logos for Church or Logos Plus?

No. The community announcement says the change does not affect Logos Plus, Logos for Church, Logos for Education, or Logos for Ministry.

Can I keep today's annual price for another year?

Yes, if you switch to annual (or buy annual or two-year) before December 7, 2026. The FAQ says you will pay the current annual price for the next 12 months.
