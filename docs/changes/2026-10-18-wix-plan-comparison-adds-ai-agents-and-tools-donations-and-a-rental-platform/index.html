Wix plans now list AI agents, donations and fundraising, and a rental platform

Wix's plan comparison adds rows for AI agents and tools, donations, and a rental platform. Prices didn't change.

What changed

Wix's plans page now lists three new rows in its plan feature comparison: "AI agents and tools," "Donations and fundraising" ("Collect donations and run fundraising campaigns on your site"), and "Rental platform" ("List your rentals, set your prices and take reservations online"). Who it affects

Wix site owners and anyone comparing Wix plans, especially nonprofits, rental businesses, and people looking for built-in AI tools
