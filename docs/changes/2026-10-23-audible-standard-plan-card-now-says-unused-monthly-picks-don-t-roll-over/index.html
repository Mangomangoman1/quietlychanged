Audible: Standard plan card now says unused monthly picks don't roll over

October 23, 2026 · Detected by QuietlyChanged's daily check

Audible's membership page now states on the Standard plan card ($8.99/mo after a 30-day trial, 1 audiobook a month): "Unused monthly audiobook selections don't roll over." The same rule was already in the page's FAQ. The $229.50-a-year Premium plan is now labeled "24 audiobooks" (it previously showed a Spanish "12 creditos/año" label). Prices are unchanged: Premium $14.95/mo, Premium Annual $149.50/yr for 12 audiobooks.

Who it affects

People choosing between Audible Standard and Premium
