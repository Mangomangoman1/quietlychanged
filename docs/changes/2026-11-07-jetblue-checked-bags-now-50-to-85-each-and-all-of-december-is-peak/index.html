JetBlue bag fees rise to $50–$85 for bookings from Nov 3, 2026 (December now all peak)

For trips booked from Nov 3, JetBlue's first checked bag costs $50 to $70 and the second $70 to $85. Every day in December now gets the higher peak fees.

Applies to bookings from Nov 37 days ago

What changed

JetBlue raised its checked bag fees for trips booked on or after Nov 3, 2026. We compared the live fees page (fetched Nov 7) with an archived copy from Oct 12, 2026 (Wayback Machine).

Change 1: First and second bags cost more

For Main Base, Main, EvenMore and their Flex versions within the U.S., Latin America, the Caribbean and Canada:

- 1st bag, off-peak: $45 → $50 if you prepay at least 24 hours before departure; $49 → $60 if you pay at check-in. - 1st bag, peak: $49 → $55 prepaid; $59 → $70 at check-in. - 2nd bag, off-peak: $59 → $70 prepaid; $69 → $75 at check-in. - 2nd bag, peak: $69 → $75 prepaid; $79 → $85 at check-in.

The page lists prepaid prices with an "incl $X savings" note. The check-in price is the prepaid price plus that saving. Waiting until check-in now costs more for the first bag: the gap grew from $4 to $10 off-peak and from $10 to $15 peak. For the second bag the gap shrank from $10 to $5 off-peak and stayed at $10 peak. This is why the second-bag check-in price rose by only $6 while the prepaid price rose by $11. A 3rd or later bag is still $200.

The same higher second-bag fees apply to Mosaic 1 members, JetBlue Plus and Premier cardholders, and the new BlueFirst Base and Mint Base fares, even though their first bag is free.

Change 2: December is now all peak

In the Oct 12 copy, peak dates were Nov 19–30 and Dec 17–31, 2026. The live page adds Nov 5–11 and makes all of December peak (12/1–12/31). Peak runs on into Jan 1–18, 2027, and the calendar now reaches October 2027. Here is the combined effect. Say you book a trip now that flies in early-to-mid December, with one prepaid bag each way. Those dates were off-peak, so the bags cost $90 ($45 × 2). Now they cost $110 ($55 × 2). With two bags each way, the cost rises from $208 to $260.

Change 3: Transatlantic bags go up too

For Main Base and EvenMore Base on transatlantic flights, the first bag rises from $75 to $80 off-peak and from $85 to $90 peak. The second bag rises from $109 to $115 off-peak and from $119 to $125 peak, for all fares that pay for one. The pound and euro prices went up by similar amounts.

What stays the same

The refund rule is not new. The Oct 12 copy has the same sentence: "checked bag fees are only refundable if the entire booking is cancelled prior to scheduled departure." You can still pay for bags with TrueBlue points before check-in. Mint, BlueFirst (non-Base), and Mosaic 2–4 members still get two free bags.

Bookings made before Nov 3

The page now says: "For bookings made prior to 11/3, check Manage Trips for applicable baggage fees." The Points Guy reports that the previous fees apply to flights bought before Nov 3. JetBlue's page does not say whether those bookings use the old peak calendar, so check Manage Trips for your own trip.

What JetBlue says

The Points Guy reported that JetBlue pointed to rising fuel and operating costs. A spokesperson said: "This helps us keep our base fares competitive while giving customers the choice to pay for a checked bag only if they need one."

A number we couldn't match

The Points Guy says the off-peak prepaid first bag was "up from $39" and calls $50 "a 28% jump". Our archived Oct 12 copy of JetBlue's own page shows $45 (incl $4 savings). TPG's figures for paying at check-in ($49 before, $60 after) match the page. We use JetBlue's archived page for the before prices. Measured from $45, the prepaid first-bag fee rose 11%.

Coverage (checked Nov 7, 2026): The Points Guy (Nov 3) reported the increase as JetBlue's second bag-fee hike of 2026. We could not load AP's article (it was blocked by a bot check).

Who it affects

Anyone booking a JetBlue flight on or after Nov 3, 2026 who checks a bag without free-bag status: Main Base, Main and EvenMore fares, plus second bags for Mosaic 1 members and JetBlue cardholders

Quick answers

How much is a checked bag on JetBlue now?

For bookings made on or after Nov 3, 2026, on flights within the U.S., Latin America, the Caribbean and Canada: the first bag is $50 off-peak or $55 peak if you prepay at least 24 hours before departure, and $60 or $70 if you pay later. The second bag is $70 or $75 prepaid, and $75 or $85 later. A third or later bag is $200.

Is December peak season for JetBlue bag fees?

Yes. All of December 2026 (12/1–12/31) is now peak, along with Nov 5–11, Nov 19–30 and Jan 1–18, 2027. Before this change, December peak started on Dec 17.

Can I get a refund on a JetBlue bag fee?

Only if the whole booking is cancelled before the scheduled departure. That rule was already on the page before this change.

I booked before Nov 3. Do I pay the new fees?

JetBlue's page says to check Manage Trips for the fees on bookings made before 11/3. The Points Guy reports that the previous fees apply to those trips.

What you can do

  • Add bags in Manage Trips at least 24 hours before departure: paying at check-in costs $10–$15 more for the first bag and $5–$10 more for the second
  • Already booked before Nov 3? Check Manage Trips for your bag fees. JetBlue says those bookings keep the fees that applied when you booked
  • Before you prepay, check the peak calendar on jetblue.com/legal/fees: Nov 5–11, Nov 19–30 and all of December 2026 are peak
  • Mosaic 2–4, Mint/BlueFirst (non-Base), and JetBlue credit cards still get a free first bag; Mosaic 1 and cardmembers still pay the new second-bag fees
