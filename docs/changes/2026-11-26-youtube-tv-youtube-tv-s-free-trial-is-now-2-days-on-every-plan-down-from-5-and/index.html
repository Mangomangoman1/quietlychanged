YouTube TV free trial now 2 days (was 5, and 10 on Nov 20)

YouTube TV's plans page now shows a "2-day free trial" on every plan, down from 5 days yesterday and 10 days on Nov 20. Your first charge comes days sooner.

The wording

struck removed underlined added

What changed

YouTube TV's [explore-plans page](https://tv.youtube.com/learn/explore-plans/) now shows "2-day free trial" on every plan card (fetched November 26, 2026). On November 22 to 25 the same cards said "5-day free trial", except the Sports plan, which already showed "2-day free trial". When we captured the page for our [YouTube TV plans and prices guide](https://quietlychanged.com/guides/youtube-tv-plans-and-prices-2026-every-price-increase/) on November 20, most plans showed a "10-day free trial". So in under a week the trial on most plans went from 10 days to 2.

What stays the same: prices and the limited-time offers for new users are unchanged. The main plan still lists $82.99/mo, with the promo at $59.99/mo, and Sports + News $71.99/mo with a $49.99/mo promo ("Save $132 total on your first 6 months"). The fine print still says "After promotional period, you will be charged the then current rate per month" and "Offer ends December 2, 2026." YouTube TV doesn't say on the page why the trial changed or whether it's temporary.

Who it affects

People thinking of trying YouTube TV, including its main plan and genre plans; current members aren't affected

Quick answers

How long is the YouTube TV free trial?

As of November 26, 2026, YouTube TV's plans page shows a "2-day free trial" on every plan. It showed 5 days on November 22 to 25 and 10 days on most plans on November 20.

Did YouTube TV prices change?

No. The plans page still lists the main plan at $82.99/mo and the new-user offers that end December 2, 2026.
