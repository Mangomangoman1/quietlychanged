Todoist: Todoist adds Comms team chat (beta), with 1 month of message history on Beginner and Pro

Todoist's plan table adds Todoist Comms (beta) on every plan. Beginner and Pro keep 1 month of message history; Business keeps it all. Free and Pro teams are capped at 5 guests.

The wording

struck removed underlined added

What changed

Todoist reworked its plan comparison table again (we covered the Ramble cap and Automations on November 19). A new "Todoist Comms" section, marked Beta, lists Todoist Comms and a "Todoist Comms MCP server" as available on Beginner, Pro and Business, with "Message history" of "1 month" on Beginner and Pro and "Full" on Business. The Business card's "A shared team workspace" line is now "Unlimited message history in Todoist Comms".

Other changes: the separate "Team members" and "Team guests" rows (1,000 each, Business only) became one "People per team" row: "1,000, max 5 guests" on Beginner and Pro and "1,000, guests included" on Business. The "People per team project" row (250 on Business) was removed. "Due dates & times" is now "Dates & times", and a new "Task deadlines" row shows deadlines aren't available on Beginner. Board, calendar and table layouts are now described as "In label, filter, and personal project views" on Pro and "In team projects, too" on Business. What stays the same: Pro at $60 billed yearly and Business at $96 per user billed yearly ($10 monthly).

Who it affects

Todoist users who work in teams, on the free Beginner, Pro and Business plans
