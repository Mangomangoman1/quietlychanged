Slack: Slack swaps 'Unlimited Slackbot' for 'no weekly message limits, subject to our usage guidelines' on Enterprise+

Slack's Enterprise+ plan no longer advertises 'Unlimited Slackbot'. It now promises no weekly message limits, subject to usage guidelines and 'advanced feature limits'.

The wording

struck removed underlined added

What changed

On Slack's pricing page, the Enterprise+ feature list changed from "Unlimited Slackbot ... It’s unlimited for your whole team" to "Slackbot with no weekly message limits": "No weekly message limits for your whole team, subject to our usage guidelines. Inclusion of advanced capabilities may be subject to advanced feature limits." In the comparison table, the "Usage limits" row that showed "Consumption based" for two plans and "Unlimited" for Enterprise+ was removed.

The Slackbot description for other plans still says "Limits vary by plan. Contact sales to add more." The Enterprise+ price shown flipped back from "Contact sales for pricing" to "$45 USD per user / month, when paying annually", a rotation we've seen before.

Who it affects

Slack Enterprise+ customers and teams comparing Slack plans for its AI assistant
