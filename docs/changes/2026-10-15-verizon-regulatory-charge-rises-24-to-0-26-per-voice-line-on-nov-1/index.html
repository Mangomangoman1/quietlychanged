Pricing Verizon

Verizon price increase 2026: fees rise to $4.95 a line (Regulatory Charge Nov 1, admin charge $4.69 Nov 15)

October 15, 2026 · Detected by QuietlyChanged's daily check

Verizon's surcharges support page now states: "Effective November 1, 2026, the monthly Verizon Wireless Regulatory Charge for voice-capable devices will increase from $0.21 to $0.26 per line." That is a 24% jump on one of Verizon's own mobile surcharges (not a government tax). Combined with the unchanged Administrative and Telco Recovery Charge ($3.78 per voice line), Verizon's two flat per-line surcharge lines go from $3.99 to $4.04. Separately, Verizon disclosures show the Federal Universal Service Charge using a 42% factor, matching the FCC's Q4 2026 contribution factor (DA-26-946), the program's highest on record after 38.8% in Q3.

UpdateOctober 23, 2026: Verizon's Important Plan Information page (figures as of October 1, 2026) now also lists a bigger increase: the Administrative and Telco Recovery Charge goes from $3.78 to $4.69 per voice line on November 15, 2026 (+$0.91). With the Regulatory Charge change, Verizon's two flat per-line surcharges go from $3.99 to $4.04 on November 1 and to $4.95 on November 15. Data-only lines stay at $3.97 plus $0.02. The myPlan 3-year price lock excludes surcharges, so it doesn't stop either increase. How it compares (each carrier's own page, checked October 23, 2026): T-Mobile charges a Regulatory Programs & Telco Recovery Fee of "$5.49 per line every month" on voice lines (plans that bill fees separately), and AT&T's Mobility fee schedule lists its Administrative & Regulatory Cost Recovery Fee at "$4.99 per line per month." After November 15, Verizon's $4.95 sits between the two. On a four-line Verizon account the two flat charges go from $15.96 to $19.80 a month, $3.84 more, or about $46 a year, before universal service charges and taxes.

Who it affects

Verizon postpaid wireless customers with voice-capable lines; federal USF percentage-based charges affect wireless bills industry-wide
