Notion Free plan storage limit 512 MB (Nov 2026): what counts and what happens

Notion's pricing page now lists total file storage per workspace: 512 MB on Free. Notion's help center says new uploads pause once a Free workspace reaches 512 MB.

The wording

struck removed underlined added

What changed

Notion's [pricing page](https://www.notion.com/pricing) has a new row in its plan comparison: "Total file storage space used by your workspace including blocks, files, images and videos." File storage is listed as 512 MB on Free, 256 GB "+ 5 GB / added seat" on Plus, 512 GB "+ 10 GB / added seat" on Business and Custom on Enterprise. The Free plan's feature list now starts with "Limited storage". The old content row, "Pages & blocks: Limited for 2+ members", is replaced by "Block storage: Limited" (fetched November 25, 2026; the November 24 page had no storage row).

What Notion's help center says (checked Nov 25)

Notion's help article [File storage limits in Notion](https://www.notion.com/help/file-storage-limits-in-notion) gives the same numbers and adds Enterprise: "1 TB, plus 20 GB for each member you add". It says the limit "covers all the files in your workspace added together" and that "The 5 MB limit on each single file is separate, and it applies on the Free plan." It also says: "If you created your Notion workspace after November 23, 2026, free plan limits may apply." Notion doesn't say on the page when or whether older Free workspaces are moved to the limit.

What happens at the limit: "When you reach 80% of your storage, you'll see a warning banner and the storage meter, and workspace owners and admins get an email." Then: "If you reach 512 MB, new uploads pause at the next daily check." Notion says "only new uploads stop. Everything you've already uploaded keeps working." To upload again you either move to a paid plan ("Uploads turn back on right away") or delete files and wait for the next daily check. "There's no single screen that lists your files", so you delete each file on the page where it lives.

What counts: files, images, PDFs, video and audio blocks, Files & media database properties, Web Clipper pages, imports and "Images Notion AI makes and saves to a page." What doesn't: "Your pages, blocks, and databases", replaced files, old versions in page history, "Anything in Trash, or on pages you've deleted or archived" and files shared with Notion AI as context.

Other changes: the Plus and Enterprise plans now carry an asterisk: "* Plans may not be available for every workspace." Business now lists "1,500 Standard credits / month" and "500 Select credits / month" per seat for Notion AI (Standard credits are for "work where Notion picks the model, like Notion Agent on Auto, AI Meeting Notes, and Database Autofill"; Select credits for "work where you pick the model, plus Custom Agents you own"). The line pricing Custom Agents at "$10 per 1,000 monthly Notion credits" after a free trial is gone; Free and Plus still show AI as "Limited Trial". The pricing page also drops the "Research mode" row and adds "Advanced MCP tools" on Enterprise.

What stays the same: Plus is still $10 and Business $20 per seat a month on this page, and the Free plan's 5 MB per-file upload limit is unchanged.

Who it affects

People and teams on Notion's Free plan who upload images, PDFs, videos or other files (especially workspaces created after November 23, 2026), plus Plus and Business workspaces with lots of files

Quick answers

How much storage does Notion's Free plan have?

Notion's pricing page lists 512 MB of total file storage for a Free workspace, shared by everyone in it. Single files are also capped at 5 MB each on Free.

What happens when a Free Notion workspace reaches 512 MB?

Notion's help center says "new uploads pause at the next daily check." Files you've already uploaded keep working; to upload again you upgrade or delete files and wait for the next daily check.

Do pages and text count toward Notion's storage limit?

No. Notion says "Your pages, blocks, and databases" don't count; uploaded files, images, PDFs, video, audio, imports and Web Clipper pages do.

What you can do

  • Check your workspace's usage in Notion under Settings → Usage (Notion says it updates once a day)
  • If you're on Free and near 512 MB, delete files you don't need or export them before uploads pause; deleted files only free space at the next daily check
  • Know what counts: Notion says uploaded files, images, PDFs, video and audio, Web Clipper pages and imports count, while pages, blocks, databases and Trash don't
