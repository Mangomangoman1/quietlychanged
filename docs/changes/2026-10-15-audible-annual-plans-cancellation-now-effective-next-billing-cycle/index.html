Audible: Annual plans: cancellation now effective next billing cycle

Audible's annual Premium plans now say cancellation takes effect at the next billing cycle, not right away.

The wording

struck removed underlined added
UpdateCorrection (Oct 15, 2026): a later check of the live Audible plans page still showed "Cancel anytime" and the "Save 16%" labels on annual plans. We could not confirm that those phrases were removed or that cancellation wording changed to "effective next billing cycle."

What changed

Audible's plan page now says its annual Premium plans ($149.50/yr for 12 credits, $229.50/yr for 24 credits) can be canceled anytime "effective next billing cycle," where it previously just said "Cancel anytime." The "Save 16%" labels on the annual plans were also removed. Prices did not change.

Who it affects

Audible members on, or considering, annual Premium plans

Source

Detected on the Audible pricing page: https://www.audible.com/ep/memberbenefits. Always check the source; this summary is not legal or financial advice.
