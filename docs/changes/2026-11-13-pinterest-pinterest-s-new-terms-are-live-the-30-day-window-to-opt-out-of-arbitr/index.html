Pinterest terms took effect Nov 12, 2026: how to opt out of arbitration within 30 days

Pinterest's rewritten terms and privacy policy took effect Nov 12. You have 30 days to opt out of its new arbitration clause by emailing litigation@pinterest.com.

Took effect Nov 12yesterday

What changed

Pinterest's rewritten Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, first posted as previews around September 1 and covered here on October 20, are now the live versions. Our Nov 13 snapshot of policy.pinterest.com shows the "We're updating our Terms of Service, effective <DATE>" banner gone and the new Section 11 in its place. Pinterest's help page says "changes are effective November 12, 2026" and "By continuing to use Pinterest after November 12, 2026, you accept the updates to the Terms."

Change 1: The new arbitration section is in force

For everyone outside the EEA, Switzerland and the UK, disputes now go to "binding, individual arbitration administered by JAMS" instead of the American Arbitration Association. The section "applies to any Claim you have not filed in court or in arbitration before the date you agreed to these Terms, and supersedes and amends any prior arbitration agreement between you and Pinterest, including as to Claims that arose before this or any prior arbitration agreement."

Mass claims are handled differently. The old terms sent 25 or more similar demands to AAA batches with "one arbitrator to resolve each batch." The live text says that if 25 or more claimants file similar claims within 90 days or through coordinated lawyers, "all of those Claims will be consolidated into one arbitration before one arbitrator," and the arbitrator may split fees with a claimant's lawyers or funders.

Change 2: The 30-day opt-out clock has started

The live Section 11 says: "You may opt out of this Section 11 within 30 days of first becoming subject to it by emailing litigation@pinterest.com with your name, account email, and a statement that you're opting out; otherwise you agree to be bound by it." The old terms had no opt-out. For existing users the new terms took effect November 12, so by our count the window runs to December 12, 2026. Pinterest doesn't print that end date.

Change 3: The privacy policy now says more about AI and message scanning

The live Privacy Policy says Pinterest uses information "(such as Pins, regardless of when they were posted) to train, develop and improve our technology such as our machine learning models and our generative artificial intelligence models." It adds "voice transcripts" to the activity used "to make inferences about you and your preferences," and says Pinterest "may also use, or work with vendors who use automated tools to scan and analyze your messages to detect harmful or unlawful content." It also says connected TVs and other devices "that use our Services" generate log data, and that "Your connected TV may allow you to manage or limit the use of data for advertising."

One difference from our October 20 write-up: two sentences we quoted from the September preview, about seeing ads "when you're streaming a show on your connected TV" and "associating devices that may relate to the same person or household," do not appear in the privacy policy text we captured on November 13. We can't tell from our snapshot whether Pinterest dropped them or moved them to another page.

Other changes: the terms add Pinterest's "GenAI Acceptable Use Guidelines" to the policies you agree to; users outside the EEA, Switzerland and the UK are told their "acceptance of these Terms constitutes your consent to the processing activities described in our Privacy Policy"; and watching YouTube content on Pinterest binds you to YouTube's Terms of Service.

What you can do: to keep your right to sue in court, email litigation@pinterest.com with your name, your account email and a statement that you're opting out of Section 11. Keep the sent copy.

Who it affects

Pinterest users in the U.S. and everywhere outside the EEA, Switzerland and the UK (the arbitration section doesn't apply to consumers there), and anyone who sends messages on Pinterest or posts Pins

Quick answers

When did Pinterest's new terms take effect?

November 12, 2026, according to Pinterest's help page. Our snapshot of November 13 shows the new Section 11 live on policy.pinterest.com.

How do I opt out of Pinterest arbitration?

Email litigation@pinterest.com with your name, account email and a statement that you're opting out. Section 11 allows this "within 30 days of first becoming subject to it", which for existing users means by December 12, 2026.
