Dropbox Black Friday 2026: 3 free months on yearly plans (ends Dec 1)

Dropbox gives new yearly subscribers 15 months for the price of 12 until Dec 1: Plus shows as $7.99 a month instead of $9.99. Then it renews yearly at the regular annual price.

Offer ends Dec 1in 4 days

The wording

struck removed underlined added

What changed

Dropbox's [plans page](https://www.dropbox.com/plans) now shows "Get 3 free months. Offer ends December 1." on its yearly plans (fetched November 25, 2026). The displayed monthly prices drop because of how the deal is counted: "The displayed monthly price for each plan is its annual purchase price divided by 15 months, which includes the 3 free months offered through this promotion."

The prices

Plus (1 person, 2 TB) shows "$9.99" struck through and "$7.99 / month". Standard shows $15 and "$12 / user / month", and Advanced $24 and "$19.20 / user / month". Our math: you pay the regular annual price once (for Plus, 12 × $9.99 = $119.88) and get 15 months, a saving of $29.97 against paying the yearly rate for those 3 extra months. Standard saves $45 per user and Advanced $72 per user on the same basis.

The fine print

"After the first 15 months, your subscription renews every 12 months at the annual renewal price shown at checkout until you cancel. Offer valid for new subscriptions only." Dropbox doesn't state a time of day for the December 1 end date on the plans page.

What stays the same: storage, restore windows (30 days on Plus, 180 days on Standard, 1 year on Advanced) and transfer limits are unchanged; the rest of the diff is layout ("30 days to restore deleted files" now on one line).

Who it affects

People and teams thinking about a new yearly Dropbox Plus, Standard or Advanced subscription; existing subscriptions don't qualify

Quick answers

What is Dropbox's Black Friday 2026 deal?

3 free months on new yearly plans until December 1: you pay the annual price and get 15 months. Plus works out to $7.99 a month instead of $9.99.

What does Dropbox charge after the free months?

Dropbox says that "After the first 15 months, your subscription renews every 12 months at the annual renewal price shown at checkout until you cancel."
