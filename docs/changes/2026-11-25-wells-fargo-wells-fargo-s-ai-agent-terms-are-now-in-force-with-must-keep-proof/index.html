Wells Fargo Online Access Agreement in effect Nov 24, 2026: AI agents section live

Wells Fargo's Online Access Agreement with the AI-agent section took effect Nov 24. One change from the preview: you now "should" keep proof you revoked an agent, not "must".

Took effect Nov 24yesterday

The wording

struck removed underlined added

What changed

The Wells Fargo [Online Access Agreement](https://www.wellsfargo.com/online-banking/online-access-agreement/) marked "Version Effective November 24, 2026" is now the live version. Our November 24 snapshot still showed the May 21, 2026 version with an "Upcoming" link; by November 25 the main page carries the new text, including Section 15, "Third-Party Artificial Intelligence Agents or Agentic AI". We [covered that section on November 6](../2026-11-06-wells-fargo-wells-fargo-s-nov-24-online-access-agreement-ai-agents-act-as-you-a/) from the preview.

One change from the preview

We compared the live text (fetched November 25) with the preview we saved on November 6. Apart from page navigation, one sentence differs. The preview said: "You must retain evidence of such revocation and must provide such evidence to us, at our request." The live version says: "You should retain evidence of such revocation and provide such evidence to us, at our request." Keeping proof is now advice rather than a requirement, but the next sentence is unchanged: "Until you notify us of such revocation pursuant to Section 17(d), you remain responsible for all the AI Agent’s activities, to the extent permitted by law or regulation."

What is now in force

The agreement's summary lists "Your responsibilities for artificial intelligence agents, if and when we permit the use of such agents." Section 15 defines an AI Agent as technology that "takes autonomous, semi-autonomous, or non-autonomous action on behalf of any person or entity" and says "Any instructions, actions, or transactions initiated by the AI Agent are deemed to have been initiated and authorized directly by you, without further verification, and we have no obligation to investigate the validity or scope of such authority". Delegates are covered too: "Unless otherwise provided by law or regulation, an Authorized Representative or Delegate's transactions (including the transactions of their AI Agents) are considered authorized", and "You are solely responsible for supervising all of your Authorized Representatives and Delegates (including their AI Agents)".

Other changes: sections are renumbered (the security section is now 17 and privacy 18); Bill Pay now says Wells Fargo "may decline to make the payment" from a credit line without enough available credit (it said "we don't have to make a payment"); the privacy section now says marketing use is "in accordance with your privacy preferences"; "Private Wealth experience" is renamed "Private Wealth offering".

Who it affects

Wells Fargo online and mobile banking customers, especially anyone who lets an AI assistant, budgeting app, script or Delegate act in their accounts

Quick answers

Is Wells Fargo's AI agent section in effect?

Yes. The Online Access Agreement marked "Version Effective November 24, 2026", including Section 15 on AI agents, is the live version on wellsfargo.com as of November 25, 2026.

Do I have to keep proof that I revoked an AI agent's access to Wells Fargo?

The final text says you "should retain evidence of such revocation and provide such evidence to us, at our request" (the preview said "must"). You stay responsible for the agent's activity until you notify Wells Fargo under Section 17(d).
