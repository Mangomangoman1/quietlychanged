Spotify: Spotify Premium free trial is now 3 months for new users, until December 31

New Spotify Premium Individual subscribers get 3 months for $0, then $12.99 a month. The offer ends December 31, 2026.

Offer ends Dec 31in 34 days

The wording

struck removed underlined added

What changed

Spotify's US Premium page changed its new-subscriber offer from "Try 1 month for $0" to "Try 3 months for $0": "Premium Individual only. $0 for 3 months, then $12.99 per month after. Offer only available if you haven't tried Premium before." A new line says the offer ends; the live page (checked November 19) reads "Offer ends December 31, 2026." The price after the trial, $12.99 a month, is unchanged. The plan comparison card further down still shows "$0 for 1 month" in one spot. In October Spotify ran a 2-month offer that ended November 1.

Who it affects

People in the US who have never tried Spotify Premium

Source

Detected on the Spotify pricing page: https://www.spotify.com/us/premium/. Always check the source; this summary is not legal or financial advice.
