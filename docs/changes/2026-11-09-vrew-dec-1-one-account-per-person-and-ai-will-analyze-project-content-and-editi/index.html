Vrew Dec 1, 2026: one account per person and AI analysis of project content

From Dec 1, Vrew allows only one account per person (no team sharing on one login) and will use AI to analyze project content, assets and editing history for personalization, kept until the account is deleted. Object via chat support by Dec 1.

Takes effect Dec 1in 20 days

The wording

struck removed underlined added

What changed

Vrew published a help article announcing updates to its Terms of Service and Privacy Policy effective December 1, 2026. The quotes below are from that notice's own current/revised comparison tables and retention table (fetched Nov 9, 2026). We are treating the vendor notice as the primary source for this due-soon item; we did not re-fetch every linked revised PDF beyond the notice.

Change 1: One account per person, not per email

Vrew's notice says that starting December 1, "you may use only one account, even if you have different email addresses." Its table moves the rule from "The Service supports only one account per email address" (Article 5, Paragraph 1) to "Only one account is permitted per User for the Service" (Article 6, Paragraph 1).

It also says multiple people may not share one account, "including within companies and teams," and asks users to prepare separate accounts before December 1. Separately, Vrew says it is working on a company/team way to manage multiple accounts and will share details later.

Change 2: AI analysis of project content and editing history

The Privacy Policy table replaces a footnote that said the company does not use project files for training and does not arbitrarily access or analyze project content without consent. The revised footnote says the company "analyzes the contents and assets of projects and the user's editing history using artificial intelligence" to provide a personalized editing experience, and "does not use the user's information for training artificial intelligence other than for this purpose."

A new processing row lists "Project categories, summaries, and assets; your editing history" for "Provision of personalized services," retained "Until you delete your account." The notice says analysis covers images, videos, audio and captions (assets), turned into topic categories and content summaries.

What you can do

Object through in-app chat support by December 1, or delete the account. Vrew says that if you do not object by the effective date, it will treat that as agreement.

Coverage (checked Nov 9, 2026): We found no press coverage of the December 1 Vrew Terms/Privacy update beyond Vrew's own help article.

Who it affects

Vrew users who share a paid login across a team, or who keep project footage, captions and edit history they do not want AI-analyzed

Quick answers

Can my team keep sharing one Vrew account after December 1, 2026?

No, under the revised Terms described in Vrew's notice. It says multiple people may not share one account, including inside companies and teams, and asks each person to use their own account before December 1.

Will Vrew train generative AI on my project files?

Vrew's revised privacy footnote says it analyzes project contents, assets and editing history with AI for a personalized editing experience, and "does not use the user's information for training artificial intelligence other than for this purpose." That is narrower than open-ended training, but it is still AI analysis of your project material.

How long does Vrew keep the AI analysis?

The notice's retention table says project categories, summaries, assets and editing history for personalized services are kept until you delete your account.

How do I object?

Vrew says: in the app, Help → Support → Chat Support, by December 1, 2026. You can also delete the account. If you do not object by the effective date, Vrew says it will consider you to have agreed.
