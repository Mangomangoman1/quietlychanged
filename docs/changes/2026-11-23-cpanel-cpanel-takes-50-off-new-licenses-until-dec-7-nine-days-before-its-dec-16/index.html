cPanel Black Friday 2026: 50% off new licenses until Dec 7 (Solo $13.73/mo annual)

cPanel's Black Friday sale halves new license prices until December 7: Solo is $13.73 a month billed annually, down from $27.46. Prices rise for new orders and renewals on December 16.

Offer ends Dec 7in 14 days

The wording

struck removed underlined added

What changed

cPanel's [pricing page](https://www.cpanel.net/pricing/) now carries a Black Friday banner: "Black Friday: 50% off new licenses. Till December 7." (fetched November 23, 2026; the page's product data also lists the prices as valid until 2026-12-07). The Solo, Admin and Pro cards swapped their "Save 8.4% with annual billing" badge for "50% OFF", and the annual prices were halved. Premier and WP Squared, which are sold monthly, show "50% OFF" too.

The sale prices (cPanel Store, excluding VAT)

| Plan | Accounts | Before (Nov 22) | Sale (Nov 23) | |---|---|---|---| | Solo, billed annually | 1 | $27.46/mo | $13.73/mo | | Admin, billed annually | Up to 5 | $32.96/mo | $16.48/mo | | Pro, billed annually | Up to 30 | $49.46/mo | $24.73/mo | | Premier, billed monthly | Up to 100 | $69.99/mo | $34.99/mo | | WP Squared, billed monthly | 10 WordPress sites | $84.99/mo | $42.49/mo |

Our math: Solo billed annually works out to $164.76 for the year at the sale price, against $329.52 at the regular annual rate.

What it replaces: The same page still says "effective December 16, 2026, cPanel license pricing will be adjusted, affecting new orders and all renewals on or after that date." We covered that increase on November 13 (Solo goes from $29.99 to $32.99 a month billed monthly). The sale ends nine days before the new prices start.

What stays the same: The plan line-up, account limits and features are unchanged. The banner speaks of new licenses only; it does not say whether the discount repeats at renewal, so check the cart. Prices from hosting partners may differ from the cPanel Store.

Who it affects

Anyone buying a new cPanel Solo, Admin, Pro, Premier or WP Squared license from the cPanel Store, especially those weighing a purchase before the December 16, 2026 price increase
