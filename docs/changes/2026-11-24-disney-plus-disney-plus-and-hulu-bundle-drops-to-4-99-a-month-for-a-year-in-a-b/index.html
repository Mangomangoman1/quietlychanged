Disney+ Black Friday 2026: Disney+, Hulu Bundle $4.99/mo for a year (ends Nov 30)

Disney+'s Black Friday offer cuts the ad-supported Disney+, Hulu Bundle from $12.99 to $4.99 a month for 12 months (Premium: $14.99, from $21.99). New and eligible returning subscribers only; ends Nov 30.

Offer ends Nov 30in 2 days

The wording

struck removed underlined added

What changed

Disney+'s [home page](https://www.disneyplus.com/) now leads with a Black Friday offer: the Disney+, Hulu Bundle "is $12.99/mo $4.99/mo for a year this Black Friday. Offer ends November 30", and the Disney+, Hulu Bundle Premium (no ads) is $14.99 a month for a year, down from $21.99 (fetched November 24, 2026). The plan table's "Black Friday Offer" badges show "Save 61%" and "Save 31%".

The fine print (Disney+ offer terms, fetched Nov 24)

Disney's terms say: "Disney+, Hulu Bundle: $4.99/mo. for 12 mos., then auto-renews at $12.99/mo. or then-current regular monthly price. Disney+, Hulu Bundle Premium: $14.99/mo. for 12 mos., then auto-renews at $21.99/mo. or then-current regular monthly price. Ends 11:59 PM PST on 11/30/26. U.S. only, 18+. New and eligible returning subscribers only." They add: "Valid for new and eligible returning subscribers; Disney+, ESPN, Hulu, and bundle subscribers are not eligible." and "Any plan switch after redemption of this offer will result in forfeiture of the discount pricing." The offer is "not redeemable via gift card" and can't be combined with a free trial or other promotions.

Our math: 12 months at $4.99 is $59.88, against $155.88 at the regular $12.99, a saving of $96. Premium: $179.88 for the year against $263.88, a saving of $84.

What it replaces: Until November 23 the same page offered the bundle at "$11.99 / mo for 6 months" (Premium "$19.99 / mo for 6 months"), with a "Cost for 6 months: $71.94, then renews to monthly plan at $12.99/month" footnote, and a student offer of the bundle for "$6.49/mo". Both are no longer shown on the home page; the page doesn't say whether they return after November 30.

What stays the same: The regular prices ($12.99 with ads, $21.99 Premium) are unchanged, as are the ESPN Unlimited bundles ($35.99 and $44.99 a month) and the Disney+, Hulu, HBO Max bundles ($21.99 with ads, $34.99 no ads).

Who it affects

People in the U.S. who don't currently subscribe to Disney+, Hulu, ESPN or a bundle and are thinking about the Disney+, Hulu Bundle; current subscribers are excluded

Quick answers

Can current Disney+ or Hulu subscribers get the $4.99 Black Friday deal?

No. Disney's offer terms say "Disney+, ESPN, Hulu, and bundle subscribers are not eligible." It is for new and eligible returning subscribers in the U.S., 18+.

What does the Disney+, Hulu Bundle cost after the Black Friday year?

It "auto-renews at $12.99/mo. or then-current regular monthly price". Premium renews at $21.99 or the then-current price.

When does the Disney+ Black Friday deal end?

11:59 PM PST on November 30, 2026, per the offer terms.
