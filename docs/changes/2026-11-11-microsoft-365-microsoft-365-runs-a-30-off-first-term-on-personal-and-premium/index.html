Microsoft 365: Microsoft 365 runs a 30%-off first term on Personal and Premium

Microsoft is offering 30% off the first year or first month of Microsoft 365 Personal ($69.99 instead of $99.99 a year) and Premium ($139.99 instead of $199.99). Both renew at full price.

The wording

struck removed underlined added

What changed

Microsoft's compare-plans page now leads with a "Limited-time offer": "Save 30% and get more from Microsoft 365." Microsoft 365 Personal shows $6.99 for the first month (regularly $9.99) or $69.99 for the first year (regularly $99.99). Microsoft 365 Premium shows $13.99 for the first month (regularly $19.99) or $139.99 for the first year (regularly $199.99). Family is not part of the offer and stays at $12.99 a month or $129.99 a year.

The fine print now says: "Limited-time offer and 1 month trial cannot be combined. Subscription automatically renews at the regular price unless cancelled in Microsoft account." The page no longer shows the "Save up to 17% when you pay yearly" banner. It gives no end date for the offer.

Who it affects

People buying a new Microsoft 365 Personal or Premium subscription
