Notion Free plan block limit: pricing FAQ no longer promises unlimited blocks for individuals (Nov 2026)

Notion's FAQ used to say a solo Free workspace gets unlimited pages and blocks. Now it says any Free workspace gets a limited number, though the plan table still says 'Limited for 2+ members'.

The wording

struck removed underlined added

What changed

Between our November 16 and November 19 snapshots, Notion rewrote the FAQ answer "What happens when I go over the block storage limit on a Free Plan?" It used to give two answers: "If you’re using a Free Plan workspace by yourself, you’ll be able to add as many pages and blocks as you like", and with multiple members "a limited number of blocks before you have to upgrade." Now there's one: "If you’re using a Free Plan workspace, you’ll be able to add a limited number of blocks before you have to upgrade." The next line is unchanged: at the limit "you can still read, edit, and organize existing content blocks as usual, but you won’t be able to add new content blocks."

The page isn't consistent yet. The plan comparison table still lists "Pages & blocks" on Free as "Limited for 2+ members", and the tooltip still says teams "can use the Free Plan with up to certain number of blocks before upgrading." Notion doesn't give a block number on the page, and we haven't seen an announcement, so it's not clear whether solo accounts are actually being limited or the FAQ was shortened.

Other changes: the cancellation answer now says that if you subscribed through the Apple App Store or Google Play, you cancel through that store, and that "You'll keep access to your paid features until the end of your current billing period."

Who it affects

People using Notion's Free plan on their own
