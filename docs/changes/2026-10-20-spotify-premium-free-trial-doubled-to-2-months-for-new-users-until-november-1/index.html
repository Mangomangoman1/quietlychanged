Pricing Spotify

Spotify Premium free trial is 2 months until Nov 1, 2026, then $12.99/mo

October 20, 2026 · Detected by QuietlyChanged's daily check

Spotify's US Premium page changed its free trial from "Try 1 month for $0" to "Try 2 months for $0" on October 20, 2026. The offer covers Premium Individual only and is "only available if you haven't tried Premium before." After the two free months it costs $12.99 per month. The page now adds "Offer ends November 1, 2026."

Who it affects

People in the US who have never had Spotify Premium; past or current subscribers don't qualify
