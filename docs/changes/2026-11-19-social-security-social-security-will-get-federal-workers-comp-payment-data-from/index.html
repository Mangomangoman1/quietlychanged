Social Security: Social Security will get federal workers' comp payment data from Labor every 28 days to offset disability benefits

A new SSA–Labor matching program sends FECA workers' comp payment data to SSA every 28 days to reduce disability benefits. Comments close Dec 21.

Comments close Dec 21in 31 days

The wording

struck removed underlined added

What changed

The Social Security Administration published a notice in the November 19, 2026 Federal Register (FR Doc. 2026-23591, docket SSA-2026-0100) announcing a new matching program with the Department of Labor. Under it, "DOL, as the source agency, will disclose Federal Employees' Compensation Act (FECA) payment data to SSA." SSA says it will use the data "to improve SSA's ability to accurately offset Title II DI benefits" (section 224 of the Social Security Act reduces disability benefits for people also receiving workers' compensation). "DOL will provide the data to SSA every 28 days, consistent with its FECA benefit payment cycle. Each data transmission is expected to include payment information for approximately 40,000 FECA beneficiaries." The people covered are "DI beneficiaries and their auxiliaries who are concurrently receiving FECA benefit payments."

Dates: comments are due "no later than December 21, 2026." The program "will be applicable on December 28, 2026, or once a minimum of 30 days after publication of this notice has elapsed, whichever is later," and runs for 18 months.

Who it affects

About 40,000 people getting Social Security Disability Insurance (and their auxiliaries) who also receive Federal Employees' Compensation Act benefits
