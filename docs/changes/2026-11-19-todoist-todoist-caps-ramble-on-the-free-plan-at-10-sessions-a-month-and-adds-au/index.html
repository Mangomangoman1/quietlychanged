Todoist: Todoist caps Ramble on the free plan at 10 sessions a month and adds Automations (beta) to paid plans

Todoist's free Beginner plan now lists 10 Ramble voice-capture sessions a month. Pro and Business get Todoist Automations in beta; Task Assist is gone from the table.

The wording

struck removed underlined added

What changed

Todoist reworked its plan table. The free Beginner plan's Ramble line, which said "Limited sessions", is now "AI-powered task capture, including Ramble" with "10 sessions/month" (paid plans: "Unlimited sessions"). "Task Assist" is gone from the Pro list and comparison table, replaced by "Todoist Automations" (Beta), available on Pro and Business with an Automations MCP server, run history, approval steps and AI steps; automations in a team workspace are Business-only.

Other changes

"1 week activity history" on Beginner is now "1 week reporting history", team activity logs become "activity reports" plus "Advanced project insights" on Business, integrations go from "90+" to "120+", and "Themes" was removed from the comparison table.

Who it affects

Todoist users, especially free Beginner users of Ramble voice task capture
