Canva Terms Nov 16, 2026: Teams seats now charged when invited, Pay as You Grow dropped

Canva's Nov 16 terms charge Teams for an added seat right away, prorated, and count a seat as soon as an invite goes out, accepted or not.

Took effect Nov 1611 days ago

The wording

struck removed underlined added

What changed

Canva updated its Terms of Use, now "effective as of 16 November 2026" (the previous version was dated 30 October 2026). The change is in section 7b, Pricing for Additional Seats on your Team.

Change 1: seats are charged when they're added. The old terms used Canva's "Pay as You Grow" model: seats you added were billed on your next Billing Date (your monthly renewal, or every 3 months on an annual plan) "unless they are removed from your Team by the Team Owner or an Administrator prior to the Billing Date." The new terms say: "When a seat is added part-way through a billing period, we’ll charge you for it at the time it’s added. The charge is prorated so you only pay for the remainder of your current billing period." The Pay as You Grow paragraph, which said the model let you "reconcile costs for anyone who has joined your Team but will no longer need access to the Service before you are billed for them", is gone.

Change 2: an invite counts as a seat. New wording: "A seat is added when a Team Owner invites a person to your Team or adds a seat, whether or not the invitation has been accepted." The Team Owner "authorises Canva to charge the payment method nominated for your Team for any seats they add."

Change 3: the warning email is gone. The old terms promised that "Three (3) days prior to the Billing Date, Canva will notify via email the billing contact" of the number of extra seats and fees. That sentence was removed. So was the line "You will not receive a refund or credit for removing seats from your Team that have already been paid for"; the new section doesn't say what happens when you remove a seat mid-period. The general cancellation clause still says you "will not be entitled to a refund of any fees already paid (except where required by law)."

What you can do: treat every Team invite as a purchase. Remove stale pending invites, and check your billing history after adding people.

Who it affects

Canva customers on plans with a Team (multi-seat plans), especially Team Owners and Administrators who invite people

  • Before inviting someone to your Canva Team, decide you want to pay for the seat: the charge happens when the invite is sent
