Todoist: Privacy policy now says user data isn't used to train any AI models

Todoist's privacy policy now says your data isn't used to train any AI models. It used to rule out only "generalized" ones.

The wording

struck removed underlined added

What changed

Doist's privacy policy (which covers Todoist) used to promise it doesn't use information collected from you, including via AI tools, to develop, improve, or train "generalized/non-personalized" artificial intelligence or machine learning models. That qualifier is gone: the sentence now says it doesn't use your information to develop, improve, or train artificial intelligence or machine learning models at all. The old wording left room for personalized models; the new wording doesn't.

Who it affects

Todoist users concerned about their tasks and data being used for AI training
