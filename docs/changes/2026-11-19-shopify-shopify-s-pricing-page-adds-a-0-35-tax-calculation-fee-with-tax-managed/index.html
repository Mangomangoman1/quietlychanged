Shopify tax calculation fee 0.35% (0.25% on Plus): what the Nov 2026 pricing page shows

Shopify's plan table now lists 'Tax managed by Shopify' and a tax calculation fee of 0.35% on Basic, Grow and Advanced (0.25% on Plus) after an introductory discount.

The wording

struck removed underlined added

What changed

Between our November 16 and November 19 snapshots, the Tax section of Shopify's plan comparison table gained three rows: "Tax managed by Shopify", "Self-managed tax mode" and "Tax calculation fee (after introductory discount)". The fee is listed as 0.35% on Basic, Grow and Advanced and 0.25% on Plus. The rows "Automated sales tax calculations", "Tax liability tracking" and "VAT validation, exemptions, and invoicing" are unchanged, and the "Advanced tax platform" is still an add-on.

The pricing page doesn't say what the percentage is charged on, how long the introductory discount lasts, or whether the fee applies in self-managed mode or only when Shopify manages tax. Check the Taxes and duties settings in your admin for the terms that apply to your store. Plan prices are unchanged: Basic $29, Grow $79, Advanced $299 a month billed yearly; Plus from $2,300 a month.

Coverage (checked November 19): PricingSaaS Pulse and Rewindex logged the same rows on November 17-18.

Who it affects

Shopify merchants on Basic, Grow, Advanced and Plus who use Shopify's built-in tax calculation
