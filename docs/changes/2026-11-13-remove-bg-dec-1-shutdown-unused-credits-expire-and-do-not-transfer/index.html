remove.bg shuts down Dec 1, 2026: unused credits expire, invoices must be exported first

remove.bg's website, accounts and self-service API stop on Dec 1 at 9:00 a.m. CET. Unused credits expire and are non-refundable; export invoices before then.

Shuts down Dec 1in 18 days

What changed

remove.bg, the background-removal tool now owned by Canva, is closing its standalone website, accounts and self-service API on December 1, 2026 at 9:00 a.m. CET. That is the time on remove.bg's own FAQ, fetched November 13, 2026. Background removal moves into Canva for everyday use, and the self-service API moves to Leonardo.Ai, which Canva also owns. Enterprise API contracts "aren't affected and continue as normal."

The facts below are from that FAQ and from remove.bg's General Terms and Conditions, which the FAQ links and which are marked "Last updated: 9th September 2026."

Unused credits expire, and nothing transfers

The General Terms say: "All credits (including pay-as-you-go (PAYG) credits, monthly rollover credits and any other type of credits) may be used until December 1 2026 only. Any unused credits will expire on that date and are non-refundable." The FAQ's pay-as-you-go answer is shorter: "Any unused PAYG credits will expire on 1 December 2026. We recommend using your credits before the migration date."

Neither the FAQ nor the General Terms describes any transfer of unused credits, invoices or subscriptions to Canva or Leonardo.Ai. Those products are presented as replacements, not as a continuation of your remove.bg balance.

What happens to subscriptions

Annual plans "will continue to work normally until 1 December 2026 at 9:00 a.m. CET." After that, the FAQ says, the plan "ends automatically and you won't be charged again." Unused time past that date is refunded "pro-rata" to the original payment method, and "No action is required from you to receive this refund."

Monthly plans also keep working until the same moment, then end automatically. "Any final renewal on or after 1 November is free." You can cancel earlier at [remove.bg/dashboard](https://www.remove.bg/dashboard).

Invoices and account access

Account data stays available until 9:00 a.m. CET on December 1. After that, "the website and accounts won't be accessible." The FAQ says to export your credit usage report and invoices from your Account page before then: "You won't be able to download them afterward."

API, plugins and what replace them

The self-service API "will stop accepting requests from 1 December 2026." remove.bg points those users to Leonardo.Ai's API and a migration guide. Enterprise contracts are unchanged.

The Photoshop plugin "won't be available after 1 December 2026." The FAQ points Photoshop users to Affinity, also part of Canva; Affinity itself is free, but its built-in background removal "runs on Canva AI, so you'll need a Canva Pro subscription to use it." Other integrations and plugins "will be discontinued on 1 December 2026."

What to do before December 1

- Spend unused credits. The General Terms make them non-refundable after that date. - Export invoices and your credit usage report from the Account page while you can still sign in. - If you are on a monthly plan, the final renewal on or after November 1 is already free; cancel earlier only if you want the plan to end sooner. - If you are on an annual plan, you do not need to file a refund request for unused time past December 1. - Self-service API users should plan the move to Leonardo.Ai now. Website and bulk users are pointed to Canva. Photoshop users are pointed to Affinity, with the Canva Pro caveat above. - Enterprise API customers can keep their current contracts.

Coverage (checked Nov 13, 2026): Several independent how-to sites have already written about the shutdown. This page cites only remove.bg's FAQ and General Terms.

Who it affects

Anyone with unused remove.bg credits or a subscription, plus people who use the website, the self-service API, or the Photoshop plugin and other integrations

Quick answers

When does remove.bg shut down?

December 1, 2026, at 9:00 a.m. CET, according to remove.bg's FAQ (fetched November 13, 2026). The standalone website, accounts and self-service API stop then.

Do unused remove.bg credits transfer to Canva or Leonardo.Ai?

No transfer is described. The General Terms (updated September 9, 2026) say all unused credits expire on December 1, 2026, and are non-refundable. The FAQ recommends using pay-as-you-go credits before that date.

Do annual subscribers get a refund?

Yes, for unused time past December 1. The FAQ says you will receive a pro-rata refund to your original payment method and that no action is required.

What happens to monthly subscriptions?

They work until 9:00 a.m. CET on December 1, then end automatically. Any final renewal on or after November 1 is free. You can cancel earlier at remove.bg/dashboard.

Can I still download invoices after December 1?

No. The FAQ says to export your credit usage report and invoices from your Account page before December 1, because you will not be able to download them afterward.
