Cloudflare Pipelines is no longer free: Basin pricing is $0.04/GB transforms, $0.03-$0.06/GB delivery

Cloudflare Pipelines is no longer free during beta. It now has real prices, with monthly free allowances before per-GB charges start.

The wording

struck removed underlined added

What changed

Cloudflare's plans page used to list Pipelines ingestion and delivery to R2 as "TBD (free during beta)". It now shows real prices for the product, now part of the Cloudflare Basin family. Stream ingress is free and unlimited. SQL transforms include 50 GB a month, then $0.04 per GB. Sink delivery includes 50 GB a month shared across sinks, then $0.03 per GB for JSON or $0.06 per GB for Parquet or Iceberg. The page also adds catalog pricing (1 million operations a month included, then $9 per million; compaction at $0.005 per GB after 10 GB and $2 per million objects after 1 million) and query pricing (10 GB of compressed data scanned a month included, then $0.0025 per GB, which is $2.50 per TB, with a 10 MB minimum per query).

R2 storage and operations are billed separately.

Who it affects

Developers and companies streaming events or logs through Cloudflare Pipelines into R2, especially anyone who set it up while it was free during beta

Source

Detected on the Cloudflare pricing page: https://www.cloudflare.com/plans/. Always check the source; this summary is not legal or financial advice.
