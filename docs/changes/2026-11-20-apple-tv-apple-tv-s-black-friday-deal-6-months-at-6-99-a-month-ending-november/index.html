Apple TV $6.99 a month for 6 months: Black Friday 2026 offer ends Nov 30

Apple TV is $6.99 a month for 6 months (normally $14.99) for new and qualified returning subscribers until Nov 30, then renews at $14.99. The 7-day trial card is gone from the page.

Offer ends Nov 30in 5 days

The wording

struck removed underlined added

What changed

Between November 19 and 20, Apple's Apple TV page swapped its standing new-subscriber card ("New Subscriber", "1 week free", "Get Apple TV for just $14.99 per month after a free 7-day trial.") for a limited-time offer: "Get 6 months of Apple TV for just $6.99 per month and save over 50%. Offer ends November 30." A banner at the top of the page says the same, and the FAQ answer on price now reads "A monthly subscription is just $14.99 per month." without the trial.

The fine print

The live page (checked November 20) says: "Offer expires on November 30, 2026, at 8:00 p.m. PT. Offer for eligible new and qualified returning subscribers redeeming on eligible Apple devices or where a subscription to Apple TV is billed directly through Apple." It is "not available if (i) you are eligible for a three months free Apple TV offer or (ii) your subscription to Apple TV is billed through a third party or your mobile wireless provider. Other new subscribers may receive a 7-day free trial. Plan automatically renews at $14.99/mo. after offer period until cancelled."

Our arithmetic: 6 months at $6.99 is $41.94, versus $89.94 at $14.99, a saving of $48.

What stays the same: the $14.99 monthly price, the 3-months-free offer with a new Apple device, the Apple TV and Peacock bundle at $17.99 a month, and Apple One from $21.95 a month.

Who it affects

People in the US thinking about subscribing to Apple TV, including former subscribers who might qualify as returning subscribers
