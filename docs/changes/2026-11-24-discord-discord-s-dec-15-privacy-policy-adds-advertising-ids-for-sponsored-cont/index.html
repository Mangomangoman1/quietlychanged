Discord privacy policy Dec 15, 2026: advertising IDs, sponsored content personalized by default

From December 15, Discord's privacy policy says sponsored content can use your device or advertising ID, and that in some regions it "may be personalized by default (and you can disable it)".

Takes effect Dec 15in 18 days

The wording

struck removed underlined added

What changed

Discord's [Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy) was updated the same day as its terms ("Last Updated: November 23, 2026", "Effective: December 15, 2026").

Change 1: Advertising IDs. Device information now "may also include, consistent with your settings, information like device ID, advertising ID (such as IDFA or Android Ad ID), or similar unique IDs (such as a click or interaction ID)" when you interact with sponsored content. Discord adds that "If you engage with third party sponsored content, we may receive interaction and measurement data (such as impressions, clicks, and conversions) from these sources", and that it may "use automated systems to process information about your activity or interactions with sponsored content and personalize, measure campaign performance, and prevent fraud."

Change 2: Default personalization. A new sentence says "depending on where you live and how old you are, we may deliver personalized sponsored content after you provide consent. But in other regions, sponsored content may be personalized by default (and you can disable it)." The policy doesn't name the regions.

Change 3: Age information. A new section says Discord collects "your IP address and limited device information to help us determine the region you’re in", that age-check information "depends on the age assurance method used", and that "We work with trusted providers so that Discord doesn’t handle the information you submit to confirm your age, such as a selfie, identify documents, or credit card details. Instead, Discord only collects, processes, or stores age signals (such as your age or age category)." Discord may also use information "to build or improve models that help us determine a user’s age group", and says safety laws require it to keep "logs about age signals we receive, the method used to confirm age".

Other changes: Server admins now control "whether the content is available off of Discord such as in search engine results"; Discord says it doesn't "access or store" voice and video call contents, while noting users can create Clips; billing information can include "in some cases tax information"; third-party apps can provide "analytics about your interactions with the server"; and the EU rights wording now covers the United Kingdom too.

Who it affects

Discord users who see Quests or other sponsored content, and anyone asked to verify their age on Discord

Quick answers

Does Discord personalize sponsored content by default?

In some regions, yes. The policy says "in other regions, sponsored content may be personalized by default (and you can disable it)"; elsewhere Discord asks for consent first, depending on where you live and how old you are.

Does Discord keep my selfie or ID for age verification?

The policy says Discord's providers handle that, so "Discord doesn’t handle the information you submit to confirm your age, such as a selfie, identify documents, or credit card details"; Discord keeps age signals such as your age or age category.
