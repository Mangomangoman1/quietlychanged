Runna UK price increase Dec 10, 2026: annual £99.99 to £109.99, monthly £17.99

Runna's UK annual plan rises from £99.99 to £109.99, and monthly from £15.99 to £17.99, at the first renewal on or after Dec 10, 2026. The Strava + Runna bundle goes to £124.99. US prices are unchanged.

New prices start Dec 10in 19 days

What changed

Runna, the running-coaching app owned by Strava, is raising its UK prices. The company's own support page, [Changes to our UK subscription](https://support.runna.com/en/articles/18298149-changes-to-our-uk-subscription), fetched November 14, 2026, lists the new rates and when they apply.

The new UK prices

The support page says:

- "Monthly subscription price: £15.99 > £17.99" - "Annual subscription price: £99.99 > £109.99" - "Strava + Runna annual subscription price: £119.99 > £124.99*"

The footnote on the bundle is: "This increase reflects Runna’s new price. The Strava price within the combined subscription remains the same." The annual plan is still the cheaper way to pay: twelve months at £17.99 comes to £215.88, against £109.99 paid upfront.

Runna's [UK pricing page](https://www.runna.com/en-gb/pricing), also fetched November 14, 2026, already shows the new UK rates of £17.99 a month and £109.99 a year, which matches the support page's rule for new subscribers.

When you pay the new price

"For existing Runna subscribers: the new prices apply from your first renewal on or after 10 December, 2026. We’ll email you on 10 November to let you know." "For new Runna subscribers: the new prices apply from 10 November, 2026." What you have already paid stays at the old price until that renewal. The support page does not say you can avoid the rise by switching plan; new sign-ups and plan changes have already been on the new rates since November 10.

US and other countries

The notice is titled "Changes to our UK subscription" and talks only about "users in the UK." Runna has not published a matching notice for other countries. The same pricing pages still list $19.99 a month and $119.99 a year among their currency prices (the default US dollar pair on [runna.com/pricing](https://www.runna.com/pricing) and the UK page, fetched November 14, 2026). We found no official US price change.

Coverage (checked November 14, 2026): [the5krunner](https://the5krunner.com/2026/11/10/quick-read-runna-price-rise/) reported the rise on November 10, including the bundle figure, which Runna's support page now confirms.

Who it affects

UK Runna subscribers, including people on the Strava + Runna annual bundle; new UK subscribers have already been paying the new prices since November 10, 2026

Quick answers

When do Runna's UK prices go up?

For existing subscribers, at the first renewal on or after December 10, 2026. New UK subscribers have already been paying the new prices since November 10, 2026. That is from Runna's official support page, fetched November 14, 2026.

How much does Runna cost in the UK now?

£17.99 a month or £109.99 a year for new subscribers and anyone changing plan, up from £15.99 and £99.99. The Strava + Runna annual bundle goes from £119.99 to £124.99.

Is Runna going up in the US too?

Not that Runna has announced. The official notice is UK-only, and its pricing pages still list $19.99 a month and $119.99 a year.
