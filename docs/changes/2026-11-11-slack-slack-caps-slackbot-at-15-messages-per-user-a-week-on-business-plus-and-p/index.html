Slack Business+ Slackbot limit: 15 messages per user a week (Nov 11, 2026)

Slack's $15-a-seat Business+ plan now limits its Slackbot AI agent to 15 messages per user each week. Unlimited Slackbot is an Enterprise+ feature, now listed at $45 per user a month.

The wording

struck removed underlined added

What changed

Slack's pricing page changed overnight in two ways that matter for anyone paying for its AI features.

Change 1: Slackbot gets a weekly cap on Business+

On Nov 10, the Business+ plan described Slackbot as "Your personal AI agent that understands conversations, files, and projects in Slack. Plan limits apply." On Nov 11 the same row reads: "Find answers, summarize channels, and work across your apps. 15 messages per user each week. Contact sales to add more." Business+ is listed at $15 per user a month billed annually, or $18 billed monthly. The feature is also renamed from "Slackbot (personal AI agent)" to plain "Slackbot" across the plan table, and the comparison table adds "Consumption based" and "Usage limits" rows.

Change 2: Enterprise+ gets a price, and unlimited Slackbot

Enterprise+ used to say "Contact sales for pricing." It now shows "$45 USD per user / month, when paying annually," still with a Contact Sales button. Its Slackbot row now reads "Unlimited Slackbot" with "It’s unlimited for your whole team."

What stays the same: The note that Business+ customers on Salesforce invoice plans can unlock higher message limits by buying Flex Credits through Salesforce is unchanged apart from formatting. Pro still lists Slackbot without a stated number.

What you can do: Business+ admins should check how heavily their teams use Slackbot; 15 messages a week is about three a workday.

Who it affects

Teams on Slack Business+ that lean on Slackbot for answers and channel summaries, and companies weighing an upgrade to Enterprise+
