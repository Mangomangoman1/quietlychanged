KYMCO Noodoe app and cloud shut down worldwide January 1, 2027

From Jan 1, 2027 the KYMCO Noodoe app will not launch and the cloud shuts down worldwide. On the dash, only the speed reading keeps working as normal.

Shuts down Jan 1, 2027in 36 days

The wording

struck removed underlined added

What changed

KYMCO is shutting down the Noodoe app and its cloud. In a notice on [kymcousa.com](https://kymcousa.com/kymco-noodoe-system-discontinuation/), marked as official product update bulletin PU-2025-1219 and dated December 19, 2025 (fetched November 14, 2026), the company says: "Effective January 1, 2027, all services, functions, and technical support will be terminated worldwide."

What stops

The impact list is specific:

- "The KYMCO Noodoe Application will no longer launch. Regardless of login status, users will be unable to access the App." - "The KYMCO Noodoe cloud services and servers will be permanently shut down." - Navigation pages will not display. - Message pages will not display. - Weather data will no longer be displayed (it was "previously calibrated via the KYMCO App"). - Photos cannot be added or deleted in the gallery.

A separate map-policy note says that on February 27, 2025, KYMCO shortened the validity of navigation map data from three years to two, and that starting January 1, 2027, "the KYMCO App will no longer launch, and users will be unable to use navigation functions or update map data."

What keeps working

"Speed Display: This function will continue to operate normally (data comes directly from instrument panel)." The clock will still show the time, but "cannot be adjusted (previously calibrated via the KYMCO App)." The scooter itself is not being discontinued; this notice is about the Noodoe application and cloud services.

What you can do

If you rely on the in-dash navigation or anything stored in the app, copy it before January 1, 2027. After that date there is no app to open and no cloud to reach. Questions, the notice says, go to your designated KYMCO USA sales representative.

Who it affects

Anyone who uses the KYMCO Noodoe app or the Noodoe-connected dash features on a KYMCO scooter, anywhere in the world

Quick answers

When does KYMCO Noodoe shut down?

January 1, 2027, worldwide. That date is in KYMCO USA's discontinuation notice (bulletin PU-2025-1219), fetched November 14, 2026.

Will the scooter still show my speed?

Yes. KYMCO says the speed display will continue to operate normally because the data comes directly from the instrument panel. The clock will still show the time but cannot be adjusted.

Can I still open the Noodoe app after January 1, 2027?

No. The notice says the application will no longer launch, regardless of login status, and the cloud services and servers will be permanently shut down.

What you can do

  • Do not count on the Noodoe app, in-dash navigation, messages, weather or the gallery after January 1, 2027; the app will not launch and the cloud is shut down worldwide
  • If you still use in-dash navigation, save any routes or map details you need before that date; KYMCO already shortened map-data validity from three years to two, and map updates end when the app stops launching
  • Plan to use a phone or other GPS for navigation, and remember that the clock on the dash will still show the time but can no longer be adjusted through the app
