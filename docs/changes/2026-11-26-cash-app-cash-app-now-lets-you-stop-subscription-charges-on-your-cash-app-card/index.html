Cash App terms Nov 2026: stop payment on subscriptions, new Bills and Subscriptions

Cash App's terms dropped "No Right to Stop Payment": you can now block recurring card charges 3 business days ahead. A new Bills and Subscriptions tool can act for you with power of attorney.

Last updated Nov 25yesterday

The wording

struck removed underlined added

What changed

Cash App's [Terms of Service](https://cash.app/legal/us/en-us/tos), now marked "Last Updated: November 25, 2026", change two things for people who pay subscriptions with a Cash App Card (fetched November 26, 2026).

Change 1: you can now stop recurring card charges

The card section used to say "6. No Right to Stop Payment" and "You do not have the right to stop payment on a transaction originated with your Cash App Card." It now reads "6. Right to Stop Payment": "You have the right to stop payment on preauthorized transfers originated with your Cash App Card. To stop payment on a preauthorized transfer, we must receive your request at least three (3) Business Days before the scheduled date of the transfer." Preauthorized transfers are defined as "automatic payments, subscriptions, or recurring payments" where a merchant stores your card.

The way to do it is to block the merchant: "Open Cash App, go to the Activity tab, and block the merchant", write through Cash App Support in the app, or call (800) 969-1940. "If you call, we may also require you to put your request in writing and if you fail to do so within 14 days, the stop payment may be removed." Two catches: "blocking a merchant will result in blocking all future Cash App Card transactions from that merchant", and "Blocking a merchant does not automatically cancel underlying subscriptions or agreements with the merchant." If Cash App misses a request made three business days or more ahead, "we will be liable for your unrecoverable losses."

How it compares (checked Nov 26): the wording mirrors the federal Regulation E stop-payment rule, which says a consumer "may stop payment of a preauthorized electronic fund transfer from the consumer's account by notifying the financial institution orally or in writing at least three business days before the scheduled date of the transfer" and lets the institution ask for written confirmation "within 14 days of an oral notification" (12 CFR 1005.10(c)).

Change 2: a new Bills and Subscriptions service

A new section XXV describes "an optional service that allows you to view and manage recurring third-party merchant bills and subscriptions connected to your Cash App Card", run directly or through an outside "Bills and Subscriptions Vendor". Turning it on authorizes Cash App and the vendor to do things including "adding, updating, or blocking your Cash App Card for payment of Linked Subscriptions" and "viewing transactions, managing, making changes, and canceling Linked Subscriptions."

To do that, "you may be required to create an account with the Bills and Subscriptions Vendor and supply the Bills and Subscriptions Vendor with your credentials including, but not limited to, usernames, passwords, PIN numbers", and you grant both "a limited power of attorney". Cash App "may use the data from your Linked Subscriptions" for the service "and for any other purposes disclosed in the" Cash App Privacy Notice. Pricing, cancellation and refund terms of each subscription "are strictly between you and the third-party merchant", supported subscriptions can be removed "at any time and for any reason" without notice, and if a connection is lost "it is solely your responsibility to update such connections". "We do not charge a fee for your use of the Bills and Subscriptions Service."

Other changes: "Cash App Credit Score" is renamed "Cash App Score" in the contents; the card section adds wording for Sponsored Accounts for a Child ("you authorize the Child to conduct transactions with the Cash App Card"); two sentences were removed from the card section ("We make no guarantees regarding your Cash App Card and we are not responsible if it gets stolen, lost, destroyed, or used without your permission, except as otherwise described in these Cash App Terms." and an older duplicate negative-balance line), while the remaining sentence still says the Company "is not responsible if your Cash App Card is lost, stolen, destroyed, or used without your permission" except as the terms or law say otherwise.

Who it affects

Cash App Card holders with subscriptions or automatic payments on the card, and anyone who turns on Cash App's new Bills and Subscriptions feature

Quick answers

Can I stop a subscription payment on my Cash App Card?

Yes, as of the November 25, 2026 terms: "You have the right to stop payment on preauthorized transfers originated with your Cash App Card." Cash App must get the request at least three business days before the charge; you do it by blocking the merchant in the Activity tab, via Cash App Support, or by calling (800) 969-1940.

Does blocking a merchant in Cash App cancel my subscription?

No. Cash App's terms say "Blocking a merchant does not automatically cancel underlying subscriptions or agreements with the merchant." Cancel with the merchant as well.

What is Cash App Bills and Subscriptions?

An optional service to "view and manage recurring third-party merchant bills and subscriptions connected to your Cash App Card". It may require giving an outside vendor your merchant usernames and passwords and granting Cash App and the vendor a limited power of attorney. Cash App says it doesn't charge a fee for it.
