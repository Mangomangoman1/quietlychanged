cPanel price increase Dec 16, 2026: Solo $29.99 to $32.99 a month, all tiers up

cPanel license prices rise on Dec 16 for new orders and every renewal on or after that date. Solo goes from $29.99 to $32.99 a month; Admin, Pro, Premier and extra accounts go up as well.

New prices start Dec 16in 32 days

The wording

struck removed underlined added

What changed

cPanel has posted a notice on its [pricing page](https://www.cpanel.net/pricing/): "effective December 16, 2026, cPanel license pricing will be adjusted, affecting new orders and all renewals on or after that date." The notice links to the [Customer Licensing Guide](https://www.cpanel.net/customer-licensing-guide/), which embeds cPanel's Store Licensing Guide 2027, marked "Valid from December 16, 2026." Both pages were fetched November 13, 2026.

The live pricing page still shows today's Store prices. The 2027 guide shows the prices that apply from December 16. All figures below are official cPanel Store prices, excluding VAT, for Cloud/VPS licenses unless noted. Partners and hosting providers may charge something different.

The new monthly prices

| Tier | Accounts | Until Dec 15 (pricing page) | From Dec 16 (2027 guide) | |---|---|---|---| | Solo | 1 | $29.99 | $32.99 | | Admin | Up to 5 | $35.99 | $38.99 | | Pro | Up to 30 | $53.99 | $58.99 | | Premier | Up to 100 | $69.99 | $76.99 | | Each account above 100 | Premier only | $0.49 | $0.55 |

Solo, Admin and Pro can also be billed annually. The pricing page today lists those as $27.46, $32.96 and $49.46 a month billed annually. The 2027 guide lists annual Store totals of $362.89, $428.89 and $648.89. Premier stays monthly only, and Metal (dedicated) is offered only at the Premier price.

When you pay the new price

The pricing-page notice is specific: the adjustment affects "new orders and all renewals on or after that date." A license that renews on December 16 or later is billed at the new rate. One that you renew before December 16 is still at today's Store price for that term.

Coverage (checked Nov 13, 2026): We are citing only cPanel's pricing page and Store Licensing Guide 2027.

Who it affects

Anyone who buys a cPanel Solo, Admin, Pro or Premier license from the cPanel Store, or whose license renews on or after December 16, 2026

Quick answers

When do cPanel prices go up?

December 16, 2026, for new orders and for every renewal on or after that date, according to the notice on cPanel's pricing page (fetched November 13, 2026).

How much is cPanel Solo after the increase?

The Store Licensing Guide 2027 lists cPanel Solo Cloud at $32.99 a month, up from $29.99 on the live pricing page. Annual Solo is listed at $362.89.

Do existing licenses stay at the old price?

Not past their next renewal on or after December 16. The notice says the adjustment affects all renewals on or after that date, as well as new orders.
