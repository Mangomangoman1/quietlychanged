Airbnb: Terms rename the Offline Fee and Off-Platform policies

October 23, 2026 · Detected by QuietlyChanged's daily check

Airbnb's Terms of Service now refer to the "Offline Fee Policy" as its "policy on pricing and payment transparency" and to the "Off-Platform Policy" as its "policy on limiting host-imposed contracts and other requirements." The rules around them read the same: hosts must describe all additional fees in the listing, and guests and hosts must not request, make, or accept bookings or payments outside Airbnb to avoid fees or taxes. The new names suggest a broader focus on price transparency and on extra contracts or requirements hosts impose on guests.

Who it affects

Airbnb hosts who charge extra fees or ask guests to sign agreements, and guests booking stays
