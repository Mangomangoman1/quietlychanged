ZWILLING Fresh & Save app shuts December 31, 2026: save your data first

The ZWILLING app, used with Fresh & Save, shuts December 31, 2026. After that you cannot log in. Vacuum boxes still work; save box contents, expiry dates and lists first.

What changed

ZWILLING is discontinuing its mobile app, which is the app Fresh & Save owners use for storage tracking and expiry reminders. In a [FAQ on zwilling.com](https://www.zwilling.com/us/magazine/faqs-app-exit.html), fetched November 14, 2026, the company says it has "made the strategic decision to discontinue the ZWILLING app by December 31, 2026." "Customers can continue using the app until December 31, 2026. From January 2027, the app will no longer be available, and app-based features and services will be deactivated."

You will not be able to log in

"From January 2027, you will no longer be able to log in or use the app, so the app will not be usable." That is true even if the app is still on your phone, and even if you try to use it offline. It also leaves the App Store and Google Play: "From January 2027, the app will no longer be available for download or re-download from app stores." Scanning a QR or NFC code on a product or its packaging will no longer open the app or its features.

What to save first

ZWILLING's own advice is to copy anything you still need before December 31, 2026. From January 2027 you will not see saved Fresh & Save boxes in the app, and you will not get expiry notifications. "We recommend writing down or taking a screenshot of the contents of your boxes and their estimated expiration dates before the app is discontinued." Shopping lists disappear too, so copy those as well. There is "currently no substitute solution for managing the content of the boxes or fridge."

Other app-only features that stop: shopping inside the app, product registration through the app, recipes and content articles in the app (recipes remain on zwilling.com in the Inspiration area), and push notifications.

What stays the same

Fresh & Save is not being discontinued. "Your Fresh & Save products will continue to work as normal. All functions that do not rely on the app remain fully intact." Your customer account "will still be available for login and shopping on zwilling.com." Warranties and product support are unchanged.

Coverage (checked November 14, 2026): we are citing ZWILLING's official app-discontinuation FAQ.

Who it affects

Anyone who uses the ZWILLING app to track Fresh & Save boxes, expiry dates, shopping lists or other app-only features

Quick answers

When does the ZWILLING Fresh & Save app shut down?

You can use it through December 31, 2026. From January 2027 you cannot log in, even if the app is still installed. That is from ZWILLING's official FAQ, fetched November 14, 2026.

Will my Fresh & Save vacuum boxes still work?

Yes. ZWILLING says Fresh & Save remains in its portfolio and that all functions that do not rely on the app stay intact. Storage tracking, expiry notifications and shopping lists in the app are what go away.

What should I save before December 31?

ZWILLING recommends writing down or screenshotting the contents of your boxes and their estimated expiration dates, and copying your shopping list. There is currently no substitute app for managing box contents.
