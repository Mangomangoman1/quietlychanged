discovery+ price increase 2026: Ad-Free is $11.99 a month as of Nov 12

discovery+ raised Ad-Free by $2 to $11.99 a month and the ad plan by $1 to $6.99. New U.S. sign-ups have paid that since Oct 13; existing subscribers are charged on the first bill on or after Nov 12.

What changed

discovery+'s U.S. help center has a page titled "discovery+ plan price changes," fetched November 13, 2026. It says plan prices "increased for new subscriptions in the U.S. on October 13, 2026."

"The discovery+ plan increased by $1 to $6.99/month (plus applicable taxes). The discovery+ (Ad-Free) plan increased by $2 to $11.99/month (plus applicable taxes)." By that wording, Ad-Free was $9.99 and the ad-supported plan was $5.99.

When existing subscribers pay it

New subscribers have been charged the new prices since October 13. For current subscribers, the page says: "You'll be charged the new monthly price on your next billing date on or after November 12, 2026."

If you are billed by discovery+ or an app store, the subscription "will automatically renew at the new price (plus applicable taxes) on your first billing date on or after November 12, 2026." If you have a promotional discount, "your price won't increase until your promotional period ends."

If you are billed through another provider, such as Amazon Prime Video Channels, DIRECTV, the Roku Channel, or Sling TV, "your provider will let you know of any price changes (as applicable)."

The help center walks through how to see who bills you and when the next date is: sign in at the discovery+ app or discoveryplus.com, open Account, and read the Subscription section.

Coverage (checked Nov 13, 2026): We are citing only discovery+'s own help article.

Who it affects

U.S. discovery+ subscribers, especially anyone on the Ad-Free plan billed by discovery+ or an app store. If you are billed through Amazon Prime Video Channels, DIRECTV, the Roku Channel or Sling TV, your provider handles the notice.

Quick answers

How much does discovery+ Ad-Free cost now?

The help center says the Ad-Free plan increased by $2 to $11.99 a month, plus tax. The ad-supported plan increased by $1 to $6.99 a month, plus tax.

When does the new price hit my bill?

If you are a current U.S. subscriber billed by discovery+ or an app store, on your first billing date on or after November 12, 2026. New U.S. subscribers have paid the new prices since October 13. Promotional discounts last until the promo ends.

What if Amazon or Sling bills me for discovery+?

The help center says your provider will let you know of any price changes. Check that provider's statement or subscription settings.
