Audible offer: $99 for a year of Premium (vs. $149.50 regular annual price)

A banner offers a year of Audible Premium for $99, about $8.25 a month. The page doesn't say when the offer ends or what its terms are.

UpdateFollow-up (Oct 16, 2026, 6:16 AM MT): a browser check of audible.com/ep/memberbenefits did not show the $99 offer. The page showed $149.50 and $229.50 annual Premium prices and a Prime $0.99/mo deal instead. The $99 offer appears to be A/B tested or shown only to some visitors.

What changed

Audible's membership plans page added a new banner: "LIMITED TIME OFFER | $99 for a year of Premium. That's like $8.25/mo. Terms apply." The regular annual Premium prices listed on the same page are unchanged at $149.50 a year for 12 credits and $229.50 a year for 24 credits, and monthly Premium is still $14.95. The page doesn't spell out the offer's terms or end date.

Who it affects

Audible members and anyone considering an annual Audible Premium plan, who should check the offer's terms before renewing or signing up
