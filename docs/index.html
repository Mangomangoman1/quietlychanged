Thursday, November 5, 2026 · 3 changes

Hulu/Disney+ terms add device type as a reason content varies; PayPal spells out its "false information" rule

Two small but real changes since our Nov 3 snapshot (the Nov 4 run did not complete, so today's diff covers two days). The Disney+/ESPN/Hulu Subscriber Agreement now lists "your device type" as a reason content can differ, drops "add-ons" from its tier and proration wording, and moves ESPN's legal notice address to Bristol, CT: https://www.hulu.com/terms. PayPal's User Agreement now spells out the "false, inaccurate or misleading information" ban (identity, account or business, goods or services, transaction details): https://www.paypal.com/us/legalhub/paypal/useragreement-full.

The other diffs were noise: Shopify and Slack flipped back into their known rotating pricing-page variants (Shopify Grow $79/$89 and Advanced $299/$359 yearly; Slack Enterprise+ "$45" vs "Contact sales for pricing"), Dropbox rewrote page headings and dropped a "Best Value" tag, Audible served a rotating localized pricing variant, 1Password swapped a masked email for plain text and changed capitalization, Venmo gained a legal-menu list, Calendly lost a stray character, Tubi's device ID rotated, and Stripe's privacy diff came from our Nov 3 baseline being saved with a different text extraction (the live policy still says it "will be updated on November 20, 2026"; today's snapshot is the new baseline). Roblox's Terms of Use page now shows the rewritten User Terms and Creator Terms as the live version, which matches the Nov 1 effective date already covered in our Oct 18 article (Roblox failed to fetch Oct 31 to Nov 3 and Nov 4 was skipped). PlayStation (SSL error) still fails to fetch.
