Cloudflare's "free during beta" data pipeline now has a price, and Audible runs a $99 Premium year

October 16, 2026 · 2 changes

What changed

  • Pricing

    Cloudflare: Data pipeline pricing replaces "free during beta"

    What changed
    Cloudflare's plans page used to list Pipelines ingestion and delivery to R2 as "TBD (free during beta)". It now shows real prices for the product, now part of the Cloudflare Basin family. Stream ingress is free and unlimited. SQL transforms include 50 GB a month, then $0.04 per GB. Sink delivery includes 50 GB a month shared across sinks, then $0.03 per GB for JSON or $0.06 per GB for Parquet or Iceberg. The page also adds catalog pricing (1 million operations a month included, then $9 per million; compaction at $0.005 per GB after 10 GB and $2 per million objects after 1 million) and query pricing (10 GB of compressed data scanned a month included, then $0.0025 per GB, which is $2.50 per TB, with a 10 MB minimum per query). R2 storage and operations are billed separately.
    Who it affects
    Developers and companies streaming events or logs through Cloudflare Pipelines into R2, especially anyone who set it up while it was free during beta
    October 16, 2026View source page for Cloudflare →
  • Pricing

    Audible: Limited-time offer: $99 for a year of Premium

    What changed
    Audible's membership plans page added a new banner: "LIMITED TIME OFFER | $99 for a year of Premium. That's like $8.25/mo. Terms apply." The regular annual Premium prices listed on the same page are unchanged at $149.50 a year for 12 credits and $229.50 a year for 24 credits, and monthly Premium is still $14.95. The page doesn't spell out the offer's terms or end date.
    Who it affects
    Audible members and anyone considering an annual Audible Premium plan, who should check the offer's terms before renewing or signing up
    October 16, 2026View source page for Audible →

Two real updates today out of nine raw page diffs. Cloudflare's plans page replaced "TBD (free during beta)" for its data pipeline with published prices, now under the new Basin name: SQL transforms cost $0.04 per GB after 50 GB a month, and delivering data costs $0.03 per GB for JSON or $0.06 per GB for Parquet and Iceberg, with new catalog and query charges alongside. Audible's plans page added a limited-time offer of $99 for a year of Premium, against regular annual prices of $149.50 and $229.50. Squarespace and Tinder flipped back to the same page versions we saw on Oct 13, and Grammarly, PayPal, Cash App, Wix, and Mailchimp changes were wording, email obfuscation, formatting, localized pricing, and marketing copy, not real policy or price changes.
