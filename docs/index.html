CPricingCaliforniaMajorOct 26

From Jan 1, California charges sales tax on software subscriptions, but not on streaming, music, e-books or games

Starting January 1, 2027, downloaded and cloud software (SaaS) sold to Californians gets sales tax of 7.25% or more. Streaming, music, audiobooks, e-books and games are excluded.

Tax starts Jan 1, 2027in 67 days

No changes today

No changes today. All six raw page diffs were noise: Slack's Enterprise+ price flipped back to $45 (it alternates with "Contact sales for pricing" between requests), Squarespace's pricing page swapped to the alternate layout it rotates through (same $25/$39/$65/$139 prices), Todoist's pricing page started rendering its billing-toggle text with no plan changes, Audible added a "Last chance" line to its Prime promo and reworded plan taglines with the same $8.99 and $14.95 prices, PayPal's privacy page had its email addresses hidden by an anti-spam filter, and Cloudflare's privacy page gained a stray space. Roblox's terms page is still blocking our fetch (HTTP 403).
