Cash App lets you stop subscription charges on its card and adds a bills tool; YouTube TV's free trial shrinks to 2 days

Cash App's Terms of Service ("Last Updated: November 25, 2026") replace "No Right to Stop Payment" with "Right to Stop Payment": you can now stop preauthorized (subscription and recurring) Cash App Card charges if Cash App gets the request at least three business days before the charge, by blocking the merchant in the Activity tab, through Cash App Support, or by calling (800) 969-1940. Blocking doesn't cancel the subscription itself. A new "Bills and Subscriptions" section adds an optional tool that can manage and cancel subscriptions for you; it may ask for your merchant usernames and passwords and gives Cash App and an outside vendor "a limited power of attorney". https://cash.app/legal/us/en-us/tos

YouTube TV's plans page now shows a "2-day free trial" on every plan, down from "5-day free trial" on November 22 to 25 and a "10-day free trial" on most plans when we checked on November 20. Prices and the new-user offers ending December 2 are unchanged. https://tv.youtube.com/learn/explore-plans/

Noise: Dropbox plans page served a version without the 3-free-months offer (a recheck at ~6:20 AM MT still shows "Get 3 free months. Offer ends December 1."); Disney+ banner now reads "Black Friday: Save on Disney+ and Hulu!" (deal covered Nov 24) plus title shuffles and "/ month" to "/ mo"; Slack Enterprise+ $45 flip-flop; Delta error-page encoding; Grammarly headline; Venmo legal menu; Audible plan-comparison table (recurring); Tubi device ID; Booking.com calendar IDs; Hunter newsroom links. Roblox terms is still HTTP 403 and Shein terms still bot-walled; PayPal terms and privacy fetched fine today.
