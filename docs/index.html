October 18, 2026 · 1 change

  • Pricing

    Wix: Plan comparison adds AI agents and tools, donations, and a rental platform

    What changed
    Wix's plans page now lists three new rows in its plan feature comparison: "AI agents and tools," "Donations and fundraising" ("Collect donations and run fundraising campaigns on your site"), and "Rental platform" ("List your rentals, set your prices and take reservations online"). Plan prices did not change.
    Who it affects
    Wix site owners and anyone comparing Wix plans, especially nonprofits, rental businesses, and people looking for built-in AI tools
    October 18, 2026View source page for Wix →

One small real update today out of three raw page diffs. Wix's plans page now lists three new rows in its plan feature comparison: "AI agents and tools," "Donations and fundraising" ("Collect donations and run fundraising campaigns on your site"), and "Rental platform" ("List your rentals, set your prices and take reservations online"). No plan prices changed. Slack's pricing page swapped its Enterprise+ "$45 per user / month" price for "Contact sales for pricing," but repeated live checks this morning returned both versions, so it looks like a rotating page test rather than a real reversal. Audible's plan page flipped its annual-plan wording again ("effective next billing cycle," credit labels, "Save 16%"), the same back-and-forth we flagged on Oct 15 and Oct 17, with no price change.
