From Jan 1, California charges sales tax on software subscriptions, but not on streaming, music, e-books or games

Starting January 1, 2027, downloaded and cloud software (SaaS) sold to Californians gets sales tax of 7.25% or more. Streaming, music, audiobooks, e-books and games are excluded.

Claude's $200 plan becomes "Ultra" with no 5-hour limit

Anthropic reworked its Claude plans. Max 20x is gone: the $200 tier is now Ultra, which promises 10x Pro's weekly usage and no 5-hour limit for verified accounts, where the old plan promised 20x Pro's usage per 5-hour session. Max is a single $100 plan with 5x Pro usage, and a new top model, Fable, is included up to half your weekly limit on Max and Ultra but costs usage credits on Pro. The other diffs were noise or already covered. Slack's and Shopify's pricing pages showed the other versions they rotate between, Delta's contract of carriage got a formatting and encoding cleanup, PayPal's privacy statement shows its contact emails again, Venmo's privacy page added a navigation menu, and Figma listed a new October 30 version of its privacy policy with no wording change in the text we track. Pinterest and PlayStation pages failed with SSL errors and Roblox's terms returned a 403.
