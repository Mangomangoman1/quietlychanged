No changes today

No changes today. All seven raw page diffs were noise: Venmo's legal-menu list disappeared again (the same capture flip-flop seen before); Audible's member-benefits page showed its recurring "$99 for a year of Premium" limited-time offer; Mailchimp rotated between its two known page versions; Tubi's device ID rotated; Booking.com's calendar IDs swapped order; cPanel's pricing page added a "Starting From $7.49/mo" teaser on its integrations row (no plan price changed). Emotiv's privacy policy fetched again for the first time since Nov 9: the page was re-templated, and apart from formatting the only real edit is that the "Effective Date: August 6, 2026" line is gone; the Global Privacy Control and Log Data passages the diff shows as removed are still on the live page. Apple pricing, Apple terms and Amazon Prime privacy hit a one-off SSLError and fetched unchanged on retry. PayPal terms and privacy and Roblox terms are still HTTP 403; Shein terms is still bot-walled.
