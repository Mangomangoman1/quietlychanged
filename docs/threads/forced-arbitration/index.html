Forced arbitration

Who has quietly taken away your day in court.

Most consumer contracts already say you can't sue the company in court or join a class action. What keeps changing is the fine print around that: who the arbitrator is, how mass claims get batched, whether you can opt out, and how long you have to do it.

This thread follows every arbitration change we catch. Watch for two things: a new opt-out window (usually 30 days, easy to miss) and changes that make it harder for many people with the same complaint to act together.
