Agree or lose access

New terms you have to accept to keep using something you already paid for.

"By continuing to use the service, you agree." Sometimes it's harder than that: an app that locks until you tap Accept, or a connection that stops working unless you sign in again.

These are the changes where doing nothing has a consequence, so each one leads with its deadline.

  • TermsGrindrNotableOct 28

    Grindr's new terms: AI can summarize your chats for other users even if you don't use AI, and opting out won't lower your bill

    Grindr's new terms say its AI may generate summaries about you from your chats and show them to other users, whether or not you use AI. Turn those settings off and paid features may stop, with no change to your bill.

    inserted: If you turn off a setting that a Paid Feature depends on, that feature will stop working for you until you turn the setting back on. Your Subscription and its billing are not affected by your settings choices.
    Read the change →Source ↗ for Grindr
  • TermsXeroNotableOct 27

    Xero can now require ID and a face scan or cut off your account; US disputes get a 1-year deadline from Dec 28

    Xero's new terms let it demand a government ID and a biometric face scan, and suspend or close accounts that don't pass. US customers also get new arbitration rules, including a one-year limit on claims.

    inserted: we may ask you to provide things like government-issued identification documents and a biometric facial scan so we can confirm the document belongs to you.
    Takes effect Dec 28 · in 59 daysRead the change →Source ↗ for Xero
  • TermsCricket WirelessNotableOct 25

    From Dec 1, a 60-day wait before small claims court and no more online dispute notices

    From Dec 1, Cricket customers must mail a dispute notice and wait 60 days before small claims. You can reject the change by mail within 30 days of Cricket's text.

    Any arbitration deleted: proceeding cannot be commenced inserted: or small claims court proceeding cannot begin until after the Informal Resolution Period has ended.
    New terms take effect Dec 1 · in 32 daysRead the change →Source ↗ for Cricket Wireless
  • TermsAT&TNotableOct 25

    From Nov 1, you must send a notice and wait 60 days before taking AT&T to small claims court

    From Nov 1, AT&T customers must send a dispute notice and wait 60 days before suing in small claims, and AT&T can make its settlement offer later in arbitration.

    Any arbitration deleted: proceeding cannot be commenced inserted: or small claims court proceeding cannot begin until after the Informal Resolution Period has ended.
    New terms take effect Nov 1 · in 2 daysRead the change →Source ↗ for AT&T
  • TermsFitbitMajorOct 22

    Third-party apps stop getting your Fitbit data Oct 30 unless you reconnect them in Google Health

    Apps linked to your Fitbit stop getting new data on Oct 30 unless you reconnect each one through Google Health.

    inserted: On October 30, 2026, the Fitbit Web API will be turned off and will no longer function or sync data to or from Fitbit users.
    Reconnect apps by Oct 30 · todayRead the change →Source ↗ for Fitbit
  • TermsPinterestMajorOct 20

    Nov 12 terms: mass claims go to one arbitrator, ads follow you to your TV, and a 30-day opt-out

    From Nov 12, 25 or more similar claims against Pinterest go to one arbitrator, and its ads can follow you to your TV. You get a 30-day window to opt out of arbitration.

    deleted: one arbitrator to resolve each batch inserted: all of those Claims will be consolidated into one arbitration before one arbitrator
    New terms take effect Nov 12 · in 13 daysRead the change →Source ↗ for Pinterest
  • TermsRobloxNotableOct 18

    From Nov 1, Roblox says it is merchant of record for most platform purchases; parents must accept for minors

    From Nov 1, purchases on Roblox are bought from Roblox itself, not from creators, and players under 18 need a parent to accept the new terms to keep playing.

    inserted: Roblox acts as the principal distributor and merchant of record for purchases made through Roblox
    Takes effect Nov 1 · in 2 daysRead the change →Source ↗ for Roblox
  • TermsOuraMajorOct 17

    App locks Nov 4 unless you accept new arbitration terms

    Oura's app stops working Nov 4 unless you accept new terms: arbitration with a jury waiver, mass claims handled 20 at a time, and no opt-out.

    deleted: at least thirty (30) days in advance inserted: in advance of the effective date and within the timeframe required by applicable law
    Accept in the app by Nov 4 · in 5 daysRead the change →Source ↗ for Oura
