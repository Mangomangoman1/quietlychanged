Prices, fees and renewals

Price rises, new fees, and the rules for leaving.

Some price changes are headline news. Most aren't: a yearly discount shrinks, a per-line fee goes up a few cents, a feature moves to a pricier tier, a cancellation now waits until the next billing cycle.

This thread tracks all of them, with the old and new numbers side by side.

  • TermsRakutenNotableNov 9

    Your cash back rate is now "personalized" and may differ from other members'

    Rakuten's Oct 23 terms and privacy policy say cash back rates are personalized by your account history, shopping behavior and membership status, so your rate at a store may differ from another member's. Cash back is now "Rewards," with partner-point conversions and a $2,000-a-day gift card cap.

    deleted: CASH BACK AMOUNTS VARY BY AFFILIATE STORE AND PRODUCT CATEGORY inserted: REWARD AMOUNTS VARY BY BRAND, PRODUCT CATEGORY, BY MEMBER, AND BY SURFACE
    Read the change →Source ↗ for Rakuten
  • TermsBooking.comNotableNov 8

    Price match will pay wallet credits, not a refund

    From Dec 10, Booking.com's US price-match promise changes from a refund of the difference to Wallet Credits. They can still lower your reservation price if possible.

    we promise deleted: to refund inserted: you Wallet Credits for the difference
    Takes effect Dec 10 · in 30 daysRead the change →Source ↗ for Booking.com
  • PricingJetBlueNotableNov 7

    Checked bags now $50 to $85 each, and all of December is peak

    For trips booked from Nov 3, JetBlue's first checked bag costs $50 to $70 and the second $70 to $85. Every day in December now gets the higher peak fees.

    November 2026 inserted: 11/5-11/11, 11/19-11/30 December 2026 deleted: 12/17-12/31 inserted: 12/1-12/31
    Read the change →Source ↗ for JetBlue
  • PricingCaliforniaMajorOct 26

    From Jan 1, California charges sales tax on software subscriptions, but not on streaming, music, e-books or games

    Starting January 1, 2027, downloaded and cloud software (SaaS) sold to Californians gets sales tax of 7.25% or more. Streaming, music, audiobooks, e-books and games are excluded.

    deleted: The sale or lease of a prewritten program is not a taxable transaction if the program is transferred by remote telecommunications inserted: A digital product and any copyright or patent interests associated therewith.
    Tax starts Jan 1 · in 52 daysRead the change →Source ↗ for California

    Senders now billed for unpaid foreign duties on packages, even if disputed, with 6% interest

    If a foreign country says duties weren't paid on your package, USPS can now bill you for them, even if you dispute it or the package is lost, with 6% interest.

    Bill negotiation moves to $15/month Premium+ on Nov 2

    From Nov 2, Rocket Money only negotiates bills for $15-a-month Premium+ members. The pay-only-if-it-works option and automatic renegotiations end.

    inserted: As of November 2, 2026, Bill Negotiation is exclusively a Premium+ feature. Negotiations already in progress or on a payment plan will continue. Auto-renegotiations will sunset on November 2, 2026.

    inserted: Use AI to detect unstructured secrets, like passwords, and block pushes that contain them.

  • TermsRobinhoodMajorOct 16

    Auto-enrolls accounts in class-action claims, keeps 20% of payouts

    Robinhood will file class-action claims for you by default and keep 20% of the payout. In those cases you also lose the right to sue. You can unenroll.

    inserted: Your Account is automatically enrolled in Robinhood's Class Action Service (the "Class Action Service"), unless you unenroll as described in this Section 36A.

    Regulatory Charge rises 24% to $0.26 per voice line on Nov 1

    Verizon's own Regulatory Charge goes from $0.21 to $0.26 per voice line on Nov 1. It's a Verizon fee, not a government tax.

    inserted: Effective November 1, 2026, the monthly Verizon Wireless Regulatory Charge for voice-capable devices will increase from $0.21 to $0.26 per line.
