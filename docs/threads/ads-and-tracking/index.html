Ads and tracking

When your data starts paying for someone else's ads.

Ad targeting rarely changes with an announcement. It changes with a new sentence in a privacy policy: an exception to an old promise, a new data partner, ads that follow you to another device.

This thread collects those sentences, and the deals some companies now offer in exchange for your data.

    From Nov 12, 25 or more similar claims against Pinterest go to one arbitrator, and its ads can follow you to your TV. You get a 30-day window to opt out of arbitration.
