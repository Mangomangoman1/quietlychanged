Pricing-page shuffles

Small edits to plans and pricing pages.

Not every change is a scandal. Plans gain and lose features, labels get rewritten, prices appear where there used to be a "Contact sales" button.

We log them anyway. Small edits are often how bigger changes start, and a dated record is the only way to show it.
