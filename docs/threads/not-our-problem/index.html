Not our problem

Companies passing responsibility to someone else.

A quieter kind of change: the company stops promising to fix things. Disputes go to another bank, broken purchases go to the developer, support becomes "a courtesy," or the business gets to decide the only way you're allowed to say stop.

None of these raise a price. All of them make it harder to get something put right.

  • TermsGoogle PlayNotableNov 12

    Dec 17 terms in many countries: same Play-services Content change as the US Nov 17 preview

    In Australia, New Zealand and several other countries, Google Play's terms preview is dated Dec 17, not Nov 17. It makes the same Play-services Content change as the US preview.

    deleted: in connection with your use and viewing of Content and Google Play inserted: in connection with Content and Google Play, including any use, installation, viewing, and maintenance on your device of Content and Google Play
    New terms take effect Dec 17 · in 34 daysRead the change →Source ↗ for Google Play
  • TermsNetlifyMajorNov 9

    Dec 4 terms: a verified email domain can see your account and public sites, and may absorb the account after notice

    From Dec 4, an org that verifies your email's domain can see that your Netlify account exists, its plan, and your public sites' names, URLs and last deploy dates. If it also has a Netlify subscription, your account can move under its control unless you change your email in the notice window.

    Takes effect Dec 4 · in 21 daysRead the change →Source ↗ for Netlify
  • TermsBooking.comNotableNov 8

    Price match will pay wallet credits, not a refund

    From Dec 10, Booking.com's US price-match promise changes from a refund of the difference to Wallet Credits. They can still lower your reservation price if possible.

    we promise deleted: to refund inserted: you Wallet Credits for the difference
    Takes effect Dec 10 · in 27 daysRead the change →Source ↗ for Booking.com
  • TermsCanvaNotableOct 30

    Canva adds e-signatures, and its new terms say it doesn't verify who signs and isn't an archive for your signed documents

    Canva's updated Terms of Use (effective Oct 30) point to new e-signature terms: Canva doesn't check signers' identity, bans wills and powers of attorney, and says keep your own copy.

    These Terms of Use are effective as of deleted: 19 August inserted: 30 October 2026.
    Read the change →Source ↗ for Canva
  • TermsFCCMajorOct 24

    From Nov 25, companies can choose the only way you're allowed to stop their robocalls and texts

    From Nov 25, a company can name the only way you're allowed to stop its robocalls and texts, and replying STOP to one kind of alert may not stop the others.

    deleted: may not designate an exclusive means to request revocation of consent inserted: Callers may designate any of the following methods as the exclusive means to revoke consent
    Rule takes effect Nov 25 · in 12 daysRead the change →Source ↗ for FCC
  • TermsSheinNotableOct 21

    New Oct 21 terms call customer support "a courtesy," not an obligation, when carriers, banks or sellers are at fault

    Shein's new terms say helping with problems caused by carriers, banks or marketplace sellers is "a courtesy," not an obligation.

    Read the change →Source ↗ for Shein
  • TermsGoogle PlayNotableOct 20

    Nov 17 terms: report broken purchases to the developer, not Google; Play services now covered

    From Nov 17, Google Play's terms tell you to report broken purchases to the developer instead of Google, and drop "defective" content as a refund example.

    notify deleted: us or the Provider
    New terms take effect Nov 17 · in 4 daysRead the change →Source ↗ for Google Play
  • TermsProtonNotableOct 20

    Inactive free accounts: deletion warnings are now only a "courtesy"

    Proton used to promise warnings before deleting inactive free accounts. Now the warnings are "a mere courtesy" it will "aim" to send.

    Read the change →Source ↗ for Proton
  • TermsUSPSMajorOct 19

    Senders now billed for unpaid foreign duties on packages, even if disputed, with 6% interest

    If a foreign country says duties weren't paid on your package, USPS can now bill you for them, even if you dispute it or the package is lost, with 6% interest.

    deleted: most countries will collect from the recipient
    Comments close Nov 18 · in 5 daysRead the change →Source ↗ for USPS
  • TermsProtonNotableOct 16

    Terms now let Proton discontinue any service or feature without notice

    Proton can now change, suspend or shut down any service or feature, with or without notice. It says it will try to warn you but isn't obliged to.

    Read the change →Source ↗ for Proton
  • TermsU.S. DOTNotableOct 15

    Airline delay-category rule pushed to Feb 1, 2027 (flights from Jan 1)

    DOT's rule letting airlines stop counting ten kinds of disruptions as their fault now starts Feb 1, 2027, so holiday flights stay under the old categories.

    Rule takes effect Feb 1 · in 80 daysRead the change →Source ↗ for U.S. DOT
  • TermsBank of AmericaMajorOct 15

    Narrows error and fraud protection for linked external-account transactions (Oct 19)

    If you pay a Bank of America card or loan from an account at another bank, BofA no longer investigates most errors or fraud on those payments. The other bank has to.

    deleted: if the problem or error relates to a transfer from an account maintained at another financial institution
    Read the change →Source ↗ for Bank of America
