Privacy policy changes in 2026: dated tracker

4 changes logged · Updated October 29, 2026

Privacy policies change more often than most people realize, and the edits that matter (new data sharing, AI training, longer retention) are usually buried in long documents. This page lists every privacy policy change our daily check catches, newest first, with a link to the source.

October 2026

4 changes
  • PrivacySocial SecurityNotableOct 29

    SSA Client Data System (60-0392): new Social Security profile on every SSN holder, comments due Nov 29, 2026

    A new SSA records system keeps one profile per SSN: citizenship, earnings, benefits, marriage history, call notes, alert flags. Email may be used for surveys. Comments close Nov 29.

    inserted: We may also use contact information (e.g., email addresses) provided by individuals who conduct business with SSA for program outreach (e.g., notification about our programs, online services, SSA events, and surveys)
    Comments close Nov 29 · in 30 daysRead the change →Source ↗ for Social Security
  • PrivacyTodoistNotableOct 24

    Privacy policy now says user data isn't used to train any AI models

    Todoist's privacy policy now says your data isn't used to train any AI models. It used to rule out only "generalized" ones.

    Read the change →Source ↗ for Todoist
  • PrivacyVerizonMajorOct 19

    Verizon Lock Screen Rewards: $5/month for keeping "Do not sell or share" off so Glance can target ads

    Verizon will pay up to $5 a month in Verizon Dollars if you let the Glance lock-screen app track you for ads and keep your Do Not Sell settings off.

    inserted: If you consent, Glance may collect, use and share personal information, including sensitive personal information, as described in the Glance AI privacy policy, for purposes such as delivering interest-based advertising.
    Read the change →Source ↗ for Verizon
  • PrivacyGoogleMajorOct 17

    Google privacy policy (Oct 16, 2026): Gmail, Photos and Drive data shared with Search or Gemini can personalize ads

    Google's promise not to use Gmail, Drive and Photos content for ads now has an exception: if you let Search or Gemini read that data, it can shape your ads.

    Read the change →Source ↗ for Google
