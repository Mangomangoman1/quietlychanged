About

How it works

Companies revise their prices, plan limits, terms of service, and privacy policies all the time, usually without announcement. QuietlyChanged keeps a public, dated record of those changes.

Our method

  1. Snapshot. Every morning we fetch the public pricing, terms, and privacy pages of every tracked service and save the main text.
  2. Compare. Each snapshot is compared line by line with the previous day's. We filter out noise such as cookie banners, scripts, timestamps, and copyright lines.
  3. Explain. Meaningful differences are summarized in plain English: what changed, who it affects, and a link to the source.
  4. Publish. The day's report goes on the front page and into the archive.

Categories

Pricing prices, plans, limits, and fees. Terms terms of service, user agreements, and contracts of carriage. Privacy privacy policies and data-use notices.

Limits

Some sites block automated readers or load their text with scripts; we only track pages we can read reliably. Summaries are not legal advice. Always check the linked source.
