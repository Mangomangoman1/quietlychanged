What to do

  1. Find the two charges on your bill. The Administrative and Telco Recovery Charge and the Regulatory Charge are flat per-line monthly charges on US postpaid mobile bills. They are Verizon's own charges, not taxes.
  2. Note November 1 and November 15, 2026. The Regulatory Charge goes from $0.21 to $0.26 per voice line on November 1, and the Administrative and Telco Recovery Charge from $3.78 to $4.69 per voice line on November 15, according to Verizon's Important Plan Information page. Data-only lines stay at $3.97.
  3. Don't count on the price lock for these. The 3-year myPlan price lock excludes taxes, fees and surcharges, so it doesn't cover either charge.
  4. Compare plans with the charges included. Verizon's Important Plan Information page says Verizon One plans include these charges in the plan price, and Verizon Home Internet isn't subject to them. Verizon's surcharge page, which we check daily: verizon.com/support/surcharges.
  5. Follow Verizon here. Tap Follow on this page and new Verizon changes show up under "Your services" on our front page, saved in this browser.

Verizon surcharge price history (US, 2015 to 2026)

Verizon started adding an Administrative Charge to postpaid bills in September 2005 at $0.40 a line. It stayed under a dollar until December 2015, was renamed the Administrative and Telco Recovery Charge in June 2022, and is $3.78 per voice line today. Verizon's Important Plan Information page says it rises to $4.69 on November 15, 2026, two weeks after the Regulatory Charge goes from $0.21 to $0.26. Together, the two flat charges go from $3.99 to $4.95 per voice line a month. The 3-year myPlan price lock doesn't cover them, because it excludes taxes, fees and surcharges.
