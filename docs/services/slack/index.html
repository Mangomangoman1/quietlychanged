Slack price history (US, 2014 to 2026)

Slack kept its Pro plan (originally called Standard) at $8 per user a month, or $6.67 billed annually, from its 2014 launch until September 1, 2022. Business+ went from $12.50 to $15 per user a month in 2025, when Salesforce folded AI features into it. Today Pro is $8.75 monthly or $7.25 annually, and Business+ is $18 monthly or $15 annually.

What Slack costs now

  • Pro (billed monthly): $8.75/user/mo
  • Pro (billed annually): $7.25/user/mo
  • Business+ (billed monthly): $18/user/mo
  • Business+ (billed annually): $15/user/mo

Latest changeOn Jun 17, 2025, Slack raised Business+ from $12.50 to $15 per user a month on annual billing, and from $15 to $18 on monthly billing, adding AI features and Salesforce integrations. Pro prices stayed the same. Business+ customers who bought on Slack's website moved to the new plan at their first renewal after August 17, 2025. (Slack)

  • The September 1, 2022 Pro increase was Slack's first since launching in 2014. Annual Pro customers could lock in $6.67 for another year by renewing before that date.
  • The same day, the Free plan switched from the last 10,000 messages and 5GB of files to the last 90 days of messages and files.
  • Enterprise+ was added in June 2025 as a contact-sales plan. Since October 15, 2026, Slack's pricing page has sometimes shown it at $45 per user a month and sometimes as 'Contact sales', which looks like a test (see our Slack changelog below).
  • Turning AI features off doesn't lower the price of a Slack plan, according to Slack's help center.
  • Enterprise Grid and contracts signed through Slack sales aren't covered here; pricing for those is negotiated.

Quick answers

How much does Slack cost per month in the US?
As of October 19, 2026, Pro (billed monthly) is $8.75/user/mo; Pro (billed annually) is $7.25/user/mo; Business+ (billed monthly) is $18/user/mo; Business+ (billed annually) is $15/user/mo.

When did Slack last raise prices?
On Jun 17, 2025: Slack raised Business+ from $12.50 to $15 per user a month on annual billing, and from $15 to $18 on monthly billing, adding AI features and Salesforce integrations. Pro prices stayed the same. Business+ customers who bought on Slack's website moved to the new plan at their first renewal after August 17, 2025.

How much has Slack gone up?
Slack Pro (billed monthly) went from $8/user/mo in 2014 to $8.75/user/mo, an increase of 9% across 1 price change.
