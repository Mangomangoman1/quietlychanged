YouTube Premium price history (US, 2015 to 2026)

YouTube's ad-free subscription launched as YouTube Red at $9.99 a month in 2015. Renamed YouTube Premium in 2018, the individual plan is now $15.99, and the family plan has gone from $17.99 to $26.99 since 2022.

What YouTube Premium costs now

  • Individual: $15.99/month
  • Family: $26.99/month
  • Lite: $8.99/month

Latest changeOn Apr 10, 2026, YouTube Premium Individual went from $13.99 to $15.99, Family from $22.99 to $26.99, and Premium Lite from $7.99 to $8.99 a month. New sign-ups paid the new prices right away; existing subscribers were emailed and moved over at a later billing date. The annual individual plan went from $139.99 to $159.99. (The Verge)
