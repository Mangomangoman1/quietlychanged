YouTube Premium price history (US, 2015 to 2026)

YouTube's ad-free subscription launched as YouTube Red at $9.99 a month in 2015. Renamed YouTube Premium in 2018, the individual plan is now $15.99, and the family plan has gone from $17.99 to $26.99 since 2022.

What YouTube Premium costs now

  • Individual$15.99per month
  • Family$26.99per month
  • Lite$8.99per month
Latest changeOn Apr 10, 2026, YouTube Premium Individual went from $13.99 to $15.99, Family from $22.99 to $26.99, and Premium Lite from $7.99 to $8.99 a month. New sign-ups paid the new prices right away; existing subscribers were emailed and moved over at a later billing date. The annual individual plan went from $139.99 to $159.99. (The Verge)

Quick answers

How much does YouTube Premium cost per month in the US?
As of October 18, 2026, Individual is $15.99/month; Family is $26.99/month; Lite is $8.99/month.

When did YouTube Premium last raise prices?
On Apr 10, 2026: YouTube Premium Individual went from $13.99 to $15.99, Family from $22.99 to $26.99, and Premium Lite from $7.99 to $8.99 a month. New sign-ups paid the new prices right away; existing subscribers were emailed and moved over at a later billing date. The annual individual plan went from $139.99 to $159.99.

How much has YouTube Premium gone up?
YouTube Premium Individual went from $9.99/month in Oct 2015 to $15.99/month, an increase of 60% across 3 price changes.

What is the YouTube Premium price increase history?
YouTube's ad-free plan launched as YouTube Red at $9.99 a month in October 2015. It became YouTube Premium at $11.99 in June 2018, then went to $13.99 in July 2023 and $15.99 on April 10, 2026. The Family plan went from $17.99 (June 2018) to $22.99 on October 20, 2022 and $26.99 on April 10, 2026. Premium Lite launched at $7.99 in March 2025 and went to $8.99 on April 10, 2026. The annual Individual plan went from $139.99 to $159.99 in April 2026.

Is YouTube Premium cheaper on the website or the iPhone app?
Subscribing on YouTube's website is cheaper. The prices above are for paying on the web; signing up through Apple's App Store costs more each month.
