Hulu price history (US, 2010 to 2026)

Hulu's ad-free plan launched at $11.99 a month in 2015 and is now $21.49, after five increases. The plan with ads, which Hulu cut to $5.99 in 2019, has since doubled to $12.49.

What Hulu costs now

  • No Ads: $21.49/month
  • With ads: $12.49/month

Latest changeOn Sep 23, 2026, Hulu (No Ads) went from $18.99 to $21.49 and Hulu with ads from $11.99 to $12.49 for new subscribers, with existing subscribers moving to the new rates at their next billing date. The Disney+/Hulu bundle with ads stayed at $12.99. (CBS News)
