What to do

  1. Check what you pay. Sign in to your HBO Max account and open the plan or billing page. Compare it with the current US list prices in our history: Basic with Ads $10.99/month; Standard $18.49/month; Premium $22.99/month; Standard (annual) $184.99/year.
  2. Set a reminder a few days before your next billing date. Renewals are charged on that date, so a reminder gives you time to switch plans or cancel before the next charge.
  3. To downgrade or cancel, use HBO Max's own account settings, or the app store, carrier or bundle you pay through if you subscribed that way. HBO Max's official plans page, which we check daily: https://help.hbomax.com/plans.
  4. Follow HBO Max here. Tap Follow on this page and new HBO Max changes show up under "Your services" on our front page, saved in this browser.

HBO Max price history (US, 2020 to 2026)

HBO Max launched in May 2020 at $14.99 a month with no ads. It became Max in May 2023 and HBO Max again in July 2025. The ad-free Standard plan is now $18.49, the 4K Premium plan $22.99, and the ad-supported plan $10.99, after the latest increase on October 21, 2025.

New subscribers paid the new prices right away; existing monthly subscribers moved over at their first billing date on or after November 20, 2025. (CNBC)

Quick answers

How much does HBO Max cost per month in the US?
As of October 26, 2026, Basic with Ads is $10.99/month; Standard is $18.49/month; Premium is $22.99/month; Standard (annual) is $184.99/year.

When did HBO Max last raise prices?
On Oct 21, 2025: HBO Max raised every plan: Basic with Ads from $9.99 to $10.99, Standard from $16.99 to $18.49 and Premium from $20.99 to $22.99 a month. New subscribers paid the new prices right away; existing monthly subscribers moved over at their first billing date on or after November 20, 2025.

How much has HBO Max gone up?
HBO Max Standard went from $14.99/month in May 2020 to $18.49/month, an increase of 23% across 3 price changes.

What is the HBO Max price increase history?
HBO Max's ad-free plan (now called Standard) launched at $14.99 a month in May 2020 and has gone up three times: to $15.99 on January 12, 2023, $16.99 on June 4, 2024 and $18.49 on October 21, 2025. The ad-supported plan (now Basic with Ads) went from $9.99 at its June 2, 2021 launch to $10.99 on October 21, 2025. The 4K Premium plan went from $19.99 at Max's May 23, 2023 launch to $20.99 on June 4, 2024 and $22.99 on October 21, 2025. Standard billed yearly went from $149.99 (May 2023) to $169.99 (June 2024) to $184.99 (October 2025).

Is HBO Max raising prices again?
No new standalone HBO Max increase had been announced as of October 26, 2026, and HBO Max's own plans page still listed $10.99, $18.49 and $22.99 a month that day. After Paramount Skydance's merger with Warner Bros. Discovery closed in October 2026, the company said HBO Max and Paramount+ will be combined over time, but it hasn't said what a combined service would cost.
