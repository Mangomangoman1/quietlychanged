HBO Max price history (US, 2020 to 2026)

HBO Max launched in May 2020 at $14.99 a month with no ads. It became Max in May 2023 and HBO Max again in July 2025. The ad-free Standard plan is now $18.49, the 4K Premium plan $22.99, and the ad-supported plan $10.99, after the latest increase on October 21, 2025.

What HBO Max costs now

  • Basic with Ads: $10.99/month
  • Standard: $18.49/month
  • Premium: $22.99/month
  • Standard (annual): $184.99/year

Latest changeOn Oct 21, 2025, HBO Max raised every plan: Basic with Ads from $9.99 to $10.99, Standard from $16.99 to $18.49 and Premium from $20.99 to $22.99 a month. New subscribers paid the new prices right away; existing monthly subscribers moved over at their first billing date on or after November 20, 2025. (CNBC)

  • Plan names have changed with the brand: the ad-supported plan was HBO Max With Ads, then Max Ad-Lite, now Basic with Ads; Standard was the Ad-Free plan; Premium was Max Ultimate Ad Free, added when Max launched on May 23, 2023.
  • Max was renamed back to HBO Max on July 9, 2025, with no change to plans or prices.
  • After Paramount Skydance completed its merger with Warner Bros. Discovery, David Ellison said on October 7, 2026 that HBO Max and Paramount+ will be run separately for now and combined over time. No price change for HBO Max has been announced.
  • Current prices from HBO Max's own plans page: Basic with Ads $10.99/month or $109.99/year, Standard $18.49 or $184.99, Premium $22.99 or $229.99, before tax.
