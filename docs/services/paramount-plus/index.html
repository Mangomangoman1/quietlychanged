Paramount+ price history (US, 2021 to 2026)

Paramount+ replaced CBS All Access on March 4, 2021, with an ad-free plan at $9.99 a month, and added a $4.99 ad-supported Essential plan that June. After three increases to each plan, Essential is $8.99 and the ad-free Premium plan is $13.99, both raised on January 15, 2026.

What Paramount+ costs now

  • Essential: $8.99/month
  • Premium: $13.99/month
  • Essential (annual): $89.99/year
  • Premium (annual): $139.99/year

Latest changeOn Jan 15, 2026, Paramount+ Essential went from $7.99 to $8.99 and Premium from $12.99 to $13.99 a month. Annual plans rose more: Essential from $59.99 to $89.99 a year and Premium from $119.99 to $139.99. (Variety)

  • Premium was called Paramount+ with Showtime from June 27, 2023 (it replaced the $9.99 ad-free plan at $11.99) until 2025, when it was renamed Paramount+ Premium.
  • The $4.99 Essential plan replaced CBS All Access's $5.99 plan with commercials and, unlike it, left out live local CBS stations.
  • In August 2024, existing monthly Essential subscribers kept $5.99; the $7.99 price applied to new subscribers.
  • After Paramount Skydance completed its merger with Warner Bros. Discovery, David Ellison said on October 7, 2026 that Paramount+ and HBO Max will be run separately for now and combined over time. No new Paramount+ price has been announced.
  • Current monthly prices ($8.99 Essential, $13.99 Premium) confirmed on paramountplus.com on October 19, 2026.
