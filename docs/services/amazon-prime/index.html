What to do

  1. Check what you pay. Sign in to your Amazon Prime account and open the plan or billing page. Compare it with the current US list prices in our history: Annual $139/year; Monthly $14.99/month; Prime Video (standalone) $8.99/month.
  2. Set a reminder a few days before your next billing date. Renewals are charged on that date, so a reminder gives you time to switch plans or cancel before the next charge.
  3. To downgrade or cancel, use Amazon Prime's own account settings, or the app store, carrier or bundle you pay through if you subscribed that way. We don't have a verified link to Amazon Prime's cancellation page, so we don't link one; start from Amazon Prime's official website or app.
  4. Follow Amazon Prime here. Tap Follow on this page and new Amazon Prime changes show up under "Your services" on our front page, saved in this browser.

Amazon Prime price history (US, 2005 to 2026)

Amazon Prime launched at $79 a year in 2005 and now costs $139 a year or $14.99 a month. Amazon has raised the membership about every four years, most recently in February 2022, and has not announced an increase in 2026.

What Amazon Prime costs now

  • Annual$139per year
  • Monthly$14.99per month
  • Prime Video (standalone)$8.99per month
Latest changeOn Feb 18, 2022, Prime went from $119 to $139 a year and from $12.99 to $14.99 a month for new members. Ad-free Prime Video costs extra on top of that: $2.99 a month from January 2024, renamed Prime Video Ultra at $4.99 a month in April 2026. (BasketPrice)
