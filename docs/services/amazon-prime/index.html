Amazon Prime price history (US, 2005 to 2026)

Amazon Prime launched at $79 a year in 2005 and now costs $139 a year or $14.99 a month. Amazon has raised the membership about every four years, most recently in February 2022, and has not announced an increase in 2026.

What Amazon Prime costs now

  • Annual: $139/year
  • Monthly: $14.99/month
  • Prime Video (standalone): $8.99/month

Latest changeOn Feb 18, 2022, Prime went from $119 to $139 a year and from $12.99 to $14.99 a month for new members. Ad-free Prime Video costs extra on top of that: $2.99 a month from January 2024, renamed Prime Video Ultra at $4.99 a month in April 2026. (BasketPrice)
