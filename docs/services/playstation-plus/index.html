PlayStation Plus price history (US, 2022 to 2026)

Sony has raised PlayStation Plus prices twice since it relaunched the service with three tiers in June 2022. On September 6, 2023, 12-month plans went up by $20 to $40 (Essential from $59.99 to $79.99 a year). On May 20, 2026, the 1- and 3-month plans went up for new subscribers: Essential to $10.99 a month, Extra to $16.99 and Premium to $19.99, while 12-month prices stayed where they were. Sony said existing subscribers keep their price unless "the existing subscription changes or lapses."

What PlayStation Plus costs now

  • Essential (1 month): $10.99/month
  • Extra (1 month): $16.99/month
  • Premium (1 month): $19.99/month
  • Essential (12 months): $79.99/year
  • Extra (12 months): $134.99/year
  • Premium (12 months): $159.99/year

Quick answers

How much does PlayStation Plus cost per month in the US?
As of October 22, 2026, Essential (1 month) is $10.99/month; Extra (1 month) is $16.99/month; Premium (1 month) is $19.99/month; Essential (12 months) is $79.99/year; Extra (12 months) is $134.99/year; Premium (12 months) is $159.99/year.

When did PlayStation Plus last raise prices?
On May 20, 2026: 1-month and 3-month PlayStation Plus plans went up for new subscribers: Essential $9.99 to $10.99 a month ($24.99 to $27.99 for 3 months), Extra $14.99 to $16.99 ($39.99 to $43.99) and Premium $17.99 to $19.99 ($49.99 to $54.99). 12-month plans did not change.

How much has PlayStation Plus gone up?
Since the June 2022 relaunch, the 1-month Essential plan has gone from $9.99 to $10.99 (+10%), Extra from $14.99 to $16.99 and Premium from $17.99 to $19.99. The bigger jumps were on 12-month plans in September 2023: Essential $59.99 to $79.99 (+33%), Extra $99.99 to $134.99 (+35%) and Premium $119.99 to $159.99 (+33%).
