What to do

  1. Check what you pay. Sign in to your Peacock account and open the plan or billing page. Compare it with the current US list prices in our history: Premium (with ads) $12.99/month; Premium Plus (no ads) $19.99/month; Select $8.99/month.
  2. Set a reminder a few days before your next billing date. Renewals are charged on that date, so a reminder gives you time to switch plans or cancel before the next charge.
  3. To downgrade or cancel, use Peacock's own account settings, or the app store, carrier or bundle you pay through if you subscribed that way. We don't have a verified link to Peacock's cancellation page, so we don't link one; start from Peacock's official website or app.
  4. Follow Peacock here. Tap Follow on this page and new Peacock changes show up under "Your services" on our front page, saved in this browser.

Peacock price history (US, 2020 to 2026)

Peacock has raised prices every summer since 2023. Its main plan, Premium (with ads), launched at $4.99 a month in July 2020 and is now $12.99, more than two and a half times the launch price.
