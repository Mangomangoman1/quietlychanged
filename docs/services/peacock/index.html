Peacock price history (US, 2020 to 2026)

Peacock has raised prices every summer since 2023. Its main plan, Premium (with ads), launched at $4.99 a month in July 2020 and is now $12.99, more than two and a half times the launch price.

What Peacock costs now

  • Premium (with ads)$12.99per month
  • Premium Plus (no ads)$19.99per month
  • Select$8.99per month
Latest changeOn Aug 18, 2026, Premium went from $10.99 to $12.99, Premium Plus from $16.99 to $19.99, and Select from $7.99 to $8.99 a month for new subscribers. People who subscribed before Aug 18 moved to the new prices at their first billing date on or after Sep 17. (Deadline)
