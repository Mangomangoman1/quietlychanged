Spotify price history (US, 2011 to 2026)

Spotify Premium Individual cost $9.99 a month in the US for twelve years, from its 2011 launch until July 2023. It has gone up three times since, and is now $12.99.

What Spotify costs now

  • Individual: $12.99/month
  • Duo: $18.99/month
  • Family: $21.99/month

Latest changeOn Jan 15, 2026, Spotify announced US increases to Individual $12.99, Duo $18.99, and Family $21.99, with existing subscribers notified by email over the following month. Our own daily snapshot of spotify.com/us/premium shows these prices today. (Spotify Newsroom)
