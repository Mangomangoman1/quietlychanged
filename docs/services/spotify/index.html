Spotify price history (US, 2011 to 2026)

Spotify Premium Individual cost $9.99 a month in the US for twelve years, from its 2011 launch until July 2023. It has gone up three times since, and is now $12.99.

Quick answers

How much does Spotify cost per month in the US?
As of October 27, 2026, Individual is $12.99/month; Duo is $18.99/month; Family is $21.99/month.

When did Spotify last raise prices?
On Jan 15, 2026: Spotify announced US increases to Individual $12.99, Duo $18.99, and Family $21.99, with existing subscribers notified by email over the following month. Our own daily snapshot of spotify.com/us/premium shows these prices today.

How much has Spotify gone up?
Spotify Individual went from $9.99/month on Jul 14, 2011 to $12.99/month, an increase of 30% across 3 price changes.

What is the Spotify price increase history?
Spotify Premium Individual cost $9.99 a month in the US from its July 14, 2011 launch until July 24, 2023, when it went to $10.99. It rose to $11.99 on June 3, 2024 and $12.99 on January 15, 2026. Duo went from $12.99 (July 2020) to $14.99, $16.99 and $18.99 on the same three dates. Family went from $14.99 (October 2014) to $15.99 in April 2021, $16.99 on July 24, 2023, $19.99 on June 3, 2024 and $21.99 on January 15, 2026.

How often does Spotify raise prices?
For twelve years it didn't: Individual stayed at $9.99 from 2011 to 2023. Since then Spotify has raised US Premium prices three times in about two and a half years (July 2023, June 2024 and January 2026), each time by $1 a month for Individual.
