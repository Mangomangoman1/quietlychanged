Changelog

  • Terms

    Contract moves to Italian affiliate Dec 1; Brazil users lose local law and get arbitration; no password-manager use

    Evernote posted Terms of Service dated "Last update: October 1, 2026," replacing the January 1, 2026 version. An Evernote help article updated the same day says that if you signed up before October 1, 2026, the changes take effect on December 1, 2026; for everyone else they are already in force. The help article's main points: your contract transfers to Bending Spoons Operations S.p.A., an Italian company in Milan; legal terms now come in two versions (EEA/UK/Switzerland vs everywhere else, with Evernote Enterprise always on the second); Brazil users no longer contract with Evernote do Brasil or use Brazilian law, and instead get the non-EEA terms "including their governing law and arbitration agreement"; EEA/UK/Switzerland users get Italian law and no arbitration agreement; software license terms are folded into the Terms of Service; Evernote Points are no longer issued. The live Terms also add an AI clause: what you provide to AI features, and the results, are yours, and "We do not use what you provide to, or receive from, those features to train artificial intelligence models or tools." Buried in the conduct rules is a new ban that was not in the January 1, 2026 Terms: "you will not use the Evernote Service to back up your hard drive or to store or archive files" and "you will not use the Evernote Service as a password manager or to store sensitive financial information." The Terms also newly say Evernote "may use human and automated means to screen, monitor, and detect Content" that may be unlawful or violate the Terms. Coverage: no news articles found; only Evernote's own help page.
