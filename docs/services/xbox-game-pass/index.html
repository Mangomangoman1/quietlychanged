Xbox Game Pass price history (US, 2017 to 2026)

Xbox Game Pass Ultimate cost $14.99 a month from its June 2019 launch until July 2023. Microsoft then raised it three times in a little over two years: to $16.99 in July 2023, $19.99 in July 2024 and $29.99 on October 1, 2025, a 50% jump in one step. On April 21, 2026, Microsoft reversed part of that, cutting Ultimate to $22.99 and PC Game Pass from $16.49 to $13.99, and said new Call of Duty games would join those plans about a year after launch instead of on day one. The next change is not a price: from November 2026, cloud gaming on every plan gets a monthly hour limit.

Quick answers

How much does Xbox Game Pass cost per month in the US?
As of October 22, 2026, Ultimate is $22.99/month; PC Game Pass is $13.99/month; Premium (formerly Standard) is $14.99/month; Essential (formerly Core) is $9.99/month.

When did Xbox Game Pass last raise prices?
The last increase was on October 1, 2025, when Ultimate went from $19.99 to $29.99 a month and PC Game Pass from $11.99 to $16.49. Since then prices have come down: on April 21, 2026, Microsoft cut Ultimate to $22.99 and PC Game Pass to $13.99. From November 2026, each plan also includes only a set number of cloud gaming hours a month (15 on Ultimate, 10 on Premium, 5 on Essential).

How much has Xbox Game Pass gone up?
Game Pass Ultimate launched at $14.99 a month in June 2019 and costs $22.99 today, up 53%. It rose to $16.99 in July 2023, $19.99 in July 2024 and $29.99 on October 1, 2025, then was cut to $22.99 on April 21, 2026.
