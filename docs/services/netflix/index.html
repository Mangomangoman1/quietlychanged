Netflix price history (US, 2011 to 2026)

Netflix has raised US prices roughly every one to two years since it split streaming from DVD rentals in 2011. Its main ad-free plan (Standard) has gone from $7.99 to $19.99 a month, and that plan has never gotten cheaper.

What Netflix costs now

  • Standard with ads$8.99per month
  • Standard$19.99per month
  • Premium$26.99per month
Latest changeOn Mar 26, 2026, all three US plans went up: Standard with ads $7.99 to $8.99, Standard $17.99 to $19.99, Premium $24.99 to $26.99. Extra members rose to $7.99 (with ads) and $9.99 (ad-free). (Reuters)
