What to do

  1. Check what you pay. Sign in to your Netflix account and open the plan or billing page. Compare it with the current US list prices in our history: Standard with ads $8.99/month; Standard $19.99/month; Premium $26.99/month.
  2. Set a reminder a few days before your next billing date. Renewals are charged on that date, so a reminder gives you time to switch plans or cancel before the next charge.
  3. To downgrade or cancel, use Netflix's own account settings, or the app store, carrier or bundle you pay through if you subscribed that way. Netflix's official plans page, which we check daily: https://help.netflix.com/en/node/24926.
  4. Follow Netflix here. Tap Follow on this page and new Netflix changes show up under "Your services" on our front page, saved in this browser.

Netflix price history (US, 2011 to 2026)

Netflix has raised US prices roughly every one to two years since it split streaming from DVD rentals in 2011. Its main ad-free plan (Standard) has gone from $7.99 to $19.99 a month, and that plan has never gotten cheaper.

Quick answers

How much does Netflix cost per month in the US?
As of October 27, 2026, Standard with ads is $8.99/month; Standard is $19.99/month; Premium is $26.99/month.

When did Netflix last raise prices?
On Mar 26, 2026: all three US plans went up: Standard with ads $7.99 to $8.99, Standard $17.99 to $19.99, Premium $24.99 to $26.99. Extra members rose to $7.99 (with ads) and $9.99 (ad-free).

How much has Netflix gone up?
Netflix Standard went from $7.99/month on Jul 12, 2011 to $19.99/month, an increase of 150% across 8 price changes.

What is the Netflix price increase history?
Netflix's ad-free Standard plan cost $7.99 a month when Netflix split streaming from DVD rentals on July 12, 2011. It then went to $8.99 on May 9, 2014, $9.99 on October 8, 2015, $10.99 on October 5, 2017, $12.99 on January 15, 2019, $13.99 on October 29, 2020, $15.49 on January 14, 2022, $17.99 on January 21, 2025 and $19.99 on March 26, 2026. Premium went from $11.99 (April 2013) to $13.99 (October 2017), $15.99 (January 2019), $17.99 (October 2020), $19.99 (January 2022), $22.99 (October 2023), $24.99 (January 21, 2025) and $26.99 (March 26, 2026). Standard with ads launched at $6.99 in November 2022, then went to $7.99 on January 21, 2025 and $8.99 on March 26, 2026.

How often does Netflix raise prices?
About every one to two years. Standard went up in 2014, 2015, 2017, 2019, 2020, 2022, 2025 and 2026. The longest gap was three years, from January 2022 to January 2025, and the most recent increase came only 14 months after the one before it. Netflix's Standard price has never gone down.

What happened to the Netflix Basic plan?
Basic, the cheapest ad-free plan, went from $7.99 (2014) to $8.99 (2019), $9.99 (2022) and $11.99 (October 2023). It closed to new US members on July 19, 2023 and ended for remaining members on July 18, 2024, so the cheapest ad-free Netflix plan is now Standard at $19.99.
