What to do

  1. Check what you pay. Sign in to your Apple TV account and open the plan or billing page. Compare it with the current US list prices in our history: Monthly $14.99/month; Annual $119/year.
  2. Set a reminder a few days before your next billing date. Renewals are charged on that date, so a reminder gives you time to switch plans or cancel before the next charge.
  3. To downgrade or cancel, use Apple TV's own account settings, or the app store, carrier or bundle you pay through if you subscribed that way. Apple TV's official plans page, which we check daily: https://www.apple.com/apple-tv-plus/.
  4. Follow Apple TV here. Tap Follow on this page and new Apple TV changes show up under "Your services" on our front page, saved in this browser.

Apple TV (Apple TV+) price history (US, 2019 to 2026)

Apple TV+ launched in November 2019 at $4.99 a month and was renamed Apple TV in October 2025. It has now gone up four times, most recently to $14.99 a month and $119 a year on August 28, 2026, three times its launch price.

What Apple TV costs now

  • Monthly$14.99per month
  • Annual$119per year
Latest changeOn Aug 28, 2026, Apple TV went from $12.99 to $14.99 a month and from $99 to $119 a year, and Apple One Individual from $19.95 to $21.95. New subscribers paid the new price right away; Apple said existing subscribers would be notified about a month before being charged it. (MacRumors)
