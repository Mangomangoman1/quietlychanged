Apple TV (Apple TV+) price history (US, 2019 to 2026)

Apple TV+ launched in November 2019 at $4.99 a month and was renamed Apple TV in October 2025. It has now gone up four times, most recently to $14.99 a month and $119 a year on August 28, 2026, three times its launch price.

What Apple TV costs now

  • Monthly: $14.99/month
  • Annual: $119/year

Latest changeOn Aug 28, 2026, Apple TV went from $12.99 to $14.99 a month and from $99 to $119 a year, and Apple One Individual from $19.95 to $21.95. New subscribers paid the new price right away; Apple said existing subscribers would be notified about a month before being charged it. (MacRumors)
