Disney+ price history (US, 2019 to 2026)

Disney+ launched in the US at $6.99 a month in November 2019. The ad-free plan is now $21.49, up more than 200%, with at least one price increase every year since 2022.

What Disney+ costs now

  • Basic (with ads): $12.49/month
  • Premium (no ads): $21.49/month

Latest changeOn Sep 23, 2026, ad-free Disney+ Premium went from $18.99 to $21.49 and Disney+ with ads from $11.99 to $12.49 for new subscribers; existing subscribers moved to the new price on their next monthly bill. (CBS News)
