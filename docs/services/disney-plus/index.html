What to do

  1. Check what you pay. Sign in to your Disney+ account and open the plan or billing page. Compare it with the current US list prices in our history: Basic (with ads) $12.49/month; Premium (no ads) $21.49/month.
  2. Set a reminder a few days before your next billing date. Renewals are charged on that date, so a reminder gives you time to switch plans or cancel before the next charge.
  3. To downgrade or cancel, use Disney+'s own account settings, or the app store, carrier or bundle you pay through if you subscribed that way. We don't have a verified link to Disney+'s cancellation page, so we don't link one; start from Disney+'s official website or app.
  4. Follow Disney+ here. Tap Follow on this page and new Disney+ changes show up under "Your services" on our front page, saved in this browser.

Disney+ price history (US, 2019 to 2026)

Disney+ launched in the US at $6.99 a month in November 2019. The ad-free plan is now $21.49, up more than 200%, with at least one price increase every year since 2022.

Quick answers

How much does Disney+ cost per month in the US?
As of October 17, 2026, Basic (with ads) is $12.49/month; Premium (no ads) is $21.49/month.

When did Disney+ last raise prices?
On Sep 23, 2026: ad-free Disney+ Premium went from $18.99 to $21.49 and Disney+ with ads from $11.99 to $12.49 for new subscribers; existing subscribers moved to the new price on their next monthly bill.

How much has Disney+ gone up?
Disney+ Premium (no ads) went from $6.99/month on Nov 12, 2019 to $21.49/month, an increase of 207% across 6 price changes.

What is the Disney+ price change history?
Ad-free Disney+ (now Premium) launched at $6.99 a month on November 12, 2019, then went to $7.99 on March 26, 2021, $10.99 on December 8, 2022, $13.99 on October 12, 2023, $15.99 on October 17, 2024, $18.99 on October 21, 2025 and $21.49 on September 23, 2026. Disney+ with ads launched at $7.99 on December 8, 2022, then went to $9.99 on October 17, 2024, $11.99 on October 21, 2025 and $12.49 on September 23, 2026. Dates are when new subscribers started paying the new price.
