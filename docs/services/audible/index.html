Audible price history (US, 2020 to 2026)

Audible has not raised the price of its main plan in years: Premium Plus, with one credit a month, has been $14.95 since Audible renamed its Gold plan in August 2020, and Gold cost the same before that. What changed in 2026 was the cheapest way in. On March 3, 2026, Audible launched a $8.99 Standard plan and stopped selling the $7.95 Audible Plus plan to new members, so the lowest price a new customer can pay went up by $1.04, while existing Plus members kept their plan.

  • Audible Plus launched in preview on August 24, 2020, with sign-ups for new customers starting August 27. The same day, Audible Gold and Platinum were folded into Premium Plus, keeping their prices.
  • Standard does not give credits. The Verge corrected its launch story to say so: you pick one audiobook a month and lose access to it if you cancel, unlike Premium Plus credits, which buy titles you keep.
  • Our daily snapshot of Audible's US membership page on October 21, 2026 shows Standard at $8.99 a month, Premium Plus at $14.95 a month, and annual Premium Plus at $149.50 and $229.50 a year. Since October 16, 2026, it has also shown a limited-time offer of $99 for a year of Premium.

Quick answers

How much does Audible cost per month in the US?
As of October 21, 2026, Premium Plus (1 credit a month) is $14.95/month; Premium Plus (2 credits a month) is $22.95/month; Premium Plus annual (12 credits) is $149.50/year; Premium Plus annual (24 credits) is $229.50/year; Standard is $8.99/month.

When did Audible last raise prices?
Audible has not raised Premium Plus, which has been $14.95 a month since August 2020. The closest thing to an increase came on March 3, 2026, when Audible stopped selling the $7.95 Audible Plus plan to new members and launched an $8.99 Standard plan, so the cheapest plan for new members went from $7.95 to $8.99.

How much has Audible gone up?
Premium Plus has not gone up: it was $14.95 a month when it replaced Audible Gold in August 2020 and is still $14.95. The entry price for new members rose from $7.95 (Audible Plus) to $8.99 (Standard) on March 3, 2026.
