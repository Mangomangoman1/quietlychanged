Audible price history (2020–2026): Premium Plus, Standard and Plus prices

Tracked since October 13, 2026 · Last snapshot October 24, 2026

QuietlyChanged checks Audible's public price pages every day and records what changes, in plain English.

Audible price history (US, 2020 to 2026)

Audible has not raised the price of its main plan in years: Premium Plus, with one credit a month, has been $14.95 since Audible renamed its Gold plan in August 2020, and Gold cost the same before that. What changed in 2026 was the cheapest way in. On March 3, 2026, Audible launched a $8.99 Standard plan and stopped selling the $7.95 Audible Plus plan to new members, so the lowest price a new customer can pay went up by $1.04, while existing Plus members kept their plan.

What Audible costs now

  • Premium Plus (1 credit a month): $14.95/month
  • Premium Plus (2 credits a month): $22.95/month
  • Premium Plus annual (12 credits): $149.50/year
  • Premium Plus annual (24 credits): $229.50/year
  • Standard: $8.99/month

Latest changeOn Mar 3, 2026, Audible launched a Standard plan at $8.99 a month in the US (one audiobook a month that you can listen to while you stay a member, plus an unlimited catalog) and closed the $7.95 Audible Plus plan to new members. Premium Plus stayed at $14.95. (Audible newsroom)

  • Audible Plus launched in preview on August 24, 2020, with sign-ups for new customers starting August 27. The same day, Audible Gold and Platinum were folded into Premium Plus, keeping their prices.
  • Standard does not give credits. The Verge corrected its launch story to say so: you pick one audiobook a month and lose access to it if you cancel, unlike Premium Plus credits, which buy titles you keep.
  • Our daily snapshot of Audible's US membership page on October 21, 2026 shows Standard at $8.99 a month, Premium Plus at $14.95 a month, and annual Premium Plus at $149.50 and $229.50 a year. Since October 16, 2026, it has also shown a limited-time offer of $99 for a year of Premium.

Quick answers

How much does Audible cost per month in the US?
As of October 21, 2026, Premium Plus (1 credit a month) is $14.95/month; Premium Plus (2 credits a month) is $22.95/month; Premium Plus annual (12 credits) is $149.50/year; Premium Plus annual (24 credits) is $229.50/year; Standard is $8.99/month.

When did Audible last raise prices?
Audible has not raised Premium Plus, which has been $14.95 a month since August 2020. The closest thing to an increase came on March 3, 2026, when Audible stopped selling the $7.95 Audible Plus plan to new members and launched an $8.99 Standard plan, so the cheapest plan for new members went from $7.95 to $8.99.

How much has Audible gone up?
Premium Plus has not gone up: it was $14.95 a month when it replaced Audible Gold in August 2020 and is still $14.95. The entry price for new members rose from $7.95 (Audible Plus) to $8.99 (Standard) on March 3, 2026.

Changelog

  • Pricing

    Standard plan card now says unused monthly picks don't roll over

    Audible's membership page now states on the Standard plan card ($8.99/mo after a 30-day trial, 1 audiobook a month): "Unused monthly audiobook selections don't roll over." The same rule was already in the page's FAQ. The $229.50-a-year Premium plan is now labeled "24 audiobooks" (it previously showed a Spanish "12 creditos/año" label). Prices are unchanged: Premium $14.95/mo, Premium Annual $149.50/yr for 12 audiobooks.

    October 23, 2026Source →
  • Pricing

    Limited-time offer: $99 for a year of Premium

    Audible's membership plans page added a new banner: "LIMITED TIME OFFER | $99 for a year of Premium. That's like $8.25/mo. Terms apply." The regular annual Premium prices listed on the same page are unchanged at $149.50 a year for 12 credits and $229.50 a year for 24 credits, and monthly Premium is still $14.95. The page doesn't spell out the offer's terms or end date.

    October 16, 2026Source →
  • Pricing

    Annual plans: cancellation now effective next billing cycle

    Audible's plan page now says its annual Premium plans ($149.50/yr for 12 credits, $229.50/yr for 24 credits) can be canceled anytime "effective next billing cycle," where it previously just said "Cancel anytime." The "Save 16%" labels on the annual plans were also removed. Prices did not change.
