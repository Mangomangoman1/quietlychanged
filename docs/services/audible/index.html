Changelog

  • Pricing

    Limited-time offer: $99 for a year of Premium

    Audible's membership plans page added a new banner: "LIMITED TIME OFFER | $99 for a year of Premium. That's like $8.25/mo. Terms apply." The regular annual Premium prices listed on the same page are unchanged at $149.50 a year for 12 credits and $229.50 a year for 24 credits, and monthly Premium is still $14.95. The page doesn't spell out the offer's terms or end date.

  • Pricing

    Annual plans: cancellation now effective next billing cycle

    Audible's plan page now says its annual Premium plans ($149.50/yr for 12 credits, $229.50/yr for 24 credits) can be canceled anytime "effective next billing cycle," where it previously just said "Cancel anytime." The "Save 16%" labels on the annual plans were also removed. Prices did not change.
