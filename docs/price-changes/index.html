Subscription price increases & pricing changes in 2026 (tracker)

9 changes logged · Updated October 16, 2026

Price hikes rarely come with a press release. They show up as a new number on a pricing page, a fee line on a support page, or a plan that quietly loses a feature. This page collects every pricing change our daily check has caught, newest first, with the date we saw it and a link to the source.

October 2026

  • Pricing Cloudflare

    Cloudflare Pipelines is no longer free: Basin pricing is $0.04/GB transforms, $0.03-$0.06/GB delivery

    Cloudflare's plans page used to list Pipelines ingestion and delivery to R2 as "TBD (free during beta)". It now shows real prices for the product, now part of the Cloudflare Basin family. Stream ingress is free and unlimited. SQL transforms include 50 GB a month, then $0.04 per GB. Sink delivery includes 50 GB a month shared across sinks, then $0.03 per GB for JSON or $0.06 per GB for Parquet or Iceberg. The page also adds catalog pricing (1 million operations a month included, then $9 per million; compaction at $0.005 per GB after 10 GB and $2 per million objects after 1 million) and query pricing (10 GB of compressed data scanned a month included, then $0.0025 per GB, which is $2.50 per TB, with a 10 MB minimum per query). R2 storage and operations are billed separately.

  • Pricing Audible

    Audible offer: $99 for a year of Premium (vs. $149.50 regular annual price)

    Audible's membership plans page added a new banner: "LIMITED TIME OFFER | $99 for a year of Premium. That's like $8.25/mo. Terms apply." The regular annual Premium prices listed on the same page are unchanged at $149.50 a year for 12 credits and $229.50 a year for 24 credits, and monthly Premium is still $14.95. The page doesn't spell out the offer's terms or end date.

    October 16, 2026Audible history →
  • Pricing Shopify

    Shopify raised annual prices: Grow now $89/mo, Advanced $359/mo (pricing update)

    Shopify's pricing page now shows pay-yearly prices of $89/mo for Grow (was $79) and $359/mo for Advanced (was $299). Pay-monthly prices are unchanged at $105 and $399, and Basic is unchanged at $29 yearly / $39 monthly. That cuts the annual-billing discount from about 25% to about 15% on Grow and about 10% on Advanced, and raises a year of Grow from $948 to $1,068 and a year of Advanced from $3,588 to $4,308.

  • Pricing Slack

    Slack Enterprise+ price is now public: $45 per user per month (annual)

    Slack's pricing page used to say "Contact sales for pricing" for Enterprise+. It now lists $45 USD per user per month when paying annually, with the Contact Sales button still shown.

  • Pricing Anthropic Claude

    Claude Enterprise now requires 20 seats minimum (Anthropic pricing update)

    The Enterprise plan on Anthropic's pricing page still reads US$20/seat/month billed annually plus usage at API rates, but now adds "20 seat minimum." That implies at least $4,800 a year in seat fees before any usage.

  • Pricing Figma

    New Figma Specs beta added to paid seats

    Figma's pricing page adds Figma Specs, a beta for writing structured specs on the canvas, available on paid seats. Full, Dev, and Collab seat descriptions now all include Figma Specs, and Full seats now explicitly list Figma Motion. Figma Make also gains secrets management and a scaffolded Payload backend (database, APIs, auth, admin panel).

  • Pricing Audible

    Annual plans: cancellation now effective next billing cycle

    Audible's plan page now says its annual Premium plans ($149.50/yr for 12 credits, $229.50/yr for 24 credits) can be canceled anytime "effective next billing cycle," where it previously just said "Cancel anytime." The "Save 16%" labels on the annual plans were also removed. Prices did not change.

    October 15, 2026Audible history →
  • Pricing Verizon

    Verizon Regulatory Charge rising to $0.26 per line on Nov 1, 2026 (and USF at a record 42%)

    Verizon's surcharges support page now states: "Effective November 1, 2026, the monthly Verizon Wireless Regulatory Charge for voice-capable devices will increase from $0.21 to $0.26 per line." That is a 24% jump on one of Verizon's own mobile surcharges (not a government tax). Combined with the unchanged Administrative and Telco Recovery Charge ($3.78 per voice line), Verizon's two flat per-line surcharge lines go from $3.99 to $4.04. Separately, Verizon disclosures show the Federal Universal Service Charge using a 42% factor, matching the FCC's Q4 2026 contribution factor (DA-26-946), the program's highest on record after 38.8% in Q3.

  • Pricing Squarespace

    Squarespace pricing now lists AI credits by plan, and Basic's 2% store fee

    The Squarespace pricing page now lists AI credit allotments by plan (Basic: 10 one-time; Core: 20/mo; Plus: 40/mo) and shows a 2% online store transaction fee on Basic, while Core and above advertise no online-store transaction fees. Listed plan prices ($25 / $39 / $65 / $139) and card rates were unchanged in the snapshot.
