Subscription price increases & pricing changes in 2026 (tracker)

16 changes logged · Updated October 21, 2026

Price hikes rarely come with a press release. They show up as a new number on a pricing page, a fee line on a support page, or a plan that quietly loses a feature. This page collects every pricing change our daily check has caught, newest first, with the date we saw it and a link to the source.

October 2026

  • Pricing Squarespace

    Plan cards rewritten; prices unchanged, Basic's 2% store fee now spelled out

    Squarespace redesigned the plan cards on its pricing page. Monthly prices are unchanged at $25 (Basic), $39 (Core), $65 (Plus) and $139 (Advanced), or $19, $29, $49 and $99 a month billed yearly, and card rates still start at 2.9% + $0.30 (2.7% on Plus, 2.5% on Advanced). The Basic card now states a "2% online store transaction fee" directly, while Core and up say "No fees on online store transactions." Each card now lists its AI credits (10 one-time on Basic, 20, 40 and 120 per month on the higher plans), which were previously shown only in the comparison table. The cards dropped the line that priced automated tax calculation at 0.15%, 0.10% or 0.05% per transaction (maximum $0.99) and now just say "Automated taxes and shipping"; the page doesn't say whether that fee itself changed.

  • Pricing Spotify

    Spotify Premium free trial is 2 months until Nov 1, 2026, then $12.99/mo

The offer covers Premium Individual only and is "only available if you haven't tried Premium before." After the two free months it costs $12.99 per month. The page now adds "Offer ends November 1, 2026."

Slack describes it as an "Enterprise+ add-on giving field workers Slack for task management, agentic support & secure Salesforce access." It appears in the feature list of every plan and in the comparison table as an add-on. The page shows no price for it.

    October 20, 2026Slack history →
  • Pricing Shopify

    Shopify yearly prices: Grow shows $79 or $89/mo, Advanced $299 or $359/mo (price test)

    Shopify's pricing page is showing visitors two different pay-yearly prices for the same plans. On October 19, 2026, ten back-to-back checks of shopify.com/pricing returned Grow at $79/mo and Advanced at $299/mo six times, and Grow at $89/mo and Advanced at $359/mo four times. Pay-monthly prices were the same in every version ($105/mo for Grow, $399/mo for Advanced), and Basic stayed at $29/mo yearly and $39/mo monthly. Our daily snapshots showed the higher $89/$359 yearly prices every morning from October 15 through 18 and the older $79/$299 prices again today, so the higher prices look like a price test shown to some visitors, not a completed price increase. Over a year, the gap is $120 on Grow ($948 vs $1,068) and $720 on Advanced ($3,588 vs $4,308).

  • Pricing GitHub

    AI secret detection now covers push protection, billed by usage

    In the GitHub Secret Protection section of GitHub's pricing page, the "Copilot secret scanning" row is renamed "AI detection for alerts" ("Use AI to detect unstructured secrets, like passwords, in existing code"). A new row, "AI detection for push protection," uses AI to "detect unstructured secrets, like passwords, and block pushes that contain them," and is listed as "Usage-based" rather than included in the plan price.

    October 17, 2026GitHub history →
  • Pricing Cloudflare

    Cloudflare Pipelines is no longer free: Basin pricing is $0.04/GB transforms, $0.03-$0.06/GB delivery

    Cloudflare's plans page used to list Pipelines ingestion and delivery to R2 as "TBD (free during beta)". It now shows real prices for the product, now part of the Cloudflare Basin family. Stream ingress is free and unlimited. SQL transforms include 50 GB a month, then $0.04 per GB. Sink delivery includes 50 GB a month shared across sinks, then $0.03 per GB for JSON or $0.06 per GB for Parquet or Iceberg. The page also adds catalog pricing (1 million operations a month included, then $9 per million; compaction at $0.005 per GB after 10 GB and $2 per million objects after 1 million) and query pricing (10 GB of compressed data scanned a month included, then $0.0025 per GB, which is $2.50 per TB, with a 10 MB minimum per query). R2 storage and operations are billed separately.

  • Pricing Shopify

    Shopify raised annual prices: Grow now $89/mo, Advanced $359/mo (pricing update)

That cuts the annual-billing discount from about 25% to about 15% on Grow and about 10% on Advanced, and raises a year of Grow from $948 to $1,068 and a year of Advanced from $3,588 to $4,308.

  • Pricing Verizon

    Verizon Regulatory Charge rising to $0.26 per line on Nov 1, 2026 (and USF at a record 42%)

    Verizon's surcharges support page now states: "Effective November 1, 2026, the monthly Verizon Wireless Regulatory Charge for voice-capable devices will increase from $0.21 to $0.26 per line." That is a 24% jump on one of Verizon's own mobile surcharges (not a government tax). Combined with the unchanged Administrative and Telco Recovery Charge ($3.78 per voice line), Verizon's two flat per-line surcharge lines go from $3.99 to $4.04. Separately, Verizon disclosures show the Federal Universal Service Charge using a 42% factor, matching the FCC's Q4 2026 contribution factor (DA-26-946), the program's highest on record after 38.8% in Q3.
