# Leave Verizon Dollars and your balance is forfeited

Verizon's loyalty-program terms gain a new opt-out section: quitting Verizon Dollars forfeits your unspent Dollars, ends refunds on anything you bought with them, and takes away the waived activation and upgrade fees. Elsewhere, Slack hid its $45 Enterprise+ price behind "Contact sales" again, and the Audible and Mailchimp pricing pages flipped between the versions we've seen before.
