# Leave Verizon Dollars and your balance is forfeited

Verizon's loyalty-program terms gain a new opt-out section: quitting Verizon Dollars forfeits your unspent Dollars, ends refunds on anything you bought with them, and takes away the waived activation and upgrade fees. Elsewhere, Slack hid its $45 Enterprise+ price behind "Contact sales" again, and the Audible and Mailchimp pricing pages flipped between the versions we've seen before.

# Also today: a January 1 cloud cut-off cluster, and a UK Runna price rise

Four services lose cloud or app features at the turn of the year, all verified against official pages on November 14. Hunter ends the HunterSMART cloud-to-cloud link to Google Home and Alexa on January 1, 2027 (the app and remote still work; Matter is only for eligible models). KYMCO shuts the Noodoe app and cloud worldwide the same day; only the speed reading on the dash keeps working as normal. The ZWILLING app used with Fresh & Save shuts December 31, 2026, with no logins after that, so owners should save box contents and lists first; the vacuum boxes still work. Nedap ends MyPowerRouter.com on December 31, 2026, along with online monitoring, energy data, remote updates and remaining support; the unit's local screen still works.

Separately, Runna is raising UK prices from the first renewal on or after December 10, 2026: annual £99.99 to £109.99, monthly £15.99 to £17.99, and the Strava + Runna bundle £119.99 to £124.99, per Runna's own support page. US prices are unchanged. A guide, [Apps and devices that stop working Jan 1, 2027](/guides/apps-and-devices-that-stop-working-jan-1-2027/), puts the four shutdowns in a table with the existing remove.bg (Dec 1) and IKEA Gift Registry (Dec 27) deadlines, plus a calendar file for each.
