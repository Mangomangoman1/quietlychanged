# Todoist drops the "generalized" loophole from its no-AI-training promise

Doist's privacy policy now says Todoist user data isn't used to develop, improve, or train AI or machine learning models, with no "generalized/non-personalized" qualifier ([privacy policy](https://doist.com/privacy)).

Everything else today was noise: Shopify's and Slack's pricing pages rotated between versions we've seen before, Squarespace swapped back to its alternate plan-card layout with the same prices, Audible and Grammarly reworded marketing copy, PayPal's privacy page un-obfuscated contact emails, and Wix's terms page loaded only partly. Seven pages (Delta, Pinterest, Verizon) failed to fetch during the run but loaded fine on a retry minutes later; Roblox's terms page is still blocked (HTTP 403).
