# Cash App can auto-sell your bitcoin for ACH, and Squarespace meters AI
Two real updates today out of five raw page diffs. Cash App added terms for a new optional feature that sells bitcoin from your Hosted Balance to cover ACH debits. PayPal, Audible, and Wix also shifted in the snapshots, but those looked like email obfuscation, trial-button copy, and localization/DOM noise—not material policy or price changes.
