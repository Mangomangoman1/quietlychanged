# Stripe's rewritten privacy policy is now in force; Dropbox puts a number on AI use

Stripe's new Privacy Policy, which we previewed on November 3, is now the live version at stripe.com/privacy, "Last updated: November 20, 2026": AI voice agents that may call you, fraud signals (your IP address, email, phone and shipping address) shared with your card-issuing bank, and a new "Agentic Payments" section for purchases an AI agent makes on your behalf.

Also: Dropbox's plans page now lists "AI in Dropbox" on its Standard ($15/user/month) and Advanced ($24/user/month) plans, with "250 AI credits per person, per month".

Noise: Shopify rewrote its pricing-page marketing blocks (the "Earn 1% back" credit lines and AI-chat selling were already on the page), Audible moved its plan cards back up the page, Wix's terms page was fully captured again (same text as November 19), Venmo's legal menu, Hunter's blog navigation and Tubi's device ID. PayPal terms and privacy and Roblox terms are still HTTP 403; Shein terms is still bot-walled. Calm terms returned 403 to our crawler but fetched normally on retry and hasn't changed.
