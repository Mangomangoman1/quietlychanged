# Bitwarden renames Collections to "shared folders" and adds vault items to Send

Bitwarden's pricing page now uses the vocabulary of its November 19 redesign: the Families plan lists "Unlimited shared folders" instead of "Unlimited collections", sharing means creating "a shared vault" rather than "an Organization", and Bitwarden Send on Premium and Families now covers "Text, files, and vault items" instead of "Text and files". Prices are unchanged ($19.80 a year for Premium, $47.88 for Families).

Noise: Audible dropped its plan-comparison table again (it has flipped in and out since November 19), Wix's terms page was only partly captured again (same flip-flop as November 20), and Tubi's device ID. PayPal terms and privacy and Roblox terms are still HTTP 403; Shein terms is still bot-walled. Calm terms again returned 403 to our crawler but fetched normally with a plain browser ID and hasn't changed; the tracker now retries that way automatically.
