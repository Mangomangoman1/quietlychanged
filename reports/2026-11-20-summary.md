# Apple TV's Black Friday deal: 6 months at $6.99 a month, ending November 30

Apple's Apple TV page now offers 6 months at $6.99 a month (normally $14.99) to new and qualified returning subscribers until 8 p.m. PT on November 30, then it renews at $14.99; the "1 week free" card is gone from the page, though the fine print says other new subscribers may still get a 7-day trial.

Also: Todoist added Todoist Comms (beta) team chat to every plan, with 1 month of message history on Beginner and Pro and full history on Business, and now caps Beginner and Pro teams at 5 guests. JetBlue added November and December 2027 to its peak checked-bag calendar (Nov 4–10, Nov 18–30 and all of December).

Noise: Slack's Enterprise+ price flipped back to "Contact sales for pricing" (it has alternated with "$45" since early November), Audible's plan cards moved down the page, Xbox's Black Friday menu link, Wix's terms page (only the opening captured again), Tubi's device ID and Booking.com calendar IDs. PayPal terms and privacy and Roblox terms are still HTTP 403; Shein terms is still bot-walled.
