# Airbnb renamed its off-platform fee rules; Audible's Standard plan card now warns picks don't roll over

A quiet day: two small real updates out of nine raw page diffs. Airbnb's Terms of Service renamed the two policies that govern fees and payments outside its platform: the "Offline Fee Policy" is now called the "policy on pricing and payment transparency," and the "Off-Platform Policy" is now the "policy on limiting host-imposed contracts and other requirements." The surrounding rules in the terms, such as not taking bookings or payments off Airbnb to avoid fees, read the same. Audible's membership page now says right on the Standard plan card that "Unused monthly audiobook selections don't roll over" (this was already in the page's FAQ), and its $229.50-a-year Premium plan is now labeled "24 audiobooks." Prices didn't change. The rest was layout or noise: Squarespace and Shopify redesigned their plan cards with the same prices (Squarespace's cards no longer list AI credit counts, and Shopify moved card rates off the cards into its comparison table, still 2.9% + 30¢ on Basic), Mailchimp's starting price didn't load in our copy, Grammarly swapped its "Try Pro for $0 today" headline for "Great Writing Starts With a Plan," PayPal's contact emails changed format only, and Canva removed a "[Placeholder]" footer link. All 137 pages fetched.
