# Proton doubles free-account inactivity window from 12 months to 24

One real change today. Proton's Terms of Service now say free accounts can be suspended or deleted after 24 months of inactivity, not 12: https://proton.me/legal/terms. Logging in on web, desktop or mobile once every two years is enough to stay active; paid accounts are still always treated as active. Warnings before deletion remain only "a mere courtesy" (the Oct 20 change). One leftover sentence still talks about staying active "in a period of 12 months" for insurmountable-circumstances support requests.

Noise filtered out: Cloudflare's privacy "diff" was a null byte in one curly quote; Shopify and Audible flipped through known pricing-page A/B variants; Venmo's legal-menu list that appeared Nov 5 disappeared again (capture flip-flop); Tubi's device ID rotated. PlayStation terms still SSLError; Roblox terms still HTTP 403.
