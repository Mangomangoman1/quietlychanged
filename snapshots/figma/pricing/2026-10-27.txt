Seats give users access to Figma’s products. The different seats are designed to support different user workflows.
The
Full seat
includes full access to all Figma products, including Figma Design, Figma Make, Figma Draw, Figma Motion, Figma Sites, Dev Mode, FigJam, Figma Slides, Figma Buzz, and Figma Specs.
The
Dev seat
includes full access to Dev Mode, FigJam, Figma Slides, Figma Buzz, and Figma Specs. It also gives users view and comment access in Figma Design files.
The
Collab seat
includes full access to FigJam, Figma Slides, Figma Buzz, and Figma Specs. It also gives users view and comment access in Figma Design files. It only includes
basic inspection
on Dev Mode.
If you’re on a paid plan, you can also let others view and comment on your files without purchasing extra seats. This view and comment access only includes
basic inspection
on Dev Mode.
Only organization admins and billing group admins are able to view and manage billing. You can view a breakdown of your bill and manage seats from the Admin Dashboard in product.

If you’re on a
Professional
plan, if you add new paid seats to your team during your annual subscription term, Figma charges you a separate monthly subscription for those seats at the monthly price.
Once an admin approves a new seat, it will be automatically added to your next monthly invoice. The cost of the seat will be prorated based on the day it was approved until the end of your current monthly billing term.
You will continue to get monthly invoices for newly added seats unless you proactively add those seats to your annual subscription or remove the seats.
If you’re on an
Organization
Enterprise
plan, admins need to approve new seats that incur costs. By default, approved seats are included in your next invoice with prorated pricing, based on the date they were approved until the end of the annual subscription term. You’ll be invoiced quarterly for new seats added during your annual subscription term.

Figma AI uses a credit system that is shared across all Figma AI features and products. Every seat in Figma includes a monthly allotment of AI credits. The number of credits and which features you can use them with depends on your plan and seat type.
Full seats include:
3,000 credits/month on the Professional plan
3,500 credits/month on the Organization plan
4,250 credits/month on the Enterprise plan
All other seats (Collab, Dev, and View) and users on the Starter plan receive 500 credits/month.
