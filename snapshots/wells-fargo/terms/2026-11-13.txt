5(a). Applicability
The provisions in this Section apply only to electronic fund transfers (EFTs) that debit or credit a Consumer's checking, savings, or other asset account and are subject to Regulation E (subpart A), which implements the federal Electronic Fund Transfer Act.
When applicable, we may rely on any exceptions to the provisions in this Section that are covered in Regulation E. All terms in this Section not defined in this Agreement but defined in Regulation E will have the meaning given in Regulation E. For example, the term "unauthorized electronic fund transfer" (or unauthorized EFT) as used in this Section shall mean an electronic fund transfer from your account that is initiated by a person other than you without actual authority to initiate the transfer and from which you receive no benefit.
A transaction you initiate or otherwise authorize, even if induced to do so through fraud or deceit, is not considered an unauthorized electronic fund transfer for purposes of this Section. To learn about common scams and how to avoid them, please visit our online Security Center at
www.wellsfargo.com/privacy-security/fraud/
Note:
Among other things, this Section does not apply to wire transfers that debit or credit a consumer’s account. This section does not apply to remittance transfers (e.g. ExpressSend
(or any successor product(s) we may offer from time to time) and consumer-initiated international wire transfers). For additional information about wire transfers or remittance transfers, please refer to Section 8 of this Agreement.
