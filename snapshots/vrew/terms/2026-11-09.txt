Upcoming changes to Vrew Terms of Service and Privacy Policy on December 1, 2026

Hello from the Vrew team.

We're continuing to develop Vrew to make creating the videos you want easier with AI, from generating images and videos to editing them. We want to better understand what you're trying to create and provide help that fits your creative process.

To support these changes, we're updating our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy effective December 1, 2026. We're changing the account rules to allow only one account per person, and we'll use AI to analyze project content and editing history to provide a personalized editing experience. Here are the details.

Key changes to the Terms of Service

Only one account per person is allowed.

Starting December 1, you may use only one account, even if you have different email addresses.

The plan changes taking effect on December 1 will give you more options to choose a plan that fits your usage. If you run out of credits, you'll also be able to purchase Additional Credits on a paid plan. If you've been using multiple paid accounts because the existing plan options were limited, please use a single account going forward. 

[Learn more about the plan changes]

Multiple people may not share one account.

Please create and use a separate account for each person, so that people's materials and usage histories don't get mixed together in a shared account.

This applies whenever multiple people currently share an account, including within companies and teams. Please prepare to use your own accounts before the revised Terms take effect on December 1.

We also understand that there are practical reasons why people may need to share an account. Separately from this update to the Terms of Service, the Vrew team is working on a way for companies and teams to manage multiple people's accounts more easily. We'll share more information once the details are decided.

Other changes to the Terms of Service

We've also clarified rules that weren't clearly covered in the current Terms, including rights to and use of content, and the procedures for restricting use when the Terms are violated. Please see the revised Terms of Service below for details.

Key changes to the Privacy Policy

We're introducing AI analysis to provide an editing experience that fits your work.

To understand creative patterns, such as video topics and editing styles, and offer help that fits them, we'll use AI to analyze project content and editing history.

The analysis will also cover materials in your projects, such as images, videos, audio, and captions (assets). We'll organize the results into information such as categories that classify video topics and summaries that condense the content, and use this information to provide an editing experience better suited to you.
