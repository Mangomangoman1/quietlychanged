Delivering Personalized Emotiv-IQ Analytics, Data Pooling, & Model Development:

You may disable Emotiv-IQ at any time in Account Settings. Doing so halts future ingestion of your Neural Data into LBM training. It does not remove contributions already incorporated into a trained model, because individual contributions cannot be isolated from model weights once training is complete. Where technically feasible we will apply retraining or machine unlearning techniques at the next full training cycle. EMOTIV does not sell raw Neural Data or Personal Information. Any model parameters, weights or API outputs derived from or licensed from the LBM are aggregated or de-identified mathematical outputs. We design, test and maintain the LBM using measures intended to prevent the extraction or re-identification of individual users from its parameters or outputs, including differential privacy techniques and periodic model inversion and membership inference testing, and we contractually prohibit any recipient from attempting re-identification.
