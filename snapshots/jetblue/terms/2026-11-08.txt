Fares
All fares are subject to availability and may change without notice. Fares may not be available on all days or on all flights. Certain fares may only be available for new bookings only and/or must be purchased at jetblue.com. All fares must be purchased at time of reservation, and are nonrefundable and nontransferable. Unless otherwise specified, fares are nonstop and one-way (some fares, including most fares to the U.K./Europe, are round-trip fares). Reservations made one week or more prior to a flight's scheduled departure may be cancelled without penalty up to 24 hours after the reservation is made.
Fares are inclusive of government taxes and fees.
