6. Automated decision-making
Sometimes we use or operate computer programs that use your personal data to make decisions about you, or to do something that substantially and directly contributes to a decision we make about you. This section explains where we do that, what personal data those programs use, and what you can do if you disagree with the outcome.
Decisions made by computer programs for sanctions and financial crime screening
We must not provide our services to people or organizations that are subject to government sanctions. We use a screening tool provided by a third party service provider to check people who apply for, or already use, our services against sanctions lists and other government watchlists to support our compliance with our obligations. Screening happens when you sign up, and on an ongoing basis while you use our services.
The tool automatically compares your personal data against those lists and produces a result. Most people are cleared automatically and nothing further happens. Where the tool identifies a possible match, it flags the result for review by a member of our team, and further screening involving your verification and identification data to match you to your identity documents may be required.
This requires the use of the following kinds of personal information:
Contact information (for example, name, email address, address).
Verification and identification data.
Account information.
Payment information.
Device and location information.
Information about you from sanctions lists, other public sources, and our screening provider.
This information, and the result produced by the tool, are used by Xero to support decision making for the following kinds of decisions:
Refuse an application for a subscription or trial.
Suspend or block access to a user account.
Suspend or block access to a Xero organization.
Terminate a subscription.
Decline a payment or other transaction.
Restrict access to particular features.
Report a matter to a regulator or law enforcement agency.
A member of our team reviews the tool's result before we make any of these decisions, and can decide that no further action is needed. The tool's result is a key factor in that decision.
