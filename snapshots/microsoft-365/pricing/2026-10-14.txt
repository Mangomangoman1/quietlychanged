Office 2024 is sold as a one-time purchase, which means you pay a single, up-front cost to get Office apps for one computer. One-time purchases are available for both PCs and Macs. However, there are no upgrade options, which means if you plan to upgrade to the next major release, you'll have to buy it at full price.
Microsoft 365 Personal, Microsoft 365 Family, Microsoft 365 Premium, and Microsoft 365 Pro are subscriptions that include powerful productivity apps and creativity tools with AI-powered features. In addition to desktop versions of popular Microsoft 365 apps like Word, PowerPoint, Excel, and Outlook with Microsoft Copilot,
you also get spacious cloud storage, advanced security, and cloud-connected features that let you collaborate on files in real time. With a subscription, you'll always have the latest features, fixes, and security updates along with ongoing tech support at no extra cost. You can choose to pay for your subscription on a monthly or yearly basis, and use your apps on multiple PCs, Macs, tablets, and phones.
Microsoft 365 Personal
is for one person.

extends many of the same benefits to multiple people in a household.

includes extensive AI usage and access to exclusive Copilot features.
The right subscription depends on whether you're purchasing for yourself, your family, or need more advanced capabilities.
If you primarily want access to desktop versions of productivity apps with Copilot,
advanced security, and cloud storage, Microsoft 365 Personal or Microsoft 365 Family may be enough. If you want extensive AI usage and access to exclusive Copilot features,
Microsoft 365 Premium can provide added value to help you tackle demanding tasks, from researching business ideas and analyzing complex data to creating high-impact presentations.
For most freelancers, solopreneurs, and side business owners looking to maximize productivity and protect their work, Microsoft 365 Premium is the recommended choice. It provides extensive AI usage and exclusive Copilot features,
advanced security, 2 TB of cloud storage, and productivity apps with Copilot
built in to help manage clients, projects, finances, and business communications. Those who need 1 TB of cloud storage, advanced security, and productivity apps with Copilot
can choose Microsoft 365 Personal, while Microsoft 365 Family is a good fit for households that want to share subscription benefits
across multiple users.
For students who want the ultimate AI and productivity experience, Microsoft 365 Premium is an excellent choice. It combines extensive AI usage and exclusive Copilot features,
advanced security, spacious cloud storage, and productivity apps with Copilot
built in that can help students stay organized, manage coursework, collaborate on projects, and tackle academic responsibilities with confidence. Students looking for cloud storage, advanced security, and productivity apps with Copilot
can also choose Microsoft 365 Personal.
