Frequently Asked Questions
Can I buy Unified Access as a platform or purchase individual products separately?
Yes. Unified Access can be purchased as an integrated platform, and individual products within the platform can also be purchased separately based on your organization’s needs.
Which products are included in the Unified Access platform?
Unified Access brings together Enterprise Password Manager, Device Trust, SaaS Manager, and Privileged Access in one platform.
How is Unified Access different from Enterprise Password Manager?
Enterprise Password Manager helps organizations protect and manage credentials. Unified Access builds on those capabilities to help govern access across people, AI agents, and machines.
Does Unified Access work with existing identity and security tools?
Yes. Unified Access is designed to work with your existing identity and security environment. Explore available integrations in the
1Password Marketplace
, or contact our sales team to discuss your specific tools and requirements.

Unified Access pricing is tailored to your organization’s needs and the capabilities you require.
